How it works:

  • Core thesis: real wealth comes from *acquiring* businesses, not building each one from scratch — buying gives you a 'multiple effect' (leverage on existing assets).
  • The smart operators eventually buy because building every time doesn't scale.
  • Wrong way to evaluate: judging purely on profitability, P&Ls, and raw numbers. Necessary, but not sufficient.

  • Takeaway for a side hustler: don't chase the highest-cash-flow listing; chase the one that compounds your existing skills, audience, or assets.

Four Intel Arc Pro B70 GPUs for 128GB local-AI VRAM.

If you're weighing local-AI hardware against a Mac mini, this is the cheap-VRAM play: 128GB across four sub-$1,000 Intel cards instead of one $2,000+ RTX 5090.

details

What it is: A test of Intel's new Arc Pro B70 GPU for local LLM inference — 32GB VRAM per card, under $1,000, ganged 4x for 128GB total.

The hardware & cost angle (your focus):

  • Arc Pro B70 — 32GB VRAM, under $1,000 each. Needs an external power cable (unlike the smaller B50).
  • Arc Pro B50 — smaller sibling, draws all power from the PCIe bus (no extra cable). Alex called it the best bang-for-buck GPU of 2025.
  • 4x B70 rig = 128GB VRAM for roughly the price of a single RTX 5090 — that's the headline. A 128GB Mac mini/Studio config costs comparably but Macs use unified memory (slower bandwidth, no multi-GPU scaling).
  • Compared against NVIDIA RTX Pro 4000 and AMD R9700 in the full video.

How it performs:

  • Test model: Qwen 34B AWQ in a VM.
  • All cards run at PCIe Gen 5, ~68 GB/s per-GPU bandwidth.
  • Scaling 1→2 GPUs: prompt processing doubled (9,281 → 18,170 tok/s).
  • But token generation dropped (72 → 52 tok/s) — GPU-to-GPU PCIe is capped at ~63 GB/s, so smaller models see no gain or even a regression from multi-GPU.
  • Takeaway: multi-card helps big models / prompt throughput, hurts small-model generation speed.

Tools & links:

  • No repos in transcript — gear links (Thunderbolt 5 SSD, T4 enclosure) are affiliate Amazon links, not relevant to the AI build.

Why it matters for you: This is the direct Mac-mini alternative for local AI — four cheap Intel cards get you 128GB VRAM at RTX 5090 money, but watch the PCIe bottleneck before assuming more cards = more speed.
