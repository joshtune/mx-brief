What it is: Alex's take on NVIDIA's newly announced RTX Spark — a consumer-facing repackage of the DGX Spark AI machine, shipping in laptops and desktops (and shipping with Windows, which is what's angering people).

The hardware (what you care about):

  • CPU: up to 20-core ARM (MediaTek die)
  • GPU: Blackwell die, ~6,144 cores — spec'd on paper like an RTX 5070 / 5070 Ti
  • Memory: up to 128GB unified memory the GPU can pull from for AI — the headline number for running large local LLMs
  • It's not a new chip: same chiplet trick as the DGX Spark — Blackwell GPU die fused to a MediaTek CPU die over NVLink. RTX Spark = DGX Spark reskinned for the mass market
  • N1 vs N1X variants give some config flexibility

Real-world expectation (the honest part):

  • The on-paper "5070 Ti" comparison is misleading — that's a discrete card with its own VRAM
  • The RTX Spark is an iGPU sharing system memory at a fraction of the watts
  • Based on Alex's DGX Spark testing, he bets real-world performance lands closer to a high-end AMD Strix Halo machine than an actual 5070 Ti
