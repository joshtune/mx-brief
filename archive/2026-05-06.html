Swap vague mentor asks for specific, low-cost proof.

What it is: A rapid-fire rewrite of the 6 worst mentor-DM phrases into asks that actually convert.

How it works:

  • ❌ "Can I pick your brain?" → ✅ "I tried what you said about X. Want to see what I built?" (show execution, not extraction)
  • ❌ "Let's grab coffee" → ✅ "Can I get you for 15 minutes on Zoom on one specific question?" (cheap, scoped, async-friendly)

The meta-move:

  • Never ask for the mentor relationship — earn it by showing up repeatedly with small wins
  • Specificity > flattery in every single rewrite
  • Always pre-bound the time ask (15 min, 60 sec, one question)

Win by inventing new product categories, not copying.

A 60-second framework for spotting white-space product opportunities — useful if you're side-hustling toward a real business, not a clone.

details

What it is: Shaan Puri's quick rule for picking winning products: don't make a better X, make a new format with no direct competitors.

The core thesis:

  • Good product ≠ better version of an incumbent (e.g., a better Grunins)
  • Good product = new white space — a form factor nobody else owns
  • Copycats might get "a little bit of success" but won't sell their business or win the category

Case study — nicotine pouch white space:

  • Zyn created a new form factor (oral nicotine pouch, no tobacco)
  • Now copycats are emerging in adjacent white space:
  • Wip — caffeine/energy pouches that look like Zyn but aren't nicotine
  • Other "ultra"-style energy pouch brands
  • The winner isn't the one making a slightly better Zyn — it's the one inventing the next format (energy pouch, functional pouch, etc.)

The pattern to copy:

  • Find an existing successful form factor
  • Ask: what's the *adjacent* form factor nobody owns yet?
  • Build there, not in the crowded incumbent lane

  • No specific tools mentioned — this is a pure framework segment
  • Sponsor plug: HubSpot CRM (free)

Why it matters for you: If you're building a side project hoping it becomes a real business, this is the filter — skip "better mousetrap" ideas, hunt for an unclaimed format. Applies directly to dev tools, SaaS categories, and consumer apps.

  • Fixed `Bash(mkdir *)`, `Bash(touch *)` allow rules being ignored for in-project paths
  • Fixed 1-hour prompt cache TTL silently downgrading to 5 minutes
  • Fixed OAuth refresh race after wake-from-sleep logging out all sessions

Breaking changes:

  • Gateway model discovery now opt-in (was auto in 2.1.126–2.1.128) — set `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_GATEWAY_MODEL_DISCOVERY=1` if you relied on it
  • Plugin manifests: `themes` and `monitors` should move under `"experimental": { ... }` — top-level still works but `claude plugin validate` will warn
