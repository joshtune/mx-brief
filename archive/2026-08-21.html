  • Plugin marketplace for installing third-party components.

  • Self-evolving software is already realistic, so a coding agent evolving *itself* is realistic too.

  • DeepSeek's plugin granularity *is* that grammar. That's why Cole calls this the shape of the future, not this specific tool.

Trade-offs Cole flags:

  • Rough, unrefined, early. Minimalism costs you the batteries-included ergonomics of Claude Code/Codex.
  • The middle path he keeps pointing at is Pi — self-extensible, but more finished than DeepSeek's harness.

The framing — Dan Shapiro's 5 levels of AI coding (car analogy):

  • L0 — stick shift: you write the code, AI is "spicy autocomplete"

  • L3 — hands off the wheel: agent writes most of the code, but *you* still do planning + validation. Cole says this is where most engineers are and mostly *should* be — most reliable, but you are the bottleneck

  • Validation gate — code doesn't ship until it's been reviewed and validated inside the factory, since you're not there to do it
