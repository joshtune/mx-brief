AI News: New ChatGPT & Google Models (14 Updates Worth Knowing)

Matt Wolfe · 2026-11-06 · 101,459 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Weekly AI news: GPT Image 2.6, open-weight image models

Skip it unless you generate images in your app: the only web-dev-relevant bit is GPT Image 2.6 in the API, and the rest is demos and consumer chatter.

What it is: Matt's weekly AI news roundup. The transcript we have only covers the first two items (OpenAI's image model and Pruna's open-weight image model), so the other 12 "updates" are unverified here.

How it works:

  • GPT Image 2.6 (OpenAI): Available in ChatGPT and the API. Roughly 2x faster than the previous image model per OpenAI's demo.
  • Animated GIFs: The new trick is that it generates animated GIFs from a prompt. Matt got a roller-skating monkey in about 20-30 seconds.
  • Editing: Follow-up edit prompts work on GIFs too (add a baseball hat, move to the street), ~30 seconds each.
  • Speed test: A standard image (San Diego skyline at sunset) took 20.4 seconds. That's faster than before but not instant, so don't plan on synchronous UX.
  • PH3 Image (Pruna AI): Turns MiniMax's H3 video model into an open-weight image model. Fine-tunable and alterable.
  • Hardware needs: Weights are about 55 GB, and the full download is nearly 100 GB. At 8 megapixels it needs about 56 GB VRAM at 16-bit and about 21 GB at 4-bit. The 4-bit version fits a 4090 or 5090. The full version wants 128 GB+ unified memory (high-end Mac or DGX Spark).
  • Quality check: Matt tried the free Hugging Face Space. The output was nowhere near ChatGPT's quality, and the "San Diego skyline" didn't look like San Diego. Treat it as a tinkerer's model, not a product dependency.
  • Format: Matt says the rest are smaller updates and promises a rapid-fire section. The transcript cuts off before it.
