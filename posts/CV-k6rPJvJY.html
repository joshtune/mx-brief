Nate shows a full Claude-driven editing pipeline (cuts, captions, B-roll, motion graphics, SFX, self-QA) packaged as reusable skills, which is a template for any instruction-heavy agent workflow.

What it is: Nate records raw talking-head footage, hands the file to Claude, and gets back a finished edit with cuts, captions, B-roll, motion graphics, music and sound effects.

How it works:

  • Transcribe: Claude transcribes every word of the recording.
  • Cut: it removes retakes and any pause longer than roughly 0.2 seconds.
  • Plan: it plans the story, then finds B-roll for each phrase. Sources are real clips, screen recordings of sites he mentions, or AI-generated video when nothing real exists.
  • Motion graphics: built in code, with each graphic timed to the exact word it illustrates.
  • Audio: music is picked by mood, and sound effects land on actions (e.g. a tap sound when a logo appears).
  • Self-check: before Nate sees the edit, Claude checks its own work for lip sync, caption contrast and loudness.
  • Style: Nate explained how he likes his reels, and every correction he gave was saved into a skill. Those skills make the style repeatable.
