AI Builds the Websites That Pay You Monthly

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-09 · 18,394 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Rank-and-rent local lead-gen sites built with AI.

It's a low-code side hustle where you build local service websites with AI and rent the leads to businesses for $300–$3,000/month each.

What it is: "Rank and rent" — build local-service lead-gen websites, rank them on Google, then sell the leads to a local business owner in that niche.

How it works:

  • Pick a growing niche + fast-growing city (he uses pool service in Bentonville, AR — validated via Google Trends showing "pool service near me" trending up).
  • Buy an exact match domain (EMD) like `bentonvillepoolservice.com` — service + city — because Google reads it as a strong relevancy signal.
  • Build a generic, SEO-optimized site on one platform (domain, hosting, email all in Hostinger — no code, no cross-platform wiring).
  • Site ranks organically → visitors call or fill out a form → that's a lead.
  • You own the site, domain, and phone number; reach out to a local operator and sell them the leads.
  • Volume game — he frames it as a "trot line": more sites = more hooks = more leads. Not every site hits, so copy-paste the winner across niches and cities.

How the AI build works:

  • Uses Hostinger Horizons (AI website builder) with two chat windows: a main build chat and Cody, a help-desk assistant that writes better prompts for you to paste into the main chat.
  • Example prompt: "Build a generic lead-gen site for a pool service business in Bentonville, AR... use SEO best practices to generate organic leads."

Tools & links:

  • Hostinger Horizons — AI no-code website + domain + hosting; code KOERNEROFFICE for 10% off
  • hostinger.com/domain-name-search — check EMD availability
  • trends.google.com — validate niche demand and seasonality

Why it matters for you: This is a concrete, low-capital side hustle — the whole build stack is one AI platform, so you can spin up and test lead-gen sites without writing code or stitching services together.
