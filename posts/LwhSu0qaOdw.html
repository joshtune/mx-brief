Don't quit to start a business — use OPX first.

If you want a side hustle, this gives you a 3-question filter to launch with other people's money, network, and infrastructure instead of risking your own.

What it is: A contrarian take on "just go be an entrepreneur" — plus the OPX framework Codie uses to de-risk any big move.

How it works:

  • The premise: 90% of startups fail, and early mistakes are inevitable — so make them on *someone else's dime* before going solo.
  • OPX = "Other People's X," where X is whatever asset you need: money, contacts, infrastructure, credibility.
  • Run any major decision through the OPX check — three questions:
  • Whose capital can I use instead of mine?
  • Whose resources can I use instead of mine?
  • Whose network can I use instead of mine?
  • If the answer to all three is "my own," you're not thinking about it right.
  • Modeled on Goldman Sachs' discipline: never take a position without limiting your own exposure first.

Tools & links:

  • No external tools or repos mentioned in this clip — it's a mental framework, not software.

Why it matters for you: Before you sink savings or nights/weekends into a side hustle, the OPX check forces you to borrow leverage (capital, distribution, credibility) so your downside is capped and your first failures don't cost you everything.
