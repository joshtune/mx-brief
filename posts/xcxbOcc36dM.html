This New AI Makes Decisions for 18 Cents

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-21 · 41,275 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A cheap, fast AI model built just to make decisions, not generate content.

If you're building anything with an LLM-in-the-loop, this could replace expensive classification/routing calls with something dramatically cheaper and faster.

What it is:

  • "Jev" — a new AI model from a researcher who worked on ChatGPT, built specifically as a decision-maker rather than a content generator.
  • Input: context + a list of possible outcomes. Output: the most likely outcome plus a confidence score.

How it works / demos shown:

  • Fed 1,700 full emails (~4.2M input tokens) to classify as spam/not, urgency, whether they need a reply, and category — total cost: 18 cents.
  • Given a long-form video, found and scored 17 potential viral clips in ~3 seconds.
  • Controlled a browser to find a flight from Zurich to London in 7.1 seconds.
  • Pitch: replaces using a full LLM for small, high-volume decisions (lead scoring, ticket routing, email triage, clip selection) with a much faster/cheaper dedicated layer.

Tools & links:

  • No public repo, docs, or pricing page mentioned/linked in the video or description.
  • Access is gated: Nate says to comment "Jev" on the video or DM "JEV" on his Instagram to get the tool — no direct product URL given.

Why it matters for you: No hands-on build here (it's an announcement/demo video), but the core idea — offloading cheap, high-volume classification decisions from a full LLM to a dedicated decision model — is directly applicable to any pipeline (like this one) doing per-item triage or scoring at scale.
