The 3 People You Must Outwork to Get Rich

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-10 · 5,307 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Outwork your past self, role model, and doubter

A 60-second reframe for side hustlers: your real competition isn't the market, it's the version of you that quit yesterday.

What it is: A short motivational pitch: you don't need to outwork the world, just three people.

How it works:

  • The instructional secret: Competition is personal and sequential. Beat yesterday's you, copy and exceed a benchmark, and outlast the skeptics.
  • 1. Past you: Do today the hard thing you postponed yesterday (gym, early mornings, late nights). Past you optimized for comfort, not results.
  • 2. The person who has what you want: Don't envy them, study them. Look at how they think, what they sacrifice, and how they execute. Then do 10% more.
  • 3. The doubter counting on you to quit: Don't argue. Keep showing up until they can't ignore you.
  • Payoff claim: Do this long enough and you get free, not just rich.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle dies from quitting, not from competition. Pick one hard task you've been dodging, one operator to reverse-engineer, and keep shipping. The '10% more' rule is the only concrete, actionable bit here.

Caveat: It's pure mindset with no tactics, numbers, or case studies. Treat it as a pep talk, not a playbook.
