Billionaires share one trait: productive delusion.

A 60-second gut check on whether you'll actually push a side hustle through the miserable stretch, because Codie's claim is that reasonable people don't.

What it is: A short clip where Codie answers "what do billionaires have in common" with one word: delusional.

How it works:

  • Every billionaire she's met shared one trait: unreasonable, delusional optimism about what they could build.
  • Her evidence is her own path: quitting a first job, raising the first millions, imagining a giant empire. None of it was reasonable at the time.
  • The core claim: builders are wrong about how hard it will be, and that wrongness is what gets them to start and keep going.
  • The filter: if you need the odds to make sense before you act, you won't make it. Not an insult, just a prediction.
  • The instructional secret: don't wait to feel rational about the plan. Treat "this is unreasonable" as the expected state, not a warning sign.
