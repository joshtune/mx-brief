Podcast: Dave on shipping software with AI agents

It's an interview, not a build-along, so you get a 10-year Python dev's view of AI-assisted shipping but no repos or step-by-step workflow, which is what you usually come to Nate for.

What it is: A sit-down interview where Nate talks to Dave, a Python dev of 10+ years whose team has shipped 50+ AI solutions with Glido, about how AI coding tools changed software engineering. Nate isn't building anything on screen.

How it works:

  • Dave's workflow: He says he writes zero lines of code now. Everything goes through Codex or Claude Code.
  • Speed claims: Roughly 10x on work he already did professionally (vs. 2022, when he was a working freelance data scientist). He says it's more on work he couldn't do before.
  • Stack stretch: He's a Python/backend/data-science guy who had never written JavaScript. Coding agents let him build front ends and dashboards.
  • Bigger scope: One prompt can now produce something useful. That makes personal automations, internal company tools, client builds and sellable software products realistic, even with limited technical background.
  • Scaling risk: Dave warns that stakes rise with user count. Data privacy and protection obligations apply, and a security breach can't be undone once personal data leaks.
  • Team cadence: His team ships new features and updates weekly.
  • Caveat: The transcript I got cuts off early, in the specialization discussion (backend vs. frontend). The 'non-techie' playbook, the landmine examples, and the 'what I'd do starting today' advice from the intro teaser aren't covered here.
