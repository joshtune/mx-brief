Stacking 0% business credit cards for free cash

If you want side-hustle seed money without giving up equity, this is the 'card stacking' play, but the transcript is a teaser and skips the actual how-to.

What it is: Credit card stacking: applying for many 0% APR cards in a short window to unlock large credit lines, sign-up bonuses, and cash you can move into your bank account.

How it works:

  • Claim: up to $500,000 in credit at rates as low as 0%, repeatable every ~6 months
  • Claim: Chase can extend up to $150,000 without documentation
  • Case study: one person opened 13 cards in 10 days and got $150K at 0% interest
  • Each card paid a sign-up bonus, totaling about $15,600 in free cash
  • He wanted cash, not travel. Taking the bonuses as travel points could have been worth 7 to 21 times more.
  • Credit limit can be transferred into your bank account, reportedly the next day
  • The transcript is only a short clip. It gives no details on eligibility, credit score needs, fees, or how to qualify.

Why it matters for you: Cheap capital can fund a side hustle, but it's debt. A 0% rate ends, balance-transfer fees usually apply, and 13 hard inquiries in 10 days can hurt your credit. Treat the numbers as unverified promo claims, not a plan.
