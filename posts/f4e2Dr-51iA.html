Seven money rules nobody taught you.

Seven gut-check spending and earning rules that decide whether your side-hustle cash compounds or evaporates.

What it is: A 7-rule money framework Codie wishes school had taught — short, formula-driven, no fluff.

How it works:

  • Rule 1 — Stack, don't flex: Your 20s are for buying *skills*, not status. Stop shopping for things, start shopping for capability.
  • Rule 2 — The 3-month test: If a purchase doesn't generate income, it should cost less than 3 months of income. Otherwise it's a luxury you're financing with your future.
  • Rule 3 — Buy-it-multiple-times rule: If you can't afford to buy it more than once, you can't afford it. "Rich people buy margin, poor people buy vibes."
  • Rule 4 — Wait a week: Most spending is an emotional urge, not a need. Sit on it 7 days; if you still want it, maybe.
  • Rule 5 — Skill ceiling = income ceiling: Your income only grows when you grow. Want to earn more? Become worth more.
  • Rule 6 — Start investing early: Time is the cheat code nobody respects until it's gone. Compounding rewards the early, not the smart.
  • Rule 7 — The 5% net-worth shock: No single purchase should drop your net worth by more than 5%. Making money is mostly surviving the downsides — don't let one buy set you back.

  • No external tools, repos, or links mentioned — this is a pure mental-model rundown.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that wealth is rule-based, not willpower-based — for a side hustler, Rules 1, 2, and 5 are the lever: pour early cash into skills and income-generating assets, not lifestyle, and your hustle's profit actually compounds instead of leaking out.
