Live pricing teardown: why underpricing is costing you profit

Codie walks through a real accounting-firm case study showing that a pricing fix — not more customers or more hustle — 3x'd profit, which is the fastest lever a side-hustle owner can pull without new capital or a sales pitch.

What it is: A livestream teaching session built around Codie's "12 profit levers" framework, with pricing as the headline lever, illustrated through a live case study of an accounting firm client.

How it works:

  • Diagnose your business type first: cash-flow vs. cash-suck (leaky bucket) vs. owner-dependent — the fix differs by type.
  • Core claim: underpricing isn't humility or integrity, it's "subsidizing your customers with your own paycheck." A pricing problem often masquerades as a confidence problem.
  • Case study — Heather's accounting firm:
  • Was charging $500/mo, 40–60% underpriced.
  • Customer acquisition cost: $101/lead, only 1 in 10 closed, ~$1,500 real cost per client — meaning a 3-month breakeven, compounded by a $300 onboarding cost and churn.
  • Fixes applied: raised price, added an onboarding fee so clients pay the CAC upfront, added a free month as a sweetener tied to a 12-month commitment.
  • Math flex: a 25% price increase can 3x profit through the "value equation" — before touching anchoring, tiered pricing (good/better/best), bundling, or charging for speed.
  • Framing: "You don't have a product problem, you have a pricing problem" — applies across business types since most businesses "rhyme."
