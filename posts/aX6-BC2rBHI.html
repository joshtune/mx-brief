Who he is:

  • Likely the youngest billionaire in the world right now (the hosts' claim, hedged with "I think")
  • Former math olympiad kid

What Scale did (the business):

  • Sold training data to AI labs. Labs need data to train their models, and Scale supplied it

  • Described as a grindy, operational business: collecting data and delivering it clean to the labs

The exit:

  • Meta paid about $15B for a huge chunk of Scale
  • Much of the value was acquiring Alexander himself, not just the company
