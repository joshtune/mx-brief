How We’d Build a Business in 2026

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-08 · 46,383 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Seven rules for rebuilding a business from zero.

It's a 90-second checklist for starting a side hustle with no capital and no audience — sell first, build second, and own your distribution before an algorithm takes it away.

What it is: A rapid-fire, seven-point playbook Codie would run if she lost everything and had to rebuild a business in 2026.

The instructional secret: Sell before you build. Everything else in the list is downstream of proving demand with a transaction, not a survey or a landing page.

How it works:

  • 1. Speed over everything — perfectionism is a losing bet in a fast market. Test, ship, iterate.
  • 2. Sell before you're ready — stop polishing, stop planning. Sell the thing first, then build around the demand you've proven. Proof of concept beats a perfect product every time.
  • 3. Sell to people with money — the middle class is squeezed; wealthy customers still buy. Go where the pricing power is.
  • 4. Build distribution before you need it — email list, community, content. If you rely on Instagram or paid ads, you don't have a business, you have a dependency.
  • 5. Relationships are the unfair advantage — AI automates tasks, not trust, referrals, or reputation. Stay human.
  • 6. AI-first on top of that — not AI eventually, not AI everything. AI *now*, aimed at low-level tasks no human should be doing.
  • 7. The obituary test — what do you want your life to add up to? Your why sets your direction. Don't build fast and forget why.

Caveat: This is a list, not a manual. There's no pricing model, no channel specifics, no example of what "selling before you build" actually looked like for her. Treat it as a filter for your own plan, not a plan.

Why it matters for you: If you're an engineer with a side hustle, points 2 and 4 are the ones that bite — you'll over-build the product and under-build the audience, and rule 6 (AI on the grunt work, not the whole business) is the one you're most equipped to execute well.
