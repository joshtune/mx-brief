What if everything you know about your business is built on fake rules?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-10 · 12,270 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Steal Musk's first-principles thinking to break inherited business rules.

Your side hustle's pricing, costs, and 'that's just how it works' constraints were inherited from competitors, not derived from reality — and that gap is where the margin hides.

What it is: A 90-second breakdown of first principles thinking — reasoning from raw truth instead of copying what everyone else does.

How it works:

  • The copying trap: Most people optimize an existing number. Rockets cost $250M → "how do we shave 10%?" That gets you average, permanently.
  • The Musk move: Ask what the thing costs from raw materials. For rockets, ~2% of the market price. The industry wasn't expensive — it was built on unexamined assumptions everyone agreed to inherit.
  • The reframe: "Average people optimize. Outliers build from scratch."
  • The drill (3 steps):
  • Pick one thing that feels too expensive, too hard, or that someone told you is "just the way it is."
  • Ask: what is *actually* true here? Not what competitors do — what's true according to physics, not opinion.
  • Rebuild from that floor up.
  • The tell: If your answer to "why is it priced this way?" is "that's the going rate," you're running on a fake rule.
