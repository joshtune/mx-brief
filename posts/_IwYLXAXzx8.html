There Are Only 3 Types Of Profitable Businesses (Pick ONE)

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-18 · 28,567 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Scale path: the brutal J-curve to big money

Before you sink nights and weekends into a side hustle, learn whether you're actually signing up for the scale path (all-in, no income early) or something far more forgiving.

What it is: Part 1 of a 3-path framework for building wealth. This excerpt covers Path 1, Scale, the high-risk, high-sacrifice route. Paths 2 and 3 are not in the transcript.

The instructional secret: Scale only works if you survive the J-curve dip by plowing everything back in. Then you prove retention, not just sales, before hiring a team of obsessed operators.

How it works:

  • Accept the dip: Scale businesses lose money first. Amazon sold books in 1995 and had no full profitable year until 2003. That year it did ~$5.2B revenue but only ~$35M profit, because Bezos reinvested everything into warehouses, tech, people, and new products.
  • Prove product-market fit with retention: Orders aren't PMF. Repeat usage is. Measure how many customers use the product and how much they use it.
  • Be oxygen, not dessert: Ask whether you solve a problem people need solved right now and come back for, or a nice-to-have. Amazon started as dessert (books) and became oxygen (get me anything, fast).
  • Go narrow first: Paul Graham's line is 100 people who love you beat a million who sort of like you. Find the smallest market you can dominate, expand to a slightly larger one, dominate that, repeat. Peter Thiel makes the same point in *Zero to One*.
  • Build a lean team of 'base camp' people: Obsessed, talented, mission-bought operators who bring solutions, not problems. Codie says the solo-entrepreneur trend isn't scale, and that work-life balance isn't part of the deal early on.
  • Sacrifice is the price: MrBeast reportedly lived at the office with a bench press and a blow-up bed for years.

Why it matters for you: If your side hustle is meant to pay the bills in six months, this is probably the wrong path. Scale means years of reinvesting, a team, and no safety net. Use the oxygen-vs-dessert and retention tests to find out fast whether your hustle even has legs.
