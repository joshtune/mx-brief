Skip the AI agency, become an in-house Claude consultant.

Nate argues the saturated 'sell automations' agency play is dying and the real money is now in AI consulting — useful context if you're deciding how to monetize AI skills without chasing clients.

What it is: A thought-leadership pitch that the best way to make money with Claude in 2026 isn't running an AI agency — it's becoming an AI consultant who drives real results inside businesses.

The core thesis:

  • The old play (start an agency, sell automations, build workflows) is saturating fast — more people entering daily, margins racing to the bottom.
  • ~95% of viewers don't actually want to run sales calls and chase clients — they just want to be good at their job and paid well.
  • The gap: companies bought AI tools, spun up pilots, and got nothing. Someone has to go in and drive real ROI.

Supporting data points cited:

  • Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta on pace for ~$725B combined AI spend in 2026.
  • MIT study: 95% of enterprise generative-AI pilots had little measurable bottom-line impact.
  • BCG: companies plan to roughly double AI spend as a share of revenue in 2026.
  • LinkedIn 2026 fastest-growing US jobs: AI consultant/strategist #2 (behind AI engineer); independent consultants/advisers cracked the top 10.

The consultant framing:

  • "Claude is the car, the company is the passenger, you're the driver." You find where a business loses time/money and use Claude to fix it.
  • Why Claude specifically: it's the current leader and what Nate runs his whole business on — but the transferable skill is turning messy business problems into reliable AI solutions (portable to open-source models next).
