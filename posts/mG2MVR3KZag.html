How Cody Ko made a #1 app before graduating college

My First Million · 2026-10-04 · 7,865 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Cody Ko's #1 App Store hit, built from a book

It's a scrappy origin story of hitting #1 with zero monetization plan, but the transcript we have cuts off before the money part.

What it is: Cody Ko recounts building "I Cap That", a meme-caption iPhone app he self-taught from a book as a college senior, which hit #1 in the App Store.

What he did (from the transcript):

  • Learned to code on the spot: bought a book and taught himself iOS development while still a senior in college.
  • Built the app: "I Cap That" (he says "cap that" / "I cap that"). You pick or take a photo and it stamps a caption on it in white font.
  • Faked the AI: the captions came from an internal database he and friends wrote while drunk. They were generic enough that users assumed an AI was reading the photo. Example given: a photo of a person gets "addicted to cocaine".
  • Beat Apple review: Apple rejected it as inappropriate. He added a timer to the caption database, because he knew review took about a week. After 7 days it unlocked the dirty captions. The reviewed build was clean and the shipped one wasn't.
  • Hit #1: it topped the App Store, above eBay and Google, while he was still on campus.
