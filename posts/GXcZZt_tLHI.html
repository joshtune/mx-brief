What it is: A short clip, with an unnamed speaker, on why CEOs should hand teams the problem instead of the solution.

How it works:

  • Bad pattern: "Sales are off, make more phone calls, we'll track them with this data." The speaker calls this disempowering, and says the conversation usually goes badly.
  • Better pattern: "Sales are off. I need a plan. Present it tomorrow on how we get sales back on track."
  • Why it works: The person owns the solution, so you aren't dictating. Per the speaker, they also can't blame you if it fails.
