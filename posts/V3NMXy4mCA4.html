What is one word that Millionaires love?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-12 · 8,298 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Millionaires work hard; billionaires hunt unfair advantages.

Sixty seconds that reframe your side hustle from 'grind harder' to 'only play games rigged in your favor.'

What it is: A short clip contrasting how millionaires and billionaires explain their success, with one actionable filter for choosing what to work on.

The instructional secret:

  • Millionaires answer "work hard." Billionaires answer "I think a lot about the actions I'm going to take."
  • The billionaire filter: before any action, ask whether you have an unfair advantage so unreasonable you'd have to be an idiot not to win.
  • The punchline: they don't just work hard or work smart, they "work lazily." Effort is applied only where the odds are already stacked.

How to apply it to a side hustle:

  • Inventory your unfair advantages before picking an idea: domain knowledge, an existing audience, a skill most people don't have, access to a network, a tool you already own.
  • Kill ideas where you'd be competing on effort alone. Hard work is table stakes, not an edge.
  • Rank candidate hustles by "how idiot-proof is my win here" instead of by market size or excitement.
  • Spend the thinking time up front. Ten hours choosing the right game beats a thousand hours grinding the wrong one.
