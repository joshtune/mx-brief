DHH: Omarchy installs in one minute, Windows takes 90.

A 60-second teaser where DHH admits he used AI agents to shave Omarchy's install to one minute, which is the exact kind of workflow-hacking Chuck is chasing in his lab.

What it is: A Short clipped from NetworkChuck's DHH interview. DHH rants about out-of-box setup time and pitches Omarchy, his opinionated Arch Linux + Hyprland distro, as the fix.

How it works:

  • DHH timed fresh-machine setup: new Windows box took 90 minutes of updates and drivers before it was usable.
  • New MacBook wasn't much better: 42 minutes of updates before it was ready. His verdict: machines ship from the factory in an unusable state.
  • Omarchy's goal is the opposite: a fully configured, beautiful desktop in one minute, no assembling a box of Legos every install.
  • The one-minute number came from weeks of optimization, and DHH explicitly credits a lot of agent help (AI coding agents) for getting the installer that fast.
  • This is a teaser; the actual Omarchy walkthrough and the AI-agent details live in the full video and interview linked below.
