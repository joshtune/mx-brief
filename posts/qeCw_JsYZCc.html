Most Unhinged Advice to Someone Graduating

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-17 · 7,668 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Chase money unapologetically; know your worth and ask.

It's a 30-second mindset nudge: for a side hustle, price high and stop apologizing for wanting money, but it's a pep talk with no playbook.

What it is: A short clip where Codie gives her most 'unhinged' graduation advice: chase money without apology until your first million.

How it works:

  • The advice: Never apologize for pursuing money until you've made your first million. Pursue it relentlessly, even if people don't like it.
  • The justification: More resources means more to give later. Wealth is the means, not the end.
  • The gendered angle: Women get told to be quiet and not ask for too much. She says ask for a lot.
  • The guardrail: You have to be worth it, and you have to know what your worth is.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is to price and ask based on your known value and skip the apology. That's the side-hustle lever most people miss (undercharging, discounting out of guilt). But there's no method here. You'd need to work out your own worth and rate, so treat this as a mindset reminder, not a tutorial.
