How MrBeast built a team that thinks like him

My First Million · 2026-09-04 · 5,164 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
MrBeast's 6-month shadowing 'cloning' method for training hires

A 90-second clip on how Jimmy Donaldson scales his own judgment across a fast-growing, young team without process docs or management layers.

What it is: A short MFM clip where Shaan and Sam recount asking MrBeast (Jimmy Donaldson) how he trains a fast-growing team of very young, new employees. His answer: "cloning."

Who's featured: MrBeast, the largest YouTuber in the world, whose business empire spans the main channel, Beast Philanthropy, Feastables (snack brand), and multiple media/merch ventures. This clip covers only his hiring/training method, not the revenue side of the business.

How cloning works:

  • New hire shadows Jimmy everywhere, every day, for roughly 6 months
  • Not a 9-to-5 arrangement: the first person to do it reportedly lived in the same house as Jimmy
  • The hire sits in on every decision and hears the reasoning in real time
  • After 6 months, the hire has internalized "what would Jimmy do" for any situation
  • Outcome: the hire becomes a proxy. Jimmy can effectively be in two places at once
  • He then repeats the process with the next hire, cloning judgment rather than documenting process

Why it works for his business:

  • MrBeast's edge is taste and decision-making on videos, not a repeatable SOP
  • Young hires with no prior playbook absorb his framework without unlearning anything
  • Removes the bottleneck of Jimmy approving everything, which is what lets output scale

What the clip does NOT cover:

  • No numbers on revenue, team size, or how many people have been cloned
  • No detail on Feastables, sponsorships, or the money-making side. This is purely a management tactic clip

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the transcript

Why it matters for you: If you're scaling a team where your judgment is the product, this is the apprenticeship model taken to its extreme: transfer the decision framework by proximity, then delegate.
