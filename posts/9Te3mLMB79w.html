The 5 People Who Will Make You Rich

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-14 · 126,097 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Five hires that buy back your time.

If your side hustle is stalling, it's probably because you're burning your best hours on $20/hr work — this gives you the exact five roles to offload first.

What it is: A 2-minute breakdown of the "buyback loop" — using money to purchase time instead of stuff — and the five specific hires Codie says gave her the biggest return.

The core idea:

  • Most people run wealth backwards: make money → buy freedom
  • Wealthy people invert it: use money → buy back time → make more money
  • Every low-value task you remove creates capacity for high-value work
  • "You don't get rich doing more. You get rich doing less of the things that don't make you money."

The five hires, in order:

  • House cleaner — a few hundred a week; buys back focus, energy, and free time. Lowest barrier to entry, first thing to outsource.
  • Virtual assistant — most people are secretly running a full-time admin job (email, scheduling, follow-ups). Offloading it returns "tons of hours every week."
  • Personal trainer — framed as performance, not fitness. You just show up; consistency compounds. Your business runs on your energy.
  • Financial adviser — explicitly *not* for investment advice. For tax returns and getting K-1s right. Buys back the highest-anxiety, highest-error-rate work.
  • Chief of staff — the endgame hire. Not a calendar manager; someone who multiplies your effectiveness and keeps your world moving while you're focused elsewhere.

The mental model to steal:

  • Price your own hour honestly, then delegate anything below it
  • Delegate by *drain*, not just by cost — the trainer and adviser are bought for energy and peace of mind, not hours
  • Sequence matters: cleaner and VA are cheap and immediate; chief of staff only pays off once there's a real business to multiply
