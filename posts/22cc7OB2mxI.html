AI-assisted ghostwriting as a $20K-per-book side hustle

It's a concrete, low-capital side hustle with a repeatable 12-step process, a weekly delivery cadence, and a 30-day path to a first client.

What it is: Jamie, a novelist and screenwriter from Florida, runs a ghostwriting business for people with book ideas who can't write. She uses AI to draft and tells clients up front. Packages run about $22,250 per book.

How it works:

  • Origin: Her husband, a real estate agent, typed a book idea into ChatGPT and had a full (rough) book by 2am. She saw a business in it.
  • Cadence: Every Friday she drops a chapter in the client's inbox. Every Tuesday the client sends back changes. It's collaborative and runs on a fixed weekly rhythm.
  • Intake: An initial development phase pins down the story, characters, settings, and the rest.
  • Her moat: After about 1.5 years she built a 12-step process that takes a client from raw idea to a chapter-by-chapter breakdown. The process is the product, not the AI.
  • Disclosure: She mentions the AI use. Clients who care get a deeper conversation. Most don't care once they read chapter one, because it doesn't sound like AI.
  • Privacy pitch: She pays for the tool monthly and runs it on her own PC, not in the cloud.
  • Typical clients: Retirees, business owners, and a mortgage broker in Nevada who wanted an alien-conspiracy thriller.
  • Finding the first client in 30 days:
  • Start with your own circle on Facebook and LinkedIn. She finds a lot of leads on LinkedIn.
  • Pitch local business owners: 'Let me tell your story. Can I guinea pig you?'
  • You don't need to be an expert. People are happy to be the guinea pig.
  • Teaser claims (not covered in the transcript excerpt):
  • She teaches free library classes as a lead funnel.
  • There's one thing AI still writes badly, which is why people pay her.
  • A $125/year list plus a prompt wins clients.
  • One commenter-style quote claims 'I could write 40 books at a time with AI' and 'six figures the first year'.
  • Math: One book a year at $20K is already a decent side hustle. Several books at once scales it up.
