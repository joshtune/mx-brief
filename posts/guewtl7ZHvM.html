Three ways to get rich; only one you control.

It reframes why building your own income stream isn't just about money — it's the only path where you're actually in charge of the outcome, the whole point of a side hustle.

What it is: A 60-second reframe of her dad's money advice — three ways to have real wealth, and why only one is worth your energy.

How it works:

  • Three paths to money: (1) Get it gifted — not happening for most people. (2) Marry into it — you'll pay for it in ways you won't like. (3) Make it yourself.
  • Why #3 wins: Hardest of the three, but the *only* one where you control the outcome. Gifts and marriages depend on someone else's choices; your own cash flow depends on you.
  • The risk math on the alternatives: Half of marriages end — a coin flip, not a strategy. Betting your financial future on someone else is unhedged.
  • The payoff framing: Make your own money → you choose your own life → you pick a partner because you *want* them, not because you *need* them. Independence is the real product, money is the mechanism.
