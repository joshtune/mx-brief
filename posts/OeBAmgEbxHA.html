LeBron's ROI on Spending $1M on His Body

My First Million · 2026-07-16 · 2,804 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Invest in your #1 asset for outsized returns.

A tight mental model for treating your core skill like LeBron treats his body — spend big on it because the ROI dwarfs the cost.

What it is: A short riff on why spending heavily on your single most valuable asset is rational, not crazy — using LeBron James as the case study.

How it works:

  • LeBron reportedly spends $1M/year on his body (training, recovery, nutrition, medical).
  • Framing flip: it's not wasteful — his body *is* the product, so the spend is a business investment.
  • The payoff: still playing in year 23 in the NBA; Shaan estimates that upkeep unlocked another ~$40M contract, twice — a massive return on a $1M/year outlay.
  • The generalizable question: *what is the $1M-on-your-body equivalent in your niche?* Find your highest-leverage asset and over-invest in it.

Case study — the executive coach:

  • Shaan's executive coach spent $1M of his own money learning from the best coaches across every modality.
  • His move: cold-approach the top 15 coaches in the world with — *"Any way I get 2 weeks of immersive one-on-one training? Name your price."*
  • Result: he compounded elite, hard-to-copy knowledge and made himself dramatically more valuable/premium as a coach.

The playbook to steal:

  • Identify the one asset that *is* your product (your skill, your body, your knowledge).
  • Spend aggressively on it — treat it as capex, not expense.
  • Go direct to the best-in-world, pay for immersive access, and buy years of compounding advantage.
