Find your avatar using three customer lenses.

Your side hustle's fastest path to sales is knowing the one buyer who pays most, and this gives you a 3-part checklist to find them in minutes.

What it is: A 45-second pitch for defining your "avatar" (the single customer who buys fastest, pays most, and is your best fit) via three lenses.

The instructional secret: Stop selling to "everyone." Profile one buyer across three layers and your messaging, offers, and sales stop being guesswork.

How it works:

  • Psychographics: What do they believe, fear, and desire? What identity are they trying to grow into?
  • Value graphics: What do they prioritize and invest in? What do they refuse to buy? Which does value mean to them: speed, certainty, convenience, prestige, or support?
  • Behavioral patterns: What do they do online? How do they make decisions? What have they already tried, and why did it fail?
  • Payoff: Clear messaging, offers that make sense, non-random sales.

Do it this week (side hustle version):

  • Pick your 3 best (or ideal) customers and answer all three lenses for each; look for the overlap.
  • Mine "what they've tried already, why it failed" from reviews, Reddit, and customer calls. That's your pitch.
  • Rewrite your offer around the one value they care about most (e.g., speed over price).
  • Say what they refuse to buy out loud; it's free positioning.
