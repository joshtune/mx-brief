How it works:

  • Artificial Analysis ranks it 4th of all the models it tests.

  • Moonshot's demos are games built in Unity and Unreal (a racing game, a zombie shooter, a zipline parkour game) and a medieval town built in Blender. They show agentic tool use inside real engines, but nothing web-related.
  • The weights are downloadable on November 4, so any company can self-host. Matt warns it needs a lot of compute.
