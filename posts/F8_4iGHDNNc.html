Stop Saying Yes

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-30 · 3,488 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Saying yes to every customer request kills your margins.

If you're running a side hustle, custom requests from every customer will eat your limited hours and your profit, so a tight offer menu is your fix.

What it is: A ~60-second pitch (and book plug) on why unlimited customization is a fast way to go broke.

How it works:

  • The secret: Cap your business at ~3 clear offers. Say no to everything outside them.
  • The cautionary tale: A client had 172 variations of one product. Every custom request added friction, confusion, and cost, and no extra profit.
  • The fix: Collapse to three clear offers. Per Codie, close rates went up, churn came down, and the business started making real money.
  • Why it works:
  • Customers understand what you do fast, so they decide faster.
  • Your team (or just you) has a repeatable way to deliver, so you don't reinvent the business with every new ask.
  • The failure mode: Trying to be everything to everyone means no money and misery, and you won't know why.
  • Caveat: The results are anecdotal and come from one client in her book. No numbers are given, and the video is a book promo, so the detail lives in the book.

Tools & links:

  • None named in the transcript. The only reference is Codie's new book, with no title or URL given.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle runs on scarce hours, so every custom job quietly drains them. Pick three offers, price them, and say no to the rest.
