No single founder profile here, just a roundup of half-baked X ideas (wedding-ticket marketplace, a looksmaxxer's skincare line) that the hosts mostly shoot down, so it's low-yield for "how did they make money" content.

What it is: Episode 875 is a tweet-reaction roundup. Ben Levy brings the X finds, and Shaan Puri and Sam Parr react. The transcript I got cuts off after two items, so the "moats" segment and anything later isn't covered.

How it works:

  • Idea 1: Wedding Crashers marketplace. A vibecoded site sells tickets to attend a stranger's wedding by filling empty seats. It reportedly has ~20,000 users. Shaan mentions a similar one in India. There are no revenue figures, pricing or business model details.
  • Hosts' verdict: Sam is a hard no ("I'd rather watch Netflix"). Their consensus is that nobody voluntarily wants to attend weddings. Neither of them says the idea works.
  • Idea 2: Clavicular's men's skincare and makeup line. Clavicular is a looksmaxxing influencer. At the All-In Summit he claimed to have invented the peptides movement, then pitched a men's skincare and makeup line and asked interested people to email him. There are no financials, no product details and no traction yet. Sam won't email him and calls it "a fine idea."
  • Tangent: Bryan Johnson. Shaan calls him the "Michael Jordan of social media" and says MFM was the first podcast he ever did. The transcript cuts off mid-thought, so there's no breakdown of how he monetizes.
  • Passing reference: Korean "fake dopamine" shopping sites, where you shop for things that never arrive. Shaan prefers this idea to the wedding one. No details given.
