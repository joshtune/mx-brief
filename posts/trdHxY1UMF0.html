Sell This AI and Get Paid Monthly (No Skills Needed)

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-09 · 15,272 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Bilingual AI voicemail service for realtors as a side hustle.

It's a concrete, low-skill recurring-revenue side hustle you could spin up in a weekend by gluing a voice-cloning API to a clear realtor pain point.

What it is: A done-for-you service that clones a realtor's voice and generates personalized voicemails in any of 80+ languages, sold as a $200–$500/month subscription.

The opportunity (the pitch):

  • 40% of US home buyers speak a language other than English at home — over 50% in Dallas, NYC, LA, Miami.
  • Realtors win on speed-to-lead and trust/rapport, but can't leave voicemails in a buyer's native language today.
  • Current alternative is fumbling Google Translate + a third-party tool — and it won't sound like them. You solve it for them and bill monthly.

How it works:

  • Get a 30–60s voice clip from the realtor — or scrape it from their existing Instagram/YouTube so they do nothing (remove all friction).
  • Clone the voice in Fish Audio (claims a clone from as little as 10–60s of audio; Chris cloned his from a 17-second memo).
  • Generate a library of plug-and-play voicemail templates: price-drop alerts, new-listing alerts, showing confirmations, missed-appointment notifications.
  • Output in the target language with matching voice, tone, and intonation; 15,000+ emotion tags control warm/excited/professional delivery.
  • Sell each template into any of the realtor's listings as a reusable package.

Tools & links:

  • Fish Audio S2 Pro — text-to-speech + voice cloning engine; code KOERNER20 for 20% off plans (API usage excluded). Claims to beat competitors on TTS/cloning benchmarks.
  • Any LLM (Claude, GPT, Grok, Gemini) to write the voicemail scripts/templates.
  • TKOPOD.com — Chris's newsletter.

Watch-outs (not addressed in the clip): Voice-cloning a person and leaving AI voicemails likely runs into TCPA/robocall rules and disclosure/consent norms — validate the legal angle before selling.

Why it matters for you: A clean blueprint for a productized, recurring-revenue AI micro-SaaS — clone voice, template the outputs, charge monthly — that a technical builder could automate end-to-end far past the manual demo shown.
