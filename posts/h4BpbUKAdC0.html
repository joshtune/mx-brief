Why being in the room still matters

My First Million · 2026-07-08 · 11,877 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Physical proximity beats remote for creative collaboration.

A quick reminder that serendipitous, in-person 'atoms bumping' still drives opportunity — worth weighing if you're all-remote.

What it is: Shaan Puri riffs on a lesson from comedian Hasan Minhaj about the underrated power of physical proximity.

How it works:

  • Shaan shadowed Hasan Minhaj to watch how his team operates day-to-day
  • Minhaj relays a Chamath Palihapitiya quote: the moment a creative office has "the hot secretary, all-glass windows, and fancy matcha," you've "already lost the plot" — polish signals you've stopped doing the actual work
  • The real value is proximity: inviting people to just show up — come work, do hair and makeup here, hang out
  • The mechanism is serendipity: "our atoms get to bump into each other" → you overhear what's happening in someone's world, spot ways to help, and they reciprocate
  • No framework or business here — it's a short thought-leadership clip on why in-person still compounds relationships
