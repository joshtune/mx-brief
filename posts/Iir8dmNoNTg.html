ChatGPT’s Latest Update Is MASSIVE

Matt Wolfe · 2026-07-11 · 18,190 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
OpenAI's six-part drop: GPT-5.6, a super app, and site-building.

OpenAI now ships prompt-to-full-app tooling — code, database, and hosting — that directly overlaps with how you build and ship web apps.

What it is: A rapid rundown of six OpenAI releases, headlined by GPT-5.6 and a unified ChatGPT app that can build and host full web apps from a prompt.

How it works:

  • GPT-5.6 — new flagship in ChatGPT and Codex; faster/cheaper, pitched as building games, interactive sites, slide decks, and complex software from one prompt.
  • Three variants — Sol (most capable, slowest, most credits), Terra and Luna (prioritize speed, fewer credits). Slide the intelligence/budget tradeoff per task.
  • The "super app" — ChatGPT + Codex + browser merged into one app. Two modes: Codex mode for devs, work mode as a personal assistant.
  • Work mode — reads email, calendar, Slack, meeting notes, docs, and projects; builds a priority list, completes tasks, and schedules tasks to run automatically.
  • ChatGPT Sites — prompt full websites/apps into existence; handles code, databases, hosting, and publishing so you can share instantly.
