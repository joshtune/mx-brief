What it is: Episode 849 — a recurring MFM format called "Drunk Ideas," where Shaan Puri pitches deliberately half-baked business concepts under the cover of alcohol so bad ideas can be floated without ego. Sam Parr and producer-turned-cohost Arie react. Nine ideas total.

The format (and why it works):

  • Shaan keeps a running list in Slack, tagging each entry literally `drunk idea` so he can search-filter the whole backlog when it's time to record
  • Premise is stated outright: "These are ideas that are half-baked... but there's a nugget in all of these"
  • Alcohol is the social permission structure — it lets a known operator pitch weak ideas without reputational cost. That's the actual transferable trick here

IDEA 1: Reverse vacations (the only idea fully captured in the available transcript):

  • Framing: a vacation moves you *down* on productivity and *up* on relaxation. There is no product for the opposite move — a week where you go way *up* on output
  • Product: a "lock-in camp" / "professional penitentiary." You leave your normal routine for a week to work, not rest
  • Deliberate anti-luxury design: cold showers only, slop for food, pull-up bars scattered around, David Goggins on the ambient audio. "You should feel a little unwell by the end of this"
  • Identity reset mechanic: issued uniform (Adidas-style tracksuit, not prison guard) so you're not "normal Sam," you're on a mission
  • Goal-setting on arrival — you commit to a specific outcome for the week
  • The real business insight: the monetization is status, not the facility. Shaan's analogy — when someone signs up for a marathon or Ironman, the first thing they do isn't train, it's *tell their friends they signed up*. Right now there's no socially legible way to signal "I'm going hardcore on my project." Reverse vacations sell that signal
