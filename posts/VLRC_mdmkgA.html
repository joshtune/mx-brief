Build Trust Better Than Everyone Else

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-01 · 5,373 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Four trust-building systems that beat any closing line

If your side hustle lives or dies on strangers handing you money, this is a four-part system for earning that trust before you ask for it.

What it is: A 4-part trust playbook from someone who's sold hundreds of millions, built on the claim that there's no magic closing one-liner, just systems that make buyers trust you.

The instructional secret: Trust is built before the pitch, with assets prepared in advance, not improvised on the call.

How it works:

  • Anti-sales playbook: Tell prospects when your product isn't right for them. Honesty filters out bad fits and makes your yes mean something. Patagonia's "Don't buy this jacket" ad is the proof.
  • Proof vault: Save every DM, and every before/after video or result. Organize by customer type so you can pull the matching case study in 30 seconds. Show, don't tell.
  • Outreach templates: Use the formula give, give, give, ask: three valuable things for every one ask, across four touchpoints. If you can't offer three valuable things first, you're just obnoxious.
  • Pitch document: A guide, not a script. It keeps you consistent without sounding robotic.

Tools & links:

  • Owner Beyond (Codie Sanchez's book), chapter six — walks through building each of the four assets. No URL given in the transcript.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle has no brand to lean on, so a proof vault and a give-give-give-ask routine are cheap ways to look credible before you have a reputation. Start saving screenshots and testimonials today.
