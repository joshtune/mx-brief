How it works:

  • The drone hovers about 300 ft up, roughly a football field, and lowers the order on a tether.
  • The pod touches down in the customer's yard, releases, and gets reeled back up.
  • Zipline says the altitude keeps it about as quiet as a flock of birds. Most delivery drones come down to about 30 ft.
  • Uber Eats claims delivery in under 10 minutes, with no extra fee and no tip needed.
  • Dallas is next. The stated goal is 1 million deliveries a day by 2029.
  • Zipline started out flying blood to hospitals in Rwanda.
