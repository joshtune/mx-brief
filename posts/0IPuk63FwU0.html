Autonomous agent runs a GTA 6 clip page

It's a short teaser of a full agent loop (scrape, edit, QA, post, learn), but the real build steps are locked behind a comment-for-guide funnel, so treat it as an architecture sketch, not a tutorial.

What it is: A ~1-minute pitch for a Grok Bot agent named "Kenji" that runs a GTA 6 clipping page on Instagram and TikTok with no human in the loop.

How it works:

  • Trigger: Kenji wakes every 2 hours and runs the full cycle on its own.
  • Sourcing: Pulls the most-viewed GTA 6 Twitch clips from the last 3 to 24 hours.
  • Filtering: Checks the clip is GTA 6, not GTA 5, and that other pages haven't already posted it.
  • Editing: Streamer face cam on top, gameplay on the bottom, captions added, and the best moment moved to the front as the hook.
  • QA pass: Checks the hook and payoff, and makes sure no stream chat is visible in the frame.
  • Publishing: Writes a caption that tags the streamer, then schedules the post to Instagram and TikTok.
  • Feedback loop: Reads views, shares and comments after posting, then logs what worked so future clips improve.
  • Account warming: Daily at 1 p.m. it scrolls and likes GTA posts so the accounts look human-run.
  • Claimed result: 2,300 views on day one, with about an hour of setup.

Tools & links:

  • No repos, URLs or tool names are given beyond "Grok Bot" (the transcript says "Grockbot"), Twitch as the source, and Instagram and TikTok as destinations.
  • The build guide is a 22-page doc, available by commenting "Kenji" on the video. It is not linked in the video.
  • Not stated: which clip downloader, video editor, captioning or scheduling tools sit under the hood.

Caveats:

  • The $100/day in the title isn't backed by anything in the video. The only number shown is 2,300 views on day one, and that's a view count, not revenue.
  • Re-posting streamers' clips and faking human activity on accounts both risk platform ToS and copyright trouble.
