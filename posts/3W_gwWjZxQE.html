Raise prices using the Three C's framework.

If your side hustle is priced low to win customers, this 60-second pep talk says you're probably leaving money on the table and hurting your own credibility.

What it is: A 60-second framework, the "Three C's", for raising prices without churning customers.

How it works:

  • Compare: Check what 5-10 competitors charge. Most people never look, and you'll likely find you're underpriced.
  • Contrast: Cheap doesn't mean better. Price signals value. The $3.97 Instagram-ad shirt reads as trash, and so does a suspiciously cheap service.
  • Compete the right way: Don't race to the bottom. Low-price competition only works if you're an elite operator with razor-thin-margin efficiency.
  • The core mistake: Amateurs think being cheap will make them rich. It won't. Aim to be *valuable*, not *affordable*.

The instructional secret: Your price is part of your product. It tells customers what to expect, so underpricing quietly undermines how good you seem.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Caveats:

  • It's a short, high-level clip. There are no numbers, no scripts, and no method for announcing an increase to existing customers, despite what the title implies.
  • "Analyze competitors" is the only concrete action step.

Why it matters for you: Side hustles are usually underpriced out of fear. A one-afternoon competitor price check is the cheapest raise you'll ever give yourself.
