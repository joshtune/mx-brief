Building the World’s Smallest AI Workstation

Alex Ziskind · 2026-07-25 · 29,602 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Backpack-sized RTX 4000 box races the M3 Ultra.

It's the head-to-head you actually want: a tiny x86 + RTX 4000 build vs. Mac Studio M3 Ultra vs. a 16GB Mac mini, with real tokens/sec on each.

What it is: Alex and Dan from Micro Center cram a full local-AI rig into a Velka case — roughly Mac Studio footprint — then benchmark it against an M3 Ultra Mac Studio and a base Mac mini.

The machines:

  • Velka mini-ITX build — NVIDIA RTX 4000 (Ada, workstation card), assembled at Micro Center. Fits in a backpack.
  • Mac Studio M3 Ultra — the incumbent to beat.
  • Mac mini, 16GB — the budget baseline, memory-capped to smaller models.

The numbers that matter:

  • Qwen 34B: 103 tok/s on the Velka build vs. ~30 tok/s on the 16GB Mac mini — roughly 3.4×.
  • Gemma 4 12B decode: M3 Ultra 45.4 tok/s vs. Velka 39.6 tok/s — the Mac wins generation, barely.
  • Gemma 4 12B prompt processing: Velka 1,505 tok/s vs. M3 Ultra 650 tok/s — the NVIDIA box is 2.3× faster at ingest.

The actual tradeoff:

  • Apple silicon wins token generation (unified memory bandwidth, huge model capacity).
  • NVIDIA wins prompt processing by a wide margin — matters enormously for long-context, RAG, and code-repo workloads where you're feeding in thousands of tokens before the first output token.
  • The Mac mini isn't in the conversation at 16GB; memory is the ceiling, not compute.

Cost reality check: Alex doesn't state prices in the transcript. Rough street context — Mac mini M4 16GB starts ~$599, Mac Studio M3 Ultra starts ~$3,999, and an RTX 4000 Ada alone runs ~$1,200–1,500 before case, CPU, RAM, and PSU. The Velka build lands in Mac Studio territory, not Mac mini territory.

Why it matters for you: If your local-AI work is long-prompt heavy, the NVIDIA box buys you 2.3× on ingest for roughly M3 Ultra money — but if you just want the cheapest machine that runs a decent model, neither of these is a Mac mini, and a 16GB mini caps you at small models regardless.
