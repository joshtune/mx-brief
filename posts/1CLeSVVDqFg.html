5 Spending Traps That Are Stealing Your Money

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-05 · 9,299 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Five hidden money leaks draining you and your business.

The mental math tricks here protect the thin cash flow that decides whether a side hustle survives its first lean months.

What it is: A breakdown of five spending traps that quietly drain personal and business cash, plus the counter-move for each.

How it works:

  • Trap 1 — Payments over price: Sellers shift your question from "What does this cost?" to "Can I handle the payment?" A $45K car becomes "$745/mo," a couch "$58/mo," a phone "$0 down." Works via the *pain of paying* — split, delayed payments hurt less, so you spend more.
  • The three-number test (the antidote):
  • 1. Sticker price — the real cost, written down first, before any monthly number can make you forget it.
  • 2. Total interest — `monthly payment × number of months − sticker price`. On the car example, that's ~$8,000+ handed to the lender for the privilege of waiting.
  • 3. Opportunity cost — future value if invested instead: `amount × (1 + return)^years`. At 7%, $45K becomes ~$88K in 10yr, ~$174K in 20yr, ~$342K in 30yr. Real price of the car ≈ $45K + interest + $342K.
  • Trap 2 — Convenience disguised as necessity: The surge ride, same-day shipping, unplanned coffee — the *convenience fee* for speed. The lie is "just this once." It bites hardest when you swipe tired, late, stressed, or overstimulated, because your brain isn't actually deciding.
  • Rule of thumb: if the *full* number embarrasses you, the *monthly* number is hiding something.
