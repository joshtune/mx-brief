He Makes $27K/Month Connecting Local Businesses

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-30 · 55,330 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Selling warm business-to-business referrals to home service companies

It's a no-employee, phone-and-text side hustle that reportedly grossed $27K last month at about 90% margin, and Aaron walks through the exact playbook.

What it is: Aaron Fuks ("Hyros") builds referral pipelines for home service businesses. He connects one contractor to another who needs their services and charges per intro.

How it works:

  • Origin story: Aaron was a part-time virtual receptionist for a small siding repair company. He noticed a much bigger siding company had sent his boss hundreds of free leads a year for over a decade.
  • Why the big guy gives leads away: Small jobs (a few melted square feet of vinyl behind a grill) aren't worth it to a full-install company. Referring them out protects its reputation as the trusted name in town.
  • Everybody wins: The homeowner gets the job fixed. The big company stays trusted. The specialist gets free leads.
  • The gap he spotted: Most of these referral relationships are informal or don't exist at all. He operationalizes them.
  • Early version: He cold-called on behalf of the siding client, using years of phone experience (about 8 hours a day on the phone as a receptionist) to find partner businesses. Roofers were the natural partners.
  • Pricing evolution: He started at $150 per connection. He now charges $6,000 for 10 intros, or $600 each.
  • Data source: He uses publicly available data to find and match business owners. There's no special software or credential involved.
  • Results: $27K last month, up from about $14K in June, so revenue nearly doubled in a few months. He has no employees and says margins are around 90%.
  • Proof point: One text to a painter's prospects led to four bids within 48 hours, ranging from $17K to $64K.
  • Pitch framing: "Do you want free money?" He positions the intros as free incremental business for the client.
  • Typical pairings: Painter to general contractor or luxury home builder. Siding to roofer. Pressure washer to window cleaner. Any trade that sits adjacent in a remodel journey.
