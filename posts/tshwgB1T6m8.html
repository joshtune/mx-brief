26-year-old flips auction book pallets into $15K/month

It's a near-zero-capital side hustle (a $70 bet, a garage corner, no car) that one guy scaled to quit-your-job money in under a year, so it's worth seeing if the playbook fits you.

What it is: Buying liquidation pallets (books, then computers, monitors, groceries) for pennies at auction and reselling them for profit, a story told as a cold open and teaser. The transcript is only the intro, so the how-to details are NOT in it.

How it works:

  • The origin: JV, 26, was a restaurant night manager about to start law school. Last December he watched Chris's episode with Mindy, who bought a pallet of books for $1 and sold it for $5,000.
  • The $70 bet: He bid $70 on seven pallets of books "as a joke" and won. Worst case he lost $70.
  • The panic: He got the pallets and thought, "What have I done?", worried they'd just take up space.
  • First traction: About $1,000 in his first week of posting books for sale, and income matched his job within 1-2 weeks. He quit roughly 2-3 weeks after that.
  • Scaling: Growth went "exponential" after that. Month four was $15K or more. He expanded beyond books into computers, monitors (about $1 each) and a pallet of groceries that doubled his money in a week.
  • Overhead: A $140/month storage unit. No warehouse, no employees, no box truck, no car, no starting capital. He started in a tiny corner of his parents' garage.
