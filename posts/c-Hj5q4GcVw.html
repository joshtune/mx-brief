Act like the person you want to become

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-15 · 13,510 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Decide your identity, then act it daily

If your side hustle keeps stalling, the secret is that your daily actions, not your goals, decide whether it ever gets built.

What it is: A ~1-minute motivational clip arguing that identity follows consistent action, not the other way around.

How it works:

  • Decide, don't pretend. "Fake it till you make it" is pretending; this is picking who you are and acting like it.
  • Actions over words. Your actions and your stated goals have to match. You can't want freedom while stacking obligations, or want success while chasing distractions.
  • Identity updates through repetition. Act like the person every day, in every choice, and your brain stops treating it as an aspiration and treats it as who you are.
  • Strength-training analogy. Every rep adds muscle, but junk food, no sleep and nightly drinking cancel the gym work. You fail by feeding the weakness, not by lacking ability.
  • Subtract first. Cut the noise, including the friend who drains you, and double down on actions that fit the target identity.
  • The trade. Sacrifice now buys the life you want later.

The instructional secret: Don't add more hustle; remove what contradicts it. Your results are capped by the worst habit you keep feeding, not the best one you add.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. This is a pure mindset clip with no tools, repos or frameworks.

Side-hustle application (inferred, not stated in the video):

  • Pick one identity, such as "I'm someone who ships offers weekly", and define the daily actions it implies.
  • Audit your evenings and weekends for the junk food: scrolling, low-value obligations, people who pull you off the work.
  • Protect the side-hustle hours like a gym session, because showing up consistently beats intensity.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle is mostly won by what you stop doing in your spare hours, so treat cutting distractions as step one, not a nice-to-have.
