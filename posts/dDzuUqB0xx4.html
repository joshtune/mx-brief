Is the way you talk about money keeping you broke?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-12 · 2,747 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stop rehearsing money excuses; redirect them into action.

Side-hustle momentum dies in complaining loops, and this gives you three dead-simple rules to stop venting and start shipping.

What it is: A 1-minute pep talk arguing that repeating money complaints wires them into your brain, plus three habits to break the loop.

How it works:

  • The instructional secret: Your brain wires whatever you repeat, so "I'm broke / the system is rigged" becomes a rehearsed belief, not a neutral vent.
  • Hack 1, timer: Give wallowing 5 minutes, then redirect.
  • Hack 2, question swap: Replace "I'll never get ahead" with "What's the one thing I could do this week to move the needle?"
  • Hack 3, change your venting partner: Skip people who just agree. Pick someone who hears you, then asks "what are you going to do about it?"
  • Self-check: After any money conversation, ask: do I feel clearer and more capable? If not, you're just rehearsing the excuse.
  • Claims cited (no sources given): 73% higher cortisol with constant financial stress; venting raising anxiety and depression by 40%. Treat these as unverified stat-drops, not gospel.
