The $500K decision that made Tony Hawk millions

My First Million · 2026-09-15 · 29,883 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Tony Hawk turned down $500K and made $4.5M instead.

A real case study in betting on your own royalty stream instead of taking the early buyout.

What it is: Tony Hawk recounts the origin of Tony Hawk's Pro Skater (THPS) and the royalty decision that made him a fortune.

How it works:

  • Activision called Hawk in to pitch a skateboarding game; the first prototype he saw was literally "Bruce Willis on a skateboard with a gun strapped to his back"
  • Hawk pushed to make a game hardcore skaters would actually respect, not a gimmick
  • Pre-release, review outlets started giving the game near-perfect scores (9s and 10s) and buzz built in the skate industry
  • Right before launch, Activision offered Hawk a buyout: $500,000 cash in exchange for giving up all future royalties on the game
  • Hawk declined — "half a gazillion dollars" sounded huge at the time, but he chose to let his royalty stake ride instead of cashing out
  • By THPS4, the franchise had 4 of the top 10 best-selling games, and Activision handed him a check for $4.5 million — 9x what the buyout would've paid
