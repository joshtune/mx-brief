I Turned GPT-6 Astra Into a 24/7 Stock Trader (tutorial)

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-07 · 79,063 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Scheduling GPT-6 Astra agents to day-trade $10K autonomously.

It's a concrete blueprint for turning a stateless frontier model into a scheduled, multi-wakeup agent with continuity — the trading part is just the demo; the pattern is reusable for any recurring-agent job.

What it is: Nate rebuilds his earlier Claude trading experiment (which beat the S&P by ~8% over a month on $10K real money) using GPT-6 Astra, and walks through the setup for a 7-day, $10K challenge before actually kicking it off.

How it works:

  • Research phase first. Nate has a plain conversation with Astra: "$10K, 7 days, higher-risk strategy is fine." Astra fans out ~10 sub-agents to research approaches and consolidates everything into a single strategy doc.
  • Three constraints drive the plan: $10,000 capital, 7 trading days, 6 wakeups per trading day.
  • The daily schedule Astra proposed (all times Central):
  • 7:45 AM — read news, check account, pick a watchlist
  • 9:30 AM — market open, look for first qualifying trade
  • 11:00 AM — review positions, consider one more new trade
  • 1:00 PM — manage existing positions
  • 2:15 PM — close out remaining positions
  • 2:45 PM — confirm flat, record day's results
  • Day-trading, not swing. Every position is closed before market close, so there's no overnight risk and each day is a clean slate.
  • Each wakeup is a stateless agent. This is the key engineering problem. On every run the agent must re-read the project files, inspect the current portfolio, and reconstruct what happened in the previous wakeup before deciding what to do now.
  • Continuity is handled via shared project state — the strategy doc, the portfolio, and a running log that each wakeup reads from and appends to. (Transcript cuts off mid-explanation of the exact mechanism.)
  • Two adoption paths Nate calls out: (1) you have no strategy and want to outsource research/thinking to agents, or (2) you already trade daily and just want agents to run your existing check-in cadence so you stop babysitting tickers.
  • Framing tip for your own version: map your day onto a one-day calendar (pre-open, open, mid-day, pre-close, post-close) and spawn one scheduled agent per checkpoint. Add or remove wakeups to taste.
  • Disclaimer up front: not financial advice; Nate recommends paper trading first, then automating a strategy you already trust.
