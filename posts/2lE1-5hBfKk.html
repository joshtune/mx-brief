Why Everyone Is Freaking Out About Mythos

Matt Wolfe · 2026-06-11 · 66,131 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Anthropic's new Fable 5 model, hype vs. reality.

Fable 5 is pitched as a set-and-forget model for huge, grindy coding jobs — the kind of long-running refactors and migrations that eat your web app maintenance time.

What it is: Anthropic's new Fable 5 — first publicly-released model from their "Mythos" tier, sitting a step above the Opus class.

How it works:

  • Claimed state-of-the-art on nearly every benchmark; lead grows the longer/more complex the task.
  • Pitched for "point it at huge grindy work and let it run" — overnight, multi-hour autonomous jobs.
  • Marquee example: Stripe ran a codebase-wide migration on a 50M-line Ruby codebase in a day — work that allegedly took a team 2+ months by hand.
  • Token-hungry: burns through a lot of tokens on long tasks.

Pricing & access (the catch):

  • $10 / million input tokens, $50 / million output — roughly 2x the price of Opus.
  • Free on Pro/Max/Team plans only through June 22, 2026. On June 23 it's pulled from plans and shifts to usage credits. Anthropic says it aims to restore it to subscriptions "when capacity allows."

Mythos vs. Fable — the misinformation cleanup:

  • What shipped publicly is Fable 5, NOT Mythos 5.
  • Mythos 5 = same underlying brain, but with safety guardrails *lifted* in certain areas; it's locked to "Project Glass Wing" partners (cyber defenders, critical infra, gov, vetted researchers).
  • Fable 5 is the safety-capped ("lobotomized") version of the same model. Anyone claiming the full frontier model is in your Claude app is selling hype.
  • Mythos Preview (April 2026) was the first Mythos-class model, given only to Glass Wing partners.

Third-party validation:

  • Dan Shipper (Every) tested it ~a week across coding, writing, marketing, editing — said it "broke their benchmarks."
  • Scored 91/100 on their senior-engineer benchmark vs. previous highs of 63 (Opus 4.8) and 62 (GPT 5.5). Called it a "one-shot wonder" you can set-and-forget for hours.

Why it matters for you: If the benchmarks hold, Fable 5 is built exactly for the unglamorous web-app work — big migrations, framework upgrades, sweeping refactors — but the June 23 paywall flip and 2x-Opus pricing mean you'd want to front-load those jobs before access tightens.
