I Turned Grok Bot Into a 24/7 GTA 6 Clipping Machine

Autonomous Grokbot agent clips and posts GTA 6 content

Nate shows a working, agent-run clipping pipeline (sources, posting stack, ~$50/mo cost), but the full step-by-step lives in a 22-page doc behind his free Skool community.

What it is: A Grokbot agent named "Kenji" runs a GTA 6 clipping page on autopilot. It finds clips, remixes them with a hook, posts them, and tunes its own strategy from the results.

How it works:

  • Results after ~12 hours: ~30 followers, ~2,300 views, 6 posts live, 9 queued. Setup took Nate about 2 hours; he claims about 30 minutes if you follow his doc.
  • Schedule: Kenji wakes every 2 hours around the clock for a "CEO cycle" (the main loop). A separate "account warm-up" routine also runs. Both are visible in Grokbot under Kenji → Activity.
  • Sourcing: The loop searches TikTok, X, Instagram, and Twitch. Twitch is the best source because it surfaces the most-viewed GTA 6 clips from the last 24-30 hours.
  • Not plain reposting: Each clip gets a hook edit.
  • It finds the best moment in the clip and plays it first with a short caption and the streamer's face and tag at the top.
  • It then does a "flashback" zoom back to the start of the clip, with captions and the streamer's tag.
  • Nate showed the same pattern on TikTok and Instagram.
  • Self-improving loop: Kenji keeps a dashboard of views over time and analyzes why top clips worked (the third clip was the best on TikTok). It feeds that back into its strategy.
  • Monetization plan: Not monetized yet. The stated goal is audience growth until December 2, then look at paid clipping campaigns once there's a following and hopefully a few viral clips.
  • Caveat from Nate: Setup is not "money printer" territory. The agent has to keep iterating and improving.
  • Cost: About $50/mo to start, which is a ~$15/mo scraping plan, a cheap posting-tool plan, and a free API.
  • Transcript gap: The transcript cuts off before the detailed walkthrough. The step-by-step is in the 22-page doc you hand to your Grokbot.
