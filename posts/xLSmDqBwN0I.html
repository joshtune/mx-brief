Top 4 Businesses You Can Get Into Right Now (Low Cost)

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-19 · 10,049 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Cheap franchises as an overlooked wealth path.

If you want a side hustle with a proven playbook instead of a blank page, this is the franchise-financing angle — SBA Express money in under 60 days and sub-$100k concepts doing seven figures.

What it is: An interview arguing that low-cost franchising — not startups — is the most accessible path to half-a-million-plus in annual income, with a specific pick: garage renovation.

How it works:

  • The thesis: Franchising is "the most overlooked path to wealth creation in America" — high revenues, low entry cost, and you can be operating in under 30 days.
  • Financing with none of your own money: SBA Express loans are the named lever — up to ~$500k, funded in less than 60 days. The pitch is that franchise concepts underwrite well because lenders can see historical unit economics (FDD Item 19) rather than a founder's projections.
  • The specific pick — garage renovation: epoxy floors, custom shelving, slatwall storage. Average unit revenue over $1M. No heavy equipment, so capex is a van + tooling instead of a build-out.
  • Why that concept: classic demand/supply gap — homeowners want it, almost nobody is organized enough to sell it as a service. High ticket, one-day install, referral-heavy.
  • The contrarian take: Subway is floated as possibly the worst franchise deal in America — the implicit lesson being that brand recognition is not the same as unit economics. Low AUV + high franchisee count + territory saturation is the anti-pattern to screen against.
  • Screening heuristic for you: high average unit revenue, low fixed capex, no real estate lease, short ramp to first dollar. That combination is what makes a franchise financeable and side-hustle-compatible.

Tools & links:

  • SBA Express Loan program — the sub-60-day, up-to-$500k financing vehicle named in the transcript
  • SBA Franchise Directory — which brands are pre-cleared for SBA financing; check here before you fall in love with a concept
  • FTC Franchise Rule / FDD guidance — Item 19 is the financial performance representation; it's the only number that matters

Why it matters for you: If you're an engineer who wants a side hustle without inventing a product or finding product-market fit, a franchise is a licensed system with bank-financeable numbers — you're buying a validated operations manual and renting someone else's demand.
