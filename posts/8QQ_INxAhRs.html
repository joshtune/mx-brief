I Turned Claude Fable Into The Ultimate Second Brain

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-06-10 · 269,466 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Building an AI second brain and OS around Claude Code.

Nate walks through his exact mental model and framework for turning Claude Code into a context-aware second brain and AI operating system you actually work out of.

What it is: Nate's setup and methodology for an "AI operating system" (he calls it Herk 2) built on top of Claude Code, framed around a new model he refers to as "Claude Fable."

How it works:

  • Mindset first, architecture second. Before any tooling, the move is to close all your scattered AI tabs/subscriptions/custom GPTs and *default* to one harness — Claude Code (desktop app or VS Code). "An OS doesn't start with architecture, it starts with a default." Defaulting to one tool is what builds up persistent context, memory, and preferences over time.
  • Two layers. (1) Second brain = the knowledge layer: does the system know what's going on in your business, life, clients, YouTube channel, etc., and can you ask it questions? (2) AI operating system = the architecture built *on top* of that knowledge — skills, automations, and actually working out of it instead of macOS/Windows. No second brain → no OS.
  • The Four C's framework (taken in order): the first two are the second brain, the last two are the OS. Covered so far: Context (who you are, who your business is) and Connections. The transcript cuts off before the remaining two C's.
  • Model context ("Claude Fable"): Nate describes it as essentially "Claude Mythos 5" with extra cyber guardrails — a model only available June 9–22 on subscription before switching to usage credits, priced at ~$10/M input and $50/M output (≈2× Opus), so it burns through a subscription faster. ⚠️ Note: "Claude Fable" and "Claude Mythos" are not real Anthropic models — this appears to be fictional/hypothetical framing, not a shipped product.
  • This isn't a same-day build — Nate says Herk 2 has been in development for months; the video is about how he adapts a new model into the existing OS and continuously improves it.

Tools & links:
