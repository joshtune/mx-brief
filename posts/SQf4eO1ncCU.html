6 Ways AI Agents Can Make You Money in 2026

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-21 · 21,666 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Six side hustles you run by delegating to AI agents

If you want a side hustle that costs about $20 a month, Chris says he tested six agent-run businesses on his own credit card and shares the exact prompts.

What it is: Chris spent about 6 weeks letting AI agents act for him (buying, negotiating, building sites, filling out forms). He ranks the six setups that made or saved him money and tells you which agent fits which task.

How it works (intro only — the transcript covers just the teaser, so the full list isn't visible):

  • Agent as a doer: one prompt, and the agent does the clicking in a browser while you watch. He claims one run produced a $50,000 outcome.
  • Quote-gathering on autopilot: the agent filled out 82 contact forms at 2am. He woke up to a spreadsheet with 8 pool quotes.
  • Lead-gen site: an $11 website sends him leads for a machine he has never seen in person. Leads he cites: $3,000, $9,000 and $23,000. This looks like the classic lead-gen play of building a site, collecting inquiries and passing them to a provider.
  • Selling AI implementation: use an agent to find a business that will pay you to add AI to its operations. He offers $500 to anyone who lands one.
  • Way #6, selling the agent itself: package an agent as the product.
  • Agent-to-agent setup: agents talk to agents while you do nothing. He calls it one of the biggest opportunities in the video.
  • He says the typical income is $6K–$12K from tasks that used to be tedious.
  • Cost is $20 a month or less for most of them. His worst case is about $2 in tokens plus something learned.
  • He frames adoption as early: only 2% of US households pay for AI.

Tools & links:

  • No URLs in the transcript. He mentions ChatGPT and Claude subscriptions (~$20/month) and says he'll show which agent suits which task.

Caveats:

  • The income claims ($50K, $6–12K) are unverified teaser numbers.
  • Details on the 6 ways and the prompts are only in the full video.

Why it matters for you: It's a concrete, low-cost menu of side hustles (lead-gen sites, outreach automation, AI consulting) where the agent does the grunt work and you find the customer.
