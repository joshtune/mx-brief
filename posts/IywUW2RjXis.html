He Lost His Job, Then Built a $11K/Month App

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-28 · 17,949 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Laid-off devs bootstrap a SaaS to $11K MRR.

It's a concrete, no-audience playbook for launching a niche SaaS side hustle from zero — build-in-public on Twitter plus AI SEO as the growth engine.

What it is: Case study of Caleb and his co-founder, who both got laid off, then bootstrapped a social-media posting app aimed at developers to $11K/month recurring revenue.

The idea:

  • A social media scheduling/posting tool — same category as Buffer or PostBridge — but pointed at technical people and developers, an underserved niche the incumbents ignore.
  • Niche positioning was the wedge: "this wasn't really being done" for devs.

How it works (the playbook):

  • Say yes to everything until you can afford not to — early on they took design and marketing work, outsourced it, and used that cash to stay alive while building.
  • Launch flopped at first: first tweet got ~200 views, friends-and-family signups, crickets for 1–2 months. $300 MRR → $500 MRR after 5 months.
  • Build in public: with zero following, the co-founder started posting a Stripe screenshot to Twitter every time he added another $100 in MRR. People noticed and engaged despite no audience.
  • Still only ~1,200 Twitter followers today, yet that build-in-public loop drives a ton of business — proof you don't need a big audience.
  • AI SEO was the next inflection point: optimizing so ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini recommend your tool when people ask related questions. Customers now arrive saying "ChatGPT sent me" or "I was chatting with Claude and they sent me your way."
  • Paid acquisition came last: only started running Google Ads *after* crossing $10K MRR — organic + AI referral first, paid to scale.
  • Pricing breakthrough (teased): a change in pricing that "changed everything" — he shares his Stripe screen, churn, and pricing model in the full episode.

Tools & links:

  • Buffer / PostBridge — incumbent social scheduling tools the app competes against
  • Twitter/X — primary build-in-public and distribution channel
  • Stripe — payment + the public MRR screenshots
  • AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini) — the AI SEO referral surface
  • Google Ads — only after $10K MRR

Why it matters for you: This is a replicable side-hustle template a technical founder can run *today* — pick a niche the big players ignore (you're the target user), build in public on Twitter for free distribution, and engineer AI SEO so the chatbots become your sales channel.
