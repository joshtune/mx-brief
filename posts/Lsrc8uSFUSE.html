How To Make $1B

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-26 · 11,792 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Ownership beats index exposure: pick the uncapped game.

The secret is to stop optimizing inside a capped game and own the asset instead, which is the whole argument for a side hustle over a bigger paycheck.

What it is: A ~45-second pitch for private ownership over public-market investing, framed as BlackRock's CEO vs. Blackstone's CEO.

How it works:

  • The comparison (her figures): BlackRock manages ~$9T, and CEO Larry Fink makes ~$25M a year. Blackstone manages ~$1T, and CEO Stephen Schwarzman makes $252M in salary and bonus, plus $1.2B in dividends.
  • The instructional secret: Size isn't the point. Schwarzman's firm is about 1/9th the size and his take is far bigger, because he *owns* the thing and collects the cash flow. Fink gets paid a salary-style package for running someone else's money.
  • Her thesis: She invests in private companies with outright ownership and no ceiling on upside. Most people buy public stocks, which are easier to do but harder to get rich from.
  • Caveat: It's a 45-second hot take. The comparison is cherry-picked, and the $1.2B dividend is a founder-owner payout, not something you earn by owning a few shares. She gives no steps, no numbers on returns, and nothing on risk.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: If you're building a side hustle, the point is to own the asset (the business, the cash flow, the equity) rather than trade hours or index returns for a capped payout.
