You Don't Have A Money Problem

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-15 · 16,287 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Track your money daily or stay broke.

The one habit that separates side-hustlers who compound from ones who guess — daily eyes on cash in, cash out.

What it is: A 90-second reframe: you don't have a money problem, you have an attention problem.

How it works:

  • The gut-check question: how much is in your account *right now*? Exact dollar. If you can't answer, that's the diagnosis.
  • Mentor's line: "Money is a cruel mistress. If you don't pay attention to her, she will leave you for somebody who does."
  • The challenge: look at your money every single day for one year — what came in, what went out — then work on growing both. Claim: you'll be richer in 365 days.
  • The thesis: rich people don't have a secret. They just *look*, daily, and focus effort on the levers that produce more.
  • Why most people quit: no tooling. No real P&L, no dashboard to analyze it — and the people selling those tools charge a lot.
