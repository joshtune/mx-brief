This Is Secretly Making Kids Dumber

My First Million · 2026-07-01 · 6,473 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Noise pollution measurably slows kids' cognitive development.

A quick science tidbit with zero business or money angle—safe to skip if you're here for the deals.

What it is: A short riff on a Barcelona study linking school/traffic noise to worse cognitive development in children.

How it works:

  • Barcelona study tracked 2,700 kids in two near-identical school environments differing only in noise levels.
  • Every +5 decibels of traffic noise made working memory 11% slower and complex/problem-solving memory 23% slower.
  • A follow-up meta-study of 21 studies found a −0.46 effect size on attention, memory, and reading — a medium-to-large hit.
  • Main culprits cited: trains passing, honking, and street traffic near schools.
