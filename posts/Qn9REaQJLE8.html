The Truth About Haters

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-21 · 38,511 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Reframing haters as proof you matter.

If you're building a side hustle in public, this is a 60-second mindset inoculation against the criticism that will inevitably show up the moment you start getting traction.

What it is: A short, punchy motivational riff that reframes criticism as a signal of relevance, value, and influence.

How it works:

  • Inverts each form of hate into a positive signal: enemies = you're important, gossip = you're relevant, imitation = you have charisma.
  • "If someone tries to take advantage of you, it means you have value."
  • "If they doubt you, it means you're doing things that are impossible for them."
  • Lands on the thesis: "Criticism is in some way a form of admiration" — your haters are your biggest fans.
  • No framework, no steps — it's pure mindset reframing, not a tactic.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the transcript.

Why it matters for you: Putting a side hustle online invites critics fast; treating their noise as a leading indicator of traction (not a reason to quit) keeps you shipping.
