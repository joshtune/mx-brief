The Most Overlooked Online Side Hustle Of 2026

A one-page website that made $80K in a summer.

It's a side hustle with a 300-customer path to $1M, fully outsourceable, and the guest claims AI just made the skill-heavy part scalable for the first time.

Heads up: The transcript available for this video is only the cold-open teaser — the full body didn't caption. What follows is what's actually verifiable from the clip, not a reconstruction of the pitch.

What it is: Chris interviews an operator who built a service business off a single-page website and went from $0 to ~$80K in one summer.

The claims made in the open:

  • "We've made millions of dollars. I don't see anybody else doing this" — the overlooked-niche framing
  • 300 customers = a $1M business — implies roughly $3.3K annual revenue per customer, so a mid-ticket recurring or seasonal service, not a $20/mo SaaS
  • "Every bit of it can be outsourced" — the operator isn't delivering the work personally, which is what makes it a side hustle rather than a job
  • "This year, for the first time, everything I've been doing that took my skill set can be done by AI... it's scalable, it's never been scalable before" — the business was previously gated on one person's expertise; AI removes that ceiling
  • One page, one call to action. No funnel, no blog, no content engine. Landing page + CTA drove the $80K

The pattern worth stealing (independent of the specific niche):

  • Expertise-gated service in a niche nobody's productizing → the moat is that it's boring, not that it's hard
  • Single-page site with exactly one CTA → conversion beats surface area at this scale
  • Delivery outsourced from day one → owner does sales and QA only
  • AI collapses the expert step → the constraint that kept competitors out just disappeared, which is the actual 2026 timing argument
