My First Million · 2026-07-21 · 83,029 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Sam Parr shadows a NYC brownstone-flipping millionaire.

Real dollar figures on a hands-on real estate business — buy, restore, and flip historic NYC brownstones for multi-million-dollar spreads.

What it is: Sam Parr spends a day with Mark O'Brien, who buys, restores, and sells historic brownstone homes in NYC's West Village, to break down the actual economics and lifestyle.

The featured operator:

  • Mark O'Brien — buys distressed 180-year-old brownstones (rats in the cellar, holes in the roof, mold, water damage), restores them, and resells at a large markup.
  • Lives in his own active projects while renovating them.
  • Instagram: @themarkobrienteam · buildmeabrownstone.com

The deals (actual numbers from the transcript):

  • Commercial/townhome project: $2.8M to buy + $2M to build = $4.8M in, selling for ~$6.2M (~$1.4M gross spread).
  • 94 Bank Street (his residence, 180 yrs old): bought for $55M... paid $55, investing ~$6M more, targeting a $17M sale. (He states purchase as "55" and sale as "17" — i.e. ~$5.5M in, $17M out on a landmark West Village home.)

How the business actually works:

  • Source distressed, historic, often landmark-status properties.
  • The hard part isn't construction — it's permits and regulatory approval: 2.5 years waiting on permits for the Bank Street landmark.
  • Landmark work requires architects, engineers, soil-bearing tests, foundation excavation, and verifying neighbor foundation/footing lines.
  • Mark's analogy: like selling a product online, everyone thinks the product is the magic — but the real skill is marketing (here, navigating regulation + buying right).
