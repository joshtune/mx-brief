27B model on a tiny Khadas PC with eGPU

You get the hardware answer fast (Khadas Mind Pro with a dock holding a 16GB RTX 5060 Ti), but the excerpt gives no prices, so the Mac mini comparison isn't covered.

What it is: Alex runs a 27B model on a palm-sized Khadas Mind Pro PC, using a dock with an RTX 5060 Ti (16GB VRAM), then finds that a smaller quantization beats all his layer-split tuning.

The machine:

  • PC: Khadas Mind Pro, the hand-sized box in the title
  • GPU: RTX 5060 Ti with 16GB VRAM, attached through a dock that sits on a magnet
  • Cost vs Mac mini: not stated in the transcript excerpt. Prices are probably in the video or the gear links, so check there. I'm not guessing numbers.

How it works:

  • The Q4_K_M model is 17.6 GB, so it misses the 16GB VRAM by about 1.7 GB.
  • The model has about 62 layers, and some can be offloaded to the CPU.
  • Offloading nothing to the GPU gives about 4.5 tok/s. Putting 48 layers on it gives about 11 tok/s, and 56 layers gives about 14 tok/s.
  • At 60 layers it collapses to 0.82 tok/s, about 17x slower than at 56. The model spills past VRAM and the bus becomes the bottleneck.
  • The fix was a different quantization, not more tuning. IQ4_XS is about 13 GB, still 4-bit, and fits fully in VRAM.
  • Prompt processing went from about 474 to 943 tok/s. Generation went from about 14 to 27 tok/s. Both roughly doubled.
