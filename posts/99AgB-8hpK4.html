If I Had To Start Over, This Is What I Would Do

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-07 · 26,993 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Buy into small businesses using your Venmo payment history.

It's a zero-capital script for acquiring equity in a business you already know is legit — perfect for a side hustler who wants ownership without buying anything upfront.

What it is: The "Venmo method" — a cold-outreach playbook for getting equity in a small business by growing its revenue on a performance basis, no cash down.

How it works:

  • Open Venmo and list every vendor/small business you've actually paid recently — these are warm, owner-run leads you already trust.
  • Message the owner as a genuine customer: "I love your product, I use it all the time, and I'm looking to buy a small business — open to talk?"
  • Qualify with 3 questions: (1) annual revenue, (2) do they own it outright, (3) do they want to grow.
  • Pitch a rev-share-for-equity deal: you grow revenue above their current baseline, and take ownership only on the upside — at a pro rata discount.
  • Example: they do $10K/month. You double it to $20K. You take equity equal to only *half* the percentage lift you created. Drive nothing → costs them nothing.
  • Downside is asymmetric in your favor: you risk time, not capital, and only get paid in ownership when you actually perform.

Tools & links:

  • Venmo (or any payment app history) — your ready-made pipeline of vetted, owner-operated small businesses
  • No external tools/repos mentioned in the transcript.

Why it matters for you: This is a concrete, do-it-today script to go from side-hustle operator to part-owner — you monetize a skill (driving sales) into equity instead of trading hours for a flat fee.
