Fastest Way to Waste Your Time

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-01 · 23,441 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stop doing everything yourself; buy back your time.

If your side hustle lives or dies by your hours, this reframes delegation as the highest-leverage move you can make.

What it is: A 60-second mindset flip on why doing everything yourself is the real time-waster.

How it works:

  • Investor Bill Perkins told Codie he wouldn't back her company while she still pumped her own gas — calling it a "poor person's mindset."
  • The punchline: delegating isn't about being "too good" for a task — it's that someone else may actually be *better* at it, and you're hoarding their opportunity.
  • Reframe delegation as capital allocation: time is the one asset you can't manufacture more of, so spend it only on the few things you're uniquely best at.
  • Everything else gets bought back — outsourced, hired out, or automated.
