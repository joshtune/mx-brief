He Told 5 AIs: Make Money or Get Deleted

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-16 · 61,578 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Guy gave 5 AI models $1K each to trade stocks.

It's a cheap, replicable side experiment — spin up AI-managed brokerage portfolios and let the models compete for real returns.

What it is: A guest ran a live experiment giving five AI models $1,000 each of real money to trade stocks, framed as a winner-take-all competition where losers get their subscription canceled.

How it works:

  • Funded 5 separate portfolios in a Charles Schwab brokerage account: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity — $1,000 each ($5,000 total).
  • Stakes were deliberate: $1K (not $20–50) so he'd take it seriously and the models would too.
  • Prompted each model that it's competing against the other four and he'd cancel the paid sub of any loser — "one winner" framing.
  • Key prompt hack: had to *repeatedly* push the models to give real, actionable trades instead of theoretical/hedged "this is not financial advice" recommendations. Told them explicitly: real money, okay to lose it all, be as risky as possible.
  • Grok complied immediately (loosest guardrails); Claude and ChatGPT resisted hardest on liability grounds.
  • He manually executes each model's picks in its named portfolio — the AI advises, he places the trades.
  • Reported results (his claims): Claude "more than doubled" / "30x'd" and led; Gemini started losing and turned into a "gambler."
  • Model rationale: Grok picked for X/Twitter firehose sentiment; Gemini for Google search data; Perplexity as a meta-model routing to others; ChatGPT/Claude as the base contenders.
  • Started late 2025, ~6 months in. Minimum ~$20/mo per model subscription.
