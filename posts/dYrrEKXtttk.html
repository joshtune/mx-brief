Claude Mythos is Finally Here.

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-06-09 · 97,819 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Anthropic ships Fable 5, its new Mythos-class model.

Anthropic just dropped a model that sits above Opus — if you build with Claude, your tooling, costs, and capability ceiling all just shifted.

What it is: Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, the first generally-available "Mythos-class" model (a tier above Opus), plus a restricted sibling, Mythos 5, locked to Glasswing Partners only.

How it works:

  • Two models, one base: Fable 5 is the publicly-available version; Mythos 5 is "the same model as Fable 5, but with cyber safeguards lifted" — restricted to existing Project Glasswing partners.
  • Mythos class = above Opus: Per the blog footnote, Mythos-class models are a tier of Claude that sits above Opus in capability. The original Claude Mythos preview shipped in April via Project Glasswing.
  • Pricing: $10/M input tokens, $50/M output tokens — 2× the cost of Opus. Notably, the earlier Mythos preview was 5× Opus, so Fable 5 is the capped/safe-for-general-use variant, not the full beast.
  • Free window (use it now): From launch until June 22 Fable 5 is included on Pro Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans at no extra cost. On June 23 it gets pulled from those plans and moves to token-based usage credits — Anthropic says they'll restore it to subscriptions "when sufficient capacity allows."
  • Access in Claude Code: Fable 5 shows up in the model picker — update your Claude Code if you don't see it, then select it.
  • Positioning: Marketed as "our newest model for complex long-running work" and claimed state-of-the-art on nearly all tested benchmarks (Nate rightly says take benchmarks with a grain of salt).
  • Nate's read: His earlier prediction held up — there's no public "Mythos button." Instead, Mythos capabilities get baked into an extended-Opus model (Fable) once safety guardrails are deemed solid; the full Mythos stays gated.
