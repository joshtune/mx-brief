Never Confuse Leadership and Management

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-03 · 16,170 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
The real difference between leaders and managers.

As a side hustler you're both the visionary and the operator — knowing which hat to wear keeps your team bought-in instead of just busy.

What it is: A 60-second breakdown of why leadership and management are different jobs — and why conflating them stalls growth.

How it works:

  • Managers own the *how* — they set tasks, delegate, and hold people accountable to the plan. Necessary, but it caps out.
  • Leaders own the *why* — they build the long-term vision that gets people bought in.
  • The metaphor: a manager makes sure the train runs on time; a leader decides where the train is going.
  • The tell: managers are *chosen by leaders*; leaders are *chosen by those they lead*. Authority vs. followership.

Why it matters for you: Running a side hustle, you'll default to manager-mode (checklists, accountability) because it feels productive — but nobody follows a checklist; growth comes from selling the *why* so people (contractors, partners, future hires) buy in without being told.
