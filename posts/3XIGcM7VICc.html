How it works:

  • Skill #1 — Become "the AI person": It's *relative*, not expert-level. Inside your circle, knowing more than everyone else is enough to get pulled into AI projects before any formal job title exists.
  • The play: build small AI side projects on weekends, then *show people at work* ("I made a 3-hour task take 20 minutes"). Visibility is the mechanism — that's how you get tapped to lead the company's coming "AI moments" (new model access, internal AI task force, etc.).
  • Applies to every role/vertical (marketing, sales, finance, ops, legal, CS) — not just engineers or IT.
  • Reframe, don't switch careers: ask "how do I get faster/better at *my specific task* with AI?" Analogy: accountants who refused to learn Excel were done; early adopters were just faster.
