He Built an $8K/Month App for $20

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-11-24 · 33,523 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
No-code AI photo-appraisal app hits $8K/month

It's a real side-hustle case study, a non-coder who went from idea to quitting his job in under 2 months, but this clip is only the teaser, so the how-to is in the full episode.

What it is: A teaser for a Koerner Office episode. Guest Gibb, a college student with a full-time job, built an AI app that tells you what any item is worth from a photo. It now pulls about $7.5–8K/month, which he says is mostly profit.

How it works:

  • The origin: Gibb bought two industrial fridges to flip, couldn't work out what to sell them for, and decided resale wasn't his thing.
  • The pivot: A couple of weeks later he heard another Koerner episode about building apps with AI without knowing how to code. The same pain (what is this worth?) became the product.
  • The product: Upload or snap a photo and the app tells you what the thing is worth.
  • The build: About $20 and one hour to a working app. He had no coding background, and the $20 was his whole startup cost.
  • The speed: Under 2 months from hearing the idea to out-earning his full-time job (he cites roughly $1,600/month there). He then quit and hired a buddy to do the day-to-day work, so it's now low-friction for him.
  • Marketing, as teased (details are in the full episode):
  • Influencer video: He convinced an influencer to make one. It got hundreds of thousands of views but only 3 paying customers, so views don't equal sales.
  • Local news segment: A segment on him reportedly grew the business 500% in 2 weeks.
  • Small creator deal: He pays a creator with only about 7,000 followers $200/month, and that creator brings in about 25% of his customers. Chris asks how good a deal that is.
  • Caveat: The clip says $8K/month revenue and $7.5K profit in the same breath, and the numbers are self-reported. The transcript doesn't name the AI tool, the pricing model or the app's name.
