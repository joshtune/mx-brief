What it is: Matt's weekly roundup. The transcript we have only covers the first story, the Anthropic S-1 filing. The other five items are named in the description but not detailed here.

How it works:

  • Anthropic S-1 filed Monday, listing expected around Nov 16 at a $1.5–2T valuation. The filing is about 85 pages.
  • Revenue: about $4.585B in 2025, and over $16B in H1 2026 alone (roughly 4x all of last year).
  • Net loss: about $80B over the same period. Matt says much of it is non-cash, because early investor loans convert to equity.
  • Who gets rich (from an X post by Andrew Benson, unverified): at about $118/share, roughly $199B goes to the 7 co-founders, $100B to the public benefit fund, and $331B to employees.
  • That averages about $62M each across about 5,300 employees.
  • About 940 people would hit nine figures.
  • Early investors: $25.91/share in April, $58.90 at Series H, and about $118 at the expected listing price.
  • Compute partners: Amazon, Google and Broadcom stand to make hundreds of billions through compute deals.
  • Bubble test: Matt frames the IPO as the first real read on whether the people closest to AI will put money behind it. The transcript cuts off mid-sentence.
  • Named in the description but not covered in the transcript: GPT-6.1 Terra (faster computer use, lower API cost), Google Genie 4 (real-time interactive world model), Gemini 4 Carbon, Kimi K3.1 Pro, and OpenAI's claim that it broke an AI math assumption that hundreds of papers rely on.
