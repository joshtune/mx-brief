She turned sourdough videos into a $70M business

My First Million · 2026-06-18 · 132,238 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
How a tradwife influencer built a $70M farm brand.

A masterclass in converting a content audience into a multi-product CPG business doing ~$70-80M in revenue.

What it is: Hannah Neeleman (Ballerina Farms) turned sourdough/farm content into a ~$70-80M direct-to-consumer food brand.

Who she is:

  • Hannah Neeleman, face of the "tradwife" influencer category
  • ~20M followers across all platforms
  • Content formula: her on the farm, making sourdough bread and recipes from scratch

How the money is made:

  • Built audience first via aspirational farm-life content, then monetized with physical products
  • Sourdough starter/mix — flagship product tied directly to her most popular content
  • Electrolyte mix — higher-margin supplement-style SKU
  • Meat — farm-raised protein sold direct
  • Milk — sold at one point (since dropped, likely regulatory/logistics)
  • Estimated $70-80M in annual revenue off the product line

The playbook:

  • Niche down hard (sourdough + tradwife aesthetic) to own a category
  • Sell the lifestyle, not the product — products are the merch for the content
  • Vertically integrate: own the farm, the content, and the storefront

Tools & links:

  • Ballerina Farms — the DTC storefront for all SKUs

Why it matters for you: Clear case study of audience-first commerce — build distribution (content) before product, then attach high-margin physical goods to ride it.
