1 Billion People Barely Anyone Is Selling To

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-11-06 · 23,388 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Cheap customer acquisition via AppLovin mobile-game ads

If you want a side hustle, Chris argues paid ads are the skill that makes any product sell, and he's pitching a new ad platform while it's still cheap.

What it is: A pitch to buy ads on AppLovin, which shows immersive ads inside mobile games, before the platform gets expensive like Google and Facebook Ads did.

How it works:

  • The thesis: Every 5-10 years a platform opens where you can buy customers for pennies. Google clicks once cost $0.02-$0.05 and now cost $2-$5. Chris says AppLovin is at the 2010 Facebook Ads or 2002 Google Ads stage.
  • The audience: Over 1 billion people play mobile games instead of scrolling short-form video. Meta, Snapchat and TikTok advertisers rarely reach them, so competition is low and ads are cheap.
  • The ad format: Immersive ads that run between levels or at natural breaks in gameplay. They are not banner ads or the accidental-click kind.
  • Proof point: Cookware brand Plateful sold titanium pans to gamers. It started at $500/day, scaled to $200,000/day and stayed profitable. About 80% of its ad spend now goes to AppLovin.
  • The scaling caveat: Chris calls it a law of marketing that cost per acquisition rises as you spend more per day. Plateful still stayed profitable.
  • Access change: Until last month you had to spend about $20k/day on Meta ads to qualify. That requirement is gone, so anyone can run ads now.
  • The plan: Chris says he'll set up a real campaign live for one of his own e-commerce brands. The transcript cuts off before the setup walkthrough, so the steps below are not covered here.
  • Not just e-commerce: He says it also works for other products and services, but e-commerce suits it best. Those brands know their cost per customer to a tenth of a cent, so they can tell quickly whether it's profitable.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle, the takeaway is to pick a product (dropshipped, DTC or a service), test small on a low-competition ad channel, and scale only while your cost per customer stays under your margin. Treat the Plateful numbers as a best case, and note the sponsor link before trusting the hype.
