Chuck and Daniel Miessler lay out the exact AI prompts to optimize your harness, security posture, and life — the practical AI-workflow playbook you follow this channel for.

What it is: Chuck sits down with Daniel Miessler (creator of Fabric + PAI/Life OS) to plan high-leverage prompts for a limited-time window on a top-tier AI model, framed as a 6-day countdown (through July 7).

How it works:

  • Three-tier strategy: (1) optimize your systems, (2) optimize your life, (3) tackle life's biggest questions if time remains.
  • Harness optimization first — Miessler's #1 move. Don't just improve a subsystem; find the systems that improve *other* systems, then upgrade the deepest layer. Your AI harness governs everything, so fix it first (his is PAI/Life OS; think interconnected hooks, prompts, system prompts, and skills).
  • Security audit — have the smartest model review everything: prompt-injection handling, plus "find every single thing we've deployed" (Vercel, Cloudflare, etc.), enumerate it, and build a continuous scanning system — attack-surface management for all your infra (no open ports, no open auth, test the APIs).
  • Self-model / life audit — a "what am I actually doing?" review for anyone who's never done a T-loss-style self-assessment: direction, stress, focus.
  • Meta-advice: prioritize hard. If you had ONE question for a super-intelligent alien, don't waste it on the next Marvel plot — throw your biggest problems at it.
