Insane Interactive World Creator (Genie 4)

Matt Wolfe · 2026-10-28 · 16,544 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Google DeepMind's Genie 4 generates playable, editable worlds

Skip it if you're building web apps: it's a cool demo with nothing you can build on yet, so this is only worth a glance to see where interactive generative worlds are headed.

What it is: Matt's hands-on demo of Genie 4, Google DeepMind's text-to-interactive-world model, currently limited to a small group of testers.

How it works:

  • You type a place and a character (e.g. "giant gorilla enters the city") and it generates a world you can move around in.
  • You can edit the world while inside it with new prompts. Matt added a breaching whale and a news helicopter over San Diego mid-flight.
  • Google's demos now include generated sound, so characters can talk to each other.
  • Limits Matt hit:
  • Each world lasts only 2 minutes.
  • Controls for the car and plane were hard, and it doesn't always do what you tell it.
  • "King Kong" was blocked, likely for IP reasons, but "giant gorilla" worked.
  • His Rainbow Road prompt came out looking little like Mario Kart.
  • Availability: tester-only for now. Google says it's coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers.

Tools & links:

  • FutureTools — Matt's AI tools directory, the only link in the video. No API, SDK, or repo for Genie 4 is mentioned.

Why it matters for you: Not much for web-app work today, since there's no API, no integration path, and 2-minute sessions. Check back if Google ships developer access or it turns into something embeddable.
