Asia Grant's personalized perfume tour business in NYC

A one-person, near-zero-capital service business clearing $250k/year by adding a personalization twist to a walk down a street of perfume shops.

What it is: Asia Grant runs guided perfume-shopping tours in New York City, walking small groups through a cluster of perfume stores with picks pre-selected for each person's personality.

Who: Asia Grant, solo operator. Reported at ~$250k/year.

How it works:

  • Before the tour, every guest fills out a Typeform about their personality and interests.
  • Asia uses those answers to hand-pick scents per person.
  • Guests get a printed sheet: three stores, three perfumes or colognes to try at each.
  • She walks the group through the three stores and explains why each pick should suit that individual.
  • No purchase pressure: buy or don't. The tour fee is the product, not the perfume.

How she makes money (from what's shown in this clip):

  • Charges for the tour itself. The curated experience is the paid product.
  • Piggybacks on existing retail. She owns no inventory and no storefront, just the stores on that NYC street.
  • Personalization is the moat. The Typeform intake plus a custom sheet turns a free stroll into something people pay for.
  • Low overhead: a form tool, printed sheets, and her own time and taste.
  • Likely upside not stated in the clip: affiliate or referral arrangements with the shops, group and corporate bookings, gift bookings.

Tools & links:

  • Typeform — pre-tour intake survey on personality and preferences

Why it matters for you: The whole business is a lightweight intake form plus curated recommendations layered on top of someone else's inventory, a pattern you could template for any niche retail category.
