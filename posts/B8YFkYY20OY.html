What it is: A short news clip on Tuesday Labs' home robot, a small droid that tidies up around the house.

How it works:

  • Built by a New York startup, Tuesday Labs. The founder previously worked on Spot's arm at Boston Dynamics.
  • Animators and a former Pixar character designer made 3D animations of the motion first. The robot was then trained on those moves, which is why it moves like a cartoon character.
  • Demos include sweeping crumbs into a dustpan, watering a bonsai, finding a lost phone, and handing over a clarinet.
  • It runs on a mix of AI and sometimes a human teleoperator. Early owners effectively help train future versions.
  • Estimated price is about $20,000, with shipping not expected until 2028.
