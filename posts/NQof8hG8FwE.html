Rundown of Apple's WWDC 2026 Siri AI features.

Apple's natural-language 'describe a shortcut' automation and on-device + private-cloud model routing hint at UX patterns and privacy expectations your web apps will be measured against.

What it is: A quick recap of the AI/Siri features Apple announced at WWDC 2026 — mostly consumer-facing, light on dev detail.

How it works:

  • New Siri does multi-step agentic tasks: checks concert tickets, sees it's a lottery, sets a reminder to enter
  • Visual understanding: feed it an Instagram photo, it identifies the location and gives directions
  • Voice-driven manipulation: rearranging photos/folders by dictation
  • Siri lands in macOS Spotlight (Cmd+Space) — prompt it to compare documents, reorganize folders, etc.
  • New Siri camera mode does live visual identification (e.g. "this appears to be a traditional cricket ball")
  • Describe a shortcut (Matt's favorite): natural-language → auto-generated Shortcuts automation, e.g. "when I'm leaving work, message Pedro I'm on my way with my ETA"
  • Model routing: Apple's own foundation models + Google Gemini, run either on-device or via private cloud where Google sees none of your data

Tools & links:

  • No repos, SDKs, or tools shared — purely a keynote recap. (Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models framework would be the dev hook, but it isn't covered here.)

Why it matters for you: The "describe what you want, it builds the automation" pattern and on-device/private-cloud model split are exactly the natural-language UX and privacy bars users will expect from your web apps — but there's nothing actionable to build with here.

Verdict: Skim-worthy at best; consumer keynote roundup with no developer payload.
