What it is: A 60-second teaser for the Aurora A7X, a real, non-CGI robot head from Face Good with startlingly human facial movement.

How it works:

  • Face Good has collected actors' facial expressions since 2015 for games like Black Myth: Wukong.
  • It reuses the AI that animates video game faces to drive a physical robotic face.
  • Only the eyes, mouth, and neck move for now.
  • Neck wires are visible, so it's not fully disguised.
  • No price yet, but there's a free waitlist.
