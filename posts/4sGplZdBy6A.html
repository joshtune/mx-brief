When a humanoid and a drone become besties

Humanoid robot carries and launches a transforming drone

Skip it: this is a robotics demo with zero bearing on building web apps, so it won't change how you ship anything.

What it is: Caltech's X1 system, a Unitree G1 humanoid that carries and launches an M4 drone, built for emergency response.

How it works:

  • The humanoid is an off-the-shelf Unitree G1 (~$16,000), not a custom robot.
  • The drone is backpack-mounted. The humanoid bends over and launches it into the air.
  • The M4 turns its rotors into wheels and drives like an RC car.
  • It moves in 8 directions on the ground.
  • In the demo it drives up to a pond, switches to flight, crosses the water, then keeps going.
  • Pitch: the humanoid walks to the scene and the drone gets deeper, faster.
