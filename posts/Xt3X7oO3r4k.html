How Do You Deal With Haters?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-21 · 5,723 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Haters cost you nothing; irrelevance costs everything.

Side-hustle launches get criticized, and this 60-second reframe stops other people's opinions from killing yours.

What it is: A short pep-talk reframing haters as proof you matter, with one honest caveat about real criticism.

The instructional secret: Judge criticism by whether it can cost you money. Haters can't, so ignore them. Useful criticism can, so fix it.

How it works:

  • Zero-dollar test — Haters never take a sale from you, because they were never going to buy from you or clap for you.
  • Obscurity is the real risk — Nobody copies or attacks someone with no value. No enemies usually means no relevance.
  • Talk behind your back = free distribution — Your name is in rooms you never entered.
  • Intimidated competitors do your R&D — If they feel threatened enough to react, they've told you where you're winning.
  • "Impossible" describes the critic — A mocking comment about your goal shows their limits, not yours.
  • The caveat — Not all criticism is jealousy. If you screwed up, own it. Sort criticism into noise (ignore) and signal (act).
  • The payoff — The people who mocked you end up copying you.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. This is a mindset clip with no tools, repos or frameworks.

Why it matters for you: The first weeks of a side hustle bring friends' doubt and strangers' snark, and quitting early over that noise is the most common way these die. Use the zero-dollar test: if a criticism doesn't change a sale, a customer or a product flaw, keep shipping.
