Backdoor into Kimi K3 via the developer platform.

If you want to test Kimi K3's 1M-token context on your app code without waiting on a consumer subscription, the dev platform is an open side door — but the whole clip is a 90-second hack, not engineering content.

What it is: A 90-second workaround for accessing Kimi K3 (and the "K3 Swarm" tier) while consumer subscriptions are paused due to demand.

How it works:

  • Kimi paused new consumer subscriptions after a signup surge — the waitlist is on the *consumer* product, not the developer platform
  • Go to `platform.kimmy.ai` (as pronounced in the video — likely Kimi's developer platform) and create an account
  • Add any amount of credit in the billing section; ~$1 is enough to unlock access
  • Open the AI Playground — K3 should now appear in the model selector
  • In settings, crank the context window to the full 1M token limit and raise reasoning effort
  • Prompt directly in the playground and use it like any other chatbot
