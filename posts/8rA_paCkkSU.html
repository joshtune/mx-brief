Don't Say These Three Phrases

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-28 · 31,689 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Three verbal tics that erase your authority.

If you're selling a side hustle to customers, partners, or a first hire, these three throwaway phrases are quietly discounting your offer before anyone hears it.

What it is: A 90-second rant on three self-diminishing phrases that make people dismiss you — triggered by one of Codie's own employees saying one in passing.

How it works:

  • "I'm probably wrong." — You've killed your own argument before making it. Codie's fix: just say the thing. If you're wrong, you're wrong — but at least you had a shot at being right instead of being auto-dismissed.
  • "I'm the worst." — Said as a joke over a missed email or a 5-minute late arrival. Your brain doesn't process the joke. Each use files another piece of evidence against yourself, and you keep ruling against yourself.
  • "Sorry." — Fine for actual mistakes. Not fine for speaking or showing up. Over-apologizing tells the room (and you) that your presence requires an apology.
  • The through-line: You narrate yourself into a small corner, then become the small person the narration describes.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle lives or dies on cold outreach, pricing conversations, and negotiations where you have no brand to hide behind — hedging language is the cheapest thing you can stop doing today, and it costs nothing to fix.
