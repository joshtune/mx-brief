RTX Spark Laptops… But they’re not all equal

Alex Ziskind · 2026-11-20 · 201,728 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Four NVIDIA RTX Spark laptops compared at Micro Center

Skip it for your Mac mini question: the first part of the video covers laptop screens, keyboards and ports, with no AI benchmarks and no prices.

What it is: Alex compares four laptops built on NVIDIA's RTX Spark platform at Micro Center, then takes two finalists back to the office to pick a daily driver.

How it works:

  • All four share the same chip: an ARM CPU and an NVIDIA GPU on one pool of unified memory. This is the same idea as Apple Silicon. He says he'll explain why it matters, but that explanation is past the point where the transcript cuts off.
  • HP OmniBook X 14: 14in 3K OLED, 120 Hz touchscreen. Small and portable, with a loose screen hinge and a non-haptic diving-board trackpad. Ports: 2x USB-C, 2x USB-A, HDMI.
  • Microsoft Surface 15: 15in mini-LED, 3:2 screen (3270x2180, 120 Hz) that gets very bright. Great keyboard and a big haptic trackpad. It weighs about 2 kg, and the screen bounces when pressed. Ports: 3x USB4, USB-A, HDMI, SD.
  • MSI Prestige N16 Flip: 16in 360° convertible with a stylus. Top config is a 4K tandem OLED at up to 1600 nits. No numpad, and the trackpad is not haptic, so he'd use a mouse. Ports: 2x USB4, 2x USB-A, HDMI, SD.
  • ASUS ProArt P16: 16in 4K tandem OLED (3840x2400, 120 Hz, up to 1600 nits), stiff and very light. Keyboard has 1.7 mm travel and there's a big haptic trackpad. The old trackpad dial is gone. Ports: 3x USB4, USB-A, HDMI, SD Express.
  • Two finalists went back to the office, plus a battery test with "unexpected" results. The transcript cuts off before the results, the winner or any AI testing.
