Is This The Easiest Business To Start?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-31 · 17,056 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Lawn care as a recurring-revenue upsell machine.

It's the clearest example of a side hustle where the mowing is just customer acquisition and the real money is the $60K backyard patio you sell later.

What it is: Lawn care / landscaping as a $2K-to-start side hustle with weekly recurring revenue and a high-ticket upsell path.

How it works:

  • The unit economics: $50–$150 per residential property per week, on the low end. A solid crew hits 10–15 properties/day.
  • The recurring part: Same customers every week. Grass doesn't stop growing, so churn is structurally low — this is subscription revenue without the SaaS.
  • The actual secret: You don't make money mowing. Mowing is the trust-building, foot-in-the-door service. The margin lives in the upsell — start at grass cutting, end at designing a $60,000 backyard patio.
  • The customer profile: Rich people have yards, rich people hate yardwork. Your best mowing customers are pre-qualified buyers for the expensive stuff.
  • Startup cost: ~$2,000 to start from scratch.
  • The acquisition play (if you have capital): Buy an existing book of business instead. Contrarian Academy member Meredith is acquiring a landscaping business valued at $900K where 85% of customers have been with it 10+ years.
  • Why that deal is interesting: No website, no Instagram, no Google Ads, no automated billing, no online booking, zero digital presence. Twenty years of loyal customers and the entire digital layer completely unbuilt.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that the boring service is a customer-acquisition channel, not the product — and if you can build the digital layer (booking, billing, CRM, site) that these businesses universally lack, you're not competing on mowing, you're buying a customer list with a software moat you already know how to build.
