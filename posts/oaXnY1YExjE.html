This Kid Found the Fastest Way to Make Money With AI Apps

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-19 · 46,094 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Teen makes $225K vibe-coding mobile apps, no coding.

A 19-year-old netted $225K in 9 months building niche mobile apps with zero coding skill — a concrete, copyable side-hustle blueprint if you want to ship AI apps fast.

What it is: A case-study interview with a ~19-year-old who built five AI-powered mobile apps in ~9 months and pulled ~$225K in revenue — with no coding background.

How it works:

  • Picks narrow, passion-adjacent niches he personally understands (started with Wrestle AI, an app for wrestlers — he was a varsity wrestling captain).
  • Builds native iOS/Swift apps using an AI app builder ("Ror" — likely Rork), described as "Replit but for Swift/mobile." No hand-written code.
  • First app (Fight AI) was a throwaway test build; Wrestle AI was the first real product and generated almost all revenue to date.
  • Distribution = posting authentically about the apps for free on social; organic momentum did the selling (~$8K in week one).
  • Monetizes via subscription (micro-SaaS model); scaled two newer apps to $10K/mo each in ~45 days. Revenue trajectory: ~$30K Dec → ~$40K Jan.
  • Runs a portfolio (3–4 revenue-generating apps) rather than betting on one; builds "systems" early so a single app doesn't need constant attention.
  • Bonus leverage: his free posting got him noticed by the app-builder's founder, who hired him onto the growth team — insider access to the tool he builds on.

Tools & links:

  • Rork — AI app builder for native mobile (Swift/iOS); the core tool referenced as "Ror."
  • Replit — the comparison point for AI-assisted building (web-focused).
  • Playbook context (host's plugs): Playmakers AI community and HighLevel — sponsor CRM, not required for the method.

Why it matters for you: This is the cleanest "start a side hustle" template in the AI-app space — pick a niche you know, use an AI builder to skip coding, ship a subscription app, and market by posting in public — all achievable nights-and-weekends.
