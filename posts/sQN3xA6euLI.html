$98K in 2 Months From a Business That Costs $9 to Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-11-09 · 51,273 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Free Skool community plus TikTok funnels into $15K coaching

If you want a side hustle with near-zero startup cost, this is a concrete, if self-reported, playbook: free community, TikTok traffic, and high-ticket DM sales.

What it is: Nico, the guest, started a free Skool community (the transcript spells it "school") about 60 days ago. He grew it to ~257 members from TikTok alone and sold coaching, consulting, and done-for-you services to ~12-15 of them for ~$98K total.

How it works:

  • Setup: a Skool community costs $9 to start. No product, no ads, no AI expertise needed up front.
  • Traffic: a few TikTok videos, usually only a few hundred views each, pointing people into the free group.
  • Conversion: ~5-6% of members have bought something (12-15 of ~250). Chris estimates ~10% will have bought within 6 months, even if growth stopped.
  • Offer ladder:
  • ~4 buyers at $15K programs
  • a couple at $2-3K, depending on payment plan
  • a few lower-ticket buyers ($27-$200) who add upsells
  • a few one-off done-for-you jobs
  • Sales: he closes entirely in DMs and never gets on a sales call. This is a $15K sale with no call.
  • Timing: most people take days to weeks to warm up. Some buy within 24 hours after seeing him on TikTok.
  • Margin: nearly all profit, since the offers are consulting, services, and courses with no ads or inventory.
  • Niche: he sells AI coaching, but Chris says most of his buyers are in fitness, mindset, or whatever they already know. The model works for any skill.
  • Promised but cut off in this excerpt:
  • the three-button poll that shows who's ready to buy
  • the exact DM scripts that close $15K deals
  • four tricks to get free promotion from Skool's own discovery

Tools & links:

  • Skool — community platform; $9 to start, with built-in discovery that Nico uses for free promotion
  • Beehiiv — newsletter platform and sponsor of the episode; code CHRIS30 gives 30% off the first 3 months
  • TikTok — top-of-funnel traffic source, no ads

Why it matters for you: The cheap, low-risk shape of the idea (skill, free community, short-form video, DM sales) is real, but the numbers are self-reported by one guest. About four $15K buyers drive most of the revenue, so treat it as a best case and not a typical outcome.
