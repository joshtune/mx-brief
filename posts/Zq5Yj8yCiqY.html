How Matt makes his AI video intro effects.

Skip unless you make video content — this is a video-editing tutorial with zero relevance to building web apps.

What it is: A step-by-step walkthrough of how Matt creates the AI visual-effect intros (bursting through walls, claw-machine pickups) that open his Friday news videos.

How it works:

  • Record yourself in DaVinci Resolve, capturing a frame of the empty room before you sit, then a frame right before you talk
  • Export both as stills (File → Export → Current Frame as Still)
  • In Runway, use Custom → Keyframe, upload frame one (empty) and frame two (seated) as first/last frames
  • Prompt the transition (e.g. "the man bursts through the back wall and sits down")
  • Generate, download, drop into DaVinci, and clean the seam with the Smooth Cut video transition

Tools & links:

  • Runway — video generation (his main tool, has all the models)
  • DaVinci Resolve — editing, frame export, Smooth Cut
  • Models: Seed Dance 2.0 (his pick), Kling, Google Veo 3.1 — also available in Krea, Higgsfield, Leonardo

Why it matters for you: It doesn't — this is content-creator craft, not engineering. Nothing here helps you build web apps.
