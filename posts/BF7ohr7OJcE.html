Don't Fall For This AI Trap

Matt Wolfe · 2026-06-30 · 11,769 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Knowing what NOT to automate separates power users.

A useful gut-check on where AI actually pays off in your app work versus where it just burns cycles — but it's a thin ad for a paid webinar.

What it is: A short opinion piece (with a sponsor pitch) arguing the real AI skill is judging *which* tasks to automate, not automating everything.

How it works:

  • Reframe the question: stop asking "Can this be automated?" and start asking "How would *I* automate this?"
  • Screen each task before automating: Is it text/image-based (good fit) or nuanced/artistic with edge cases and unclear directions (bad fit)?
  • Matt's own miss: automating YouTube thumbnails with AI — looked impressive but took heavy trial-and-error, was inconsistent, and lost to a human designer.
  • Caveat: AI-generated art is fine on a tight budget or for a one-off launch; just don't expect top consistency yet.
