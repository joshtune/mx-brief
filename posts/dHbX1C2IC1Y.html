17 Lessons I Needed to Learn Before I Made My First Million

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-12 · 5,263 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Seventeen rapid-fire money lessons from Codie's pre-million years.

Under two minutes, it hands a side-hustler the one rule that actually moves income: be more useful to people who already have money, and get paid on outcomes, not hours.

What it is: A rapid-fire listicle short. No story, no framework, just 17 one-liners on money, risk, and self-permission. Zero tools or links mentioned.

The instructional secret: The list hides one real thesis. Wealth comes from proximity to money plus outcome-based pay plus speed, not from intelligence or saving. Everything else is permission-giving.

How it works (the 17, grouped):

  • Permission to chase money: it's hard to change the world broke (1); being rich is awesome, don't apologize (17); learn to be disliked or you're stuck in other people's beliefs (16); you're not everyone's cup of tea (4).
  • Volume early, focus later: say yes to everything until you have cash, then narrow (2); do it all, you'll die eventually (3); protect your hope from jaded older people (11).
  • Where the money actually is: be more useful to people who have money (15); saving won't make you rich, earning does (14); in your 20s learn to invest, apprentice, and get around money (5); work only for companies that reward outcomes, not hours (9).
  • How winners behave: most successful people aren't that smart, they move fast and take risk (6); do favors, don't take them (8); be wealthy, don't look wealthy (7).
  • Environment and energy: leave your hometown for a bigger pond (10); don't take a remote job if you're prone to isolation (12); burnout comes from work that doesn't light you up with no end in sight, not from working hard (13).

Side-hustle translation:

  • Lesson 15 is the business model. Pick customers who already have budgets. Selling to broke people is a charity, not a hustle.
  • Lesson 9 is the pricing model. Charge for results, not time. A side hustle billed hourly is just a second job.
  • Lesson 2 is the launch strategy. Take every gig for the first year to find what pays, then cut everything that doesn't.
  • Lesson 6 is the excuse killer. Speed and risk tolerance beat cleverness, which is good news for anyone shipping nights and weekends.

Why it matters for you: Low-view, low-effort short, but lessons 9 and 15 together are a complete side-hustle filter: sell outcomes to people with money, everything else is noise.
