3 Businesses I Would Never Buy

Three hyped businesses Codie refuses to buy.

A fast filter for avoiding side hustles that look like cash flow but are actually low-margin traps or platform-dependent jobs.

What it is: A quick teardown of three popular "start this business" ideas Codie considers structurally broken.

How it works:

  • ATM routes — Margins are tiny (1–3% of surcharge) and payback is slow. An average ATM makes only $1,500–$5K/year in *revenue* (not profit). Contrary to the pitch, she says ATMs are nothing like vending machines.
  • Amazon FBA — The platform owns you: Amazon can suspend your account overnight and copy your product. The catalog is flooded with Chinese me-too copycats, so no durable moat.
  • Consulting firms — In an AI age, anyone can spin up SOPs and claim expertise. It's transactional (a job, not a business), has little recurring revenue, and clients trust *you* the consultant, not an asset that compounds — unless you're a Deloitte-scale brand.

The through-line:

  • Avoid businesses with thin margins, platform dependency (someone else can kill you), or no recurring revenue.
  • "Don't build your business on a house of sand" — buy assets that compound and that *you* control.
