It's a 2-3 hour/week, 90%+ margin side hustle where you broker demand you already have — exactly the low-lift, high-upside play if you want income without another full-time job.

What it is: A guest breaks down a site-rental / brokering business that did $192.5K in one month at 90%+ net margins on 2-3 hours/week — mostly passive.

How it works:

  • The operator controls multiple "sites" that get rented out. Smaller ones (~$4-5K/mo) run fully passive; bigger ones (worth hundreds of thousands each) get more attention because the upside is $30-40K per site.
  • The core move is dead simple brokering: he calls a site owner and says *"I've got customers that want this — if I send you people, can we work out a cut?"* Most say yes.
  • So it's demand-first arbitrage: you bring the buyers, the owner has the asset, you take a cut. Minimal capital, minimal ongoing work.
  • He knows three people who've each cleared $1M+ since 2022 doing this on the side of a day job; several quit their jobs off it.
