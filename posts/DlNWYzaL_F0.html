The Most Overlooked Online Side Hustle Of 2026

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-31 · 149,111 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Selling high-converting one-page sites to local service businesses

It's a low-lift, fully outsourceable side hustle where the product is a single conversion-focused page — and the guest claims 300 customers is a million-dollar business.

What it is: A 15-year internet-marketing veteran's business selling conversion-optimized one-page websites (plus supporting marketing assets) to local service businesses — plumbers, HVAC, pest control, roofing, tire shops. One client went zero → $80K in a summer off a single new page.

The core insight:

  • Local service businesses have a *problem-urgency* customer: leaky roof, bugs in the house, dead AC. They don't browse, they need someone they trust *right now*.
  • Those businesses think about neither traffic nor conversion — they buy "a website" as a checkbox.
  • Web designers ship award-winning sites, not high-converting ones. Hero images eat the fold, social proof lives below it, nobody scrolls.
  • Bounce rate for local business sites is ~90%. Per the stats cited, under 10 seconds with no second action = bounce.
  • ~90% of local business traffic is mobile. Many visitors never leave the Google Business Profile at all.
  • Recurring client complaint: "I spent $5,000 on my plumbing website and can't track one piece of business."

How the offer works:

  • Sell the outcome (booked calls), not the deliverable (a website).
  • Build one page, one call to action — tap-to-call above the fold, trust signals and reviews immediately visible, no wasted hero real estate.
  • Optimize for the 3-second decision: within three seconds of landing, the visitor must be convinced you're the one to fix their problem.
  • Traffic comes from exactly three places — paid, organic/free, or borrowed (affiliate/referral). Pick one and pair it with the converting page.
  • Unit math: ~300 customers = a million-dollar business. Every piece of delivery can be outsourced.
  • The video's 2026 hook: the video/studio production that used to require thousands in gear and a studio can now be generated with AI tools — which is what makes this newly *scalable* rather than skill-gated.

Tools & links:

  • beehiiv — newsletter platform, sponsor read (30% off first 30 days); relevant if you bolt a newsletter onto the offer
  • Google Business Profile — where a large share of local traffic actually stops; part of the surface you're optimizing
  • No specific AI video/site tools are named in the transcript excerpt — the "use AI instead of a studio" claim stays generic

Caveats:

  • Transcript is truncated mid-sentence; the actual step-by-step delivery process and pricing are likely in the unseen portion.
  • "300 customers = $1M" implies ~$275/mo retainers — that's a sales-volume business, not a build-once business. The hard part is local B2B sales, not the page.

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle, this one is well inside an engineer's skill floor — a single static page with a tap-to-call button is a weekend of work; the entire moat is understanding conversion and being willing to cold-sell plumbers.
