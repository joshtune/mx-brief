Build In Public

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-16 · 12,873 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Say your goal publicly to force yourself to deliver.

If your side hustle only exists in your head, it has no deadline and no cost to quitting — going public installs both for free.

What it is: A 90-second pitch for public accountability as the cheapest forcing function you can install on a goal.

How it works:

  • Pick the goal you'd be embarrassed to miss — Codie's was Main Street Millionaire hitting the NYT Bestseller list, roughly a 0.0001% shot.
  • Post it publicly, by name, with the specific outcome. Vague goals give you an exit; specific ones don't.
  • The mechanism is fear of public failure, not motivation. Once it's out there, quitting has an audience.
  • Her framing: the moment she posted it, "there was no way in hell I was not going to achieve that thing."
  • Default bias is toward attempting: "I'd rather fail than never try."
  • Selection filter — ask what your own 0.0001% goal is. If it doesn't scare you to say out loud, it's not the one.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or URLs mentioned. The whole method is a post.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle competes with a day job for your evenings, and the day job has a boss — building in public is how you give the side hustle one.
