He Makes $16K/Month Renting Out a Space He Doesn’t Own

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-19 · 23,055 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Rent an empty church, flip it into a party venue

It's a concrete, weekend-only side hustle with no employees and a startup cost under $3K, and the video walks through how the guest set it up.

What it is: A rental-arbitrage event venue. The guest leases an empty church in a strip mall and rents it out for birthday parties, anniversaries and similar events. He clears about $16K/month part-time, with just him and his wife.

How it works:

  • Space: An empty church unit in a strip mall parking lot. He leased it rather than bought it, so he doesn't own the property.
  • Setup: A modest cleanup, "not as much as you might think." He says you could start for under $3,000.
  • Revenue: Highest month was about $22.5K (the transcript says "225"). The average is about $16K. The business started roughly a year ago.
  • Staffing: No employees. He and his wife run it on weekends, and he still has a job.
  • Rent covered by anchor tenants: Three churches rent the space every Sunday. They cover his rent and never throw a party.
  • Paid ads: He spent $100 on Facebook ads and got about $10K in bookings in one day. He calls it "an infinite money glitch."
  • Viral loop: A QR code on the wall turns every party into roughly four more parties through guest referrals.
  • Risk: Renters do weird things. He once found a goat hanging from the ceiling and guests torching pieces of it.

Tools & links:

  • Facebook Ads — the main demand channel ($100 test spend). No other tools or URLs are named in the excerpt.
  • QR code on the wall — referral capture at every event.

Caveat: The transcript is only the intro and teaser. Pricing, lease terms, insurance, permits and the ad creative are likely covered in the full episode, but not here. Church-to-event-venue zoning and occupancy rules vary by city, so check yours before signing anything.

Why it matters for you: It's a low-capital, weekend-friendly side hustle where recurring anchor tenants cover the rent and paid ads plus a referral QR code fill the calendar.
