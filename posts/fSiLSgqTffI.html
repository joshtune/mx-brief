The Most Overlooked $1K/Hour Business Anyone Can Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-25 · 9,254 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Laid-off guy turns one Facebook post into $50K in 5 months.

It's a real, copyable side-hustle blueprint: near-zero startup cost, upfront payment, and a funding source (state reimbursement) most people don't know exists.

What it is: Case study on "Mike," a 50-year-old who got laid off, posted a no-frills Facebook ad from his garage at 3:30am, and built a kids'-focused, small-group class/camp business that hit $50K in 5 months.

How it works:

  • No website, no photos, no listed price in the original post — just a plain Facebook post describing the offer.
  • Parents pay $75/hour per kid; sessions run mornings (9am–noon), so it's ~3 hours of active work per session.
  • Money collected upfront, so there's no float or AR risk — startup costs were under $450.
  • Revenue scaled fast: ~$8K → $8K → $12K → $16K/month as word spread, then a spring break week alone cleared $12,000.
  • Landed a recurring $30,000 contract for just 2 hours of work every Friday.
  • A hook mentioned mid-episode: one well-crafted sentence got a tool company to send $5,000 of free gear (sponsorship/barter angle for gear-heavy side hustles).
  • Key funding detail: "the state of Florida pays him within 24 hours" — implies the business taps a state reimbursement/voucher program (e.g., Florida's education savings account ecosystem via Step Up For Students) rather than relying solely on parent-paid tuition, which is what lets it scale past a normal tutoring rate.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, URLs, or platforms were named in this transcript beyond "a Facebook post."

Why it matters for you: If you're weighing a side hustle, this is the low-capital, high-margin template — pick a skill parents will pay per-kid for, post it plainly on Facebook, collect upfront, and look into whether a state voucher/reimbursement program (like Florida's ESA funds) can pay you directly instead of relying only on parents.
