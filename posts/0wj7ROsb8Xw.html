I saw this quote that said...

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-05 · 18,428 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Ignore critics who aren't building anything themselves.

A 60-second filter for whose feedback on your side hustle you should actually act on — everyone else is noise.

What it is: A short motivational riff built around one quote — "It's okay if you don't clap for me, because I've seen the teams you clap for."

How it works:

  • Premise: when you build something unconventional (buying businesses, starting a side hustle, posting publicly), a chunk of people won't get it. That's structural, not personal.
  • The reframe: instead of arguing with critics, look at what they celebrate. If their scoreboard is different from yours, you're not even playing the same game — so their score doesn't apply to you.
  • The actual filter: only weight opinions from people currently in the game, building in real time. Not people who used to build. Not people who talk about building. People with live skin in it.
  • The exit: for everyone else — "wish them love and let them go play in another stadium." No debate, no block-and-rant, just declining the engagement.
  • Codie's own note: it used to bother her, and the fix wasn't thicker skin — it was a better filter.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. This is a pure mindset clip with no frameworks, tools, or URLs.

Why it matters for you: Side hustles die in the gap between "launched" and "profitable," and the loudest voices in that gap are almost always people who've never shipped anything — this gives you a one-question test (are they in the game right now?) to decide whose criticism gets a rewrite and whose gets ignored.
