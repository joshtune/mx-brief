What it is: A paid sponsorship (#ad) for Alia by Dotdigital, an AI popup tool for e-commerce that personalizes and auto-optimizes email-capture popups per shopper.

How it works:

  • Prism AI: Runs multiple popup variants split by landing page, UTM, or traffic source. It tracks which variant wins opt-ins and revenue in each context, then shifts more traffic to the winners. Think continuous, self-running A/B testing.
  • Smart offers: Reads buying intent. A shopper who looks ready to buy gets a smaller discount than a first-time browser, which protects margin on sales that would have happened anyway.
  • Smart triggering: Decides when to re-show the popup. Alia claims this captures up to 40% more subscribers (vendor claim, unverified).
  • Downstream optimization: Optimizes on what happens after signup (purchase, AOV, LTV), not just email capture rate.
  • Timing pitch: Tune it before Black Friday/Cyber Monday traffic spikes.
