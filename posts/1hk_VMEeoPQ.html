It’s Never Been Easier To Be In The Top 1% (Do This NOW)

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-01 · 128,031 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
AI made average free; originality and depth now pay.

If you're building a side hustle, this tells you where to spend your scarce hours: go deep on one thing, hand off the paperwork, and say the unpopular thing out loud.

What it is: A thought-leadership pitch that AI and algorithms are making everything homogeneous, so standing out is now the cheapest edge. Codie argues you get there through depth, not dabbling.

How it works:

  • The secret: Average is now free because machines produce it, so original is scarce and valuable. You don't need to be a genius. You need to be willing to have an original idea, even if it's a worse one, and say what everyone's thinking but nobody says.
  • Evidence of sameness: Plastic surgery grew from ~7M to ~30M procedures since 2000. Kyle Chayka's book *Filter World* coined "airspace" for the identical white-wall, subway-tile Airbnbs. A UCLA/Exeter study found that AI-assisted stories scored higher individually but sounded alike collectively.
  • Scale: ~900M people use the same AI chatbot every week, which is where the sameness comes from.
  • Generalist trap: Dabbling in investing, literature, photography and YouTube gets you mediocre at everything. The cause is rarely too little work. You are too wide and not deep on what matters.
  • Two profiles win the next decade:
  • The extreme expert: one engineer doing a team's work with 10-100 agents underneath.
  • The extreme generalist: a first-principles founder who covers any role and gets things done.
  • Delegate the rest: If you're the CEO-type generalist, you need depth somewhere and people obsessed with everything else. She has someone else run her personal investments.
  • First cut: Paperwork such as filings and annual reports. It has never made her better at business.
  • Small deviations compound: One degree off course lands you miles away, so start with one degree.
