What it is: Matt covers the "Midjourney Scanner" — a sound-based body scanner from the AI image company, pitched as a faster/cheaper MRI alternative.

How it works:

  • You step into a ring lined with ~9,000 tiny transducers, each acting as both speaker and microphone
  • They fire ultrasonic waves into your body and listen for echoes, picking up vibrations finer than an atom's width
  • Echoes feed 21 servers running ~2 petaflops; the system reconstructs hundreds of slices into a 3D organ map accurate to half a millimeter
  • Claimed vs MRI: 60x faster (60s vs up to 2hr), 10x cheaper, no radiation, near-zero per-scan cost
  • Plan: "Midjourney spas" (first one next to the SF Apple store) with 10 scanners each, scaling to 50,000 units and a billion scans/month
