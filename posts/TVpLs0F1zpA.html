How a $5B founder is using AI (3 tutorials)

My First Million · 2026-08-26 · 28,986 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Zapier's Wade Foster demos his daily AI stack.

Wade Foster turned ~$1.3M of seed capital into a ~$5B bootstrapped-adjacent company, and here he shows the actual AI workflows he runs daily — the prompts and connections, not the philosophy.

Who's featured: Wade Foster, co-founder/CEO of Zapier (@wadefoster), on MFM Episode 856 with Sam Parr and Shaan Puri.

The business, in numbers:

  • Zapier started as dead-simple app-to-app plumbing: form submission over here → data lands in an unrelated app over there. "Universal plugs."
  • Funding: went through Y Combinator, raised ~$1M and change — that is the *only* primary capital ever taken. Wade calls it "seed-strapped," not pure bootstrapped.
  • Valuation cited in the episode: ~$5B.
  • Name trivia: they couldn't afford the second "P" in the domain, and liked that "API" was hiding inside the name. Wade's mnemonic: *"Zapier makes you happier."*

The strategic pivot (what they're selling now):

  • Positioning moved from "connect your apps" to "the automation layer for agentic AI."
  • Wade's thesis, three rungs: (1) AI as a chatbot is *generally* smart — it knows what the internet knows, nothing about your business. (2) Connect your tools — CRM, help desk, email, team chat — and feed that context in. (3) Now the answers are hyper-specific to your business, not generic.
  • The commercial wedge: you're already living inside Claude, Codex, Cursor, or ChatGPT. Zapier installs *into* that surface and hooks up any tool in their integration catalog. Zapier stops being a destination app and becomes infrastructure under whatever chat UI you already use.

How Wade uses AI personally:

  • Connects his own work tools into his AI assistant so it answers with company context instead of internet averages.
  • Uses AI as a deliberate ladder: ask it to explain something "like I'm five," keep going down the simplicity stack until it clicks, then ask it to teach you back up. He frames AI as an *advantage* for people who don't already know the domain.
  • The episode is billed as 3 tutorials — hands-on demos of the workflows that save him hours daily.
