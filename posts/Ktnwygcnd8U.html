No, Seriously. Claude Code is Starting To Get Dangerous

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-27 · 58,649 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Fable/Mythos shutdown and what it means for Claude users

This is not a build tutorial: it's a commentary on the government-forced Fable shutdown and where AI is heading. It has no tools or repos to copy, so watch it only if you want the context.

What it is: Thought-leadership piece on the 18-day US-government-forced shutdown of Anthropic's Fable and Mythos models, plus Nate's predictions and "three skills" to stay safe. The transcript cuts off partway through the first prediction, so the three skills aren't covered here.

How it works:

  • The order: No foreign nationals could access Fable or Mythos, including foreign-national Anthropic employees working in the US.
  • Why it became a full shutdown: Anthropic can't verify an API caller's nationality in real time. The order took effect immediately, so shutting both models off for everyone was the only way to comply.
  • The trigger (per the government): A jailbreak got Fable to scan software for weaknesses and produce an exploit demonstration.
  • Anthropic's pushback: That test found only a few minor, already-known vulnerabilities. Every model they tested, including older Claude and GPT models, could reproduce the demo.
  • Fable vs Mythos: Same underlying model with different safety layers. Fable is public. Mythos has fewer safeguards and is limited to a handful of trusted defenders.
  • Capability evidence: Mythos preview found thousands of unknown vulnerabilities and built related exploits, often without human steering. That included a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug and a 16-year-old FFmpeg bug that automated tests had hit 5 million times without catching.
  • The fix: During the 18-day shutdown Anthropic trained a new safety classifier that it says blocks the reported technique in more than 99% of cases. The government tested it and lifted the restrictions.
  • The deal: Designated government partners get expanded pre-release access to future models. Anthropic will help build a shared standard for judging jailbreaks.
  • Nate's thesis: It's a race with no referee, and the incentive is to win. Chinese labs kept releasing open models while Fable was offline. He notes there's no public evidence that competition caused the reversal.
  • Prediction #1: Governments will treat AI literacy as workforce infrastructure. He cites the White House AI education task force and NSF's AI Ready America program.
  • Also mentioned: The government had blacklisted Anthropic months earlier. Dario Amodei is quoted saying AI will be smarter than most or all humans in most or all ways.
