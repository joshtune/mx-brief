Shaan and Sam frame the AI personal-assistant race

No business teardown or revenue playbook in the part I could see: it's Shaan's framing of AI assistants as the next Uber-style wave, and the actual product tests come later in the episode.

What it is: Episode 867. Shaan Puri and Sam Parr set up a test of new AI personal assistants (Instinct, Muse, Grokbot). The transcript I got cuts off during the intro, so no product was actually tested in what I saw.

How it works (what the intro covers):

  • Thesis: AI personal assistants are 'everyday intelligence'. You text or talk to one on your phone and it does things for you.
  • Wave analogy: The 2012-era Silicon Valley wave (Uber, Lyft, Airbnb, food delivery) was 'push a button, something happens in the real world'. Shaan says AI is the next wave, and that it follows the same pattern of a fast, brutal race.
  • Race dynamics: Ride-share had Uber, Lyft and Sidecar. Sidecar was early and doing well, then got smashed once competition intensified. Shaan implies the same fate for weaker AI assistants.
  • 'Highest ELO game': He quotes Elon Musk that AI is the highest-ELO game in the world. The smartest players are all competing at the same thing, unlike Bezos, Zuckerberg and Musk, who were in different spaces. He compares it to the show Physical 100.
  • Democratization: Shaan has had a human assistant for years. She had inbox and Slack access, flagged emails (e.g. from his tax person), drafted replies and caught schedule conflicts. AI now gives everyone that, the way Uber gave everyone a private driver.
  • Monetization: Nothing in the visible transcript. No revenue, pricing or business-model details for any of the three products.

  • Founder backgrounds, funding or how these companies make money.
