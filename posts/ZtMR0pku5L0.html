I Let AI Start an Online Business for Me With Just $2

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-11-22 · 28,855 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
AI agents hunt price-gap niches for dropshipping

If you want a side hustle, this gives you a six-rule filter for picking a niche, plus a live demo of an AI agent picking one and building the store.

What it is: Chris gives an AI agent six rules for spotting an unorganized, overpriced product category. The agent picks one, and he builds a dropshipping store around it on camera.

How it works:

  • The origin story: He sent an AI agent across ~100 nursery sites to buy his dead replant list at the best price. It saved hundreds of dollars with almost no effort from him. His point is that this price-arbitrage gap is all dropshipping is, and that he was the consumer in that example.
  • The business model: List a product that already sits in someone else's warehouse on your store. When a customer buys, software buys it from the supplier and ships it to the customer. You never touch the inventory, and you keep the margin.
  • What's new: Old dropshipping needed supplier agreements and API integrations. Now an AI agent opens a browser on a supplier site that doesn't know you exist. It buys with the customer's money and enters the customer's address. That makes ugly 1998-era niche sites usable as suppliers.
  • Why the usual advice fails: iPhone cases, LED strips and posture correctors are saturated. His rule of thumb: once a category has a YouTube video about it, you're too late.
  • The six rules (all must be true). The transcript excerpt only covers the first four:
  • 1. Triggering event: The buyer's life just changed (new puppy, new tree, built a chicken coop). They need stuff now and won't comparison shop for days.
  • 2. Loss prevention: The product stops a loss bigger than its price. A $30 watering bag protects a $120 tree. Sell painkillers, not vitamins.
  • 3. Unorganized category: If buyers have to piece it together from forums, Facebook groups, Reddit and five random sites, that's your opening.
  • 4. Crazy price spread: The same item costs $30 on one site and $180 on another. Wide spreads mean nobody is competing.
  • 5 and 6: Not in the transcript excerpt. They're in his free Google Doc, linked in the description.
  • The workflow:
  • Feed the six rules to an AI agent and let it pick the category.
  • Build the store with BuildYourStore (walkthrough starts at 12:38).
  • Use AutoDS for product research and order automation.
  • Run the three ad angles from his doc.
  • The catch: He promises to name the one part that's still hard, which every other video skips. That part is past the excerpt, so watch for it.
