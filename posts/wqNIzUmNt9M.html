Anthropic's record IPO and the AI bubble test

Mostly market commentary with little for web app builders, so only skim it if you want the intro's quick mentions of new models and a Google one-sentence website builder.

What it is: A weekly AI news roundup. The available transcript covers only the opening segment, Anthropic's IPO and what it means for the "AI bubble" question.

How it works:

  • Anthropic priced at $83/share, sold ~980M shares, and raised $81.3B. That makes it the largest IPO ever, passing SpaceX from June.
  • It trades on the Nasdaq as ANT. It opened at $111.50 and closed day one at $97.17 (+17%), for a valuation of roughly $1.27T.
  • Lockups are staged rather than the usual six months. Employees could sell up to 10% on day one (~315M shares). Another batch unlocks mid-December, three more tranches run Jan 5 – Feb 2, and up to ~1.6B insider shares become sellable. Co-founders and top holders wait up to a year.
  • Matt's thesis: heavy insider selling at each unlock would signal doubt about AI. Insiders holding would signal confidence. His first watch date is mid-December.
  • Public filings now mean quarterly numbers. H1 revenue was $16.3B (vs ~$1.2B a year earlier) against $8.9B in compute spend.
  • OpenAI's CFO reportedly told staff they plan to go public in 2027 or sooner. Anthropic's stock behavior sets the stage for that.
