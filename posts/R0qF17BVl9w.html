Fable 5 Just Built Me a Business With One Prompt

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-07-08 · 54,303 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
One prompt makes Fable 5 build an entire company.

See exactly how Nate structured a single agentic prompt to spin up hundreds of agents that shipped a product, brand, landing page, and launch videos end-to-end.

What it is: A case study where Nate hands Claude Fable 5 one `/goal` prompt and it autonomously builds a full company — product, brand, website, market research, and launch videos.

How it works:

  • Nate uses the `/goal` command in Claude Fable, but the `/goal` prompt has a ~4,000 character limit — so he puts the full instruction set in a file instead.
  • The kickoff prompt: *"Read this file and execute everything below the divider line as your goal. That file is your full instruction set — mission, guardrails, phases, deliverables, definition of done. Follow it exactly, including the never-ask rule. Do not report back to me until the definition of done is met. Start now."*
  • The instruction file's actual goal: *"Build me a complete company from scratch. Start with nothing but the open internet. Find a real, painful, underserved problem people are complaining about right now, design a business around it, build the product/brand/website, and hand me a finished package I could take to market this month. Then prove why it would work. I am not going to answer questions."*
  • Fable spun up hundreds of agents, did research + verification, and shipped the result: Counter Brief — a tool that pulls real evidence (tracking, emails, order history, fraud analysis) so Shopify store owners win chargebacks instead of sending banks a generic template. $19 flat.
  • Deliverables produced: a working product dashboard, landing page, brand guidelines, business plan, market research, pricing, and two launch videos (a fast-paced "viral" SaaS demo + a slower voice-over version).
  • Notable: Fable clicked through the actual product, recorded/screenshotted the UI, and edited it to music with motion graphics and mouse movement for the demo video.
  • It also scanned Nate's project for available API keys and auto-used his HeyGen avatar + ElevenLabs voice clone to generate a "note from the founder" video without him filming anything.

Tools & links:

  • Claude Fable 5 — the agentic model doing all the work via `/goal`
  • HeyGen — AI avatar used for the founder video
  • ElevenLabs — voice clone for the founder narration
  • Glaido voice-to-text — free month, Nate's dictation tool

Why it matters for you: The real takeaway isn't the product — it's the prompt architecture: offload the full spec to a file, define mission/guardrails/phases/definition-of-done, add a hard "never ask" rule, and let a long-horizon agent run unattended until done.
