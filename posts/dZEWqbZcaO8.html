Rules To Live By

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-09 · 8,841 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A billionaire's five rules for thinking and networking bigger.

It's a fast reframe on why your side hustle stays small — usually your ambition and your circle, not your ability.

What it is: Codie relays five mindset rules from her near-billionaire friend Bill for thinking and building bigger.

How it works:

  • You're thinking too small — Years around small business can "infect" your ambition; the prompt to think bigger is what pushed Codie to build BizScout.
  • Find the billionaire to your millionaire — Cultivate friends who see you as larger than you see yourself and are genuinely invested in your success.
  • Buy stuff to give it away — The point of money is reciprocity; generosity compounds (law of reciprocity).
  • Find the people advancing the world — Surround yourself with winners if you want to move forward.
  • Keep a menagerie of friends — People are multifaceted; different friends serve different roles, and someone not winning in one area doesn't spoil the whole relationship.

Tools & links:

  • BizScout — Codie's business-buying/deal-sourcing tool, cited as the direct result of "thinking bigger"

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that scale is a network-and-ambition problem before it's a tactics problem — if you want your side hustle to become real money, upgrade who you learn from and how big you let yourself plan.

Note: Loose, anecdotal, and thin on mechanics — inspiration over instruction.
