The $7K/Month Side Hustle Most People Think AI Killed

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-18 · 15,305 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Freelance writing is booming again — if you use AI right.

If you can write and can prompt AI well, companies are paying $900-$1,200 per article for 'AI-assisted' content, and this breaks down exactly how to position yourself for it.

What it is: Interview with freelance writer/editor Steph Spectre on how she charges $900-$1,200 per blog post (roughly $900-$1,200 for ~5-6 hours of work, effectively $90+/hr) writing AI-assisted content for companies like Apple and Zapier.

The market signal:

  • Google Trends for "hire a writer" cratered when ChatGPT launched in 2023.
  • It's been climbing steadily for the last 2 years — demand for good writers is back, now paired with AI tools.
  • Companies don't want raw AI output; they want a skilled writer directing AI to sound human and on-brand.

How the idea is carried out:

  • Background matters: she was a contract editor at Apple (sales enablement copy), then a full-time writer at Zapier, and only went full-time freelance ~3 weeks before this interview.
  • She uses different AI tools for different jobs rather than one-size-fits-all: Gemini for processing video/audio, Claude for marketing content, ChatGPT for writing and research.
  • "Training" the AI on brand voice = feeding it the company style guide (capitalization, formatting, bolding rules, banned words) plus reusable templates for common content types, so AI fills in structure rather than starting blank.
  • Her differentiator: she can spot "robotic" or generic AI phrasing because she's been writing since 2016 — the editing eye is the actual skill being sold, not the typing.
  • Workflow example from Zapier: she'd screen-record herself (Loom-style) building a workflow while narrating her real-time reactions and confusion points — that raw, unfiltered narration became the "gold" material fed to AI to produce the polished tutorial. Her framing: "writing is thinking," so capturing your first reaction to a problem is the raw material AI can't fake.

Tools & links:

  • No specific paid tool or course was linked for this idea — it's a skillset/positioning play, not a product purchase.

Why it matters for you: If you already have decent writing/editing chops, this is a low-cost side hustle to start now — the entry cost is learning to direct multiple AI tools by task and packaging your own "style guide + template" system to sell to companies at $900-$1,200 per piece.
