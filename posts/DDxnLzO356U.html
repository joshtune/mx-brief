AMD Built the DGX Spark Rival I Predicted… But There's a Catch

Alex Ziskind · 2026-07-06 · 153,101 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
AMD's $4,000 Strix Halo AI box vs DGX Spark.

It's the machine question you care about: a tiny $4K AMD AI box benchmarked head-to-head against the Spark and Mac.

What it is: AMD's answer to NVIDIA's DGX Spark — a tiny dedicated AI mini-PC built on the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 ("Strix Halo") chip, 128GB unified memory, $4,000.

How it works:

  • Same Strix Halo chip already found in 2025 boxes (Beelink GTR9, GMKtec Evo X2, Framework Desktop, Minisforum MS1 Max, ASUS laptops) — AMD just put it in a smaller dedicated case.
  • $4,000 matches what NVIDIA charged for the Spark; the whole Strix Halo market quietly drifted up to ~$4K over the past year (Framework 128GB/2TB, Minisforum, GMKtec Evo X2 all near that now). So AMD isn't gouging — it's market rate.
  • Smallest machine Alex owns — smaller than the Framework, the other Halo boxes, and even the Spark.
  • Ships with AI software pre-installed: LM Studio + Ollama (both on llama.cpp), Lemonade, and ComfyUI.

Benchmarks (dev/CPU work):

  • Mandelbrot multi-core Python test pegs all cores: 164W on the Halo, 164W on the Spark, ~80–90W on the Mac.
  • Compile times: Mac 17.3, Spark 15.4, Halo 18.4 — all very close for regular dev work.
  • Verdict for coders: don't buy a $4K box just to compile; cheaper options exist. The real audience is AI tinkerers.

Cost vs Mac mini: At $4,000 with 128GB unified memory, it's far above a base Mac mini — this competes with a maxed Mac Studio tier, not the mini. For pure CPU/dev work an M4 Pro Mac keeps pace at lower power (~80–90W vs 164W).
