The Most Overlooked $1M Business Every Town Needs

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-23 · 5,099 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Laundromats quietly print $1M/year, remotely.

If you want a boring, recession-proof side hustle you can run remotely without much cash of your own, this breaks down how someone actually built a laundromat empire.

What it is: Chris Koerner visits Tyler, a laundromat owner in Cincinnati who did $1,025,000 in revenue last year, to reverse-engineer how a "boring" laundromat becomes a seven-figure business.

How it works:

  • Single high-volume machines can generate $30,000+/year on their own — the unit economics are absurdly good once you have scale and the right location
  • Laundromats are largely recession-resistant and AI-resistant — people have to wash clothes regardless of the economy or automation
  • Sales reportedly spiked during COVID, reinforcing the "essential service" nature of the business
  • Much of the operation can be run remotely — not a business that demands you stand behind a counter all day
  • Financing is accessible even without much personal cash — Tyler's approach (per the video) centers on creative financing structures rather than needing six figures up front
  • Location selection is framed as the make-or-break variable — the video promises to dig into how to find the "perfect" laundromat location
  • Koerner frames the opportunity size bluntly: "the bar is so low... you could go in and own that city" — implying under-saturated markets are common
