What it is: A short clip where Robert Greene says 50 Cent's charisma comes from calm, real confidence. Greene spent 6-7 months with him writing *The 50th Law*.

How it works:

  • Greene calls confidence "by far the most charismatic quality" a person can have.
  • 50 Cent's charisma came from "incredible" confidence and inner strength, plus a calm demeanor.
  • Greene doesn't think you can teach it to people from troubled backgrounds. He does say you can build an outward-directed attention, because attention is a "quotient of confidence."
  • Faking confidence doesn't hold up. People read through it as bravado, which has an "emptiness" to it.
