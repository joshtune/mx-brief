Fable 5.1 Just Dropped. It Looks Unreal.

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-01 · 81,458 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
First-look reaction to Anthropic's Fable 5.1 release.

It's a launch-day walkthrough of Fable 5.1's pricing, benchmarks, and availability — but it's reaction commentary, not a build, so there's no workflow or repo to copy here.

What it is: A same-day reaction video walking through Anthropic's Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 announcement blog, with Nate running both models side-by-side in the Claude desktop app while he reads.

The actual news (per the blog he reads on screen):

  • Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 shipped as Anthropic's top models for coding and knowledge work.
  • Token pricing is unchanged — still $10/M input, $50/M output. The savings come from efficiency, not rate cuts.
  • ~25% cheaper for typical token-billed workflows, driven by *reduced pricing on cache reads*. For highly agentic work Anthropic claims savings up to ~50%.
  • Effort tiers beat the old ceiling: Nate points at charts showing Fable 5.1 on *low* effort outscoring Fable 5 at its highest effort — and at a fraction of the cost (~$34–44 vs ~$1,110 on the benchmark he's reading).
  • Benchmarks shown: agentic terminal coding, multidisciplinary reasoning, agentic coding. He explicitly says take them with a grain of salt until you've run your own work through it.
  • Mythos 5.1 is functionally identical to Fable 5.1 but with *more permissive safeguards* for vetted orgs — gated behind two trusted-access programs: the Cyber Verification Program and the Life Sciences Verification Program. Apply if you qualify; otherwise you can't get it.
  • New EFS option lets enterprise customers opt into zero data retention.
  • Reduced safeguard false positives — landing right after Anthropic's own blog about training an older Claude model to hack, which Nate had covered hours earlier.

Availability (today):

  • Claude desktop app and terminal
  • Claude API directly
  • Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud (Vertex), Microsoft Azure

Meta detail worth noting: the release blog itself was built by Fable 5.1 (credited bottom-right), with theme/color switching — a departure from Anthropic's usual launch-post design. Nate reads that as a signal about the model's design chops.
