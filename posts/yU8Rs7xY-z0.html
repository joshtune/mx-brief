What is a good deal?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-25 · 7,196 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Filter deals with a pre-set 'deal box'

It gives you a five-minute pass/fail filter so your side hustle search stops eating months on businesses that were never right for you.

What it is: The "deal box" — a set of pre-committed filters you define *before* you look at a single business, so deal flow gets triaged instead of pondered.

How it works:

  • Codie's analogy: Zillow filters. Three beds, two baths, under $500k turns thousands of overwhelming listings into the right 10.
  • Define your filters up front, in this order:
  • Minimum cash flow — what you actually need to walk away with each month/year.
  • Maximum price — the ceiling you're willing to pay, decided when you're unemotional.
  • Operate or absentee — do you want to run it daily, or own it from a distance?
  • Industry — what you genuinely understand, not what looks sexy.
  • Anything outside the box is a no. Not a bad business — just not *your* business.
  • The core reframe: "the worst deals aren't inherently bad deals, they're bad for you."
  • Payoff: without a box you burn months evaluating everything that crosses your desk; with one you know in 5 minutes whether a deal deserves your time.

Tools & links:

  • No repos or tools named in this clip — the framework is the artifact. Write your four filters down before your next deal call.

Why it matters for you: If you're hunting a side hustle while holding a day job, your scarcest resource is evaluation time — the deal box converts that from an open-ended search into a boolean check.
