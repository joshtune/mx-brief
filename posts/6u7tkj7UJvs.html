The Laziest One-Person Business You Can Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-14 · 10,079 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
AI agent that finds trends and makes content autonomously.

It's a sponsored walkthrough of pointing an AI at trending topics to spin up a low-effort content or AI-agency side hustle while you sleep.

What it is: A sponsored demo of Higgsfield's "Supercomputer" — an agentic AI (branded "Hermes") that researches trends and produces content on its own, framed as the seed for a one-person business.

The idea (two side hustles pitched):

  • Faceless content brand: Let the AI scan TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Instagram for viral trends in your niche, then auto-generate videos/images. Copy proven formats to ride the same wave.
  • AI agency ("Firepower"): Chris's actual recommendation — help small/medium businesses adopt AI. Run paid AI audits to get in the door, then charge $1K–$5K/month for implementation.

How it works:

  • Connect your "cloud account" (Claude) to Higgsfield via a custom MCP connector — Settings → Connectors → "+" → Add custom connector → paste URL → Connect → Allow.
  • In Supercomputer, hit "+" → Use skill: Trend Picker → prompt e.g. "show me viral trends in the business ideas niche."
  • It scans TikTok/Shorts/IG (~3 min) and returns top videos, winning hooks/formats, hashtags, key creators, and ad-spend insights.
  • Underlying gen models: Seedance 2 (AI video) and GPT Image 2 (stills); Supercomputer is the decision/intelligence layer on top.
  • Upload brand assets (fonts, colors, logos) as context; it learns your taste over time and stops asking for input.

  • Claude MCP / connectors — how third-party tools plug into your Claude account with full context/history
