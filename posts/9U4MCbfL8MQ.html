From DoorDash to $81K/M With This Overlooked Business

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-11 · 54,331 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
DoorDash driver builds $81K/month water filtration business in 85 days.

This is a $500-startup, subcontract-everything home service model with a full playbook on finding cheap customers and the right installers — exactly the kind of side hustle you can run without touching a wrench.

What it is: Parker, 25, went from DoorDash to $81K/month revenue in 85 days by selling residential water filtration systems and subcontracting all installs to licensed plumbers.

The numbers:

  • Started with less than $500 in setup costs
  • $81K revenue in August (month 3), $54K already booked for September
  • Began as a one-man show; Parker visits job sites about twice a month
  • Prior business: a remote cleaning company launched in 2023 with a $3K loan from his girlfriend

Why water filtration is the gap:

  • Skilled trades are shrinking fast: far more plumbers retiring than entering
  • Pricing in niche services is set by whoever the five local providers happen to be, so a $10K quote often has no real cost basis behind it
  • Customers assume it's harder or more technical than it is, so nobody competes on it

How it works:

  • Parker is not licensed and never touches the install
  • He owns the customer: marketing, sales, scheduling, follow-up
  • Licensed subcontractors handle the physical install only
  • Installer math: roughly 16 jobs each at ~$500/job = an extra ~$8K/month per sub, which keeps them loyal
  • Business owner's job collapses to lead gen + sub management

The playbook covered in the full episode:

  • How to find customers for cheap (the end of the episode gets specific)
  • Which platforms to source reliable subcontractors from
  • Chris frames it as: no experience, no degree, no real capital needed
