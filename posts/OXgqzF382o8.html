What it is: Sam & Shaan bring on three NYC founders with strange businesses doing $10M / $20M / $30M in revenue and dig into how each actually makes money.

The format (the "MFM stick"):

  • Each founder opens with a number, then what they do — no preamble, no false humility.
  • Hosts reverse-engineer the business model on the spot.
  • Each founder is asked two closing questions: (1) what *adjacent* opportunities they see from their niche vantage point, and (2) what they'd most want host advice on.

Founder 1 — Alex Daniels, real estate magazine empire ($10M):

  • Owns 40 magazines / publications, ~$10M in revenue.
  • Model: real estate agents pay to promote their listings — each magazine is a printed "lookbook" of currently-on-market listings.
  • Classic boring-but-lucrative print-advertising play, multiplied across 40 local titles.
