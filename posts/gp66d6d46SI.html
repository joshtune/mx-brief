Why Git still matters when agents do the work

It's a 60-second short with no home lab content, but it shows how Chuck works with AI agents: he delegates the Git grunt work and keeps enough knowledge to audit it.

What it is: A YouTube Short arguing you should still learn Git even though your AI agent handles commits, branches, and PRs for you.

How it works:

  • Know the lingo: Even if the agent does the work, you still need to talk to managers, coworkers, and other vibe coders without sounding lost.
  • Impress your agent: Joke, but it makes the point that you should understand what's being done for you.
  • Spy on your agent: Read the diffs and the PRs. Check what it actually did, and catch the crappy PR before it merges.
  • Be the meat suit backup: AI goes down, so you need to be able to do the work by hand when it does.
  • It's fun to know things: Learning a real skill makes it more fun to watch the agent work. You'll know what a merge is when you see one.
