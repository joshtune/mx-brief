Why Delegation Fails

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-01 · 22,516 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Delegate the standard, not the steps

If your side hustle dies the moment you stop doing everything yourself, this is the 10-minute fix: record what good looks like once, then hand off the outcome instead of the checklist.

What it is: Codie's "standards install": record what good looks like on camera once, then delegate the standard instead of the steps.

How it works:

  • The failure: She delegated tasks ("cut this frame, move this text, redo the caption") without showing what good looks like, then micromanaged every clip herself.
  • The trigger: Her lead editor stopped her mid-rant: "Can you actually just show us what good looks like?" Creative work is subjective, so verbal instructions don't transfer.
  • Step 1 – Record it: Open Loom, hit record, and walk through ONE edit start to finish.
  • Step 2 – Add the why: Cover *why* it works, *where* she'd cut, and *what* the bad version looks like next to the good one.
  • Time cost: About 10 minutes, and she never had to explain it again.
  • Step 3 – Delegate the destination: Hand over the outcome and don't dictate the path.
  • Result: Her team went from her micromanaging every clip to posting hundreds of pieces a week without her touching them. They produce 250 pieces of content a month.

Tools & links:

  • Loom — screen recording tool she uses to capture the walkthrough

Why it matters for you: A side hustle only scales if it runs without your hands on it, and a 10-minute recording with good vs. bad examples is the cheapest way to hire help, whether that's a freelancer or a part-timer, without becoming their full-time manager.
