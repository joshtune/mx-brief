7 phrases that quietly end every money conversation in your marriage.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-27 · 23,765 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Reframing 7 money-killing phrases to keep partners aligned.

If your side hustle eats shared cash, these scripts keep your partner a co-investor instead of an obstacle.

What it is: A swap-list of 7 partnership-ending money phrases, each replaced with a question that keeps both people on the same team.

How it works:

  • "We can't afford it" → "What would have to be true for us to afford it?" (turns a wall into a plan)
  • "It's just money" → "I can tell this is heavier than the number. Tell me more." (validates the fear, not the figure)
  • "I just need to know we're safe" → "What number on paper would let you sleep tonight?" (makes safety concrete, not a veto)
  • "This is your project, not mine" → "What would I need to show you for this to feel like ours?" (kills resentment compounding)
  • "Trust me on this one" → "Here's the math, here's the downside, walk me through your worry." (trust is a deposit, not a demand)
  • "We've talked about this already" → "What's still sitting with you?" (reopens instead of shutting down)
  • "I'll handle it" → "What's the version of this we both feel good about?" (keeps both on the team)
