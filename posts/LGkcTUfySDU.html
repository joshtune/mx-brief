The Most Profitable Side Hustle No One Is Doing Yet

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-11 · 42,278 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Inflatable backyard movie theater rental as a side hustle.

A $300 Amazon impulse buy turned into $10K booking weeks at 80% margins — a low-capital, low-competition side hustle you could literally start this weekend.

What it is: Derek (from Backyard Movie Theater) accidentally built a party/event rental business renting out inflatable movie screens — now doing $10K weeks targeting $100K/year at 80% profit margins.

How it works:

  • Started as a $250–300 Amazon inflatable screen bought as a *Christmas gift*, not a business.
  • Hosted a movie night for his son's friends → neighborhood parents started asking "do you do this?"
  • Listed it on Facebook Marketplace "just to see" — immediately got bites. First paid gig was an HOA event.
  • Learned the hard way: cheap consumer inflatables fail in wind (the HOA gig got blown around like a tube-man). Comped the client, moved indoors.
  • Reinvested into a commercial 20ft industrial screen (~$3,500) once demand proved real.
  • The real unlock: In Texas summer it doesn't get dark until ~9:30pm — too late for little kids. So he built an *indoor, air-conditioned inflatable movie theater* — a niche he claims literally no one else is doing.
  • Customers: HOAs, birthday parties, neighborhood events (food-truck-style event circuit).

Playbook takeaways:

  • Low startup cost (~$300 to test, $3,500 to go commercial).
  • High margin (80%) — it's rental, the asset gets reused.
  • "If you're prepared, you've waited too long" — launch before you feel ready, let demand pull you in.
  • Differentiate on a local constraint (climate/daylight) competitors ignore.
