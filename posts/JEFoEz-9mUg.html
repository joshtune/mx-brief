THIS Is The Most Valuable Asset

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-11 · 22,262 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Distribution beats product as your real moat.

If you're starting a side hustle, owning your own audience is a cheaper, more durable edge than out-spending competitors on ads.

What it is: Codie's argument that distribution — not product — is the true competitive advantage, and that building your own audience makes you a self-owned ad platform.

The instructional secret:

  • "It's not product that's king. It's distribution." The thing nobody sophisticated wants to admit is that getting in front of customers is harder than building the thing.
  • ~40 cents of every venture dollar flows to the "big four" ad channels (Google, Amazon, etc.) — i.e. VCs are funding distribution, not product.
  • If you own both the product AND the distribution (an audience), you skip the ad tax and gain an "unfair advantage."
  • Reframe of "personal brand": stop calling it that — instead become "your own walking ad platform."

How to apply it to a side hustle:

  • Build an audience BEFORE or alongside the product, not after — it's the cheapest customer acquisition you'll ever have.
  • Treat content/attention as a business asset that compounds, not a vanity metric.
  • Every dollar you don't spend on ads because people already follow you is margin.
