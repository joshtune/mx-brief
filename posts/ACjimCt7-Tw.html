Seven scripted phrase swaps to sound more authoritative and close more deals.

It's a copy-paste script for sales and vendor conversations that any side-hustle owner can use today to stop sounding needy and start sounding in control.

What it is: A rapid-fire list of 7 phrases to cut from your business vocabulary, each replaced with a psychologically sharper alternative.

How it works:

  • "That's our policy" → "Here's what we can offer" (rigid rules kill goodwill; frame it as flexibility)
  • "What's your budget?" → "What outcome would you want?" (budget questions anchor low and trigger defensiveness; outcome questions sell value first)
  • "I haven't heard back" → "Is this still worth solving?" (avoids guilt-tripping; re-frames as their problem, not your follow-up)
  • "I think maybe" → "I recommend" (soft language reads as low authority/low certainty)
  • "I'll take a second" → "This takes 7 minutes, I'm keeping us tight" (vague time estimates erode trust; specific numbers build it)
  • "It sells itself" → "Here's the proof of why this works" (lazy claims read as salesy; evidence reads as credible)
  • "Sign today" (pressure close) → "Price holds through Friday" (urgency framed as fact, not manipulation)

Tools & links: None mentioned — this is a pure scripting/framing tactic, no software or resources referenced.

Why it matters for you: If you're running a side hustle and doing your own sales/client calls, these are drop-in phrase swaps that make you sound like an operator with leverage instead of someone begging for the deal.
