Replacing his Facebook ads guy with an AI agent

If you want a side hustle, the idea here is selling AI-run ad management as a service, and Chris shows the agent-plus-prompt setup that beat his human ad buyer in about 2 days.

What it is: Chris fired his Facebook ads person and handed newsletter ad *operations* (not just ad copy) to Meta's AI agent, Muse. Reported result: $1.62 per newsletter subscriber, beating his human buyer in about 2 days.

(Note: the transcript reads "a $162" and the figure is garbled. It is almost certainly $1.62 per subscriber. Check the video before quoting it.)

The problem he was solving:

  • Newsletter (The Koerner Office, on Beehiiv, free, weekly) has about 270,000 subscribers
  • Every send costs subscribers: unsubscribe rate about 0.14%, roughly 256 people on the last send
  • Daily attrition is 50-100 even with no send, so about 1,000 subscribers a week leave
  • Breaking even needs about 150 new subscribers a day, and organic from YouTube, Twitter and Instagram only brought 50-100
  • Net loss: -450 subscribers in August, -1,700 in September
  • Ad tracking broke: Facebook and Beehiiv reported different subscriber counts, and nobody could say which ad brought which person
  • He paused ads and the list shrank faster
  • On his other business, Playmakers, an agency charged $200-$600 per booked sales call, whether or not the lead showed up or was qualified, with little to no tracking for weeks
  • Common root cause: he couldn't see what the spend was buying

How the idea is carried out:

  • He set it up on a Thursday night from the couch in about 20 minutes
  • He used Muse, Meta's AI agent. It's built by Meta, so it plugs straight into Ads Manager, can see campaigns, and can change budgets
  • He had already connected it to Beehiiv
  • He gave it a single prompt to *run* the ads, not write them. He didn't make new ads and didn't touch budgets
  • Promised in the video: the exact prompt, a day-by-day log of the first week, a mistake the agent caught that was costing him money, and five ways to copy this for under $100
  • Promised at the end: his shortcuts and a claimed $71,000 in sales from these cheap leads. The transcript cuts off before any of this, so none of it is verified here

The side hustle angle (my read, not stated in the excerpt):

  • Small businesses and creators hate Ads Manager and pay agencies or freelancers for it
  • Pitch: an AI agent runs the account with tracking, reporting and budget changes, and you charge a monthly retainer
  • The title, "Get Paid Monthly. Let AI Do the Work," points the same way
  • Your edge is fixing tracking first, since broken attribution is what killed both of his setups

Tools & links:

  • Muse (Meta AI agent) — runs Facebook/Instagram ads via Ads Manager access. I'm assuming this URL; the transcript gives none
  • Beehiiv — newsletter platform whose subscriber data the agent reads
  • Bizee: 50 business ideas under $500 — sponsor link from the description
