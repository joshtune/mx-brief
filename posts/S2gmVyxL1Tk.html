The Four Bucket Theory

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-08 · 15,197 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Success drains a bucket; audit your calendar before it does.

If you're building a side hustle on top of a job, one of your four buckets is already leaking and this gives you a 15-minute calendar audit to pick which one deliberately instead of by accident.

What it is: Codie's short riff on the "four leaky buckets" model (family, work, health, friends) plus her four-step calendar audit to make sure your trade-offs are chosen, not accidental.

The theory:

  • Your life is four buckets: family, work, health, friends. Every one leaks; you pour time in to keep it full.
  • To be successful, you let one drain. To be extremely successful, you may let two.
  • You can argue with the theory, but your calendar can't. Every builder has one bucket getting most of the hours, one getting scraps, and one they keep promising to fill "after this next stretch."

The instructional secret (the four-step audit):

  • Review the year. Open the calendar and look at where the hours actually went, not where you think they went.
  • Define non-negotiables. Pick the things that get a protected slot before work expands to fill every gap.
  • Block the calendar. Priorities that only live in your head lose to work every time. Put them on the grid.
  • Reflect monthly. Ask: "When was the last time the people you love got the version of you that you want them to remember?"

Why it matters for you: A side hustle is a fifth demand on four already-leaky buckets. Do the audit before you start so the hustle drains the bucket you chose (probably friends or downtime) and not the one you'd regret (health or family), and so the business never ends up owning you.
