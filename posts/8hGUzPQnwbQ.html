Stop buying the beauty industry — own a slice of it.

It reframes a $580B+ consumer market as a buy-the-business side hustle, showing where the actual cash flow lands instead of where you spend.

What it is: A contrarian pitch to flip from consumer to owner in the beauty/wellness/self-help economy.

The instructional secret:

  • The beauty industry runs a *coherent aspirational identity system* — it swaps the "ideal woman" every 12–18 months (curvy → BBL → skinny → soft → strong → Ozempic) so you're always one purchase from "enough" and never cross the finish line.
  • "A satisfied customer is a lost customer" — the business model literally depends on dissatisfaction, which is exactly what makes it a durable cash machine to *own*.
  • The play: don't fight the system from outside ("you can't change a system you have no power in") — get power by owning a piece of it first.

Where the money actually is (the markets):

  • $580B beauty
  • $1.8T wellness
  • $50B self-help
  • Consumers fund all of it, own none of it.

The acquisition targets she names (boring, profitable, no/low inventory):

  • Laser clinic doing ~$400K/yr with no inventory
  • Salon with loyal clients on a 6-week rebooking cycle (recurring revenue)
  • Supplement/skincare brand with ~80% margins
  • Fitness studio on memberships (predictable MRR)

The filter for a buyable side hustle:

  • Recurring, clockwork customers (memberships, rebookings)
  • High margins, low/no inventory
  • "Not sexy" but genuinely helps people — less competition, stable cash flow
