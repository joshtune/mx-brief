How To Break Through Your Income Ceiling

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-08 · 12,731 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Three bottlenecks that cap every owner's income.

If your side hustle stalls the moment you stop touching it, this names the exact reason in 90 seconds.

What it is: A short, book-promo clip arguing every stalled business (from $50K to $5M) is stuck on one of three owner-created bottlenecks.

The instructional secret: The ceiling isn't the market, the product, or the price. It's you being load-bearing in one of three ways. Name which one, and the fix becomes obvious.

How it works:

  • Decision bottleneck — the team can do the work, but hiring, refunds, and client calls all wait on you. You're the traffic light and you're usually red. Fix: pre-decide with written rules (refund policy, hiring bar, pricing floor) so the team acts without you.
  • Delivery bottleneck — customers are buying *you* (your input, your relationship), so every new sale adds work to your plate. Growth makes life worse. Fix: productize the thing they're actually paying for so someone else can deliver it.
  • Direction bottleneck — everyone's busy, but ask three people the top priority this quarter and you get three answers. Fix: one written quarterly priority, repeated until people are sick of it.
