The actual play, step by step:

  • Read the market's blind spot. Wall Street was fixated on the Xbox and PlayStation launches. Nobody modeled a new *platform* with a motion controller aimed at non-gamers.

The transferable pattern:

  • Foreign winners are usually reachable via ADRs — check for one before assuming a company is untradeable.
