What it is: A short vox-pop where Nate asks attendees at Oracle AI World to name their favorite AI model.

How it works:

  • Rapid-fire answers from attendees, no demo, no walkthrough, no build
  • Opus dominates: Opus 5.5 called "a beast", Opus 4.6 repeated by several people, "probably Opus right now" described as the common theme
  • Gemini gets multiple mentions, and one person says it's "getting up there"
  • Grok gets a firm "for sure" from one respondent
  • 5.5 is named by a few people without a vendor, so it's ambiguous
  • Others name Luna ("definitely the cheapest"), Astro, and "6.1 Soul" (a contrarian pick over Astro)
  • Some people answer "I like all of them" or "don't have one"
  • One respondent runs a Fable orchestrator with Opus subagents, and Nate says he's made videos on that setup
