How I Would Start A Small Ecommerce Store

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-10 · 13,408 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Price handmade goods high using story and proof

The secret is that story plus good photos lets you charge a premium, so a side hustle can win on perceived value instead of undercutting on price.

What it is: Codie answers an 11-year-old who crochets stuffies and asks how to price and sell them. The answer is a short playbook for any handmade side hustle.

How it works:

  • Research first: Browse Etsy to see what comparable handmade items actually sell for. Don't guess at a price.
  • Pictures are proof, and proof increases price: Get good photos that look unmistakably handmade. They carry the price argument for you.
  • Price as high as possible: Start at the top of the range and come down only if the market pushes back. Don't start low.
  • Tell the story everywhere: Put the maker's story on the listing, and give it its own segment on the Etsy shop. An 11-year-old making plushies is a hook in itself.
  • Pick a buyer who overspends: Baby gifts are the target because people overspend on them, especially handmade ones.
  • Position it for that buyer: Use the angle 'handmade by kids for kids'. Codie says it can sell for 'quite a pretty penny'.

Tools & links:

  • Etsy — marketplace for price research and for selling the handmade goods

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that your price comes from story, proof, and a gift-giving buyer, not from your cost of materials. That lets a side hustle earn real margin with almost no capital.
