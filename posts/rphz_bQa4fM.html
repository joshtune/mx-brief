5 Paths To Wealth

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-06 · 21,942 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
The five ways people actually get rich.

It names the one wealth path that's actually reachable for a side-hustler — owning (or buying) a business — and backs it with data from people who've already made it.

What it is: Codie relays a framework she heard at a Merrill Lynch ultra-high-net-worth conference: there are only five ways to get seriously wealthy.

How it works:

  • The five paths, per Merrill's $50M+ client base:

1. Inherit it — born into money

2. Marry into it — married into money

3. Fortune 500 exec — climb to the top of a giant corporation

4. Own an asset management company — run other people's money

5. Own a business — build or buy your own

  • The kicker: 95% of Merrill's $50M+ clients did it by owning a business. Only 5% got there any other way.
  • Paths 1 and 2 aren't strategies — they're luck. Path 3 is a lottery-odds corporate grind. Path 4 needs capital and pedigree.
  • That leaves ownership as the only path you can actually choose — and Codie's pitch is that you don't have to build from zero, you can buy an existing business.

Tools & links:

  • No specific tools, repos, or URLs mentioned in this clip — it's a short framing video.
  • Contrarian Thinking — Codie's platform for learning to buy and grow small businesses

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that ownership isn't the rich person's path — it's the *only* repeatable one, and buying beats building; if you want a side hustle that compounds into real wealth, acquire cash-flowing assets instead of trading time for salary.
