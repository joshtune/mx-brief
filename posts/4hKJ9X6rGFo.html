Two-hour course on building agent teams in Grokbot

It's a full build-along for standing up a delegating multi-agent team in Grokbot — the architecture pattern (one narrow agent per job, a pinned 'C-suite' that routes work) transfers to whatever agent framework you're already wiring up.

What it is: A 2-hour course where Nate spins up a fresh Grokbot account live and builds a full team of AI agents, then pitches the same setup as a productized service you can sell into other businesses.

How it works:

  • Grokbot = AI teammates, not a chatbot. Agents can sign in to services, use your connected tools, and take action autonomously — including overnight while you're asleep.
  • Telegram-style interface. Left rail is DMs with individual agents; you also get group channels (Slack/ClickUp-style) where multiple agents talk to each other in one thread.
  • Agents delegate to each other. In a channel they @-tag each other, read each other's replies, and converge on a decision — Nate demos a `leadership` channel where four agents debate Q4 strategy and land on five agreed decisions (no new business units, spend only to remove the founder from the critical path, ownership handoffs to reduce key-man risk).
  • One agent = one narrow job. His 10 agents each do exactly one thing: watch X, make animations, own content strategy, check Fireflies meeting notes, etc. Deliberately not general-purpose.
  • The routing layer is the actual trick. Because narrow agents multiply fast and get overwhelming, he pins a four-agent "C-suite" on top — an EA ("chief"), COO, CCO (content), CFO — whose job is to receive the ask and delegate down to the specialists. That's the structural insight the rest of the course builds on.
  • The business angle: setting other companies up with their own Grokbot teams is framed as a clean agency offer — low technical lift, visible output, recurring.
  • *Note: the available transcript covers the framing and architecture walkthrough; the bulk of the runtime is the live account setup and agent-by-agent build.*
