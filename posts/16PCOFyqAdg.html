How To Change Your Entire Life In 24 Hours

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-18 · 112,746 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Kill borrowed goals before picking a business.

Before you sink nights and weekends into a side hustle, this gives you a 10-minute filter to check whether you actually want it or just saw it on someone's feed.

What it is: A goal-pruning exercise (the "Goal Autopsy") that strips out goals you inherited from other people, so you can commit to a business you actually want.

How it works:

  • The core idea: Most desires are mimetic, meaning you want things because others want them. Codie calls the belief that desires come from inside you the "romantic lie."
  • The evidence: A music study showed people's brains released dopamine when experts agreed with their pick. Disagreement produced nothing. Humans are wired to reward conformity.
  • Step 1, Goal Autopsy:
  • Write every goal you're chasing on a sticky note or index card. Include money, business, career, and the Maserati.
  • Ask of each one: *if I achieved this and nobody ever knew, would I still want it?*
  • Hell yes means keep it. Anything that needs someone else's approval to feel good gets cut.
  • Step 2, Cut for real: Let the rejected goals disappear completely. An MIT "disappearing doors" experiment showed that people who couldn't let options shrink switched rooms twice as often and lost 14%+ of their earnings. Codie calls this the "curse of optionality."
  • Step 3, Pick the business on data: Don't choose based on what you saw on someone's feed. The transcript cuts off before the method is detailed, and it moves straight into a sponsor pitch for business formation.
  • Caveat: The transcript is truncated. It promises three steps and only covers the first one and part of the second, so the "24 hours" framing is mostly clickbait.
