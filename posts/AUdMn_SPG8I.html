Solo founder sells League of Legends companion app for $54M.

A one-person software business hit a $54M exit—proof that a focused companion app around a game you know can print serious money.

Who & what: A solo developer built Wargraphs, a companion app for League of Legends, and sold it for $54 million.

What the product did:

  • Tracked your in-game stats
  • Helped players improve/get better at the game
  • Positioned as a companion/overlay tool alongside LoL

How he made money:

  • Built the app solo (no co-founders, no team mentioned)
  • Grew to ~1 million players using it
  • Monetized into "serious revenue" off that user base (likely ads/premium tiers typical of stat-tracker apps — not specified in transcript)
  • Ran it for ~7–8 years before exiting

The exit terms:

  • $54M total, structured as:
  • ~$27M cash up front (half)
  • ~$27M earn-out tied to hitting milestones
  • Founder was ~30 by the time of sale
