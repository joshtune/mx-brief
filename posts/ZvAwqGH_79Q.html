Don't Become A Content Creator, Build A Content Business.

A repeatable framework for monetizing social media in 2026.

If your side hustle needs an audience, this shows you where to concentrate effort instead of burning out spraying content everywhere.

What it is: Codie's "Six C's" framework for building a content *business* (not just a following), with a platform-by-platform monetization breakdown.

How it works:

  • Core rule — win one platform first. Don't spray-and-pray 25 posts/week across six platforms. Be "undeniable in one place," then expand. Pick where you'll "win your Emmy."
  • Match content to the platform's personality. Each platform is a different audience you must teach differently — different hooks, different viral mechanics, different business value.
  • X (Twitter) — "angry middle-aged guy": cerebral, contrarian one-liners, predictions that pick a fight. Loves *horizontal* podcast clips (unlike other platforms). Content dies in ~a day. Lowest pay: ~$200–$1,000/post at 100K followers. Drove ~1% of event ticket sales — basically none.
  • TikTok — "14-year-old cousin": 2-second attention span, reaches *cold* audiences (only ~38% of reach hits followers vs Instagram's ~68%). Great for awareness, terrible for sales. Stripped-down, zero-production "yapping" videos win. Strong hook + problem + framework/solution = wins (e.g. 290K views); weak hook, no solution = flops (9K views). Pays $2K–$7K/sponsored post at 100K followers; TikTok Shop works, but off-platform selling is hard (2M followers → <12 event tickets sold).
  • Monetization insight: rank platforms by how much money they actually make you, not by vanity reach.
