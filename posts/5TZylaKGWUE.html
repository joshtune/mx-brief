He Made $6K in 3 Hours Selling Lemonade

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-18 · 15,417 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Fresh-squeezed lemonade at farmers markets, scaled to $250K/year.

A near-zero-startup-cost weekend side hustle (lemons, sugar, water, a tent) that one family grew into a quarter-million-dollar business with cart rentals stacked on top.

What it is: Sell fresh-squeezed lemonade at farmers markets, then rent out a branded lemonade cart for events on top of it.

How it works:

  • The product is dead simple: water, cane sugar, lemons. That's the entire recipe.
  • Execution detail that matters — they wedge the lemon and smash it all the way down to extract the oils, drop it in a 32oz cup, then use simple syrup (1:1 sugar-to-water) instead of granulated sugar so there's zero graininess and no dissolving. Shake with filtered water.
  • Upsell flavors (strawberry, mango) for anyone who wants more than classic.
  • Started 3 years ago at Frisco Fresh Market as a 'side thing'; grew enough that the couple quit their other business. Now also at Dallas Farmers Market.
  • Revenue ladder: ~$25–30K first year → ~$250K last year, all farmers-market lemonade. One pop-up reportedly cleared ~$6K in 3 hours.
  • Cart rental is the second engine: corporate/family events run $1,000–$3,000 for 2–3 hours; wedding cocktail-hour rentals at $500 (just drop-off). They target classic-car/lowrider shows because a cart gets you in easier than a plain tent.
  • Margins are absurd (cane sugar + lemons + water against $7–8/cup pricing).
  • Startup cost: near zero — 'a muddler, a knife, a cutting board,' plus a table and a tent.

Tools & links:

  • beehiiv — sponsor, newsletter platform (30% off first 30 days); not core to the business idea.
  • No repos or software — this is a physical, cash-flow side hustle.

Operator advice straight from the episode:

  • 'Just do it. Stop overthinking it.' Don't agonize over whether to form an LLC first — get a table, get a tent, post up.
  • Bring your kids in; it doubles as a family weekend gig before it becomes the main thing.

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle with genuinely low capital risk, a 4-ingredient product, and a clear path from weekend stand → full-time → cart-rental B2B, this is a concrete, copy-able playbook with real numbers attached.
