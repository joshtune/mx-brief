Stop Rambling: 7 Tricks to Make People Respect You

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-22 · 2,762 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Seven communication tactics that win deals and respect.

If your side hustle lives or dies on cold pitches and closing conversations, these are the specific communication moves that get replies and respect.

What it is: A tactics rundown on business communication — how founders, salespeople, and operators get people to actually listen, framed through Chris's own wins and flops over ~80 businesses and ~300 podcast episodes.

How it works:

  • Origin lesson — communication beats gimmicks: His first business (iPhone repair in Tuscaloosa, AL, opened April 2011) was dying until he ordered 2¢ custom bar wristbands reading "Phone Restore." Drunk college kids broke phones, woke up hungover with the band on, and walked in. Reframe: it wasn't marketing, it was *being the message the brain grabs first*. Grew to 4 stores, sold for ~$1M.
  • Tactic 1 — The hook / first sentence is everything: Worth more than everything after it combined. Generic openers ("Hi, my name is… would love to hop on a quick call") get deleted in 3 seconds — even the CMO of Best Buy deletes them.
  • Specificity is the differentiator: Not fake-specific ("I see you went to Alabama") — specific to a *tweet you posted yesterday* or *an episode aired last week*. Proves you actually paid attention. AI-generated is fine *if it's genuinely relevant*.
  • Proof it works: A cold email opened with "You said on a recent episode that your favorite business is one a high schooler could run. Well, I built one. Here's what it looks like." → Chris replied in 30 min and booked them on the show.
  • Reverse-engineering as leverage: When cold-calling Best Buy's CMO, he "Googled" (his words) that Geek Squad was tiny when acquired — Best Buy bought a system and a process, which was his angle.
