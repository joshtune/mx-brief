I Ran A 27B Model On A Hand-Sized PC… Didn't Expect This

Alex Ziskind · 2026-09-13 · 93,209 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Khadas Mind Pro runs Qwen 27B; speculative decoding doubles it.

The machine is a Khadas Mind Pro (Intel Panther Lake, 64GB, ~100 GB/s real bandwidth) that lands roughly in base Mac mini territory on decode speed, and the real takeaway is that a free draft model beats buying hardware.

What it is: Alex benchmarks a palm-sized Intel Panther Lake mini PC on a 27B coding model, then shows speculative decoding more than doubles token output for free.

The machine:

  • Khadas Mind Pro — smallest Core Ultra X7 358H (Panther Lake) box he's seen
  • 64 GB shared system memory, Intel Arc B390 integrated GPU
  • Proprietary magnetic dock connector; the big dock houses a discrete GPU (an RTX 5060 Ti in his unit), a smaller dock just adds ports
  • Price: not stated in the transcript — check Khadas for current Mind Pro + dock pricing

Mac mini comparison (bandwidth is the whole game):

  • Mind Pro measured real bandwidth: ~100 GB/s (model size 17.66 GB × 5.42 tok/s)
  • Base M4 Mac mini: ~120 GB/s rated — roughly the same class, similar 5–6 tok/s on a 27B Q4 model
  • M4 Pro Mac mini: ~273 GB/s — expect ~2.5× the decode speed of the Mind Pro out of the box
  • The Mind Pro only pulls ahead when you bolt on the discrete-GPU dock, which adds cost and size

How it works:

  • Model: Qwen3 27B at Q4_K_M (~18 GB weights); 4-bit with sensitive layers kept at higher precision
  • Four backends tested on the iGPU: CPU, Vulkan, SYCL, OpenVINO
  • Prefill (reading): CPU 17 tok/s → OpenVINO 446 tok/s, a 27× jump
  • Decode (writing): CPU <5, Vulkan 5.4, SYCL 5.7, OpenVINO 2.6 tok/s — backend choice barely matters
  • Bottleneck is memory bandwidth: every token streams all 18 GB of weights past the chip
  • Speculative decoding: a ~1.5 GB draft model guesses several tokens ahead, the big model verifies them in one pass; iGPU went from ~5 to ~11 tok/s
  • Sweet spot is draft depth (n_max) of 3 on both the iGPU and the RTX 5060 Ti; n_max 8 gets ugly from wasted guesses

Tools & links:

  • Khadas Mind Pro — the mini PC and dock ecosystem
  • Qwen on Hugging Face — quants often ship the draft model alongside
  • ChatLLM / Abacus AI — sponsor

Why it matters for you: A base Mac mini already matches this box on local-LLM decode, so before spending on hardware, turn on speculative decoding — it's the cheapest 2× you'll ever get.
