I Gave GPT 6 Astra $10,000 to Trade Stocks...And This Happened

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-28 · 56,661 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Codex agents trade $10K of real money for a week

You get a working blueprint for chaining scheduled Codex routines into one stateful agent via handoff messages, plus an honest look at the first day's dud results.

What it is: Nate gives GPT-6 Astra (in Codex, high reasoning) $10,000 of real money for a 7-trading-day stock challenge against the S&P 500. The transcript covers only setup and day one.

How it works:

  • Planning: He asked Codex to research and plan the strategy first. It delegated to multiple trading sub-agents, which produced the strategy.
  • Six wake-ups (routines) per day:
  • Pre-market: read news, check the account, pick a watchlist
  • ~1 hour after open: look for the first qualifying trade
  • Midday: review positions and consider the last new trade
  • Afternoon: manage existing positions
  • 2:15: close remaining positions
  • Just before close: confirm flat and record the day's results
  • Two extra notification actions are set up alongside the wake-ups. He says he'll explain them later, but the transcript cuts off first.
  • Schedule: the routines fire at 7:45, 9:30, 11:00 a.m., 1:00 p.m., 2:15, 2:45 and 3:15. Times are as stated in the video (he's on Central).
  • Continuity trick: each agent writes a handoff message and the next one reads it before acting. Stateless runs end up behaving like one long-lived agent.
  • One thread: all routines run in the same Codex thread, so he watches a single thread for everything.
  • Rules:
  • Up to 2 changes per day allowed. He broke his earlier no-touch rule from the OpenClaw version of this experiment.
  • He can't stop the trading, even if the money is all lost.
  • Result is compared to the S&P 500. If he loses, one viewer gets a free VIP ticket to his Oct 17-18 event.
  • Day 1: Portfolio ended at $9,991, down about $9. It bought about 9 Tesla shares (~$3K), and the stop-loss triggered immediately on a drop.
  • Mid-challenge tweak: He told it the agent was too passive with only 6 days left. It acknowledged it should have acted on his feedback sooner and activated a more active plan with three entry rules (the transcript cuts off before the details).
  • Early status: Some clips show about 80% of the portfolio deployed and a small gain, but the excerpt doesn't tie them to a specific day. The full result isn't in this transcript.
