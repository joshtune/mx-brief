Codex Ultrafast benchmarked: speed gains versus usage burn

If you're weighing the $500/mo Codex plan, this gives you real speed and usage numbers on two tasks, but it's a test, not a build tutorial, so there are no workflows, repos or links to copy.

What it is: Nate runs Codex's new Ultrafast mode against standard on three tasks and reports speed, quality and weekly-usage cost.

How it works:

  • Plan: Ultrafast is only on the new $500/mo plan. It's marketed as 8x faster than standard and 4x faster than regular fast mode.
  • Test 1, Q&A lookup: He asked "What did I talk about in this week's community Q&A call?" Codex found the right call, pulled the transcript and summarized a 54-minute call in about 19 seconds.
  • Test 2, 60-second showreel: He pointed it at a folder of 145+ GB of footage from his Google trip in San Jose and asked for a showreel.
  • Ultrafast: about 19 min, 17% of weekly usage.
  • Standard: about 50 min, 4% of weekly usage.
  • That's only about 2.5x faster, not 8x, at about 4.25x the usage.
  • He judged the Ultrafast output better and more Google-branded. The standard run felt like a worse model even though it ran longer.
  • Test 3, YouTube resource guide from a video: Standard took 13 min and Ultrafast took 1 min 43 sec. That's close to the marketed 8x, and the usage bump was small.
  • Verdict: Use it in a pinch, like 5 minutes before a meeting, and expect to pay a lot more.
