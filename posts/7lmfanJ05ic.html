A Crash is just a Sale

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-21 · 26,742 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Buy boring cash businesses from tired owners during downturns.

It gives you a concrete acquisition filter — tired owner + recession-proof cash flow + certain terms — that works at side-hustle capital levels instead of requiring a war chest.

What it is: A 3-step framework ("the three Bs") for buying small businesses cheap in a fear-driven market, pitched as doable with a couple thousand dollars.

How it works:

  • Bleed — hunt tired *owners*, not failing businesses. The target profile: a guy in his late 60s, 30 years in, still cash-flowing, just can't stomach another bad quarter. Or the opposite end — a young operator with a $5K newsletter who's already mentally onto the next AI hype cycle.
  • Buying — buy where cash actually lands. Not stocks, not crypto. Pest control, landscaping, cleaning companies. The thesis: nobody cancels their cleaning service because the market dropped, so revenue survives the crash that's scaring everyone else off.
  • Certainty — the counterintuitive one. Don't lowball. Offer full price, clean terms, 30-day close. The claim is that Buffett doesn't win auctions on price, he wins on being the buyer who definitely closes — and in a scared market a seller will trade a few extra dollars for the deal that doesn't fall apart.
  • The whole play inverts the usual crash reflex: fear compresses the buyer pool, so the discount comes from *less competition*, not from a lower offer.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or URLs mentioned in this video — it's a pure framework clip. Codie's own paid education is soft-pitched ("if you don't know all that stuff, I can teach you").

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that certainty is the cheapest leverage a small buyer has — you can't outbid a PE fund, but you can be the one who closes in 30 days without drama, and that's what actually wins a tired seller.
