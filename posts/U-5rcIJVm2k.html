The $0 Side Hustle Anyone Can Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-24 · 7,442 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Affiliate video review hustle: free products, commission checks.

It's the rare side hustle with literally zero capital at risk — you spend time, not money, and the transcript names real people clearing $10K–$40K/month on it.

What it is: A product-review video hustle — you get free products shipped to you, film short review videos, and earn affiliate commissions when viewers buy through your links. The transcript never names the platform outright, but the mechanics (send your address, products arrive, upload videos, earn commission) point squarely at TikTok Shop / Amazon-Influencer-style affiliate programs.

How it works:

  • Sign up, get accepted, then request products from brands — "you literally send the address in the first message, and products just start showing up"
  • Film a short video per product. No face required. No editing skill implied. "As long as you can read and talk, you can make a video that's good enough for the platform"
  • Upload. Repeat. Volume is the entire strategy
  • Commissions are net — no inventory purchased, no fees, no overhead. "That's straight to your bank account"
  • The guest's own numbers: $10,000+/month at 4–5 hours of work per week

The volume→income math (the actual signal here):

  • ~700 videos → $25,000–$30,000/year in net commissions
  • ~1,100 videos → $40,000 in a single month
  • Videos are a compounding back catalog, not a one-shot — old uploads keep earning
  • Brother-in-law did $700 in his first week; a mom of four in Idaho is at $12,000/month part-time

Barrier to entry (why he calls it $0):

  • No inventory, no ad spend, no fees — the only input is time
  • Demographics cited: 70–75 year olds, people who "can barely even work technology"
  • Failure mode is singular: "You only fail if you just don't upload"
  • Side effect worth planning for — they received so much free product they ran two free garage sales to clear it out

Tools & links:

  • No specific tools, repos, or URLs were named in this clip — the pitch is deliberately platform-agnostic. Expect the full episode to name the program.

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle that can't put you underwater, this is the cleanest risk profile going — the only thing you can lose is the evenings you spend filming, and the numbers scale with uploads, not with luck.
