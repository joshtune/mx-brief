Skip the MBA; learn business by doing it

If you're starting a side hustle, this says to skip the $150K credential and learn by running the thing, because theory doesn't survive contact with customers.

What it is: A short clip arguing that an MBA is the most useless thing Codie did for her business, and that real business skill only comes from doing.

How it works:

  • The secret: Practice beats theory. MBA programs teach theory and then make graduates feel like experts. That's the dangerous part.
  • The hiring tell: When her team hires an MBA, the line is "we're going to rub it off you real quick". Credentials get unlearned on the job.
  • The builder view: Builders and billionaires she knows say the path to leadership shouldn't run through business school. A leader parachuted in without knowing how things work is "just good at PowerPoints".
  • The nuance: Learning from other people's mistakes is valuable. It just doesn't need to cost $150,000. Books, operators, and your own small bets are the cheap versions.
  • Caveat: This is a short, opinionated clip. It gives no framework, steps, or numbers beyond the $150K sticker price.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle, the first paying customer teaches you more than any course. Spend the $150K-equivalent on small, fast experiments instead: sell something this week, see what breaks, fix it.
