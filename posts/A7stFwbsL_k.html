Intelligence Does NOT Make You More Money…

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-10 · 28,915 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Focus and brute work beat raw intelligence.

If you're sitting on a side-hustle idea, this is the kick that says execution and daily focus—not being the smartest person in the room—are what actually print money.

What it is: A short, blunt pep talk arguing that money follows focus and effort, not IQ.

How it works:

  • The core claim: Intelligence has near-zero correlation with how much money you make. People "dumber than you" lap you because they work harder and take more shots.
  • The 2–3 hour rule: 99% of problems get solved with 2–3 hours of deep focus work per day — phone off, no new browser tabs, dark room, coffee, do-not-disturb sign.
  • The 6-month test: Do that single-focus block every day for 6 months and your life changes. The point: this level of focus is genuinely rare, which is exactly why it works.
  • The second lever — outwork everyone: Win continuously by working "harder than you could ever imagine wanting to, for longer than you want to, even when you don't want to — and maybe especially then."
  • The contrarian wedge: Most people opt for vacations and hangouts. The ones who build real wealth choose sustained work instead.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is a repeatable routine, not a trait — a daily phone-off deep-work block plus a willingness to outwork the field is the side-hustle operating system you can start tomorrow regardless of how smart you are.
