Startup pays users for their personal data

Almost nothing here for a web app builder — it's a 2-minute opinion segment on a data-broker startup with no technical substance.

What it is: A short commentary piece on Verb, a company that installs a tracking script on your phone and pays you for the data it collects.

How it works:

  • You install Verb's tracking script/app on your phone
  • You pick which categories to share: browsing activity, shopping habits, platform exports (Instagram, TikTok, ChatGPT)
  • You can block specific companies from buying your data and set your own asking price
  • Verb claims it excludes passwords, text messages, and health data
  • Matt's take: this style of data-for-cash payout is a more likely "UBI" than a government one — the buyers want training data and ad targeting badly enough to pay

Tools & links:

  • Verb — data marketplace that pays users for shared personal data; no URL given in the video
  • futuretools.io — Matt's AI tool directory (channel boilerplate, not video content)

Why it matters for you: Effectively zero build value — the only tangential angle is that consumer expectations around data collection are tightening, which is worth a thought if your apps do any behavioral tracking or analytics, but there's no implementation guidance here.
