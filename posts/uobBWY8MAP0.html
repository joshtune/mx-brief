Your City Posts This Online Every Day. Use AI to Sell It.

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-23 · 50,755 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Sell daily city building-permit alerts to contractors

It's a concrete, low-cost side hustle: repackage free public permit data into a $99/month daily email for contractors, built with one Replit Agent prompt.

What it is: A micro-SaaS called "Permit Central" that emails contractors each morning the new building permits matching their trade and zip code, built from free city open data.

How it works:

  • The arbitrage: Cities are legally required to post permits online for free, usually as a daily-updated spreadsheet on an open data portal. Contractors would pay to have it filtered and delivered to their inbox.
  • Why leads are valuable: A permit means someone is about to spend five to six figures. The contractor who calls first usually wins the job. Chris uses this on his own tree service and is building a pool himself.
  • Versus paid lead sites: Those sell the same homeowner to about five contractors, so you pay roughly $40 to race four others. Permit data is exclusive to whoever sees it first.
  • Proof people pay: Companies already sell cleaned-up permit data as a subscription. Chris says each city can fit 5–10 operators.
  • The offer: The contractor picks a trade and zip code and gets one email every morning with the relevant new permits.
  • The math: $99/month × 20 contractors ≈ $2k/month. Your costs are Replit credits and an email service. Once it works, you point the app at the next city's data.
  • Trades to sell to:
  • Landscapers: builders usually stop at sod.
  • Gutter companies: new homes and additions.
  • Blinds and shutters: nobody moves in with bare windows.
  • Pool builders: a new home on a big lot is a pool conversation waiting to happen.
  • Fence companies: Chris builds for these first. They get leads from new homes, new pools (many cities legally require a fence around a pool) and tear-downs.
  • The build (Replit Agent):
  • Find your city's open data portal by Googling "[city] open data portal URL". Chris's example is Dallas at dallasopendata.com.
  • Give the agent one prompt: build a web app for local contractors that checks the portal every morning for new building permits.
  • The prompt also tells it to tag each permit with the trades that would want it.
  • The agent works on several tasks in parallel, such as the website, the data agent and the email automation.
  • The sales motion: Chris says the video ends with him cold-contacting real contractors live, with no planted audience. He promises the exact prompts, the trade list and the outreach message in a Google doc in the description.
  • Caveat: The transcript cuts off mid-prompt, so the email setup, pricing flow and outreach results aren't covered here. Check the description doc for those.
