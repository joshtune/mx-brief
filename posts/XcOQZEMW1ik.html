Why You Need Two North Stars

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-12 · 3,851 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Why one business metric quietly destroys you.

If your side hustle optimizes a single number, you'll hit it and find you've gutted the thing that made it work.

What it is: Codie's argument against the single "North Star" metric, replaced by a two-metric system with a built-in guardrail.

How it works:

  • Reject the single North Star. Optimizing one number all the way down leads somewhere ugly — revenue-only content becomes "buy, buy, buy," follower-only content becomes OnlyFans (where the eyeballs actually are).
  • Run two oars, not one. Track two opposing metrics that keep the boat straight. Her example: revenue *and* followers.
  • One oar is the engine, one is the collar. Revenue is "unleashed" (the thing you grow), followers are the "collar metric" that stops you from chasing revenue at the expense of audience.
  • The pairing is the point. Each metric checks the other's worst failure mode — growth without the constraint that kills the business.

Tools & links:

  • No external tools, repos, or links mentioned in the transcript.

Why it matters for you: Pick a growth metric *and* a collar metric for your side hustle from day one — revenue paired with retention, or sales paired with reviews — so you don't optimize your way into killing it.
