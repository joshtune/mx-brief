There's no featured founder or revenue playbook here, just Palmer Luckey's pitch for an oxytocin business and two more ideas, so watch only if you want idea fodder.

What it is: Episode 868, where Shaan and Sam pitch three ideas. This is idea brainstorming, not a case study of a business that already makes money.

Idea 1: Oxytocin dosing (3:08)

  • Shaan saw a clip of Palmer Luckey (founder of Oculus, CEO of Anduril) answering the question "if you started from zero, what would you do?"
  • Luckey's answer: end the "tragedy of divorce" with oxytocin doping for marriage counseling.
  • His reasoning:
  • Mammal pair-bonding is well understood.
  • Oxytocin is the bonding hormone, and it's already used on infants.
  • It's unregulated, so it's easier to build in than a regulated space.
  • You can buy it in bulk from Sigma-Aldrich.
  • Shaan's read: a textbook first-principles pitch. It starts with divorce affecting about 50% of couples, then works backward to a lever.
  • Shaan, by his own admission, knows nothing about oxytocin beyond this. No operator, pricing or business model is covered in the part of the transcript I have.

Track record the hosts claim (pre-pitch banter):

  • Early on GLP-1s, before Ozempic reached market.
  • TRT clinics. They didn't build one, but both invested in one that worked.
  • Sperm freezing, as well as egg freezing.
  • IVF. The unbuilt idea is a trusted IVF chain, "the Chick-fil-A of IVF".
