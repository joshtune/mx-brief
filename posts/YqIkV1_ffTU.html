Five-filter checklist for picking a business worth buying

Run any side hustle idea through these five filters in 60 seconds and you'll know if you're building an asset or a second job you can't quit.

What it is: A rapid-fire screening checklist for choosing industries and businesses, where failing any filter is a warning sign that the business will own you.

How it works (the instructional secret: five filters, each a yes/no question):

  • Leverage — Can you use other people's money instead of yours? Think financing, seller notes, customer prepayments, so your own cash isn't the bottleneck.
  • Velocity of capital — How fast does cash come back after you put it in? Short payback means you can recycle the same dollar into the next move.
  • Lindy effect — Has it survived 20 years? Then it's statistically likely to survive another 20. Favor old, boring industries over trendy ones.
  • Simplicity — Can you explain it to grandma? If yes, it's easy to operate, hire for, and hand off. If you can't, skip it.
  • Recurring revenue — Do you get paid once for work done once, repeatedly? Or do you re-earn every single dollar? Subscriptions, contracts, and retainers beat one-off jobs.

How to use it:

  • Score each side hustle idea against all five before spending a dollar.
  • A one-off freelance gig typically fails recurring revenue and leverage, which is why it feels like a job.
  • Treat the list as a filter, not a formula: the video gives no weighting or numbers.

Caveats:

Why it matters for you: A side hustle that fails these filters is just a second job with extra paperwork, so use them to pick something that pays you while you sleep.
