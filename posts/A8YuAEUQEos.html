What it is: A short news reaction to Oracle announcing Fusion Claw, an enterprise agent framework that uses frontier models only where reasoning is needed. Nate doesn't build or demo anything.

How it works:

  • Frontier models handle the hard parts: reasoning, planning and adapting on complex, high-value work.
  • Once the system knows what needs to happen, it switches to deterministic computation for precise, high-volume execution. Result: no frontier-model prices for work that doesn't need AI.
  • Target workloads are big workflows in finance, staffing, supply chain, logistics and sales.
  • Authority is configurable. You define what agents may do, what needs approval, and when a human must step in.
  • Oracle says it already has 25 Claw-powered agentic applications built on this design.
