The Smartest People Never Stop Listening

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-09 · 24,767 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Listen and ask more than you talk.

Deal-making and running a side hustle is won by the operator who shuts up and extracts information, not the one who pitches loudest.

What it is: A 15-second Stoic reminder (via Epictetus) that questions and listening beat talking.

How it works:

  • Epictetus's line: two ears, one mouth — listen twice as much as you speak.
  • Codie's split: the "dumbest" people never stop talking; the smartest never stop asking questions and listening.
  • The instructional secret is a posture, not a tactic: default to inquiry. In any room, the person asking questions controls the direction and walks away with the most information.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or URLs mentioned in this clip.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustler chasing acquisitions or clients, listening is leverage — the seller's motivation, the customer's real pain, the terms nobody volunteers all surface when you ask and stay quiet, and that's where the margin lives.
