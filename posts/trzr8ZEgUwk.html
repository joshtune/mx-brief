Renting equipment as a near-passive side hustle.

If you want a low-maintenance side hustle that pays off its equipment in a handful of rentals and then runs almost passively, this is the model to study.

What it is: An equipment-rental side hustle — buy gear once, rent it out, collect checks with near-zero ongoing work (the example: rented panels/displays earning ~$20–30K per job).

How it works:

  • Each job nets ~$20–30K; you can set one up in a single day.
  • Equipment pays for itself in 1–6 rentals — everything after that is profit ("gravy").
  • Labor is front- and back-loaded: ~2 days at the start, ~2 days at the end of a rental, nothing in between.
  • No maintenance, low/no startup capital claimed — panels just sit rented out.
  • The guest built it to several hundred grand in revenue, then sold the company for $600K ~18 months later — a sale he regrets, because it was "too passive" and he got bored.
  • Scales if you want more units, or stays a lean one-person side gig.

Adjacent thread (kids + auctions):

  • Chris made his ~15-yr-old buy his own first car instead of gifting it.
  • They tracked auctions on B-Stock (liquidation/returns auction marketplace), won a "rug auction," and the kid funded the car — a teachable resale-flip angle.
