Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-19 · 13,123 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Kill failing projects fast, without punishing the people.

Side hustles die from zombie projects eating your limited hours, and this gives you a 3-rule system for killing them on schedule.

What it is: A short framework for ending underperforming projects, using Google's 200+ killed products as proof that pruning makes you richer.

How it works:

  • Rule 1: Every launch gets a number and a date. Define "working" before you start. Example: hit 50K in 6 months and someone leads it, or someone kills it.
  • Rule 2: Quarterly kill review. Ask of everything you run: "Knowing what you know now, would you still start this today?" If no, kill it.
  • Rule 3: Reward the kill. Whoever ran the dead project presents what they learned, and the room applauds.
  • Why rule 3 matters: If killing gets people punished, they hide losers, and that's how companies die.
  • The proof point: Google has killed 200+ products and is still worth billions. Pruning isn't failure, it's maintenance.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

The instructional secret: Decide the kill criteria *before* you start. A pre-committed number and date turns a painful emotional decision into a boring lookup. You never have to argue yourself out of a project, because the deadline does it for you.

Why it matters for you: On a side hustle your scarcest resource is hours, and every zombie offer, channel, or client eats time from the thing that works. Write the number and date on day one, run the "would I start this today?" test every quarter, and treat a clean kill as a win. Solo, rule 3 means you don't beat yourself up for it.
