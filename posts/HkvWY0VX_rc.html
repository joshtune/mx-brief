Mom of 3 Makes $50K/Month With AI (No Experience)

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-25 · 15,324 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Non-technical mom builds $50K/month AI service business

It's proof that an AI side hustle can run on 1–2 hours a day alongside a full-time job — the constraint you're actually operating under.

What it is: Interview with Sandy, who went from broke and homeless to $50K/month in ~9 months selling AI-powered services to clients, starting with a single $3,000/month retainer — while keeping a 9-to-5 and raising three kids under eight.

How it works:

  • Start with a retainer, not a product. First money online was a $3,000/month client retainer. No app, no audience, no launch — one client paying monthly. That's the fastest path from zero to real revenue.
  • Sell the outcome, deliver with ChatGPT. She had zero relevant experience when she applied for that first gig. The delivery layer is a $20/month ChatGPT subscription. Clients buy the result; the tooling is invisible to them.
  • Design for a 1–2 hour/day ceiling. She explicitly built around the constraint: max ~1 hour/day on client work. If a service model can't fit in that window, it's the wrong model for someone with a job and kids.
  • Apply before you're qualified. Chris's framing: "irrational confidence" and fake-it-till-you-make-it have never been more accessible, because AI closes the capability gap between what you can sell and what you can actually deliver.
  • 9-month timeline, not overnight. $0 → $50K/month took about nine months of compounding client work.

Tools & links:

  • ChatGPT Plus — $20/month; the entire production stack for the client work described

The mindset pitch (Chris's half of the conversation):

  • "If Sandy can do it, I can do it" — the whole point of the interview is removing the who-am-I objection
  • "Either you win or you learn" — he's started many failed businesses and regrets none, because each one answered a question
  • Deathbed regret is about things not done, not things done

Caveat: The transcript cuts off right as Chris says "here's some secret sauce" — the specific service being sold and the client-acquisition channel are in the part not captured here. Watch the back half for the actual mechanics.

Why it matters for you: This is the side-hustle shape that survives a full-time engineering job — a single monthly retainer, AI-delivered, capped at an hour a day — and as someone who can actually build, you'd be starting well ahead of where she did.
