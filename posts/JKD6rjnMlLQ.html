Financial independence isn't what you think

My First Million · 2026-06-29 · 15,961 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Real financial freedom means decisions unbound by money.

A mental model for when to stop trading life hours for dollars that no longer improve your life — useful if your equity or salary is already past the point of utility.

What it is: A reframe of "financial independence" — not about buying anything you want, but about making decisions that aren't driven by money at all.

How it works:

  • "Slay the money monster" — the speaker's term for true financial independence, which he claims very few people ever actually achieve (mentally, not financially).
  • The redefinition: Financial independence ≠ ability to buy whatever you want. It = making decisions *not based on money*.
  • The money utility curve:
  • In debt → money has *maximum* utility (every dollar matters).
  • Comfortable → money has *less* utility.
  • Wealthy enough to buy back your time → utility spikes again (freedom from work).
  • Past that point → you're "trading very valuable hours of life energy for useless dollars" you can't even spend to improve your life.
  • The core observation: Most of their already-wealthy friends *still* make the bad trade — valuable life hours for useless dollars — and find it nearly impossible to stop. The psychological habit, not the bank balance, is the real prison.
