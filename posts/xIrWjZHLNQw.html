Sounds Too Simple, But One Client Pays $15K/Month

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-25 · 4,745 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
College students land $15K/month client by texting contacts

It's a zero-capital side hustle playbook: text everyone you know for advice, then turn the answers into a paying B2B client.

What it is: Three college students (21-23) landed a $30M construction company paying $15K/month by texting their contacts for advice. No office, no employees, no connections.

How it works:

  • The trigger: James heard an episode of this podcast on a treadmill, called Brendan, and they acted that day.
  • The outreach: They texted every contact in their phones asking for advice, not money. Chris's framing: "Ask for money and you get advice. Ask for advice and you get money."
  • The win: One of those texts turned into the construction company client.
  • The service (inferred): Their pitch seems to cut proposal creation from about an hour per proposal to about 5 minutes. The intro doesn't say how they do it, so treat the mechanism as unconfirmed.
  • Pricing: They offered three tiers at $10K, $15K, and $25K/month. The client closed at $15K. The $25K tier was a decoy price anchor, a trick they credit to Alex Hormozi.
  • Credibility: Chris stresses there was no rich uncle and no connections. One founder dropped out of BYU for this.
  • Promise: The episode is supposed to reveal the exact text message they sent. That's not in this excerpt.

Tools & links:

  • None named in the excerpt. Check the full episode for the exact text and the service details.

Caveats:

  • The transcript covers only the cold open and intro, so the real how-to (the script, the service, the delivery) is in the full episode.
  • This is Chris's own framing of the story, so the $15K/month claim is unverified.

Why it matters for you: It's a cheap-to-test side hustle. Text your network for advice, find a business with a painful manual workflow, sell a monthly retainer, and use a decoy tier to anchor the price.
