You Don’t Need To Start A Business, Just Buy One Of These

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-09 · 56,167 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Data-backed tier ranking of 9 buyable businesses.

It tells you which side-hustle businesses actually print cash versus which ones quietly bankrupt you, ranked by real failure rates and margins.

What it is: A data-driven tier list ranking 9 business types by how well they actually work for a would-be owner-operator — built from Codie's experience buying/starting 30+ businesses ($100M) and an advisory firm of 5,000 owners.

How it works:

  • E-commerce → F tier: 7 of 10 fail in year one (2x any other type). Margins only 10–30% (a $40 shirt nets $4–$12) *before* ad spend, with $30–50k launch costs. You build on "rented land" — Amazon/Meta own your algorithm and CPMs. Only works if you bring an unfair advantage: existing audience, customer base, or a product nobody else has.
  • Landscaping → B tier: Recurring weekly revenue ($50–150/property), a crew hits 10–15 properties/day. Real money is in upsells (mow the lawn → sell a $60k patio). Start for ~$2,000 (mower + door-knocking). Case study: Contrarian member Meredith is acquiring a $900k landscaping biz, 6 employees, 85% of customers 10+ years, 60% for 20+ — zero digital presence to optimize. Capped at B only because competition is heavy.
  • Laundromats → (cut off): Codie left Wall Street to buy washers/dryers in a strip mall — thesis: people always have dirty laundry, durable demand.

The instructional secret: Don't chase hyped, zero-moat businesses (dropshipping). Buy boring, recurring-revenue businesses with loyal customers and an un-built digital layer — then add the obvious upsell/digital upgrades the previous owner never did.
