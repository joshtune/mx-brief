AI website concierge agency: $25-30K/month recurring revenue

It's a concrete, nights-and-weekends side hustle (sell AI lead-capture chatbots to local businesses) with real revenue, pricing, and close-rate numbers from a self-described non-tech 60-year-old.

What it is: An "AI concierge" agency. You install an AI helper on a small business's website that answers visitors' questions and turns them into leads, then bill monthly for it.

How it works:

  • The offer: An AI chat helper on the client's site that captures leads. One client sells $13,000 engines and got 50 new leads in month one. That's an easy sell when one engine sale pays for years of fees.
  • The numbers (Bob, ~1 year in): ~$25-30K MRR across ~26 clients, excluding one-time setup fees. Clients range from $300/month to nearly $4,000/month, averaging about $1,000.
  • Origin story: He scratched his own itch. He applied it to his own phone repair business first and understood the questions customers ask repeatedly. That industry knowledge is the edge.
  • Sales motion: Build a demo for the prospect *before* walking in, then show it to them. Claimed ~30% close rate.
  • Pricing: Priced against the value of the leads generated, so it's hard to say no. The video promises the pricing math later, but the transcript here cuts off before it.
  • Time per client: About 1-2 hours per month once set up.
  • Margins: Software runs ~$300-400/month plus some token costs. Bob claims 50%+ net margins, with one-time setup revenue being lower-profit.
  • Realistic timeline: Bob says ~6 months to $5-10K/month cash flow, with much of it profit. He calls it not get-rich-quick and says the video gives you roughly 80% of the knowledge.
  • Market math: 33M US small businesses. 100 clients at $1K each is $100K/month.
  • Upsells not yet tapped: Voice AI / AI phone receptionist. Bob hasn't gone after it yet. He's also eyeing bigger corporate clients for stickier contracts.
  • Teased later in the video (not in the excerpt): three words on websites that changed everything, and a five-question trick that wows business owners.
