How it works (the story as Nate tells it):

  • Late Oct: NYT reports US officials believe a woman tied to a company that buys Nvidia chips was connected to Chinese intelligence. China calls it groundless, and no charges have been reported.
  • Nov 12: Jensen resigns after admitting to an affair. The CFO becomes interim CEO.
  • Nov 17: Nvidia reports a $115.8B quarter, more than double the prior year. On the same call the board chair says it doesn't intend to reinstate him.
  • Friday: Reuters reports the SEC is looking at possible accounting issues, and the stock drops about 14%. That caps seven straight losing weeks and about $2.6T in lost value.
  • Weekend: Trump posts that he hopes the board rehires Jensen.
  • Monday (recording day): The board votes unanimously to bring him back, citing input from senior US government levels and no national security risk. The stock jumps more than 30%, about $900B in a few hours.
  • Still open: An independent investigation, the SEC question, and Congress wanting to question him. Sen. Warren called the decision reckless. The stock is still well below its October record.

Nate's takeaways:

  • Revenue doubled and the stock still fell for weeks, so investors were pricing in who runs the company, not chip demand.
  • Ben Thompson's framing is that Nvidia is an exoskeleton around one man, with dozens of direct reports to Jensen and no obvious number two.
  • AI chips are now treated like national infrastructure (oil, defense), and the government has a say in who runs an AI chip company.
  • Nvidia works as the AI index. When it fell, everything tied to AI fell, and the sector ripped back on the news.
  • Claude angle: Anthropic reportedly IPO'd on Nov 19. Nvidia supplies the chips and agreed to buy about $10B of Anthropic stock alongside the IPO. Anthropic closed down about 8% on the Friday crash.
