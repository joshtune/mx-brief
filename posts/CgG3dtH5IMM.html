It's a sponsored demo, but it shows Chuck's real AI workflow: a phone talking to a Mac Studio, and Home Assistant wired into a game that controls the actual house.

What it is: A sponsored (OpenAI #ChatGPT_Partner) demo where GPT-6 Astra turns a phone walkthrough of his house into a 3D game linked to real smart-home devices.

How it works:

  • Input: One ~10-minute phone video of every room. He told Astra "make my house" and it generated a walkable 3D world.
  • Iteration: It wasn't right on the first try. He chatted back and forth to fix it, then asked for more features.
  • Features added: Multiplayer, with a character modeled on each family member. Then the dogs, who walk around and play fetch.
  • Remote build: He built it while at OpenAI HQ, using ChatGPT on his phone to reach his Mac Studio at home.
  • Long-running work: Astra kept track of the project across context compactions and multiple chats.
  • Self-testing: It tested its own work, opening and inspecting things with computer use and browser use.
  • Home Assistant link: Turn a light off in the game and the real light turns off. He can control the Apple TV from the game, and the game shows what's on the TV.
  • Two-way sync: Game events change the house, and house state shows up in the game.

Tools & links:

  • ChatGPT download — the app where GPT-6 Astra is available, per the sponsor link
  • Home Assistant — the smart-home hub Astra was hooked into (no link given in the video)
  • Mac Studio — his always-on build machine, driven remotely from the phone
  • Apple TV — controllable from the game, and its screen is mirrored into the game

Reality check:
