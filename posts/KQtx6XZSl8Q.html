Stop making these rookie entrepreneur mistakes

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-21 · 11,777 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Seven first-timer mistakes ranked, worst last.

It's a 60-second gut-check on the exact cash and pricing errors that quietly kill side hustles before they ever scale.

What it is: A rapid countdown of seven rookie entrepreneur mistakes, ranked from #7 (least fatal) to #1 (most fatal), drawn from coaching 14,000 business builders.

How it works:

  • #7 Handshake deals — Never do them. Get it in writing or it didn't happen.
  • #6 Not measuring stats and data — If you don't track it, you can't fix it.
  • #5 Hiring too fast — Counterintuitively flipped: first-timers actually hire *too slow*. Hire faster.
  • #4 Doing everything yourself — Businesses stay small by default, not design. Delegate or stay stuck.
  • #3 Quitting too soon — Self-explanatory; most give up before the curve bends.
  • #2 Pricing too low — ~90% of the 14,000 they've helped are underpriced, anywhere from 30% to 100% too cheap. Raise prices today.
  • #1 Ignoring cash flow — The #1 reason businesses fail is running out of cash. Profit on paper doesn't pay bills.

The instructional secret: The two that matter most aren't about hustle or grit — they're financial. Underpricing and cash-flow blindness outrank quitting, hiring, and doing-it-all-yourself. Fix your prices and watch your cash before anything else.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle, the secret is that survival is a cash-and-pricing problem first — raise your prices (you're almost certainly too cheap) and never let the bank balance surprise you, because that's what actually kills the thing.
