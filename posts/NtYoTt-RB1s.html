The Non-NVIDIA AI Card Everyone’s Ignoring

Alex Ziskind · 2026-07-19 · 80,636 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Testing Tenstorrent's Wormhole N300 AI card for local LLMs.

A $1,400 non-NVIDIA card runs Llama 3.1 8B locally at 30 tok/s — a concrete price/performance data point versus buying a Mac or NVIDIA rig.

What it is: A hands-on review of the Tenstorrent Wormhole N300 — a $1,400 AI accelerator that isn't a GPU, isn't NVIDIA, and has no video output.

The hardware & cost:

  • Card: Tenstorrent Wormhole N300 — two chips on one board, 24 GB memory, ~$1,400
  • Not a GPU: can't draw a single pixel, no video outputs, no CUDA, no one-click Ollama
  • Two ports on the back (for chaining/scaling multiple cards)

Performance numbers:

  • Single stream: 30–31 tokens/sec on Llama 3.1 8B (after a one-time warm-up; first request was a misleading 3.6 tok/s)
  • Concurrency: 8 simultaneous requests → 164 tokens/sec total (~5x throughput) — shines as an inference server, not a single-user chatbot
  • Prompt processing: 3,700–4,000 tokens/sec

How it works (the weird part):

  • Not a sea of tiny lockstep cores like CUDA — it's a grid of 106 bigger cores wired by an on-chip network
  • Each core packs 5 RISC-V CPUs (the open-source ISA); CPUs orchestrate, dedicated units do the math

The catch:

  • Not plug-and-play yet — card came up dead after sitting idle, needed a risky forced firmware reflash + fighting version mismatches
  • Software stack is immature but improving

Tools & links:

  • Tenstorrent Wormhole N300 — the AI accelerator under test
  • Llama.cpp-style project "Llama Beni" on GitHub (Alex is now a contributor) — the inference runner used
  • vLLM server — ran Llama 3.1 8B
  • ChatLLM by Abacus AI — sponsor, multi-model chat at $10/mo

Why it matters for you: The bottom line you want — a non-NVIDIA local-AI box at $1,400 for 24 GB doing 30 tok/s solo and 164 tok/s under load — a clean price/performance yardstick to hold against a Mac mini, though it's an inference-server play, not a plug-and-play desktop yet.
