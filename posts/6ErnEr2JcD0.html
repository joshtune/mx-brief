The Most Hands-Off $192K/M Business Anyone Can Copy

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-11 · 35,198 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Building and renting local lead-gen websites to businesses.

It's a 90%+ margin side hustle that leans on skills you already have as an engineer—SEO, building sites, ranking them—and turns them into a passive, own-the-asset income stream.

What it is: Build local lead-generation websites, rank them in a niche + geography, then rent them out to a business that wants the leads (aka "rank and rent").

How it works:

  • Pick a niche + geo (e.g. tree service in Irving, TX) and build a site targeting it.
  • Do the SEO to rank the site so it pulls in real customer leads/calls.
  • Cold-call one local business in that niche: "I've got customers who want this—if I send you people, can we work out a cut?" Most say yes.
  • You own the asset, so the client can't fire you or dictate terms—if one is a pain, you rent to one of the 50 other businesses in that niche.
  • Small sites (~$4-5K/mo) run fully passive; big sites (worth hundreds of thousands) get lighter monitoring. Total time cited: 2-3 hrs/week at 90%+ net margins.
  • Guest Luke Vander got here by burning through eBay, Amazon, dropshipping, affiliate, Facebook ads, and an SEO agency—the agency worked but "turned into another job." The pivot: stop driving results for clients, vertically integrate and drive them for yourself.

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle, this is the rare one where your technical skills (site building, SEO, ranking) *are* the moat—build once upfront, rent it out, and the income keeps coming with a few hours a week.
