Mostly consumer-agent news with little for web app builders, so skip it unless you want a read on where OpenAI's agent product is heading.

What it is: A weekly AI news recap. The transcript covers only the first segment: OpenAI Dev Day and its new always-on assistant, Dot.

How it works:

  • Dot is OpenAI's proactive agent inside the ChatGPT app. It has no model selector and decides on its own whether to use chat, Codex or other modes.
  • You can type to it or call it like a phone call.
  • It watches connected accounts, such as email and Slack, and pings you about urgent items. It offers to draft replies, flags meeting invites and warns about flight delays.
  • It builds a daily 'personal scratchpad' document that pulls together emails, Slack messages and the day's agenda.
  • It reuses the ChatGPT connectors you already set up: Notion, Todoist, Slack, GitHub, Granola, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Gmail and iMessage.
  • Matt told it to draft only and not send, and it followed that instruction.
  • Availability is limited. It's for Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets only.
