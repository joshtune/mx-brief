GPT-6 Astra FINALLY Kills AI Website Slop

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-04 · 111,155 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Astra one-shots premium 3D parallax websites; dethrones Fable 5.1.

Nate shows what GPT-6 Astra plus his ScrollCraft skill produces from single prompts, so you can see whether it's worth swapping your design model and grabbing the skill.

What it is: A showcase of one-shot website and motion-graphics outputs from GPT-6 Astra, which Nate now ranks above Fable 5.1 (his previous pick) for AI design work.

How it works:

  • Every site shown was a single prompt, no iteration. Nate basically told Astra "make me a bunch of websites" across different industries: content agency, physical products (3D cans flying in), consulting firm (sidebar layout), luxury watch brand, and a first pass at his own AI Automation Society site.
  • All prompts ran with his ScrollCraft skill loaded. The skill encodes layering, scroll behavior, typography, and spacing rules so the page feels good beyond the hero section.
  • Layering is the big unlock. Text, background image, subject (a bike), and foreground (rocks) sit on separate parallax layers. That's what reads as premium depth without tipping into overwhelming.
  • Outputs are not template-clones. Each site has different section structure and vibe that matches the brand.
  • Caveat Nate concedes: his Fable-built AI Automation Society site still wins today because he iterated it ~15 times. Astra's one-shot was on-brand but unpolished. He also agrees with commenters that flashy sites don't automatically convert; it depends on his "three Ps" framework (pain, person, promise).
  • Motion graphics too: Using HyperFrames, two prompts and 20-30 minutes produced a 3D-element promo video with music synced to the animation, plus a vertical cut. Not viral-grade, but far cheaper than a human editor.
