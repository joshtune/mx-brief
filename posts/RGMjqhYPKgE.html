Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-21 · 14,251 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Run a weekend AI hackathon to build real tools fast.

It hands you a repeatable one-weekend playbook to turn AI from talk into working side-hustle tools while spotting who on your team can actually build.

What it is: A weekend "hackathon" format for teaching yourself and your team to build real tools with AI in hours, not weeks.

How it works:

  • Host a short, intense sprint — no slides, no meetings
  • Small teams each bring one real problem and fix it the same day
  • Everyone shows up with: open mind, crazy ideas, charged laptop, and AI tools
  • Codie's team built 5 rough tools in ~3 hours over a weekend
  • Goal is not polish — it's shipping something real, fast

Why the format wins:

  • Compresses weeks of coding ideas into hours
  • Signal over talk — you instantly see who turns ideas into reality (find hidden talent)
  • Reveals what you *actually* want to build
  • You build the tools you need in real time instead of guessing at requirements

Tools & links:

  • ChatGPT — AI assistant for ideation/coding
  • Claude — AI assistant for building/coding
  • Replit — in-browser environment to build and ship apps fast

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is speed as a moat — a side hustler who can build a working tool in an afternoon beats one who plans for weeks, and this hackathon is the forcing function to get there.
