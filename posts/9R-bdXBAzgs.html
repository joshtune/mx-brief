Hunt Brothers Pizza: a $500M chain hiding inside gas stations.

A no-royalty, no-franchise-fee business model that scaled to 9,000 locations by piggybacking on convenience stores instead of building standalone restaurants.

What it is: Hunt Brothers Pizza — a pizza chain with ~9,000 locations doing 500M+/year in revenue, almost entirely unknown to consumers because it operates inside gas stations and convenience stores rather than as a standalone brand.

How it works:

  • Instead of building freestanding restaurants, Hunt Brothers installs tiny 50–100 sq ft pizza stations inside existing convenience stores and gas stations
  • The store owner runs the pizza operation themselves — no separate staff needed
  • Pizza cooks in ~5 minutes, served "broiling hot"
  • Sold by the slice (called a "hunk" = 1/4 of a pizza): $2.89 per hunk, or two hunks for $5.49

How the money works (the actual business model):

  • Store owner pays ~$10,000 upfront for the equipment
  • No contract, no licensing fee, no royalty fee — explicitly positioned as different from a traditional franchise
  • Ongoing revenue for Hunt Brothers comes from selling ingredients to the store owners (transcript cuts off before full detail, but this is the stated model)
  • Low capex + no recurring franchise fees removes the biggest barriers that stop convenience stores from adding food service

Why it matters for you: A blueprint for embedding a product inside someone else's existing foot traffic and infrastructure instead of paying for your own real estate — margin comes from a captive ingredient supply chain, not fees.
