I Coached a Security Engineer Through His Failed Meta Interview (Live)

Live coaching a security engineer's failed Meta behavioral interview.

You can ace the technical loop and still get rejected on behavioral — this shows exactly how to fix the storytelling that decides mid-level offers.

What it is: A live teardown of a real candidate's Meta behavioral interview, where Steve coaches an application-security engineer (Muhan) who passed the technical rounds but tanked the behavioral.

How it works:

  • The core failure: Muhan *winged* the entire behavioral loop, including "tell me about yourself." His stories became tangents with no structure.
  • Behavioral stories are about your behavior and actions — not a one-sided "I'm the hero" narrative. Interviewers want decisions, not highlight reels.
  • Fix for "tell me about yourself": state your name + what you do, then give three most-important things about you.
  • Lead with impact, not process. Muhan opened with his tooling (Semgrep + Claude pipeline); Steve flipped it — open with the *result* ("I found seven CVEs in WordPress plugins"), then explain *how*.
  • Match the story to the level. A story that's impressive for a junior can read as *too junior* when you're targeting mid-level — calibrate scope and ownership up.
  • Don't exaggerate. Steve explicitly reins in inflating numbers — credibility over hype.
  • Steve frames behavioral interviews as the new differentiator: "AI can write your code. It can't tell your story."
