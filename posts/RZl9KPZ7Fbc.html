What it is: A short clip of a legendary (unnamed in-transcript, but this is investor Howard Marks / Oaktree territory — the son "Andrew" and AI-memo details match) billionaire investor describing how his son pushed him to update his AI memo, and how he used Claude to do it.

How it works:

  • Wrote a first AI memo around Dec 9; son Andrew told him in early February "so much has happened, update it and get current."
  • Asked Claude for an AI tutorial — got a 9-section walkthrough he called "mind-blowing."
  • Rewrote the memo entirely, then fed it to Claude for feedback; Claude praised it but flagged "a couple of factual errors."
  • Suspected Claude was an "ass kisser"; son's fix: prompt it to be "hyper critical."
  • Punchline: when asked to be "hyperritical," Claude joked back "do you want hyper*critical* or hypo*critical*?" — model cracking wordplay.

The actual takeaway (workflow, not wealth):

  • Use an LLM as a tutor (structured multi-section curriculum) to ramp on a new domain fast.
  • Default LLM tone is sycophantic — explicitly instruct "be hyper-critical" to get real red-team feedback on your writing.
  • Round-trip your own drafts through the model as a fact-check/editor pass.
