All That VRAM Needs a Bigger Brain

Alex Ziskind · 2026-09-06 · 149,578 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Alex buys a Threadripper Pro to feed four RTX Pro 6000s.

Skip the build porn: this is what a top-tier local-LLM workstation actually costs and why a $4K CPU is the entry ticket, which tells you exactly how far a Mac mini is from this tier.

What it is: Alex upgrades his 4×RTX Pro 6000 (384GB VRAM total) rig with an ASUS Pro WS WRX90E-SAGE board and shops Micro Center for a Threadripper Pro to drive it.

The machine:

  • GPUs: 4× NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 (96GB each). One Max-Q edition (300W cap), two workstation editions pulled from his old build, plus one more. ~$8–10K *each* street price, so ~$35K in GPUs alone.
  • Motherboard/case: ASUS Pro WS WRX90E-SAGE in an ASUS ET900 AX9 case, both sent by ASUS. Supports up to 2TB ECC RAM and 128 PCIe 5.0 lanes.
  • CPU: Threadripper Pro 9000 WX series. Alex leans toward the 32-core (~$4K), which the Micro Center rep calls the sweet spot for multi-GPU. Options: 16-core (2-GPU entry), 24, 32, 64 (128 threads), 96-core 9995WX ("financially irresponsible").
  • Cooler: Silverstone Threadripper cooler, 2800 RPM fans, loud but needed for a 350W CPU.
  • RAM: He already has it; store stocks ECC for this platform.

Why the bigger CPU:

  • More cores don't make one model generate tokens faster.
  • They give GPUs more CPU workers for data loading, preprocessing, model conversion, VMs, and multiple concurrent users (i.e. coding agents hammering it in parallel).
  • All Pro chips have the same 128 lanes; Pro (WX suffix) vs non-Pro (X suffix) is about lanes and RAM capacity, not GPU count.
  • Someone has 16 Pro 6000s on one of these boards.

Cost vs Mac mini (rough, street pricing):

  • This rig: ~$35K GPUs + ~$4K CPU + ~$1–2K board/case/cooler + RAM = ~$42–45K, plus ~1.5kW under load.
  • Mac mini M4 Pro 64GB: ~$2K. Mac Studio M3 Ultra 512GB unified memory: ~$10K.
  • The Mac gets you *capacity* (512GB) at 1/4 the price; the Threadripper rig gets you ~5–10× the *bandwidth and compute* for that 384GB, plus real multi-user throughput.

Tools & links:

Why it matters for you: This is the $45K ceiling of local AI; a Mac mini isn't competing with it, a Mac Studio Ultra is the sane 1/4-price alternative if you want big models without a space heater.
