How Women Win Online

Codie Sanchez  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Mindset for women building an online presence.

If you're building a personal brand to fuel a side hustle, Codie's anti-victim mindset is the difference between getting paralyzed by hate and using attention as leverage.

What it is: Codie's mindset playbook for women putting themselves out there online to build an audience and business.

How it works:

  • Refuse the victim frame: Don't tell yourself (or others) it's harder to be a woman online — "you make yourself a victim, then you get treated like one." Mindset is the lever.
  • Treat everyone as human: She succeeded early by never dwelling on a men-vs-women divide. "Who cares? We're all human."
  • Expect the hate, pre-accept it: There's roughly a 9:1 ratio of men to women talking online, so women get disproportionate hate. Expect comments like "did you sleep with him?" or "was it daddy's money?" — and don't let them land.
  • Reframe scarcity as opportunity: Because so few women post, the ones who do have an outsized lane — "the best opportunity ever."

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or external resources mentioned — this is a pure mindset clip.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that resilience to online hate is a learnable mindset, not a personality trait — and for a side hustle that lives or dies on audience attention, refusing the victim frame is what keeps you posting long enough to win.
