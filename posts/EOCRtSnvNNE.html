AI News: GPT-5.6 and the new Super App are a Massive Leap!

Matt Wolfe · 2026-07-10 · 105,643 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
GPT-5.6 launches plus ChatGPT's merged super app.

GPT-5.6 codes full 3JS games and slide decks cheaply, and ChatGPT's new merged app collapses Codex/Atlas/chat into one build-and-ship surface you'll likely use daily.

What it is: OpenAI's weekly drop — GPT-5.6 (new flagship) plus "ChatGPT work," a merged super app replacing separate Codex/Atlas/chat apps.

How it works:

  • GPT-5.6 feels like a generational jump (Wolfe calls it "should've been GPT-6"), not the marginal 5.4→5.5 kind of bump.
  • Three tiers referenced: Soul, Terra/Terror, and cheaper Luna variants; "Soul Pro" is the top performer.
  • Benchmarks: near-Fable-level intelligence for far less cost; on GDPval it beats everything except Claude Fable (barely); leads on browser/computer use; ties Mythos on GPQA.
  • Cost/speed win: top run cost 77¢ / 45k tokens / ~1 min vs GPT-5.5 Pro at $4 / 6 min for the same task — roughly 5x cheaper, 6x faster.
  • Strong at code gen (a polished 3JS "Clockwork Village" game was built with it) and slide decks.
  • ChatGPT work / super app: Codex, ChatGPT, and Atlas browser folded into one app. A dropdown toggles Codex mode (dev/writing code) vs Work mode (assistant that decides whether to answer, call a tool, or build something). Wolfe notes the UI barely changes between modes — the distinction isn't obvious yet.
