Forget AI. Make $10K+ With These Offline Side Hustles

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-24 · 68,651 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Five offline, low-tech side hustles that print cash.

If you want a side hustle with near-zero competition and almost no ongoing work, these dirt-simple offline businesses beat anything AI-flavored.

What it is: Five "sweaty startup" offline business ideas — no AI, no internet — each targeting $10K in a year, month, week, or day. The transcript covers two in depth.

Idea #1 — Temporary fence rental:

  • A friend in a Sacramento suburb started with zero domain expertise — just sales skill.
  • Model: buy plastic barrier panels + fencing, rent them to construction sites that legally need temporary fencing.
  • Sales tactic: literally drive to new construction sites, find the site manager in the trailer, pitch "want a temp fence? I'll rent you mine, cheap."
  • Pricing: charge ~$4/linear foot, dropping to ~$2/foot for longer rentals (construction always runs long → more passive months).
  • Hot buyers by default: zoning/liability laws force them to have fencing within X days of breaking ground.
  • Edge: building permits are public — filed days/weeks/months ahead — so you can find buyers before competitors even know the job exists.
  • Work profile: active ~2 days setup + ~2 days teardown, then pure passive income for months/years. No maintenance.

Idea #2 — (referenced) equipment rental / panels at scale:

  • One operator made $20–30K per job, set up in a day, banked several hundred grand, then sold the company for $600K in 18 months (regretted selling — it was *too* passive).
  • Pay off all equipment in 1–6 rentals; everything after is gravy.

The core playbook (applies to all):

  • Don't pre-buy inventory — be *ready* to buy when you land the first job, then fund it from that job.
  • Target buyers who are legally/practically forced to buy.
  • Front-load the work, then collect checks.
