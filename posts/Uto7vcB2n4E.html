The Biggest Problem I See With First Time Entrepreneurs

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-13 · 17,614 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stop pricing products based on your own wallet.

If you undercharge for your side hustle, this names the exact mental bug doing it — and how to kill it.

What it is: A short coaching clip diagnosing why first-time entrepreneurs underprice — what Codie calls the "wallet trap."

How it works:

  • The trap: you assume buyers think and spend like *you* do in financial decisions.
  • You unconsciously project your own budget, price sensitivity, and definition of "expensive" onto the customer.
  • That projection creates the false belief "I could never charge that much" — even when you yourself believe the product is worth more than the price.
  • The fix is a reframe: your wallet has nothing to do with your customer's wallet. Price to the value delivered and the buyer's budget, not your own comfort level.
  • Practical move: if you privately think a $1,000 product is worth *more* than $1,000, that's your signal the price is too low, not too high.

Tools & links:

  • No external tools, repos, or links mentioned in this clip — it's a pure mindset frame.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is naming the bug — "the wallet trap" — so side-hustlers stop anchoring prices to their own broke-founder budget and start charging what the market will actually pay, which is where real margin lives.
