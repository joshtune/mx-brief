Why owning businesses beats chasing a bigger paycheck.

If you're grinding for raises while wanting a side hustle, this reframes the goal: keep your job, but funnel earnings into owning cash-flowing businesses instead of just out-earning the next guy.

What it is: A short thesis pitch: the wealth gap is an *ownership* gap, not an income gap — and a funnel into Codie's paid program.

How it works:

  • The hook: US household income is the highest it's ever been, yet household wealth lags — so income isn't the lever.
  • The 'trap': you've been trained to optimize for *earnings* (raise, promotion, title), not *ownership*.
  • The stat: the top 10% own ~93% of all business equity. They simply own more — that's the whole game.
  • The formula: 'own things + compound them' — keep your W-2, but buy small businesses on the side that throw off cash for decades.
  • The instructional secret is really a *mindset reframe*, not a tactic: redirect your existing income into acquiring assets rather than upgrading your lifestyle or your title.
  • No actual how-to here (how to find, value, or finance a business) — that's gated behind the upsell.
