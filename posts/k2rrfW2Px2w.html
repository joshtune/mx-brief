Fast And Wrong Is Better Than Undecided

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-15 · 18,055 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Why fast wrong beats deliberate indecision.

The instructional secret here is a decision-speed heuristic — treat indecision as a hidden recurring cost, not a safe default — and it directly attacks the #1 killer of side hustles: never launching.

What it is: A mentor-sourced mental model Codie uses for business decisions — moving fast and being wrong beats deliberating and staying undecided.

How it works:

  • A wrong decision has a bounded cost: some time, some money — and you find out fast, so you can correct.
  • An undecided decision has an unbounded, hidden cost: you pay it every single day you refuse to choose, but because nothing visibly happens, you never see the bill.
  • Markets (and opportunities) move while you deliberate — someone else makes the call for you, and whether you'd have gotten it right stops mattering because the option is gone.
  • The core asymmetry: a fast wrong answer teaches you something today; a slow right answer teaches you nothing until it's made — and by then it may be moot.

Tools & links: None mentioned — this is a pure mindset/framework clip, no tactics or resources cited.

Why it matters for you: If you're sitting on a side hustle idea, a pricing decision, or a niche pick — the real cost isn't picking wrong, it's the weeks or months of "still researching" that never show up on a P&L but quietly compound against you.
