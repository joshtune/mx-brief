What it is: A tutorial on Nate's six-step process for building Codex "skills" (SKILL.md files) that reliably produce the output you want, demoed live in Codex.

How it works:

  • Skill basics: A skill is just a SKILL.md (markdown) file — natural language instructions (headers, bullets) that an agent follows so it doesn't have to guess your preferences each time. Can be a simple prompt (e.g. rewriting an email in your voice) or a complex multi-step process (e.g. researching and ranking 50 stocks).
  • Step 1 — Reverse engineer: Start from the finished output (the "definition of done"), not a vague ask. Example: instead of asking an agent to "build me a YouTube dashboard skill" (risking a mismatch between your vision and the agent's), give it a real finished deliverable (e.g. a completed Excel report) and have it work backwards — what data was used, how was it calculated, how was it formatted — to construct the skill. Framed via his "chicken vs. chicken parmesan" and chocolate-chip-pancake-recipe analogies: vague requests get inconsistent results; a documented recipe (skill) gets the same output every time.
