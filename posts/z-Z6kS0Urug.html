Skip it unless you're shopping GB10 boxes: it's a short unboxing with no benchmarks and no pricing, so it tells you nothing about cost versus a Mac mini.

What it is: A short unboxing of the Dell GB10, Dell's version of NVIDIA's DGX Spark AI mini-workstation.

How it works:

  • Same footprint and ports: The Dell matches the Spark's size and has the same inputs and outputs, but Dell actually labels them.
  • Back panel: The NVIDIA back panel pops off to reveal the Wi-Fi antenna.
  • Extras: Dell adds more magnets and includes cables. The power brick looks like the one that ships with the NVIDIA Spark.
  • Stacking: The NVIDIA rubber feet grip better than Dell's, so Dell's stack is slightly less secure.
  • Verdict: Once booted, the Dell performs about the same as the NVIDIA unit and draws about the same power.

Tools & links:
