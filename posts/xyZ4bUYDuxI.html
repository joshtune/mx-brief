How To Make Money On Social Media

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-25 · 22,570 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Codie tier-ranks social platforms by actual revenue.

If you're building a side hustle, this tells you which platform to stop wasting nights on and where the money actually converts.

What it is: A tier list of social platforms ranked by real dollars earned, ending on the channel nobody ranks — email.

How it works:

  • X — F tier. Content dies fastest here. Zero shelf life, no monetization path she'd defend.
  • TikTok — D tier. Views ≠ dollars. With 2M followers she sold roughly a dozen units of her last product at her last event. Only worth it if you have a visual product.
  • LinkedIn — B tier. Sponsored posts run $1,500–$8,000. The under-counted return is inbound clients and hires.
  • Instagram — A tier. Sponsorships $2,500–$7,500 per post, but the real edge is audience quality: 100x more event tickets sold than TikTok.
  • YouTube — A tier. People sit with you 30 minutes, so trust compounds fast. Lower-tier sponsorships $5,000–$15,000.
  • Newsletter — the winner. Highest-intent audience, and the only list you own rather than rent.

The instructional secret:

  • Sponsorship money is the visible metric and the wrong one to optimize. It's rented attention on someone else's platform.
  • The real question is *where does your audience convert*, then move that audience onto an asset an algorithm change can't delete.
  • Watch the conversion gap, not the follower count: 2M TikTok followers moved a dozen units; Instagram moved 100x the tickets.

Why it matters for you: Side hustles die on vanity platforms — pick the one or two that convert, then funnel every follower into a newsletter you own so your distribution survives the next algorithm reset.
