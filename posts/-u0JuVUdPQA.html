The Most Passive Business You Can Start With $20

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-31 · 112,114 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Build a niche directory website with AI, earn passively

If you want a side hustle that costs one AI subscription and about 30 minutes every few months, this walks through a directory site that has made ~$150k.

What it is: A directory is just a curated list (thrift stores, things to do in Denver, government links) that helps people decide, monetized with ads or high-value lead forms.

How it works:

  • The idea: Pick a narrow niche where people are researching or deciding and would otherwise open 20 tabs. Example: a list of thrift stores. Another: "Things to do Denver."
  • Avoid the Yellow Pages trap: Don't build a 50-category everything-site. Go narrow, because broad directories won't survive AI and copycats.
  • The build: Guest Frey had no coding background. He watched YouTube, learned how people find sites on Google, and built it. Now he says a Claude Code subscription is about all you need.
  • Monetization 1, ads: The thrift store site made $1,200 in its first month on ads and peaked at about $3,000/month.
  • Monetization 2, leads: Add a lead form and each submission can become a $5k-$15k lead. One site had lorem ipsum on the home page and still landed a $20,000 lead.
  • The $50 backlink: Frey found an existing directory ranking well and asked for a link swap rather than a link insert. Swapping removes a link from a competitor and gives you a strong one. Their owner said "PayPal me 50 bucks." That one link got him onto page one for many keywords.
  • Then it compounds: Once you rank #1 on page one, journalists and bloggers link to you naturally, so you barely have to build links after that.
  • Time commitment: About 30 minutes every 4 months. Sometimes he didn't touch it for 6 months.
  • Results he cites: That site has made ~$150k over 4 years. The highest acquisition offer for his first directory was about $50k. A separate 3-month-old government-links directory gets 42,000 monthly organic visitors, and one site hit $10k/month in 6 months.
  • Still to come in the full episode (beyond this excerpt): the exact five-sentence outreach email for links, and how to find the idea.

Tools & links:

  • Claude Code — the AI tool used to build the site without writing code yourself
  • Viktor — sponsor, an AI employee in Slack (code KOERNER100 for $100 of free work). Not part of the directory method.

Why it matters for you: This is a low-cost, low-maintenance side hustle: pick a narrow niche, have AI build the list, get one good backlink, and monetize with ads or leads. Caveat: the numbers are guest self-reports, and the excerpt cuts off before the niche-picking and email details.
