What it is: A free Claude Code setup called the "roast room". It's a board of AI advisors, each modeled on a real expert's public work (Hormozi, Buffett, Bezos), that debate your decision instead of one chat agreeing with you.

How it works:

  • Each advisor persona is built from that expert's public material.
  • Every advisor gets your decision and responds on its own.
  • They then argue with each other and poke holes in each other's ideas.
  • They vote until the best option wins.
  • You end up with a real answer and a score for every option.
  • Setup: open Claude Code, paste in the link, and say "Set this up for me."
  • The goal is to avoid generic, flattering advice from a single chat.
