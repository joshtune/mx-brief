I Thought I Understood Credit Cards Until I Talked to This Guy

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-28 · 54,235 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stacking 0% APR cards to fund a business

If your side hustle is stuck on startup cash, this is a walkthrough of how to raise $500K+ at 0% interest using business credit cards, though the transcript only covers the opening minutes and the risks are glossed over.

What it is: Guest Brandon (in the funding game since 2011, using it for real estate since 2015) explains how to stack 0% APR credit cards and other bank products to raise $500K to $1M+ in cash for a business, real estate, or an acquisition.

How it works:

  • The idea: Apply for many business credit cards that carry 0% intro APR. The cash they unlock funds the side hustle, and you repeat the process about every six months.
  • Claimed scale: $500K up to $1M+ in cash. Chase is said to give up to $150K with no documentation.
  • Case study: One person got 13 cards in 10 days. Each card paid a sign-up bonus, which added up to about $15,600 in free cash. The claim is that the same points could return 7 to 21x if used for travel.
  • Cash access: Brandon says a card's credit limit can be moved into your bank account, often the next day. Chris says he had never heard of this.
  • Bank product priority:
  • Mortgages come first.
  • Credit cards come second.
  • Lines of credit and loans come third.
  • Auto loans come last.
  • Banks look harder at applications you filed in the last 6 months, mortgages especially, so order matters.
  • Working capital plan: If you don't need real estate, go for 0% business cards, and put everything on the business side. The goal is to leverage the full amount with none of your own money in.
  • Vehicle hack (Bank of America):
  • Open a business account.
  • Wait about 2 weeks and put some money in.
  • Apply online for up to four business auto loans of up to $50K each.
  • Dealership hack:
  • Go on the last day of the month, or the quarter.
  • Find the sales leaderboard and skip the person in last place (they may be on the way out). Pick the salesperson just above them.
  • Show up 2 to 3 hours before close, when finance is desperate to hit quotas.
  • Credit repair: The teaser says the episode covers how to build good credit fast. That part isn't in the transcript excerpt.

Tools & links:

  • Bizee — LLC formation service sponsoring the video. Chris uses it to start his LLCs.

Caveats:

  • This is a sponsored, hype-heavy intro, and the claims are unverified. The transcript is cut off before the actual step-by-step.
  • The "150,000% interest" line is clearly a transcription slip for 0%.
  • Card churning hurts your credit score, and the 0% periods end. If you can't repay before the intro APR expires, you owe standard card interest (often 20%+) on the balance.
  • Cards are usually personally guaranteed even when issued to a business, so a failed side hustle can leave you personally liable.

Why it matters for you: This is a funding play, not a business idea. It's worth watching if you have a side hustle and need startup capital without giving up equity or paying interest, but only if you have a firm plan to repay before the promo APR ends.
