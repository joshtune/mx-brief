Four frontier models dropped in one week: the TLDR.

Gemini 3.8 Flash matches Opus-tier coding at roughly a tenth of the price, which directly changes what you should wire into your app's LLM calls.

What it is: Matt's weekly roundup, but the transcript covers only the model-release segment: Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Meta Muse Spark 1.3, and GPT-6 Astra. Deep dives on each live in separate videos.

Claude Fable 5.1 (plus Mythos 5.1, restricted to security testers):

  • Topped benchmarks on release day: Scientific Research 52.6 vs Opus 29, Terminal Bench 55.8%
  • Still leads the Artificial Analysis composite index even after the other three launched
  • Anthropic claims ~25% cheaper than Fable 5 for typical workloads, but Artificial Analysis measured it as the most expensive model per task at $3.69
  • Slow on some tasks: 18 minutes and $4.35 for one SVG on Beauty Bench
  • 60% fewer false-positive refusals than before

Gemini 3.8 Flash:

  • $0.75 per million input tokens, $3.75 per million output
  • Opus 5 is $5 / $25; Fable is $10 / $50
  • Deep SWE score of 73.7% vs Opus 5's previous best of 74%, a 0.3 point gap at a massive cost cut
  • Matt's hands-on note: coding is where it shines most, and it's fast

Muse Spark 1.3 and GPT-6 Astra: Announced, but the transcript cuts off before Matt covers them. He also teases a side-by-side Megabonk clone comparison across all four models.

Tools & links:

Why it matters for you: If your web app makes LLM calls, Gemini 3.8 Flash is the obvious new default for cost-sensitive coding and generation paths, and Fable 5.1 is the reach-for-it option when quality beats cost and latency.
