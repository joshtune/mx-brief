Beelink SER10 mini PC runs local AI three ways.

It's a small AMD mini PC that rivals M4 Pro for local LLMs — and the bare box is cheaper than a comparable Mac mini.

What it is: Review of the Beelink SER10 mini PC (AMD Strix-class chip) running local LLMs, benchmarked against Apple's M4 Pro.

The machine:

  • 12-core CPU
  • Radeon 890M iGPU
  • 55 TOPS XDNA 2 NPU
  • AMD claims 86 TOPS combined across all three
  • Upgradeable RAM + 10GbE port (vs. soldered RAM on a Mac mini)

How it performs (local LLM tokens/sec):

  • Llama 3.2 3B: 27 → 37.5 tok/s (CPU vs iGPU)
  • Qwen 2.5 1.5B: 47.5 → 68.3 tok/s
  • Qwen 7B prefill (long prompt): CPU 255, Vulkan iGPU 240, NPU hybrid 631 tok/s — ~2.5× the iGPU

The three-chip split:

  • CPU = fallback
  • iGPU = streaming chat
  • NPU = long prompts / prefill (RAG, long context)
