Real wealth isn’t built loudly… it’s built through flywheel acquisitions.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-06 · 7,345 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Buy businesses that feed each other, quietly.

If you want a side hustle that compounds instead of plateaus, this is the 60-second case for buying things that plug into what you already own.

What it is: A short thought-leadership clip on "flywheel acquisitions": the quiet-money playbook of billionaires who buy companies that boost their *other* companies, cheaply.

How it works:

  • The instructional secret: don't buy a business in isolation. Buy ones that feed what you already own.
  • Three things swap between companies: products, customers, and talent. That cross-pollination is the claimed path to 10x.
  • Cross-promote on day one. The acquired company's audience is a free distribution channel for your existing ones, and vice versa.
  • Buy cheap. The pitch is acquiring "old beat-up businesses" for pennies on the dollar, from owners who are burned out on running them.
  • Be the white horse. Sellers who've given their blood, sweat, and tears and can't stand it anymore will take an exit. The clip frames the payoff as a ~20x exit.
  • Examples cited: the fifth richest person in the world and the richest in India (unnamed in the transcript), plus Louis Vuitton and Dior as brands that were *bought*, not founded.
  • Caveat: it's a teaser. No numbers, deal structure, financing, or how-to. The full playbook is gated behind a comment CTA. The "20x exit" line is also vague about what's being multiplied.

Tools & links:

  • None named in the transcript. The full "playbook" is offered via comment on the video.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle, the takeaway is to scale by picking your *next* purchase for how it connects to your *current* one (shared customers, products, or people) instead of chasing unrelated deals. That's the cheap version of a billionaire's moat.
