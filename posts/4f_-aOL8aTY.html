The school kids love more than vacation

My First Million · 2026-07-11 · 3,787 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A school built on hard challenges kids crave.

A case study in engineering intrinsic motivation through high standards and hard, gamified challenges — the same lever that makes great products and teams sticky.

What it is: A short clip on a school model designed to be more compelling to students than vacation, built on high standards and physically/mentally hard "alpha tasks."

How it works:

  • Core thesis: you can't build something kids love more than vacation *if you have low standards* — the difficulty is the draw, not the deterrent.
  • Motivation hack: nothing drives a student harder than going head-to-head with their parent and winning.
  • Social pull: excitement comes from getting their friends to do hard things together.
  • "Alpha tasks": busy workshops you must complete and then *pass a gate* to clear the level (game-loop mechanics applied to learning).
  • Escalating physical challenges by grade:
  • 45-ft wall climb
  • 2nd graders run a 5K
  • 8th graders complete a Tough Mudder — and everyone has to cross the finish line at the same time (forced team cohesion, no one left behind).

Why it matters for you: The featured play is a repeatable pattern — raise the bar, gamify the gate, make it social — to manufacture the kind of pull that most products, onboarding flows, and teams fail to create.
