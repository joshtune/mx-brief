Oxygen Vs Dessert Business

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-29 · 5,162 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Sell what customers can't live without, not what's nice.

Before you sink nights and weekends into a side hustle, this gives you a 30-second test for whether anyone will actually pay you.

What it is: Codie's two-bucket framework for judging whether a business sells something customers *must* buy (oxygen) or merely *like* to buy (dessert).

How it works:

  • Dessert business — sells a birthday cake: life coaching, a brand refresh. Customers can put it off forever and nothing bad happens. Budget is small, pain is low.
  • Oxygen business — sells a need: emergency plumbing at 2 a.m., payroll software when 40 employees expect a check Friday. Customers find the budget, and the pain is acute.
  • The secret (the part everybody misses): need is only half of it. There are two axes: (1) how *painful* the problem is, and (2) how much *budget* exists to fix it. Oxygen businesses win on both.
  • The 30-day test: what happens to your customer if they buy nothing from you for 30 days? If the honest answer is "nothing much," you're selling dessert.
  • The fix: move your offer into the top-right corner of the pain/budget grid, and customers come to you instead of you chasing them.
