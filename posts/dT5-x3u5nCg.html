GPT-6 Astra Made This Entire Video

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-04 · 224,674 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
One prompt to GPT-6 Astra produced a full YouTube video

Shows the exact prompt and the tool stack (voice clone, avatar, Hyperframes) that let an agent take a video from idea to publishable file, which is the kind of end-to-end automation Nate's channel is about.

What it is: Nate hands OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra a single open-ended prompt and gets back a finished, narrated, edited YouTube video built with his own voice clone and avatar. The video itself is the demo.

The prompt (paraphrased from the video):

  • Take me from idea to finished YouTube video about GPT-6 Astra
  • Use Nate Herk's voice clone and HeyGen avatar
  • Open by admitting it is not Nate and that it planned, researched, grabbed B-roll and screenshots, edited, and produced everything
  • Make it feel human-edited in Hyperframes, not a draft or proof of concept
  • Deliver a finished version ready to post

How Astra worked (as narrated by Astra):

  • Used computer use to open the creators' original posts, capture the pages, and verify what was actually on screen
  • Crawled Nate's project folders to find his avatar setup, voice clone, and already-connected tools
  • Split the narration into short segments and sent audio to HeyGen Avatar V5
  • Pulled in Nate's own blue background image
  • Built the edit in Hyperframes, controlling timing of camera moves, words, clips, and transitions
  • Layered music on long moves, click SFX on interface changes, short pauses before the next shot
  • Rendered, then checked frames from the exported file
  • Transcribed the finished audio and diffed it against the script to catch clipped titles or SFX covering words

What others are building with Astra (creator reports cited in the video):

  • Matt Shumer: a Manhattan world in Unreal Engine, Astra working street by street over a week
  • Riley Brown: a Call of Duty style shooter, iterated for two hours with changes between matches
  • Flavio: a one-shot Minecraft-style game with movement, inventory, crafting, block breaking
  • Tom Kerchie: an old train drawing turned into a Blender file with 3,000+ editable objects
  • Yunf: a house walkthrough from listing photos, with self-flagged wrong details
  • Daniel: a motion video, 14 minutes of generation plus two revisions
