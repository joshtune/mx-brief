What it is: Part one of a series where Chuck rebuilds his editor Mike's entire home network, starting by throwing out the ISP-issued router and replacing it with a mini PC running OPNsense.

The AI angle (your focus):

  • The setup walkthrough is manual, but Chuck explicitly refuses to hand-configure the advanced stuff — he gives it to an AI agent and tries to let the firewall manage itself
  • Stated goal: "so Mike never has to text me at 3AM" — AI as tier-1 support for someone else's infrastructure, not as a toy
  • First agent task: block an entire country at the firewall level via geo-IP rules
  • He admits on camera he has no idea if it'll work — this is a live experiment, not a polished demo
  • Pattern worth stealing: human does the bootstrap, agent owns the ongoing config surface

Why replace the ISP router:

  • FCC is banning future home routers after Chinese state actors were found living inside consumer firmware
  • Existing routers are grandfathered in — legal, but the vulnerability didn't go away
  • Mass-market "goblin gateways" ship with known CVEs that expose LAN devices (cameras included)
  • OPNsense gives you whole-house ad blocking, a real stateful firewall, free and open source, under your control

Hardware:

  • Chuck uses a mini PC he bought in Japan — but any x86 box works: spare laptop, VM, old appliance
  • Two Ethernet ports minimum — one WAN, one LAN. One NIC? A USB Ethernet adapter is fine
  • Specs scale with network size and which features you enable

The trap most people miss:

  • OPNsense is *only* a router + firewall — no switch, no wireless
  • Your ISP box bundled all three; replacing it means buying two more devices
  • You need a network switch (managed recommended — unlocks VLANs so IoT junk lives on its own segment)
  • You need a wireless access point — covered in part two
  • To just test it today: plug a laptop straight into the LAN port, no extra gear required

OPNsense vs pfSense:

  • Chuck used pfSense in his last router video, switched to OPNsense here
  • pfSense closed-sourced their best build in 2021 and put it behind a paywall
  • OPNsense is 100% open source
