The Most Profitable AI Business You've Never Heard Of

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-27 · 30,299 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Get paid to wire AI into email lists and communities

It's a low-barrier side hustle: use Claude plus a tool's MCP connector to do setup work for business owners with email lists, and bill them monthly.

What it is: A service side hustle where you use an AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok) connected to beehiiv via MCP to build email-list and paid-community setups for yourself or for business owners, then charge a monthly fee. Note: the transcript is truncated at the connector-setup step, so the later demos aren't covered.

How it works:

  • The pitch: Chris says a business owner would pay ~$1,000/month if you help generate ~$12,000/month. Find 10 clients and that's ~$10K/month for "using AI to do stuff."
  • Two entry paths: (1) you already have an email list, or (2) you know someone who has one. About 10% of adults are business owners, so most people know someone with a list. Lists can be newsletters, small business lists, local, niche, church, real estate, friends and family.
  • The gap: Most lists never turn into revenue because they're not used well. Almost no list owners have connected their email list to a community, and Chris thinks it will be 5-10 years before they know it exists.
  • The product: beehiiv Communities lets you upload any email list and attach a community room. Readers can find each other and discuss the content, so a quick read becomes a place people return to.
  • His example: ~273,000 newsletter subscribers and a paid community that were disconnected (two products, two logins). He connects them by talking to Claude instead of clicking through dashboards.
  • No expertise required: Chris had never opened beehiiv Communities before recording. His point is that if he can figure it out on camera, you can figure it out for a client.
  • MCP setup steps:
  • Google "beehiiv MCP URL" and copy the URL (he says it'll be in the description).
  • In your AI, go to your name, then Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  • Name it (e.g. "Beehiiv Communities") and paste the URL.
  • Approve access, then ask the AI to log in and do the setup for you.
  • Chris's framing of MCP: "use AI inside of another software tool." It needs a URL to connect.
  • Billing angle: "Every click I make, you're going to be able to bill for."
