He Told 5 AIs: Make Money or Get Deleted

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-19 · 20,433 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Five AI models compete to grow real money.

A cheap, replicable experiment you could run yourself as a content-driven side hustle or to stress-test which AI is actually worth paying for.

What it is: Chris hands real money to five AI models — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Perplexity — and pits them against each other in a winner-take-all money-making contest.

How it works:

  • Each model gets its own pot of real cash and the same mandate: grow the money.
  • The stakes are framed as survival — win or Chris cancels that model's paid subscription ("make money or get deleted").
  • Only one winner is allowed, so every model is told it's racing the other four.
  • Results so far: Claude more than doubled the money and is reported "30x" / "killing it," while the rest scramble to catch up.
  • Watch for emergent behavior — Gemini, once it started losing, "basically became a gambler," chasing losses like a degenerate trader.

Tools & links:

  • Claude — the runaway leader in this run
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's model, competitor
  • Gemini — Google's model; turned to gambling when behind
  • Grok — xAI's model, competitor
  • Perplexity — competitor

Why it matters for you: The format itself is the side hustle — a repeatable, low-cost "AI vs AI money challenge" is prime shiny-object content, and running it teaches you which model to actually bet on for real automation.
