Building a personal AI operating system on GPT-6 Astra + Codex.

It's a concrete blueprint for wiring your business knowledge, tools, and daily rhythms into one AI-driven workspace, with free skills to bootstrap or audit your own build.

What it is: Nate turns GPT-6 Astra (driven through Codex) into an "AIOS" — a second brain plus operating dashboard that knows his whole business and can act inside his tools.

How it works:

  • Knowledge graph, not a dump. His "Herk Brain" is a graph of nodes with explicit relationships: business wiki (AIS coaching, corporate structure, AIS Plus), meetings, video knowledge (every YouTube video he's made), Claude memory, Codex memory, projects, skills, and agents.
  • Staleness detection. Each wiki node shows how old it is and flags specific facts (e.g. outdated numbers) that need refreshing, plus every cross-link into other business areas.
  • The Four C's framework:
  • *Context* — slow-changing facts: background, goals, quarterly/yearly priorities, business model, customer avatar, pain points. (Second-brain half.)
  • *Connections* — live tools whose data changes daily: email, Slack, ClickUp, project management, financials. The AI reaches for these just-in-time. (Second-brain half.)
  • *Capabilities* — what the OS can actually do (skills, agents). (OS half.)
  • *Cadence* — recurring rhythms and routines. (OS half.)
  • OS dashboard, built with GPT-6 Astra: calendar (fully synced), live stats (YouTube subs, AIS members, AIS Plus members), brain search/management, unified comms (Slack + ClickUp + email, reply from inside), upcoming and past meetings with AI summaries, "community pulse" (top YouTube comments, top AIS Plus posts, what people are stuck on), and "industry pulse" from YouTube and X.
  • Proof of context depth: He asked Codex to "make me a sizzle reel for AI Automation Society" with no further spec. It pulled context from the brain, navigated to his Skool community on its own, and took its own screenshots to build it.
  • Free skills giveaway: Skills to set up the AIOS structure from scratch, audit an existing AIOS, and level it up. (Exact link not in the transcript excerpt — check the video description / Skool community.)
