What it is: A short promo for OpenArt's new "Director" — a conversational tool for generating video content by describing it in chat instead of stitching clips manually.

How it works:

  • Describe story, characters, visual style, pacing, and voice performances in a chat interface
  • Refine iteratively through conversation rather than prompting clips one at a time
  • Generates up to 5 minutes of continuous video
  • Persists project state so characters, voices, and story stay consistent across generations
  • Timeline editor for shot-level edits; Director rebuilds surrounding shots around your changes
