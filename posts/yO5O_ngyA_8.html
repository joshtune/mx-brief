I Tried The “Easiest” Ways To Make Money With AI

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-11 · 337,594 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Five AI bots tested to turn $100 into $1,000

The instructional secret is a 'spawn bot' that builds role-specific bots, plus a handoff chain with a profit-math kill switch. Both are patterns you can copy for a hands-off side hustle.

What it is: Codie gives herself $100, 10 days and three rules (no hands-on work after setup, max $100 spend, only real cash or real savings counts) to see if Grok Bot agents can 10x her money across five bot-run hustles.

How it works:

  • The secret: a 'spawn bot'. It interviews you about the goal, then creates the other bots. You describe the job in plain English, such as 'Wolfie scans Reddit, news and X for trade ideas and executes via Robinhood', and it wires up the brief.
  • Bot 1, Wolfie (trading): Scans Reddit, news and X, then trades through a Robinhood account. It started at a loss. Codie says she has no conviction in it and tells viewers not to try it at home.
  • Bot 2, the flipping crew (Alvin, Simon, Theodore): A three-bot pipeline that finds free marketplace listings and flips them.
  • Alvin (sourcing): Finds free listings that could resell for profit.
  • Theodore (logistics): Gets a courier quote for pickup and books the pickup if the numbers work.
  • Simon (selling): Relists the item at a competitive price and handles buyer inquiries.
  • The kill switch: If delivery cost exceeds expected profit, the item dies automatically. The target is about $10 profit per item, and anything that clears the math gets approved.
  • Why it works now: Grok Bot acts as an operator. It clicks, scrolls, browses and uses software you already have, so it needs far less human interaction than the earlier AI she tried.
  • Early results: The bots passed on a sectional (too big) and a tiny fridge (risk of it being broken). One item was already sold, and another find turned out to be a bowling ball. Codie admits she's 'slightly concerned'.
  • Caveat: The transcript cuts off early. The final profit numbers and bots 3–5 weren't available, so the 10x claim is unverified here.

Tools & links:

  • Grok Bot — agent platform that logs into apps, sends follow-ups and updates spreadsheets (no direct URL given in the transcript)
  • Robinhood — brokerage account the trading bot executes through

Why it matters for you: The reusable lesson is to split a side hustle into single-job bots with a hard profit gate between them, so the system rejects bad deals before you spend time or money. Treat the trading bot as entertainment, and don't judge the flipping model until you see the real margins.
