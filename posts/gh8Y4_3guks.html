SBF's stolen-money portfolio would be worth $114B today.

A brutal breakdown of the specific bets Sam Bankman-Fried made with stolen customer funds — and how absurdly well they'd have paid off.

What it is: Shaan & Sam tally up Sam Bankman-Fried's investment portfolio — funded by illegally-used FTX customer deposits — and note it would be worth ~$114B today.

The portfolio (SBF's picks):

  • Anthropic — the crown jewel, stake would be worth ~$80B today
  • SpaceX — position that would have grown to ~$15B
  • Solana (SOL) — ~$5B stake
  • Robinhood (HOOD) — ~$5B
  • Cursor (Anysphere) — ~$3B+ stake in the AI coding startup
  • Total: ~$114B

The catch:

  • Every dollar came from his "slush fund" — customer funds he was illegally redirecting into venture bets
  • On FTX's bankruptcy/liquidation, the estate liquidated these positions to repay creditors — so the estate, not SBF, captured (a fraction of) the upside
  • Selling early during the 2022–23 wind-down means creditors never saw the full $114B; those valuations are hypothetical mark-to-today figures
  • SBF is serving a 25-year federal sentence for fraud

The real lesson: Genuine pattern-matching on winners (Anthropic, SpaceX, Solana, Cursor pre-hype) means nothing if the capital is stolen — the returns accrue to the bankruptcy estate, and you go to prison.

Why it matters for you: A concrete, name-by-name look at which frontier AI/space/crypto bets a sharp allocator was making in ~2021-22 — Anthropic and Cursor especially read as prescient calls on where value would concentrate.
