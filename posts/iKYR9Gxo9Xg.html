Hiring is one of the most important things an owner can do.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-20 · 26,024 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Forget inspiring people. Align their incentives.

If your side hustle will ever involve a contractor, VA, or first hire, this is the cheapest lesson in getting them to care about outcomes without a pep talk.

What it is: A short rant on why vision speeches fail and incentive alignment works.

How it works:

  • The trap: Owners announce a big vision ("billion-dollar business!") and expect the team to get fired up. Employees hear "more work, none of the upside."
  • The secret: Translate the vision into specific, measurable outcomes that pay the employee directly.
  • Her example: On a construction site, hit on-time delivery, a high NPS-style customer score (she says "MPS"), and stay on budget, and everyone on the team gets a bonus.
  • Why sales works: Salespeople always know if they're winning (did you close?), and they get paid for it. Copy that clarity for every other role.
  • The rule: Want everyone to work hard? Give them a reason to. "Forget about inspiration. Obsess on incentive alignment."

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Steps to apply it:

  • Pick 2-3 outcomes that actually move your business (speed, quality, cost).
  • Make each one measurable and visible to the person doing the work.
  • Attach a payout (bonus, rev share, per-task kicker) that triggers when they hit it.
  • Skip the vision speech. Show them what's in it for them.

Why it matters for you: On a side hustle you have no time to manage people, so pay structures that reward the results you want let contractors self-manage while you stay at your day job.
