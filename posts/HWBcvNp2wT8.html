Core ideas she sells:

  • Warmth + competence = 82% of first impressions. Warmth comes first and acts as 'lubricant' for your smarts.
  • Competence without warmth breeds suspicion, not just dislike. Smart people over-index on data and numbers, so negotiations and closes get harder.
  • Warmth is not bubbly extroversion. It shows up as humor, sarcasm, vulnerability, and visible mistakes or stumbling.
  • Her examples: Elon Musk's warmth is unpolished vulnerability. Zuckerberg's is a flat, 'everyday guy with a good idea' affect.
  • Charisma is learned. You can often spot fake charisma, but you can't tell learned from natural until you see someone's 'flavor' of humor, gestures and ideas.
  • Rewrite your 'what do you do' answer. Replace the boring job title with a story-shaped line like hers.
