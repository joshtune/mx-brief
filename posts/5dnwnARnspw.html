When Bill Clinton knew he was going to be president

My First Million · 2026-09-25 · 8,049 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Clinton beat Bush Sr. with body language, not words.

A quick lesson in nonverbal communication as competitive advantage, not a business breakdown — skip if you came for money-making tactics.

What it is: A short anecdote about the 1992 Bush vs. Clinton debate, used to illustrate how nonverbal communication can outweigh substance.

How it works:

  • During a town-hall debate, a woman asks a question about being unable to afford groceries.
  • George H.W. Bush checks his watch while she's talking.
  • Bill Clinton walks toward her, asks her name and background, and responds with visible empathy.
  • A decade later, researchers ran two tests: one group watched the video with no audio, another only heard the audio.
  • The video-only group could accurately predict the election outcome from body language alone; the audio-only group could not.
