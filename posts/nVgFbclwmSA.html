The Easiest Way to Make Money From Home

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-31 · 19,809 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Amazon Influencer Program: film phone reviews, earn commissions.

It's a side hustle with near-zero startup cost and no inventory — you film short product review videos on your phone, upload them to Amazon product pages, and collect commissions when viewers buy.

What it is: The Amazon Influencer Program — you record short video reviews of products, upload them to Amazon listings, and earn a commission every time someone watches your video and buys.

How it works:

  • Get accepted into the Amazon Influencer Program, then film reviews of products with your phone and upload them to the matching product page
  • Someone watches your video on the listing → buys → you get a net commission straight to your bank. No inventory, no overhead, no fees
  • Product selection is the real strategy — a tumbler with 1,000 existing review videos will get you buried; you need to find under-served listings with buyer intent
  • Video quality is the second lever: longer videos keep shoppers on Amazon longer, watch time goes up, and Amazon's algorithm surfaces your video more
  • Face on camera is optional — POV/hands-only footage works. You can also script it (guest suggests ChatGPT: "here's product XYZ, write a script") and do a voiceover
  • Best-performing style is unscripted phone talking, like you're explaining the product to your mom or brother
  • Volume is the compounding engine: the guest cites ~700 videos → $25–30k/mo, another creator at ~1,100 videos → $40k/mo, and a mom of four in Idaho at $12k/mo part-time
  • Brands eventually start shipping you free product to review — the guest says they ran two free garage sales to clear the pile
  • Guest's own numbers: $10k+/mo on 4–5 hours per week. His framing: "you only fail if you don't upload"
  • The guest runs a tool that automates product research and finds winning listings to review — but he claims the video gives you enough to do it manually

Why it matters for you: This is about the lowest-friction side hustle on the board — no code, no inventory, no audience required — and the constraints are pure volume and product selection, which is exactly the kind of thing an engineer can systematize (scrape listings, score them by video-count-vs-sales-rank, batch-film the winners).
