The Most Useful AI Tool You’ve Never Heard Of

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-15 · 27,597 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Chris interviews the founder of Violoop, a screen-watching AI agent.

If you're weighing an AI-agent side hustle, this shows the pitch and positioning behind an early-stage screen-watching AI product before it's widely known.

What it is: Violoop is an AI hardware/software product that watches your screen continuously (like a live video feed, not just screenshots) and acts as a proactive "AI coworker" — working on tasks in the background before you even prompt it.

How it works:

  • Captures your screen as a continuous video stream (not periodic screenshots) to understand context in real time
  • Runs agents in the background that keep working while you're away — described as turning an 8-hour workday into 16-30 hours of output
  • Surfaces suggestions/actions at the moment it judges relevant, rather than waiting to be asked
  • Privacy angle: raw screen video is processed locally and never uploaded to the cloud
  • Founder frames the evolution as: blog-era AI (text) → Instagram-era AI (screenshots + captions) → Violoop-era AI (live video understanding)

Tools & links:

  • Violoop on Kickstarter — the crowdfunding campaign for the hardware/software
  • Violoop Discord — community/support
  • Violoop Facebook Community — community group

Why it matters for you: This is an early Kickstarter-stage product, not a proven business model yet — worth watching as a signal of where "AI employee" tooling is heading, but the transcript cuts off before Chris gets to the actual low-budget side-hustle angle he was building toward.
