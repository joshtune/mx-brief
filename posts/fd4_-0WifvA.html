Separate your creator brain from your editor brain

It's a short creative-process hack, not a business breakdown, so there's no money-making playbook here and you can skip it for your focus.

What it is: A short clip on Jerry Seinfeld's "two minds of creativity" framework, applied to brainstorming and creating in business.

How it works:

  • Mind 1, the child: Scribble first and don't judge. Writer's block comes from fear of looking bad, so people put nothing down to protect themselves.
  • Treat early drafts kindly: Treat the rough draft like a kid's crayon drawing worth hanging on the fridge. Good comes later.
  • Mind 2, the ruthless editor: Come back about 4 hours later and cut hard. Trim the fat, drop extra words and syllables, and rearrange.
  • Need both: All free play and no taste produces nothing usable. Bringing judgment into the brainstorm early ruins the brainstorm.
  • Hats as a trigger: Their team made two physical hats, "Rick mode" (Rick Rubin style, just messing around) and "prick mode" (the editor). Wearing one signals which mind is in charge.
  • Cut off: The transcript ends mid-sentence, just as the speaker starts listing other great people who work this way. Treat the rest as unknown.
