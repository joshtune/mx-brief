The habits (transcript only fully covers the first two before cutting off):

  • 1. Give it the "why" (any model): Provide intent and context, not just the task. Instead of "Write me an email to a client about the delay," frame the bigger task, who it's for, and what they need — Fable connects the task to the right info instead of guessing. Pairs well with a "second brain"/AIOS setup that points it at the right context files.
  • 2. Negative prompt (any model): Explicitly tell it what NOT to do. Since the model predicts the next likely token, it improvises; naming the off-limits behavior reins that in.
