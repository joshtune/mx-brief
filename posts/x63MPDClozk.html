AI Makes Starting a Business Way Too Easy

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-11-19 · 21,655 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Using an AI agent to find underserved local businesses

If you want a side hustle but don't know what to start, this shows a one-prompt method for finding markets with high demand and low supply, then spinning up the website and pitch deck around it.

What it is: Chris uses the Genspark AI agent to run a competition-gap analysis that used to take him hours of manual spreadsheet work, then uses it to pick a business to start.

The idea (supply/demand gap hunting):

  • Pick a benchmark city that's demographically similar to yours (he uses Houston as the benchmark for Dallas).
  • Count businesses per capita in a category in the benchmark city, e.g. 1 grill-cleaning business per 100,000 people.
  • Compare your city's count. If Dallas has 0.3 per 100,000, there's more demand than supply, which makes it a good launch candidate.
  • Repeat across about 20 trending home service categories and rank the gaps.
  • Origin story: he did this by hand for a friend's rehab clinic, spending about 2 hours scraping and dividing before landing on Allen, Texas as the lowest-competition location.

How it's carried out:

  • Sign up at Genspark, which bundles multiple AI models (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok) into one roughly $20/month subscription.
  • Start a Super Agent task. It's agentic, so it picks the right model per subtask and runs tools on its own.
  • Prompt: your city, the benchmark city, the per-capita comparison, 20 trending home service businesses, and output as a clean spreadsheet or Google Sheet.
  • Voice mode can dictate a longer prompt.
  • The agent looks up population numbers, finds trending growing industries, and does the math. He times it, and the first steps take about 15 seconds.
  • The agent pins results on Google Maps and builds the spreadsheet.
  • Teaser results from the video's intro: a spreadsheet validating his thesis, startup cost ranges and expected startup cost, three professional images usable in ads, and a finished pitch deck, ad creative and live website. The transcript cuts off before the final picks.

The side-hustle angle:

  • You don't have to run the underserved business itself. If plumbing or grill cleaning is under-supplied, you can start a business that *sells to* those operators, such as leads, software or supplies.
  • Human plus AI: Chris adds his own judgment on top of the AI output. He has launched 80-something businesses over 18 years and wants you to filter the AI list through experience, not just ask a chatbot.
