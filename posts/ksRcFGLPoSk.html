Building a business from scratch using AI tools.

It hands you a repeatable, AI-powered workflow for validating and launching a side hustle without guessing what to sell.

What it is: A live walkthrough of building a business from zero using a curated stack of AI tools, with reusable frameworks at each step.

How it works:

  • Start with demand, not ideas: Find what people already want to buy before falling in love with a product.
  • The RRT test (Resist, Raise, Technology): A 5-minute filter for any idea — (1) Does it resist a recession? (2) Can you raise prices without losing customers? (3) Can tech improve margins/output? Yes to all three = worth building.
  • Worked example: Hands-free dog leash — exploding search volume, ~30K monthly sales, pet spending tops $100B/yr and rises every year (recession-proof), premium collars prove pricing power, and AI automates the selling.
  • Define an 'avatar': Not a vague 'dog owner' but one hyper-specific buyer who thinks 'this was made for me.' Narrower = more money.
  • Three-tool research stack with division of labor:
  • Perplexity → sourced, cited real-world data (avoids hallucinations)
  • ChatGPT → fast first draft, Swiss Army knife for structuring
  • Claude → deep reasoning, reconciles Perplexity data vs. ChatGPT's avatar into one databacked buyer profile; also best for agents

Tools & links:

  • Exploding Topics — AI trend spotter scanning search, social, and retail for hot products
  • Perplexity — cited research, source data
  • ChatGPT — fast drafting and structuring
  • Claude — nuanced reasoning, synthesis, agents
  • BuizScout Radar (promo via Main Street Millionaire Live ticket — https://contrarianthinking.biz/aitools)

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret isn't the tools — it's the RRT filter plus the Perplexity→ChatGPT→Claude pipeline, a copy-paste process for validating demand and nailing your buyer before you spend a dollar on a side hustle.
