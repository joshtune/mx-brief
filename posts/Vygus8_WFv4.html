I Can Predict Your Bank Account With This One Number

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-22 · 38,114 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
How fast you decide reveals your earning power.

If you're running a side hustle, your speed of decision-making is the cheapest leverage you have over your time and your customers'.

What it is: A rant-style mental model: Codie claims decision speed (how fast you order in line) correlates directly with how much money you make.

How it works:

  • The 'secret' is a proxy metric — she watches a woman agonize over a latte syrup for ~10 minutes and reads it as three failures: (1) no respect for her own time, (2) no spatial/situational awareness, (3) no awareness of the cost she imposes on the business.
  • The instructional takeaway: low-stakes decisions (syrup, pastry) should be made fast and pre-committed, so your mental bandwidth is reserved for high-stakes ones. Treat reversible, trivial choices as instant defaults.
  • Indecision has a compounding externality — multiply one slow customer across every customer and the business owner loses real throughput/revenue. As an operator, decision drag is a hidden tax on your margins.
  • It's framed as thought-leadership, not a step-by-step — there's no system here, just a heuristic to self-audit and apply to anyone you hire.
