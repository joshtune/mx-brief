The Secret To A Winning Environment

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-18 · 7,825 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Your environment, not willpower, drives your effort.

If your side hustle keeps stalling, the fix may be swapping the people around you for ones who out-grind you.

What it is: A short pep-talk on why proximity to high-effort people beats motivation and discipline.

How it works:

  • Cites a study: people in *competitive* group workouts (F45-style intensity) showed up 90% more than people in supportive/kind/solo workouts.
  • The takeaway: effort is contagious. Being near people grinding hard pulls you forward; being near coasters drags you down.
  • The reframe: stop optimizing for comfort (people who say nice things, won't push you). Optimize for a room where you're the least driven person there.
  • Core claim: "proximity is contagious" — you rise to the level of effort you're surrounded by, passively, just by watching others do more.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or resources named in the transcript — this is a pure mindset clip.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle dies in isolation; deliberately placing yourself among harder-working operators (masterminds, founder communities, competitive peer groups) is a cheap, passive multiplier on your own output.
