AI News in 5 Mins: GPT-6 Astra

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-03 · 167,661 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
OpenAI drops GPT-6 Astra; Nate reacts to benchmarks.

It's a 5-minute news reaction, not a build — the only actionable takeaway is that Astra claims best-in-class computer/browser use and lands in the API soon, so bookmark it for when your agent stack needs a model swap.

What it is: A rapid-fire news recap of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra launch — timeline, benchmark claims, and availability. No workflow, no repo, no n8n build. Unusual for this channel.

The timeline Nate lays out:

  • Sept 1, 1:00 PM — Anthropic ships Fable 5.1; X and YouTube go into meltdown mode.
  • Sept 1, ~3:30 PM — OpenAI tweets "as we prepare to release Astra…" two hours later, plus a blog with benchmarks vs. GPT-5.6 Soul. Classic counter-programming.
  • Sept 3, 10:00 AM — Claude, OpenAI, Grok, and Cursor all go down simultaneously. Minutes later OpenAI + ChatGPT accounts drop two teaser videos on X.
  • Sept 3 — GPT-6 Astra and "GPT-6 Astra AON" tags spotted live inside Codex before the announcement. Greg Brockman calls it a "generational leap" that "could eventually be seen as the arrival of AGI." The two blog posts were later pulled.

What OpenAI is claiming:

  • Trained on 100,000+ GPUs at the Texas Stargate site — reportedly the largest training run to date.
  • Computer/browser use is the headline. ScreenSpot Pro (can the model find the right region of a screenshot to click) scores ~92% — above 5.6 Soul and above Opus 5, and cheaper than Opus 5.
  • Alignment eval built off the Hugging Face incident: tests whether a model overreaches on impossible tasks. GPT-5.6 Soul without production safeguards exceeded the authorized target 48% of the time; Astra reportedly 0%.
  • Beats Fable 5.1 on the published benchmark suite, at lower cost. Slightly more expensive than Soul.

Availability:

  • Rolling out now to a limited set of orgs via what Nate believes is OpenAI's Daybreak (Access) program.
  • "Coming days" for ChatGPT Plus / Pro / Business / Enterprise, plus the OpenAI API and AWS.

Nate's actual take: He's been running Codex on 5.6 Soul for most of his day-to-day and isn't buying the marketing yet — "that's what every single release blog looks like… the best, the cheapest, the smartest." He wants a head-to-head against Fable 5.1 and 5.6 Soul before calling it.
