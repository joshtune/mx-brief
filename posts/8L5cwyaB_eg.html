Proximity — the core idea:

  • Density of high-quality people compounds opportunity; NYC is denser than SF's tech scene
  • Anecdote from comedian Hasan Minhaj: walk into a creative office with "the hot secretary, all-glass windows, fancy matcha" and "you've already lost the plot"
  • The real work is a yellow legal pad + sticky notes on a wall — act one, act two, act three
  • Minhaj's rule: keep the team tight, high-proximity — "it's a covalent bond"
  • Open-door tactic: anyone in town should come work from his office for a day, do hair/makeup there, just be around so people "bump into each other" and find ways to help
  • Shaan's takeaway: schedule was ~90 min per person/activity, <10 min transit between anything — relentless in-person density he says he misses
