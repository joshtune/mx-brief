Try This Awesome AI Video Transition!

AI keyframe trick for seamless location-jump video transitions.

Pure video-editing content with zero relevance to building web apps — skip it.

What it is: A no-VFX editing trick using AI video keyframes to create seamless "teleport" transitions between two locations.

How it works:

  • Film your intro in location A, then re-film in location B wearing the same shirt and holding the same pose.
  • Grab two still frames: the last frame of clip A and the first frame of clip B.
  • Load both into an AI video tool as keyframes — frame A as start, frame B as end.
  • Prompt the transition (e.g. "the camera shoots up like a big jump transition and reveals the second location in one smooth motion. The man does not speak").
  • The "does not speak" line matters — models otherwise inject random gibberish speech.
  • Generate a few versions, pick the best, drop it between the real clips, hide the seam with a smooth cut or short crossfade.

Tools & links:

  • Runway — AI video generation with keyframe support
  • Kling — AI video model with start/end frame control
  • Leonardo — AI image/video tool supporting keyframes
