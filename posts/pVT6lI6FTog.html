A billionaire's five mindset rules for thinking bigger.

If your side hustle has plateaued, this names the mental ceiling—'small has infected your thinking'—that quietly caps how big you let it get.

What it is: Five mindset rules Codie got from a near-billionaire friend ("Bill") for thinking and building bigger.

How it works:

  • You're thinking too small — Bill asked if being "around small business for so long" had "infected" her thinking with small. That one question pushed her to build something bigger (referenced as "business biscout").
  • Find the billionaire to your millionaire — surround yourself with friends who see you as larger than you see yourself, and are genuinely invested in your success.
  • Buy stuff to give it away — a play on the law of reciprocity: money is a tool for generosity, not just accumulation.
  • Find the people advancing the world — surround yourself with winners; the company you keep sets your trajectory forward.
  • Keep a menagerie of friends — humans are multifaceted; different friends serve different roles. Someone losing in one area can still be a winner in their segment—don't write off the whole relationship.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret isn't tactics—it's that your environment and self-image cap your side hustle's ceiling before the market ever does; curate winners around you and stop pre-shrinking your own plans.
