Start This Business With No Experience

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-02 · 9,768 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Run a global-talent staffing agency for blue-collar businesses.

It's a near-zero-startup-cost side hustle you can run with a laptop and Wi-Fi, earning ~30% of a placed hire's first-year salary.

What it is: A recruiting/staffing agency that places overseas talent (Latin America, Philippines, Eastern Europe) with U.S. blue-collar small business owners for a placement fee.

How it works:

  • Source strong, cheap overseas workers (example: a full-time hire for $1,600/month) who earn 5x their local wage.
  • Place them with U.S. small business owners — 33M business owners = ~10% of Americans are potential customers.
  • Charge ~30% of the placed person's first-year salary as your fee (~$30K per contract in the examples).
  • Guest 'Alex' hit $100K in sales in his first 100 days, mostly profit, starting with ~$200.
  • Client acquisition is pure outbound hustle: text friends & family a 3-sentence pitch (how Alex made $10K in 45 days mowing lawns before this), be proactive, and 'fake it till you make it' (he literally faked a meeting to land a $30K/year client).
  • Positioned as AI-resilient: 'global talent + AI is the sauce' — after ChatGPT decimated his content-marketing agency, staffing became the pivot.

Tools & links:

  • Lovable — no-code/AI app builder cited as the entire 'tech stack' needed (plus Wi-Fi + a computer)
  • 32-page, 9,000+ word step-by-step business plan referenced in the video description (linked in the podcast show notes)

Why it matters for you: Textbook shiny-object side hustle — no capital, no experience, no code required; it's a sales-and-arbitrage play you could stand up on nights/weekends and validate with a few texts.
