I Tried to Make $10K/Month on Autopilot With AI

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-02 · 23,328 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Selling $5 five-minute consulting calls via AI-run email

It's a zero-cost side hustle blueprint: package what you already know into a $5 booking link, then let Claude handle the email outreach.

What it is: A side hustle where you sell 5-minute consulting calls for $5 (a $60/hour rate), promoted by emailing your network, with Claude running the email tooling through an MCP connector.

How it works:

  • The pitch: Sell your knowledge in 5-minute slots at $5 each. Chris's math is $1/min, $60/hr, about $120K/yr at 40 hrs/week with no costs. That's a ceiling, not a forecast; it assumes the calendar is always full.
  • The real play: The $5 call is a foot-in-the-door. The money is in upselling coaching packages, recurring work, higher-ticket offers, and AI audits.
  • Who can do it: Anyone who knows something people want. His examples are LEGO building, Pokémon card grading, and vacuuming. Demand matters more than credentials.
  • Step 1, connect Claude to Omnisend: In Claude go to Settings → Connectors → Custom connectors. Name it Omnisend and use the URL `mcp.omnisend.com/mcp`. Log in to Omnisend and grant read and write permissions.
  • Step 2, randomize your list: In Google Sheets, add `=RAND()` next to your contacts and sort by that column. Take a small random slice, because he's only emailing a limited number of people. Export it as CSV.
  • Step 3, load contacts via Claude: Upload the CSV to Claude and ask it to add the emails to an Omnisend segment. Approve the tool permissions as prompted.
  • Step 4, build the booking page: Create a 5-minute event in TidyCal. Set the price to $5 through the Stripe integration, allow no coupons, and require payment to book. Sync it to your calendar and restrict availability. He starts at 1pm and only offers certain weekdays.
  • Step 5, send the email: Claude is meant to write and send the campaign through Omnisend to the segment. The transcript cuts off before this part, so the actual results aren't covered.

Tools & links:

  • Omnisend — email marketing tool (Mailchimp alternative). Code KOERNER gets 30% off paid plans for 3 months. Sponsor link.
  • Omnisend MCP — the connector URL that lets Claude manage segments and campaigns
  • TidyCal — calendar booking with Stripe payments
  • Stripe — takes the $5 payments
  • Claude — the AI agent driving the workflow
  • Finding newsletter subscribers playlist — Chris's follow-up for building an audience

Caveats:

  • The video is partly a sponsored Omnisend demo.
  • He emails his existing network, which is warm. A cold list will convert far worse.
  • No results are shown in the portion provided, so the $10K/month claim is untested.
  • The $5 price mostly filters for curiosity. Whether upsells convert is the real question.

Why it matters for you: It's a cheap, fast test of a consulting side hustle, needing only a booking link, a small list, and an AI agent to handle the email setup. Whether it earns money depends on your network and your upsell.
