For every Bill Gates, there are thousands who followed the same playbook and went broke

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-11 · 17,586 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Survivorship bias, and why your business shouldn't own you.

Before you pour nights and weekends into a side hustle, learn the one question that keeps it from becoming a jail.

What it is: A short rant against hustle-culture survivorship bias, ending with one practical test for whether your business is healthy.

How it works:

  • The secret: Ask daily, "What can I not do today so I don't own this thing forever?" If the answer is "nothing," you are the single point of failure.
  • Survivorship bias: Success stories (Gates, Goggins, Musk) are the survivors. The thousands who followed the same playbook and went broke don't have podcasts.
  • Entrepreneurship is a casino: The winners get the spotlight. The 500 who walked out broke stay invisible.
  • Hours aren't the problem: 80-hour weeks are fine when you choose them. The trouble starts when the business collapses the moment you step away.
  • Hero vs. heroin: Playing the hero who holds it all together is an addiction, and a way of lying to yourself.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle that only runs while you work it is a second job with worse pay, so design it to run without you from day one: document processes, delegate, automate.
