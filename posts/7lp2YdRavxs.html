Lesson: attention compounds faster than money.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-17 · 4,621 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Use content to subsidize a business and revenue follows.

It shows how a side hustle can win by building an audience and story first, instead of competing on capital you don't have.

What it is: A breakdown of the "content-subsidized model" using Ryan Reynolds & Rob McElhenney's Wrexham AFC turnaround.

How it works:

  • Buy or build something undervalued with an existing story (Wrexham: 5th-tier club, 150 years of history, nearly broke).
  • Make the business the *product*, and content the *marketing engine* — the "Welcome to Wrexham" docuseries was the growth lever, not ad spend.
  • Convert the global audience that falls in love with the story into a new revenue stream.
  • Result: sponsorship income jumped from $1.9M to $13.2M in a single year (~7x).
  • The bet was on connection and attention, not on the sport itself — and neither founder was even a football fan.

The instructional secret: Don't buy what's trendy. Buy (or build) something with a latent story, then manufacture attention around it. Attention compounds faster than money because a viral story keeps paying without proportional reinvestment.
