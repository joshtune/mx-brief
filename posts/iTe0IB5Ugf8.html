Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns amid scandal

It's pure industry drama with zero build-your-app content, so skip it unless you want context on Nvidia's leadership shake-up and its possible effect on AI compute supply.

What it is: A news recap of Jensen Huang's sudden resignation as Nvidia CEO after reports of an affair and alleged ties to a suspected Chinese spy.

How it works:

  • Background: The US and Singapore investigated MegaSpeed over Nvidia chips possibly getting around the China export ban. One of its executives, Alice Huang (no relation to Jensen), allegedly had a close relationship with him.
  • Oct 29: NYT reported the relationship. Nvidia called it part of being a CEO.
  • A week later: A House committee chair requested a closed-door briefing. NYT reported a secret government memo calling her a spy for China's Ministry of State Security.
  • Sunday: Jensen sent a company letter admitting infidelity. The board opened an independent investigation into the national security concerns.
  • The Information: Companies tied to her and her family allegedly helped move at least $7.5B of Nvidia chips to Chinese companies.
  • Day before resignation: NYT reported a second affair, with a VP who reported directly to him. This is unconfirmed. WSJ said the board was preparing to push him out.
  • Today: Nvidia announced his resignation, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. The stock dropped more than 13% at one point.
  • Caveats: The spy allegations are unproven. China calls them a smear. The board says it has no reason to believe any secrets got out.
