Google Cooked With This New Model!

Matt Wolfe · 2026-11-22 · 23,225 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Gemini 4 Neon surprises as a cheap, strong coding model

It's a two-minute heads-up that a cheaper model may now beat pricier ones for web app coding agents, but it's light on specifics, so treat it as a prompt to test it yourself.

What it is: Google's Gemini 4 Neon is a midsize model that's reportedly outscoring Google's own larger model and challenging Anthropic and OpenAI on coding.

How it works:

  • Leaderboard: Third overall on Artificial Analysis, right behind the top Anthropic and OpenAI models.
  • Coding agents: Ranked #1 on Artificial Analysis's coding agent test at about half the cost of Claude (Matt's claim, with no numbers shown).
  • Real-world anecdote: Theo had OpenAI's models spend over a week speeding up a project, and it got slower. Neon did the job in about 10 hours.
  • Matt's test: Same prompt to Neon and both new OpenAI models: "Build me a website that'll truly impress me."
  • Both OpenAI models produced nearly identical deep-sea jellyfish sites.
  • Neon built a watchmaker site where you inspect the gears through a magnifying glass. Matt liked it a lot.
  • Weak spots: Theo and Matthew Berman both say it's a step behind on 3D and games.
  • Cadence: It's Google's fourth Gemini 4 model in about 7 weeks, named after elements.
  • Access: It's already in the Gemini app for paying users.

Tools & links:

  • Artificial Analysis — the independent leaderboard the rankings come from
  • FutureTools — Matt's AI tools directory and newsletter

Caveats:

  • It's a news recap, not a tutorial. There's no API, pricing or benchmark detail, and no framework-specific web dev testing.
  • Evidence is mostly anecdotes (Theo's speedup, one website prompt) plus one leaderboard result.
  • Matt's "best model on the planet" quote comes from Matthew Berman, so it's hype from a third party.

Why it matters for you: If you run coding agents on web apps, benchmark Neon against your current model on one real task, since the pitch is near-top quality at roughly half the cost. Skip it for 3D or game work.
