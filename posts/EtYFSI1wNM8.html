Stop monetizing your looks; sell your skills.

If you're building a side hustle, this is a blunt reminder that durable income comes from skills and trust, not from selling your appearance.

What it is: A short, opinionated rant from Codie Sanchez arguing that using your body or looks as your main money-maker is a weak, short-lived strategy. Not a how-to, it's a mindset take.

How it works:

  • The instructional secret: Lead with your skills, not your looks. Looks are "the least interesting thing about you," so build your offer around expertise people will pay for.
  • Durability argument: Appearance-based income fades with age. Skill-based income compounds.
  • Positioning argument: Selling yourself as a visual product signals you don't believe your skills are enough. Audiences and buyers pick up on that.
  • Her framing: "Just because you can doesn't mean you should." Being able to monetize attention isn't the same as building a business.
  • Caveat: It's opinion with no data, frameworks, or steps. It's also pointed at women in particular, so take the tone with a grain of salt.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle, the lesson is to productize what you know (a service, a niche skill, a local business) rather than leaning on your image. That's an asset you can still sell, scale, and eventually hand off when you're 60.
