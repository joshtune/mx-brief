I Let AI Work for Me for 6 Weeks. Here’s What Actually Worked

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-21 · 115,160 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Six AI-agent side hustles, tested with his own money

If you want a side hustle where an AI agent does the tedious work and you collect the margin, Chris shows the first concrete example with the exact workflow.

What it is: Chris spent 6 weeks letting AI agents act for him (buying, negotiating, building sites, filling out forms) and pitches six businesses from what worked. Most cost $20/month or less.

How it works (the one example the transcript covers: pool quotes):

  • The problem: Getting 10 contractor quotes is brain damage. Two weeks of phone calls and site visits with 4 pool companies produced zero quotes, not even a ballpark. Quote spread on a pool can be $50-100k.
  • The setup: One prompt to Muse (Meta's new agent): find every pool builder in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, then go to each site and fill out the contact form. If there's no form, get their email and email them.
  • The inputs: Address, the pool plans (Chris had Claude draft them), the 10-year-old property survey, and a spec: travertine decking, 35x18 ft, 3-7 ft deep, a 23-inch grade drop.
  • Inbox access: He connected his email. The agent found the old survey in his inbox on its own, with and without the house on it, and handled replies.
  • The message: Pre-answers every objection ("I know you want to come out, I own a business too, I'm not ready for that yet") so contractors quote without a site visit.
  • The result: Quotes landed in a spreadsheet overnight, 8 of them while he slept. He says the agent filled out 82 contact forms at 2am. His claimed saving is $50,000 on the pool.

The business angle (teased, not fully shown in the available transcript):

  • Lead gen for a machine he's never seen: An $11 website sends him leads, and he mentions $3k, $9k and $23k leads.
  • Selling the agent itself: Way #6. Help local businesses implement AI and charge for it.
  • Agents talking to agents: The setup he calls one of the biggest opportunities in the video.
  • Consulting pitch: He says AI consultants can charge $500-$5k/month. Only about 2% of US households pay for AI, so most small businesses are still clueless.
  • Challenge: Use an agent to find a customer who'll pay you to implement AI. He'll pay you $500 if you do.

Why it matters for you: The repeatable pattern is: one prompt, an agent with inbox access, mass personalized outreach, results in a spreadsheet. You can run it for yourself or sell it as a service to businesses that hate this work.
