True Value of Your Biz

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-30 · 8,913 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Business value is (profit − risk) ÷ (pain − process).

If your side hustle's value depends on you grinding nonstop, this shows exactly what to fix so it's worth something to a buyer.

What it is: A 45-second reframe: your business is worth more than its profit, and the formula tells you what to fix.

How it works:

  • The myth: Profit alone sets value. Wrong.
  • The formula (as stated): Profit minus risk, pain minus process, then divide the two.
  • Profit minus risk: Not how much you earn, but how likely that profit is to keep showing up. One client, one platform, one algorithm = fragile profit = lower value.
  • Pain minus process: How much suffering the owner endures to keep that profit flowing. Documented processes cut the pain and the risk at the same time.
  • The payoff: Lower risk and less pain mean a higher price when you sell. The reverse is how you sell for peanuts.
  • Caveat: The transcript is short and the math is a mental model, not a literal calculation. The exact grouping of the divide is loosely spoken, so treat it as a framework.

Instructional secret:

  • Value is *durability of profit* plus *independence from the owner*. Buyers pay for a machine, not a job.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Do this now (side-hustle checklist):

  • List your top 3 revenue risks: customer concentration, a single supplier, a single platform.
  • Write down every task only you can do. Each one is pain, so document it or delegate it.
  • Turn the repeat tasks into SOPs, checklists or templates.
  • Ask yourself: could someone else run this for a week without calling me?

Why it matters for you: A side hustle that pays you well but collapses when you stop is a job; one with low risk and real process is an asset you can sell.
