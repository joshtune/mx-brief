What it is: An opinion/commentary video (not a tutorial) reacting to OpenAI's and Anthropic's near-simultaneous public calls to slow down AI development.

How it works:

  • Nate frames the setup: the two labs racing hardest (OpenAI, Anthropic) both publicly asked the world to help slow AI — while also both taking steps toward going public.
  • Walks through OpenAI's June 8 post *"Built to benefit everyone, our plan"* (Sam Altman + chief scientist), filed same day as their intent to go public. Mission line: "ensure that AGI benefits all of humanity."
  • Breaks down OpenAI's three stated goals:
  • 1. An AI-automated AI researcher — predicted ~March 2028.
  • 2. Accelerate the economy and share gains — "personal AGI" for everyone; explicitly not automating everything, not concentrating power in a few companies.
  • 3. Call for an international body that can "take coordinated action, including slowing frontier development when needed."
  • Notes Anthropic put out a report days earlier asking for the same: a *verifiable* way to slow/pause AI if it gets too dangerous.
  • Core thesis: a huge gap exists between builders (who grasp the tech's power) and the public (who see AI as a toy or a threat). Nate argues AI is a neutral amplifier — intent and judgment stay human; models get guardrails for malicious use — so giving society time to catch up isn't crazy.
