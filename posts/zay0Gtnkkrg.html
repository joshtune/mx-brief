Building a profitable newsletter from zero in 30 days.

It's a real-time, zero-budget playbook for spinning up a newsletter side hustle that actually generates cash by month one.

What it is: A 30-day case study turning a brand-new newsletter (no audience, no budget) into $4,400 in revenue.

How it works:

  • Step 1 — Build fast: Stand up the newsletter in ~1 hour on beehiiv. Connect domain, set up welcome email + lead magnet page. Pitched over Facebook/IG because *you own the audience* instead of renting it.
  • Step 2 — Cold-start subscribers: Mail everyone you know (friends, family, colleagues), push it via Instagram stories, post in communities. Brute-force distribution.
  • Step 3 — Sell expensive first, not cheap: The counterintuitive core lesson. With low volume, cheap products can't generate enough margin to sustain you. Higher-ticket = more profit per sale = runway to keep going.
  • Step 4 — Monetize: Sold a "YouTube Master Rulebook" at $400/pop. Distribution kept compounding (AI-powered content system, community posting — got booted from ~1/3 of groups for being too promotional).
