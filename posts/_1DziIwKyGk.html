The Most Profitable Side Hustle to Start With No Money

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-14 · 46,614 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Land wholesaling: flip raw land you never own.

It's a zero-capital, phone-only side hustle where you broker land between sellers and home builders and pocket the spread — exactly the kind of actionable, low-barrier idea you're hunting for.

What it is: Land wholesaling — getting raw land under contract and assigning that contract to a home builder for a markup, without ever owning the property or putting up money.

How it works:

  • Two brothers (Jackson & Carson) started ~4 years ago at 19 & 21; now done deals in 25–30 states, all virtually.
  • Step 1 — Find the buyer first: Call home builders who resell homes. Ask what they pay for buildable lots in a specific neighborhood (e.g. "I'll pay $50k for anything in this area").
  • Step 2 — Source the land: Find off-market sellers in that neighborhood via cold calls, postcards, yellow letters, MLS/Zillow.
  • Step 3 — Lock it up: Get the seller under contract (e.g. agree at $40k) with an assignment clause: "I have the right to sell this contract."
  • Step 4 — Assign & collect spread: Sell the contract to the builder. Spread is the profit ($10k on that example; typical ~10% margin, ~$8,600/deal).
  • The mechanics are dead simple: Mail a contract, seller signs and returns it, deal done — often zero conversation with the seller.
  • Time per deal: 30–60 minutes. One deal/month ≈ $100k/year.
  • Tools needed: A phone, internet connection, and willingness to talk to people. They ran it pen-and-paper/whiteboard for the longest; claim you could do it from a public library on a flip phone.

Receipts (their claims):

  • Best month: 37 deals, $532,000 profit.
  • $100k+/month for 40 straight months.
  • First breakthrough deal: 4 lots bought at ~$40k, netted ~$140k.
  • One student: land at $1M, builder at $1.11M → $110k wire 30 days later, first deal.

Tools & links:

  • Zillow — find land and comps for free
  • MLS — source off-market and listed lots
  • beehiiv — sponsor (newsletter platform), not core to the method

Why it matters for you: This is the textbook no-money side hustle — no license, no degree, no inventory, just arbitrage between builders and landowners — making it one of the lowest-risk ideas Chris has surfaced to actually test.
