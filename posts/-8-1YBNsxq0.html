What it is: A short clip pitching the core diversification principle behind Ray Dalio's 'Holy Grail of Investing.'

How it works:

  • The mantra: find ~15 *good, uncorrelated* return streams.
  • Uncorrelated = they don't move together, so their individual risks partly cancel out.
  • Stacking to 15 cuts ~80% of your risk without cutting expected return.
  • Net effect: return-to-risk ratio improves by roughly 5x — upside without the matching downside.

The catch (not said in the clip):

  • The hard part isn't the math, it's *finding* 15 genuinely uncorrelated streams with real positive expected return.
  • Correlations drift toward 1 in a crisis, which is when you most want them low.
