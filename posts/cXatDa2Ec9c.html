A $20 startup cost, $1K–$20K per project, and zero cold-calling — this is the lowest-friction side hustle a technical person can start with skills you already have.

What it is: A self-taught 26-year-old with zero sales experience nets $15K/month profit implementing AI into small businesses — started for $20 total.

The economics:

  • Startup cost: $20 (essentially just AI tool subscriptions)
  • Per-project pricing: $1,000 to $20,000 depending on scope
  • Reported monthly profit: $15,000 (not revenue — Chris presses him on this)
  • Sales background: none. Never door-to-door, never a boiler room. Calls that stuff "the most terrifying" thing he's done

Why it works right now:

  • Small operators now have the *same* AI access enterprises have — the historic capability gap is gone
  • You arguably have better tooling than enterprise teams, because they're throttled by procurement, policy, and governance review cycles
  • Speed is the moat: you can ship an implementation while a Fortune 500 is still routing a vendor request
  • Small businesses have obvious, unautomated workflows and no internal engineering — you're not competing with an IT department
