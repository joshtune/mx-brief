GPT-6 Astra Voice Mode Automates Literally Anything

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-05 · 107,856 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Voice-driving Codex agents to run a whole business OS

Nate shows the actual mechanics of a voice-controlled agent that spawns Codex threads, uses the browser, and touches real calendars, Slack, and X, which is exactly the build-it-yourself detail you want.

What it is: A demo of GPT-6 Astra voice mode acting as a voice front-end to Codex, delegating multi-step tasks (content repurposing, landing page builds, video editing) against Nate's personal "AI operating system".

How it works:

  • Click Start new voice chat in the Codex/Astra interface, talk to it; Astra spins up separate Codex threads per task so you can multitask and kick off work from your phone.
  • Codex on the back end has read access to his whole knowledge base (the "Herc brain") plus live connections to Google Calendar, Slack, ClickUp, email, YouTube, Skool and X.
  • Task 1, YouTube to X article: Astra pulls yesterday's video, analyzes and screenshots it, writes an X article with a 5:2 thumbnail matching the YouTube one, and places screenshots inline based on what each image shows. Publishes to his X account.
  • Task 2, landing page: Astra crawls the revamped AI Automation Society Plus course inside Skool, learns the curriculum structure, and generates an AIS-branded curriculum landing page.
  • Personal OS dashboard: built entirely by voice. Includes a functional two-way calendar (events added in the dashboard hit his real calendar), a unified Slack/ClickUp/email inbox where he can reply or start threads, plus community polls (YouTube, Skool) and industry polls (X, YouTube).
  • Browser use: Astra's browser control is shown painting a portrait in Canva stroke by stroke from two reference images. Slow, but it combines vision plus browser control end to end.
  • Video editing: He pointed Codex at 150+ GB of AIS Live event footage and got a 60-second sizzle reel in two prompts, with quotes, music and narrative arc selected by the agent.
  • Key insight: capabilities alone aren't enough. The system works because Codex has full context on his business and authenticated connections to every tool, so voice becomes a control layer rather than a chatbot.
