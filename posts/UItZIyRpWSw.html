Watch Me Start an Online Business With AI in 85 Minutes

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-02 · 33,985 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Vibe-coding a niche waiver app, hunting a paying customer live

This is the whole side-hustle loop compressed into 85 minutes — pick a problem you personally have, buy a $12 domain, vibe code it, then cold-hunt a paying customer without leaning on an audience.

What it is: Chris builds OnlyWaivers.com live — a niched-down liability-waiver signing tool — using Replit Agent 4, then tries to land a real paying customer inside the video without touching his own audience.

The idea:

  • Problem he actually has: he holds a leadership position in his church ward and runs youth activities for 60–70 kids. Every activity needs signed liability waivers.
  • Current flow is PDFs emailed out, printed, signed, lost on the floor of a truck. Kids show up without them and nobody turns them away — so you either eat the risk or push everyone through friction.
  • Existing tools (DocuSign, generic e-sign) technically solve it but are cumbersome and not niched to this use case. That gap *is* the business.
  • He's customer zero. Explicit constraint: find a different paying customer by end of video, via Facebook ads, Reddit, cold outreach — anything but his audience.

How the build runs:

  • Name + domain first. Wanted "waiver" in the name, ≤3–4 syllables, `.com` over `.ai`/`.net`/`.online`. Bought OnlyWaivers.com on Namecheap for $12, on camera. Notes it pigeonholes him into one vertical and ships anyway.
  • Claude writes the prompt, Replit writes the code. He opens Claude (using Fable 5, the strongest model) and prompts it to generate the build prompt — front-loading the thinking so Replit doesn't wander.
  • Claude pushed back that "OnlyWaivers" was too close to OnlyFans; he overruled it as a non-issue and bought the domain. Model as advisor, not decider.
  • Token-maxer posture: deliberately uses the most expensive model regardless of efficiency — "driving a Ferrari to the grocery store." Also explicitly rejects prompt-perfectionism: don't overthink the garbage-in-garbage-out thing, just start.
  • Reproducibility is the point. Whole thing is scoped to what anyone with a $20/mo vibe-coding subscription can do. He states repeatedly he has no CS background and doesn't know what HTML stands for.

Tools & links:

  • Replit + Agent 4 — the vibe-coding agent doing the actual build (referral link from the description)
  • Claude — used upstream to generate the build prompt; he runs Fable 5
  • Namecheap — domain registrar, $12 for OnlyWaivers.com
  • TKOPOD.com — his newsletter and private community
  • Distribution channels named for customer hunting: Facebook Ads, Reddit

Why it matters for you: The transferable pattern isn't the waiver app — it's *niche down on a problem you personally suffer, where a bloated generic tool already exists* — and the whole thing is de-risked to a $12 domain plus a monthly subscription, which is about the cheapest side-hustle experiment you can run.
