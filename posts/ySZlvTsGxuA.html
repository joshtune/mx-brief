How to Hire an A Player

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-01 · 9,051 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Hire proven winners, don't train unproven hopefuls.

If your side hustle needs an operator, this shows you how to hire someone who runs the business without you — by poaching proven performers instead of gambling on potential.

What it is: A hiring framework for finding operators (A players) who can run your business day one.

How it works:

  • Define "good" first: Build a written profile for every role before you hire. You can't spot an A player if you haven't defined what winning looks like in your business.
  • Past behavior predicts future behavior: Only bet on people with a documented history of winning — athletics, sales, top of some field. Winners keep winning.
  • Screen out whiners: No track record of winning + a history of complaining = near-zero odds of a top performer.
  • Hire for the exact thing, not potential: Stop hiring people you'll have to train on a skill they've never done. Don't spend your hours turning maybes into maybes.
  • Poach your competitors: Map your full competitor set, find people already crushing it in your wheelhouse, and target only those who can hit the ground running on day one.
  • Operator ≠ employee: An operator (A player) is an independent center of gravity — someone who owns outcomes, not someone who waits for tasks.
