$532K in One Month Selling Land They Didn't Own

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-14 · 19,738 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Virtual land wholesaling — flipping contracts, not property.

It's a near-zero-capital side hustle you can run from a laptop nights and weekends — no license, no inventory, just contracts.

What it is: Land wholesaling — you put rural/vacant lots under contract cheap, then assign that contract to a buyer at a markup. You never own the land; you middle-man the paper.

How it works:

  • Find undervalued vacant land (owners who bought lots decades ago for hundreds of dollars and forgot about them).
  • Send signed purchase contracts directly via mail — guests describe sellers signing and returning deals with zero phone conversation.
  • Lock the lot at a low price, then assign/flip the contract to a builder or buyer for a spread (they cite ~10% on a $750K deal = $75K profit).
  • Done 100% virtually and nationwide — 25–30 states, never seeing the lots in person.
  • Each deal takes ~30 min to an hour; one deal/month ≈ $100K+/year. Their best month: 37 deals, $532K profit; 40 straight months over $100K.
  • Breakthrough story: at 19 and 21, four lots optioned for ~$37K became a ~$140K payday.

Tools & links:

  • Zillow — sourcing and comping land; they joke you could run this from a public library computer
  • Phone + internet connection — the entire stated tech stack ("iPhone, internet connection")

Why it matters for you: This is the textbook "shiny object" side hustle — startable for free with tools you already have, asynchronous enough to fit around a dev job, and the workflow (source data → automate outreach → arbitrage the spread) maps cleanly onto how an engineer thinks.
