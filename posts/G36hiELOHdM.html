How Buffett bought American Express during a crisis

Buffett's Amex bet during the 1963 salad oil scandal

A 90-second clip on how one fraud crater created Buffett's best trade — the money came from correctly judging that a company's liability was temporary and its brand wasn't.

What it is: A short retelling of the 1963 Salad Oil Scandal and how Warren Buffett turned American Express's blowup into one of his most profitable trades.

The scam (per the transcript):

  • A scam artist realized he could take out loans collateralized by his soybean oil inventory
  • He went to ~50 different banks and stacked loans against the same claimed inventory
  • To make the collateral credible, he hired American Express to verify the oil in the tanks was actually there
  • The tanks were filled with seawater — oil floats on water, so surface inspections "confirmed" full tanks
  • Soybean oil prices crashed, the loans blew up, and the fraud surfaced
  • American Express was the guarantor of the inspection warehouse receipts, so Amex was on the hook for the massive payout

What Buffett actually did:

  • Amex stock cratered on the liability headline
  • Instead of modeling the balance sheet, he did field research: sat in restaurants, talked to waiters and cashiers, watched whether people were still handing over the green card
  • Conclusion: cardholders and merchants did not care about a soybean oil scandal — trust in the card as a payment instrument was intact
  • Thesis: the loss was a one-time cash hit, not damage to the franchise (brand, merchant network, cardholder habit)
  • He bought heavily into the panic and held while the market re-rated Amex back to its earnings power

The transferable playbook:

  • Separate a one-time liability from a permanent impairment of the business — the market routinely confuses them
  • Verify demand where it actually happens (point of sale, usage logs, checkout), not in the press release
  • The asset that survives a crisis is the one customers keep using out of habit
  • Being the *verifier* is a liability business: Amex got destroyed for certifying someone else's collateral — margin looks great until you eat the loss
