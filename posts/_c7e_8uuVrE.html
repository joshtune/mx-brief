Unboxing MSI's DGX Spark clone, the EdgeXpert GB10.

Skip it unless you want to see the box — it's a 90-second unboxing of a $4K-class Nvidia GB10 mini box with zero benchmarks and zero pricing, so there's no Mac mini comparison to extract yet.

What it is: A short unboxing of the MSI EdgeXpert, one of the third-party DGX Spark clones built on Nvidia's GB10 Grace Blackwell platform.

The machine:

  • MSI EdgeXpert — same GB10 chip and 128 GB unified memory class as Nvidia's own DGX Spark, just an MSI shell
  • Alex notes the MSI box has more plastic than the Nvidia unit, a bottom-fed screw-accessible chassis (he hints he'll crack it open), a honeycomb grill, and a 240 W external power brick
  • No specs, no benchmarks, no LLM runs in this clip — it's purely physical hardware

Cost vs Mac mini (not covered in the video — street pricing from the links):

  • DGX Spark / MSI EdgeXpert / ASUS GX10 all land roughly in the $3,000–$4,300 range depending on SKU and sales
  • A Mac mini M4 Pro maxed to 64 GB unified memory is roughly $2,000; Mac Studio M4 Max 128 GB is the closer Apple comparison at ~$3,500+
  • GB10 boxes win on CUDA compatibility and FP4 throughput; Macs win on price-per-GB of memory and quiet, no-fuss local inference via MLX/llama.cpp
  • Alex is also linking 400 Gbps switches, which signals he's planning to cluster two or more of these — that's the real story, coming in a later video
