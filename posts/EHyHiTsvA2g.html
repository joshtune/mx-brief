  • Expect 4–5 near-quits. She names quitting-adjacent moments as part of the standard path, not a signal to exit. Knowing they're scheduled makes them survivable.
  • The honest timeline: "Way longer than you want, shorter than you think you can survive." No number, deliberately — a decade in her case.
  • Eating glass is the moat. The reason the payoff is large is that most people leave during the flat part. Duration is the filter.
