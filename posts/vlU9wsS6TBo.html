Is AI Actually Making Teams More Productive?

A Life Engineered · 2026-10-20 · 5,612 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Skeptic's challenge: where's AI's killer app, and real gains?

Gives you a sober, number-backed baseline (~20% value gain, not 10x) for what to expect from AI on your team and how to set your own career expectations.

What it is: A short clip where a guest challenges the AI-productivity hype with three tests: breakthrough apps, cheaper/faster/safer delivery, and bulk team output.

The message to engineers:

  • Hype claims 10x. The speaker has seen no evidence of it.
  • AI isn't a magic multiplier. It's a modest, real, measurable gain.
  • Don't panic about being replaced overnight, and don't assume AI makes you instantly 10x.

How it works (his three tests):

  • Application as end result: Is there one amazing app that couldn't have existed without AI? He knows of none.
  • Business process: Is there a very good app built at 10% of the cost, time, or risk? He hasn't seen one.
  • Bulk productivity: Give a team of engineers AI, watch them for six months, and compare the economic value produced with and without AI. His best estimate for sophisticated teams is about +20%.
  • He invites counterexamples: if you have one, he wants to see it.

What to prepare for:

  • Expect modest gains, roughly 20% over six months, not step-change gains. Calibrate your own and your manager's expectations accordingly.
  • Build a measurable case. Track the economic value you deliver, not lines of code or tickets closed.
  • Be skeptical of 10x claims in planning, hiring, and estimates, and expect pressure from leadership who believe them.
  • Keep your fundamentals sharp. If AI adds about 20%, judgment, design, and ownership are still the bulk of the value.

Instructional content: None. This is an opinion clip with no step-by-step guidance.

Caveats:

  • The 20% figure is a self-described 'best estimate', not a study.
  • The clip is short, with no methodology, and the guest isn't named in the transcript.
