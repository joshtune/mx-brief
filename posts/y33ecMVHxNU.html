Using AI Agents Is Way Easier Than You Think

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-12 · 21,782 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Spinning up an AI team to rent out ranch land

It's a live demo of turning one prompt into a working land-rental side hustle (inquiry form, social posts, Google Business setup guide) in about 3 minutes, but it's a sponsored Symphony pitch, so watch it for the playbook, not the product claims.

What it is: A sponsored demo where Chris prompts Symphony, an AI-agent platform, to launch an RV parking and storage rental business on 40 unused acres of his new 231-acre Texas ranch.

The idea (the side hustle):

  • Rent idle land for RV/camping spots and vehicle/equipment storage
  • Chris bought the ranch ("Triple Eight Ranch", north of Dallas) to work the land with his kids, so the rental has to run with almost no screen time
  • Low-capex model: the land is already owned, and demand comes from RV owners and people who need storage

How it's carried out (the steps in the demo):

  • One prompt: "I just bought a 231-acre ranch... at least 40 acres are empty, rent for RV trailers and storage. I need a public CRM link for inquiries and next steps."
  • Space creation: Symphony makes a "space" (a virtual project workspace) and asks clarifying questions, such as whether RV/camping and storage share one form (he said yes) and what form questions to include (he let the AI draft them to stress-test it)
  • First agent: "Noah," a lead-qualifier agent, greets each prospect, finds out what they need, collects details, and lines up next steps
  • Asking the AI for a plan: he told it to hold off on email and asked what else he needs. It suggested:
  • a pricing page
  • deposit collection (Stripe integration)
  • a website
  • an occupancy tracker
  • a Google Business Profile listing
  • Hiring more agents:
  • Ava: pricing research on local rates
  • Mia: social posts and the Google Business Profile
  • Olivia: marketing manager
  • (the transcript also names "Liam" for the pricing research, so the narration is inconsistent)
  • Parallel launch: he told it to run everything at once ("momentum is everything"). The agents hit snags and fixed them without asking him, which he compares to a great employee who reports problems *after* solving them
  • Result after ~3 minutes: a live public inquiry form, five social posts, and a Google Business Profile setup guide with exactly what to paste and SEO tips. Pricing research was still running, and the pricing page was blocked on it
  • Space view: shows the running tasks, goals and routines (the transcript cuts off here)

Tools & links:

  • Symphony — AI agent team platform, with named agents, shared "spaces" and parallel task execution. Promo code CHRISKOERNER10 (sponsor link)
  • Koerner Office newsletter — Chris's newsletter
  • TKOwners community — Chris's private community
  • Stripe — suggested by the AI for deposit links
  • Google Business Profile — local listing for discovery and SEO

Caveats:

  • Sponsored content. There are no pricing, accuracy or booking-conversion numbers
  • The 3-minute result is scaffolding (form, posts, guide). It isn't a business with paying customers
  • Zoning, insurance, utilities and liability for RV/storage on rural land are not covered, so check local rules before copying this
  • Transcript is truncated, so the routines and ongoing automation part is unseen

Why it matters for you: It's a repeatable template for a low-effort side hustle (rent an asset you already have, then use agents for lead capture, pricing research, deposits and local SEO). You could replicate most of it with any AI tool plus a form, Stripe and a Google Business listing.
