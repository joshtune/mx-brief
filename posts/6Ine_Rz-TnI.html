AI virtual staging for real estate photos

A concrete, low-cost side hustle: sell AI-furnished room photos at $25 each, with the build prompt and cold email handed to you free.

What it is: Virtual staging. A customer sends a photo of an empty room, you send back the same room with AI-generated furniture, and you charge per photo.

How it works:

  • The problem: Empty rooms photograph badly (small, cold) and buyers scroll past them. Physical staging costs thousands and takes a week. Agents already pay $30-50 per virtually staged photo.
  • Proof it's real: The market has existed for about 10 years. BoxBrownie charges about $24/image, Stuccco about $25-35, and high-end agencies charge $50-100.
  • Pricing: $25 per photo or $149 per listing (about 6 photos). Chris's math: 20 listings a month is about $3k/month, which undercuts the incumbents.
  • Cost to you: Roughly a penny per image through the Higgsfield API. You pay per picture from a prepaid wallet (put in about $20) and get a price quote before each generation. There's no subscription.
  • The build: Chris builds a small app on the Higgsfield API using one AI prompt that generates the whole app. A separate staging prompt is what makes the rooms look realistic, and he calls it the one that matters.
  • Who pays:
  • Solo agents with vacant listings
  • Property managers (rentals are never staged)
  • Spec home builders who need identical empty houses to look different
  • Flippers
  • Real estate photographers (his target)
  • The go-to-market trick: Sell to photographers, not agents. A photographer already shoots about 50 listings a month, has the agents' trust and invoices them, so you sell to one person who sells to many. His line: "Sell the one to sell the many."
  • Outreach: A cold email, which he says is the exact one he sent. In the video he has an AI agent email 100 real estate photographers.
  • Compliance warning: He says one rule gets staged photos pulled from the MLS, and people are breaking it without knowing. The rule is not in the part of the transcript provided, so check the full video.

Tools & links:

  • Higgsfield — image/video model API, pay per picture. This is a partner link: 15% off launch models and $15 in free credits.
  • Higgsfield API console — where you sign up, add wallet credit and explore models. The transcript garbles the domain as "hixfield.ai", so verify the URL.
  • Free Google Doc in the video description — the app-build prompt, staging prompt, outreach prompts and cold email. The link is truncated in the snippet, so grab it from the YouTube description.
  • Competitors to study: BoxBrownie and Stuccco.

Why it matters for you: It's a side hustle you can start this weekend. Costs are near zero, there are no meetings, and the demand is proven. The only real work is the staging prompt quality, the MLS disclosure rule and landing a few photographers.
