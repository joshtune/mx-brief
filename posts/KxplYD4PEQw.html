35-year-old dad trains for the World Cup

My First Million · 2026-06-22 · 500,286 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
How a viral fitness creator monetized a soccer challenge.

A clean case study in turning a personal-transformation stunt into brand deals and a media brand from zero.

What it is: Shaan & Sam break down Zack Duke's "World Cup Dad" — a 35-year-old who'd never played soccer documenting his attempt to make the 2026 World Cup team.

How it works:

  • Picked a clear, absurd-but-relatable hook: "Can a dad who's never played soccer make the 2026 World Cup?"
  • Hard daily training, documented and posted everything — the content IS the journey (build-in-public applied to fitness)
  • Genuine transformation as proof: got in great shape, became legitimately decent at soccer
  • Big swing + visible progress = viral attention, which compounded into legitimacy

How he made money / what he got:

  • Brand deals off the viral following
  • Paid play in small-format events — 3v3, 7v7, the Adidas Cup
  • LA Galaxy invited him to train with them (credibility + reach)
  • Never made the US national team — that was never the real product; the audience and brand were

The takeaway model:

  • Pick a bold, legible goal anyone can root for
  • Document relentlessly so the attempt becomes the content
  • Let real progress create earned media; monetize the audience, not the outcome

Why it matters for you: The featured individual is Zack Duke — the business was the documented attempt itself: a viral hook + build-in-public consistency converted into sponsorships, paid events, and a personal brand, with the literal goal as marketing rather than the revenue source.
