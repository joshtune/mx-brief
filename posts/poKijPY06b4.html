Give Me 59 Seconds And I'll Teach You How To Sell

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-16 · 22,897 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A 3-step sales framework to make people buy faster.

If your side hustle stalls because you can't close, this gives you a repeatable script to sell without chasing or convincing.

What it is: Codie's "NOW" method — a 3-move sales framework built on pain, contrast, and scarcity instead of persuasion.

How it works:

  • N — Name the pain: Vague problems don't sell; specific pain does. Don't say "do you want to grow?" — say "you're stuck at $500K because you're the only salesperson." If they don't feel it, they won't fix it.
  • O — Open the gap: Contrast current state (slow, manual, founder-dependent) vs. future state (a team better than you running it). The bigger the gap, the more you can charge.
  • W — Withdraw the path: Stop overselling and chasing (the 52-follow-up trap). Flip the posture: "I only work with people ready to fix this now — if you're not, you're probably not right for us." Scarcity creates urgency.
  • The equation: Bigger pain → bigger need → more withdrawal → more urgency → more sales.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that selling a side hustle is about engineering urgency and contrast, not convincing — a script you can run on your next sales call today.
