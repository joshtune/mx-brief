The Science Of Making Money

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-13 · 5,868 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Three levers for making money: people, systems, focus.

It's a 60-second checklist for a side hustle: pick better people, build repeatable systems, and chase one money-making thing instead of ten.

What it is: A short Codie Sanchez framework saying money-making has three parts: the right people, the right systems, and focus.

How it works:

  • Right people: Work with people better than you. She cites Michael Jordan, who didn't win a ring until he was surrounded by better players. Going alone is faster, but you don't get far.
  • Right systems: Don't hunt for unicorns (rare superstar talent). Build processes good enough that average people ("donkeys") can still win the race.
  • Focus: Ask which single action would make more money than anything else, then do that. She says this is where most people lose money.
  • Core claim: Businesses rarely fail from lack of skill. They fail from lack of focus.

The instructional secret: The third lever is the one that counts. Skill is not the bottleneck. Picking the one highest-leverage action and ignoring the rest is.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle gives you limited hours, so the one-thing question decides where they go. Systems let the hustle run without you, and better partners fill in the skills you lack.
