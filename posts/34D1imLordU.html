the PERFECT Raspberry Pi wall dashboard?

Wall-mounting a Raspberry Pi touchscreen for a Home Assistant dashboard.

Chuck's studio-wall home-lab dashboard is exactly the kind of home-lab flex you envy — this is the build to copy.

What it is: Chuck mounts the new Raspberry Pi Touch Display 2 (10-inch portrait) on his studio wall to run his Home Assistant and home-lab dashboards.

How it works:

  • Screen connects to a Raspberry Pi 5 with just two cables: a DSI ribbon cable and a GPIO power cable — no HDMI, no external PSU for the panel.
  • Full desktop with zero mouse/keyboard: on-screen keyboard auto-pops when you tap a field.
  • Gotcha that cost him a full day: a fresh Pi OS image is not enough — the Pi 5's EEPROM firmware must be updated or the display won't be recognized. Symptom is a completely dead screen (no output). Fix: update the bootloader/EEPROM firmware.
  • The flimsy DSI ribbon cable + fiddly seating of the connector is the hardest physical step.
  • Screen sticks out from the wall a lot, so mounting cleanly is an unsolved problem he punts on.
  • UI is small/dense on a 10-inch panel — expect to bump up text size.

Specs:

  • 10.1" diagonal, 1200×1920 native portrait, 2.3M pixels, 224 PPI (denser than a 1080p 13" laptop).
  • IPS, 85° viewing angle, 400 cd/m² (60% brighter than v1), anti-glare, 24-bit color, 16mm thin, driver board now inside the enclosure.
  • 10-finger multitouch, 35ms max touch response.
  • Hard requirement: Raspberry Pi 5 or Compute Modules only — no Pi 3/4. $80.

Tools & links:
