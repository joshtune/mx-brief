The 3-2-1 Speaking Trick To Gain Respect

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-02 · 25,469 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Pause, two points, one question: win hard conversations.

Side hustles live or die on tense conversations (pricing, clients, vendors), and this 30-second framework keeps you calm, clear, and in control when they get heated.

What it is: A three-step script for difficult conversations: pause 3 seconds, make 2 points, end with 1 question.

How it works:

  • The secret: Structure beats volume. You don't win by talking more or louder; you win by constraining yourself.
  • 3 — Pause 3 seconds: When someone says something you dislike, count "one, two, three" before replying. It "clears your mental windshield" so you respond to what's true rather than react.
  • 2 — Give only two points: Brains can't process much at once. Codie's claim is that two points let the verbal and visual tracks sync, so your message sticks. Cut the other seven arguments.
  • 1 — End with one question: Closing on a question pulls drifting listeners back in and hands them the next move, so you steer the conversation.
  • Caveat: The "verbal and visual track" explanation is asserted, not sourced. Treat it as a useful heuristic, not settled science.
