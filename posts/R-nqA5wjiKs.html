NVIDIA RTX Spark Made Everyone Mad

Alex Ziskind · 2026-06-28 · 35,289 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Why NVIDIA shipped the RTX Spark on Windows, not Linux.

If you're eyeing NVIDIA's AI mini-PCs as a Mac alternative, this explains the Windows-first gamble — but skip it if you want actual specs and prices.

What it is: An opinion piece defending NVIDIA's decision to launch the RTX Spark AI machine on Windows-on-ARM instead of Linux.

How it works:

  • The RTX Spark ships with no Linux support at launch, which set off the dev community.
  • Alex argues Windows is the *smarter* play: getting Windows (with its emulation layer and 40-year app catalog) running well on ARM is the hard part — NVIDIA is dropping CUDA onto a platform that's already been beaten into shape.
  • For Linux holdouts, he points out you already have the DGX Spark — it runs Linux today and will inherit next-gen chips (Vera Rubin, Feynman) with matched CPUs mentioned in the keynote.
  • Verdict: not a wasted move, just one that needs patience.
