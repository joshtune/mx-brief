The 5 Paths To Wealth

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-13 · 23,148 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Only five routes to wealth; four are closed.

It's a 90-second gut-check on why your side hustle should be a business you own rather than a skill you rent out — because ownership is the only path with a resale multiple attached to it.

What it is: A sub-two-minute framing video — Codie relays a list she heard on stage at a conference full of $100M+ net worth people, then hard-pivots to a book promo.

How it works:

  • The five paths, as stated: (1) inherit it, (2) marry into it, (3) climb the ladder at a public company as an employee, (4) manage other people's money, (5) own a business.
  • Her claim: only ~5% of people land in the first four. (Stated as-is on stage — no source given, and the phrasing is muddy. Treat it as a rhetorical device, not a statistic.)
  • The actual instructional secret is the last 20 seconds, and it's the only part with mechanics in it: every $1 of profit you add to a business is worth $2–$5 when you sell it. That's the multiple. A business isn't just a cash flow — it's an asset priced off that cash flow.
  • Why that matters operationally: $10K/yr of new profit isn't $10K. At a 3x multiple it's $30K of enterprise value, on top of the $10K/yr you keep collecting. Nowhere else does labor compound like that.
  • The unspoken corollary: paths 3 and 4 pay you a salary. Salaries don't have multiples. You cannot sell your job.
  • No case study, no framework, no numbers beyond the multiple. This is a hook for the September 18th live book breakdown, not a lesson.

Why it matters for you: If you're picking a side hustle, pick one that could theoretically be sold — a small operating business with recurring revenue and someone other than you delivering the work — because freelancing, consulting, and contract dev are just path 3 with worse benefits and no exit multiple.
