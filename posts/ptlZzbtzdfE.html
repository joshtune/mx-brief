What it is: A sponsored demo where Matt builds an "AI finance dashboard" that tracks IPOs, funding rounds, and market sentiment — powered entirely by Airtable's Hyperagent.

How it works:

  • Hyperagent spins up a team of specialized agents: one monitors news, one tracks funding/IPOs, one analyzes sentiment, one compiles a daily briefing/dashboard.
  • Agents run continuously in the background, re-checking sources and updating reports even when he's logged out.
  • User creates watchlists (companies, stocks, founders) and the agents "self-tune" over time.
  • No code, no architecture, no data model shown — the whole thing is "I built this in a day."

Tools & links:
