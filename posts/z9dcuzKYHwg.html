  • Results claimed: launched March 1, $100K in 100 days.

Tools & links:

  • Lovable — AI app builder used to stand up the whole tech stack ("Lovable, Wi-Fi, and a computer").

Startup requirements:

  • Laptop, Wi-Fi, and an AI builder — no capital, no prior experience claimed.

Why it matters for you: Concrete, low-cost side hustle with a clear model — build a lightweight tool with AI, arbitrage global labor, and cold-sell placements to small business owners.
