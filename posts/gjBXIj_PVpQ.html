Put effort into your appearance for YOU

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-12 · 6,196 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Polish your appearance; it changes how you operate.

If you're running a side hustle, how you show up sets your price and your own standards before you've said a word, and it costs almost nothing to fix.

What it is: A 60-second thought-leadership clip arguing that dressing and carrying yourself well is leverage, mostly on your own behavior.

How it works:

  • The secret: Treat "being ready" as a rule, not a mood. Her grandma showed up polished every day, for nobody in particular, and that consistency is the whole lesson.
  • Outside signal: Presentation tells people who you are before you speak. Codie cites a claim that women who are put together and wear makeup earn 15-30% more. No source is given, so treat the number as unverified.
  • Inside effect: Her stronger claim is that it changes you. Posture, confidence and standards shift, so you operate like a pro.
  • Pricing link: "You look like a pro, so you get paid like a pro." Perceived professionalism feeds what you charge and what people accept.
  • Framing: She pitches it as self-respect, not vanity or impressing people.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle has no boss or dress code to enforce standards, so a fixed "show up ready" rule keeps you charging pro rates and acting like the owner. Do it on days you're only talking to your laptop too.
