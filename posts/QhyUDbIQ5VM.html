The busiest people don’t have time to gossip.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-24 · 17,212 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Filter feedback by whether the source has what you want.

If you're building a side hustle, this is a fast mental model for ignoring critics who've never built anything and protecting your focus.

What it is: A one-minute filter for deciding whose feedback to absorb and whose to discard.

How it works:

  • The core test: *who's giving the feedback, and would you be proud to be associated with them?*
  • Codie's pattern observations — the people who try to talk her out of her life all share a tell: they don't have what she wants (admirable partner, bigger bank account, longer list of adventures).
  • "I've never had constructive criticism from someone who hasn't constructed." Criticism only counts if the critic has built the thing they're critiquing.
  • Secure, happy people don't gossip or tear others down — so trash-talk itself is a signal the source is unqualified.
  • If the giver fails the test: pass. No engagement, no rumination.

Tools & links:

  • No external tools, repos, or links in this video — it's pure thought-leadership/mindset.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle attracts cheap opinions from people who've never run one; this gives you a sharp rule to tune out the noise and only weight feedback from people who've actually built.
