How Whatnot went from $2M to $1B in sales

My First Million · 2026-09-21 · 2,564 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Whatnot's founder breaks down the 2M-to-1B sales explosion.

If you want the concrete playbook for scaling a live-commerce marketplace from niche to category-crushing, this is the founder explaining exactly what he did.

What it is: A founder interview breaking down Whatnot's sales trajectory — $2.3M (2020) → $168M (2021) → ~$1B (2022) — and the mechanics behind it.

What Whatnot is: A live-shopping marketplace app where sellers stream and auction/sell items (starting with collectibles like comic books) directly to buyers in real time.

How they made money / grew:

  • COVID tailwind, not the core driver — physical shops (e.g., comic book stores) shut down, sellers moved their entire business onto Whatnot livestreams and outsold their brick-and-mortar volume.
  • Elite early hiring — the first 30 employees are called out as exceptional; strong early team execution compounded into growth.
  • Multi-category expansion as the core unlock — the real growth lever wasn't one hot category, it was building a repeatable mechanism to launch new verticals fast.
  • Cross-pollination effect — as new categories launched, buyers/sellers crossed over between them, so playbooks (engagement tactics, seller tooling, community mechanics) that worked in one category compounded across all categories, accelerating overall GMV growth (2M → 168M → 1B).
