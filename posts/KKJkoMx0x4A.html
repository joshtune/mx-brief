He Made $27K Last Month Connecting Contractors

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-09-30 · 7,032 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Paid intros between contractors: a $27K/month side hustle

It's a no-employee, no-special-skills side hustle that runs on public data and a few text messages, and the episode promises the exact scripts.

What it is: Aaron sells paid business introductions (lead brokering) to contractors. He started by noticing a big siding company was sending his boss hundreds of free leads a year, then made those connections himself for money.

How it works:

  • The origin: Aaron was a part-time receptionist at a Denver siding company. He saw a much bigger siding company nearby sending his boss hundreds of leads a year for free. That showed him the leads had value.
  • The model: He connects one business owner to another using publicly available data. No specialized skills are needed.
  • Pricing: He started at $150 per connection. He now charges $6,000 for 10 intros, or $600 per intro.
  • Margins and team: He says profit is about 90% and he has no employees.
  • Revenue growth: June was $14K and September was about $27K, so revenue nearly doubled in a few months.
  • Proof point: He sent one text that got a painter four bids ranging from $17K to $64K within 48 hours.
  • Outreach: The intros are made by text message. The episode says it will share the exact texts, word for word.

Tools & links:

  • No tools or URLs are named in the excerpt. The transcript covers only the intro. The data sources and the text scripts are in the full episode.

Caveats:

  • Revenue figures are self-reported by the guest.
  • The excerpt doesn't say how he finds the public data, who pays and who receives the intro, or how he sources the contractors.
  • Check local rules on lead-gen and referral fees for licensed trades before copying this.

Why it matters for you: It's a low-capital, solo, high-margin side hustle you could test with a few intros before you commit, and the full episode apparently walks through the exact process.
