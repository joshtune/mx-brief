Three months wrong about why my 4-node AMD cluster was slow

Alex Ziskind · 2026-06-16 · 65,194 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Clustering four Strix Halo mini PCs for local AI inference.

If you care about which box runs local AI and what it costs, this is four 128GB AMD mini PCs beating Mac mini-class hardware on unified memory — but the speed payoff is tiny.

What it is: A 4-node cluster of Minisforum MS-S1 Max mini PCs (AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Strix Halo) running DeepSeek-R1 inference, after 3 months of debugging why it was slow.

The hardware (what you actually want):

  • Chip: AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 "Strix Halo" — the most powerful x86 APU you can buy
  • Memory: 128 GB unified memory *per node* — more accessible to the GPU than a 5090 or RTX Pro 6000
  • Box: Minisforum MS-S1 Max — Alex says it might be his favorite Strix Halo machine (he's also tested GMKtec, Beelink, Framework, Asus versions of this chip)
  • Cluster: 4 nodes meshed at 4ms ping via 400Gbps switches
  • Power draw: ~180–187W per machine under load

How it performs:

  • Model: DeepSeek-R1, 37B active parameters, 4-bit quantized
  • 4,058 tokens in 10 minutes
  • 6.23 tokens/sec with vLLM tensor parallelism — vs Minisforum's own demo at 5.94 tok/s
  • The 3-month lesson: he was wrong about the bottleneck the whole time; the fix was tensor parallelism config, not the hardware

Tools & links:

  • Minisforum MS-S1 Max — the mini PC under test
  • 400Gbps switch (2-port): https://bhpho.to/4r9vJi1
  • 400Gbps switch (4-port): https://bhpho.to/4qQqOlI
  • Thunderbolt 5 external SSD: https://amzn.to/3XqetZO

Cost vs Mac mini reality check: Each Strix Halo node with 128GB unified memory lands in the ~$1,500–2,000 range — comparable to a maxed Mac mini but with far more GPU-accessible memory. The catch: four of them networked still only hit ~6 tok/s on a big model, so the value is the 512GB total memory pool, not raw speed.

Why it matters for you: This is the clearest answer yet on a non-Mac box for local AI — one 128GB Strix Halo mini PC rivals a Mac mini on memory for similar money, but don't expect a 4-node cluster to be fast.
