$200K in 45 Days From One Airbnb (Start With $0 Down)

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-05 · 23,880 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Airbnb co-hosting as a no-money-down side hustle.

If you want a side hustle with zero capital, co-hosting other people's short-term rentals is the low-risk on-ramp this guest used before buying his own properties.

What it is: A breakdown of how one uniquely-positioned Airbnb pulled $200K in 45 days, and the $0-down way for you to copy the model: co-hosting.

How it works:

  • The flagship property hit $200K in bookings in 45 days, then $300K by month 3.
  • Growth came from one $500 Instagram Reel ad — static images, not even video.
  • Average booking size was $4,000–$5,000; some days booked 5–6 reservations.
  • Demand outran supply: 10–15 people/day couldn't get dates, creating a waitlist of hundreds for a second property. That signaled a real market gap worth scaling full-time.
  • The no-capital entry point isn't owning — it's co-hosting: you manage someone else's property ("property manager on steroids"), take on more, and earn more.
  • Realistic ramp cited: some operators land 10–20 co-host clients in ~6 months, then reinvest cash flow into buying their own units.

Tools & links:

  • Instagram Reels ads — the entire top-of-funnel was one paid static-image reel (~$500).
  • Airbnb co-hosting — no repo/tool URLs mentioned in the transcript.

Why it matters for you: Co-hosting is the closest thing to a $0-down, low-risk side hustle in short-term rentals — you validate the skill and build cash flow on other people's assets before risking your own capital.
