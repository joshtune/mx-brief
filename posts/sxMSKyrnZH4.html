AMD's Strix Successor Just Caught the M4 Pro

Alex Ziskind · 2026-06-12 · 107,637 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Beelink Ser 10 mini PC vs Mac mini M4 Pro.

If you want a cheaper-than-Mac box for local LLMs, the Ser 10's 64–96GB configs undercut the M4 Pro by a wide margin.

What it is: A three-way price/performance shootout for AI + dev work: Beelink Ser 10 Max (new AMD chip) vs older Ser 9 vs Apple Mac mini M4 Pro.

The machines & prices (the part you actually want):

  • Beelink Ser 10 Max — new AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX470 "Gorgon Point" chip. Ships in 32GB and 64GB configs, all with 1TB storage. A loaded 96GB + 2TB SSD = ~$2,300.
  • Beelink Ser 9 — last year's model, still sold, usually a couple hundred bucks cheaper. Alex runs one as a media server.
  • Mac mini M4 Pro — base M4 Pro (not 20-core GPU), 48GB RAM, 1TB, 10GbE = ~$2,000, and only 48GB. The 2TB version hits ~$2,500 with half the RAM of the comparable Beelink.
  • Skip the base M4 Mac mini for AI — it caps at 24GB, too little for these workflows.

Ser 10 chip specs:

  • 12 cores / 24 threads, Zen 5 + 8× Zen 5c, boost to 5.2GHz
  • Shared memory: up to half can go to the GPU (29GB on his 64GB unit)
  • iGPU is the Radeon 890M — same one as the Ser 9 (so GPU's not the upgrade)
  • RAM is DDR5-5600, user-upgradeable (unlike Ser 9); storage up to 8TB
  • 10GbE (Ser 9 was only 2.5), USB4, HDMI 2.1, 4K@240Hz triple monitor
  • An orange variant ships with OpenClaw preinstalled for $100 more

Tools & links:

  • ChatLLM Teams — sponsor; one dashboard for all top LLMs, $10/mo
  • Abacus AI DeepAgent — sponsor; build/deploy apps from prompts

Bottom line on cost: Beelink stays well under the Mac mini at every comparable tier — the 96GB/2TB Beelink at ~$2,300 beats a 48GB/2TB Mac mini at ~$2,500.

Why it matters for you: This is exactly your question answered — what to run AI on and how it stacks up against a Mac mini on price; the Beelink line wins on RAM-per-dollar, the M4 Pro costs more for less memory.
