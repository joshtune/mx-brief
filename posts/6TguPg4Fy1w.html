Retail trader's social-signal method and his 2026 bets

Camillo turned a normal job into a ~68% annualized, 15-year audited track record using nothing but observed changes in consumer behavior — a repeatable pattern-recognition skill, not a finance credential.

Who he is: Chris Camillo — no finance background, no analyst job, self-taught retail investor who built a publicly audited multi-decade track record by reading culture instead of spreadsheets.

The method — "social arbitrage" / observational investing:

  • Detect *change* in the world first: tech adoption, culture shifts, consumer behavior
  • Connect that change to public companies that benefit or get hurt by it
  • Buy before the change shows up in financials — the whole edge is the lag between people *talking* about a behavior change and the numbers reflecting it
  • Explicitly rejects both technical analysis and fundamentals: "I hate technicals. I hate fundamentals."

Where the signal comes from:

  • Social media comments, Reddit forums, TikTok virality
  • Core insight: people talk about changing their life *before* the evidence exists — e.g. developers debating AI adoption in Reddit threads is the leading indicator, not the earnings call
  • Not just tech — a large share is plain consumer behavior

Worked example — Sphere (Vegas):

  • Publicly traded stock most people don't realize is investable
  • Thesis: Sphere hadn't hit product-market fit until the *Wizard of Oz* show — old movie + AI-upscaled visuals + 4D effects (tornado, wind) on the giant projection screen
  • That combination created a shareable human experience → went viral on TikTok → international travel demand to Vegas
  • He heard people talking about it, started tracking, bought. Stock up ~220% over the past year; entry was in the low-$20s, roughly 6x from his cost basis

Track record (and how he wants it judged):

  • ~15-year audited results cited around 68%
  • Last MFM appearance he named three picks: Palantir, Bloom Energy ($92 → $240, +165%), Nvidia (up modestly)
  • He *refuses* the credit: individual picks are noise, could be beta or luck, and new information could flip him to the other side of a trade the next day
  • His stated standard: long-term, total-portfolio, audited, across multiple market regimes — nothing shorter counts
