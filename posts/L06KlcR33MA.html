Sometimes an invite lost in the mail truly is a blessing in disguise

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-13 · 20,050 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Getting excluded is your cue to build your own thing

If you're sitting in a day job waiting for a seat at someone else's table, this is the nudge to start the side hustle that you'd actually own.

What it is: A 45-second pep talk reframing exclusion as a push to build something you own.

How it works:

  • The instructional secret: a closed door means one of two things: that was never your room, or it's time to build your own. Both are a win.
  • Begging for a seat means you never own anything at that table, and the chair can be pulled out from under you.
  • Codie says she was pushed out of a company she helped build, and wasn't in the room when her own future got decided.
  • She says the people who left her out gave her back the years she'd have spent auditioning for them.
  • Reframe the rejection: your calendar just cleared, so spend that time building.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. No repos, tools, or frameworks. It's pure mindset.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle is the way you stop auditioning for other people's rooms. Own the table instead of fighting for a chair at theirs. There's no playbook here, though. It's motivation, not instruction.
