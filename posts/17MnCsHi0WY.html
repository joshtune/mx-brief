Who Actually Brings You Down

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-10 · 30,046 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Ignore critics who've never built anything.

If you're launching a side hustle, learning to filter signal from jealous noise protects the mental capital you need to keep building.

What it is: A short mindset pep-talk on separating supporters from detractors as you grow a business.

How it works:

  • The tell: winners (happy, successful, secure people) amplify you — they share, intro, clap, say your name in rooms you're not in.
  • The anti-tell: people who "poke holes in your business" or "humble you every time you level up" aren't being wise — they're spectators watching a builder build.
  • Reframe criticism as a status signal, not feedback: threatened people want you small so you stay useful to them (her PE/finance analogy: keep you employed, keep you shrunk).
  • Action: stop editing yourself to keep "uncomfortable people comfortable" — cut the drag, keep the connectors.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, frameworks, or URLs mentioned — this is pure thought-leadership, not a tactical playbook.

Why it matters for you: Side hustles die from founder morale as often as bad economics; knowing whose critique to weigh (builders) vs. discard (spectators) keeps you shipping.
