Flipping NYC brownstones for eight-figure exits.

Marco Brian's brownstone flip shows the exact buy/renovate/sell numbers behind a screen-free, blue-collar path to real millions.

What it is: Sam Parr profiles Marco Brian, a New Yorker who buys, restores, and resells historic brownstone homes in Manhattan.

The featured operator — Marco Brian:

  • Found via Instagram; buys/restores/sells brownstones in NYC
  • Positioned as a hands-on, physical-work alternative to a screen-based career
  • Sam sought him out specifically to understand the life *and* the economics

The deal breakdown (94 Bank Street):

  • Asset: 180-year-old brownstone, 94 Bank Street, West Village
  • Purchase price: $5.5M
  • Total invested (incl. renovation): ~$6M at this stage
  • Target resale: $17M
  • Implied gross spread: ~$11M before carrying/transaction costs

How the model works:

  • Acquire a distressed/dated historic property in a premium NYC neighborhood
  • Restore it (renovation capital layered on top of purchase price)
  • Resell to the top of the market where scarcity + location drive price
  • Margin comes from the gap between a fixer purchase and a finished, trophy-home comp

Tools & links:

  • No repos, tools, or software mentioned — this is a physical real-estate play

Why it matters for you: A clean, number-by-number case study of a single operator's money machine — what he does (buy → restore → flip brownstones) and the exact spread ($5.5M in, ~$6M invested, $17M out) that makes it work.
