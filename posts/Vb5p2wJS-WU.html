Faith-based pep talk: trust your business idea and start

Skip it for tactics, since the whole point is that waiting until you feel ready is what keeps most side hustles from ever starting.

What it is: A ~30-second faith-flavored motivational quote about trusting a business idea enough to act on it.

How it works:

  • The claim: An idea arrives with the capacity to execute it, so your bank account isn't the deciding factor. Your belief is.
  • The instruction: Don't ask "why me?" or list reasons you can't. Trust the idea and move.
  • The punchline: "Most don't listen, so they never get started." The real competitor is inaction.
  • The catch: It's a religious framing with no steps, numbers, or method. Nothing here is verifiable or repeatable.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in the video.

Why it matters for you: The only usable secret is to start before you feel funded or qualified, because most side hustles die at "someday" rather than from a bad idea. Pair it with Codie's actual cash-flow and acquisition content for the how.
