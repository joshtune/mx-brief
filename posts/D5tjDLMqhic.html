Stay focused. Narrow your group.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-11 · 25,182 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Cut your circle down to allies who invest in you.

Your side hustle's momentum dies faster from lukewarm friends than from bad ideas — this reframes who gets access to your plans.

What it is: A 60-second mindset clip on guarding your inner circle while you build.

How it works:

  • Treat ideas like equity — only share with people who've proven they can invest (energy, capital, or real feedback), not everyone who asks.
  • Sharper, not bigger — the goal isn't a larger network, it's a smaller one that pushes you, protects you, and pours into you.
  • Proximity ≠ loyalty — being close to you doesn't mean someone's on your side; alignment does. People quietly resent a win that reminds them they've stalled.
  • Play for freedom, not followers — filter your circle by who advances the mission, not who inflates your ego.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or external resources mentioned — this is a pure thought-leadership short.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret here is an operating filter — vet who gets your unbaked ideas the way you'd vet a co-investor — which protects a side hustle's fragile early momentum from well-meaning skeptics.
