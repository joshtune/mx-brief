How To Hire Top Talent

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-05 · 10,608 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Earned-equity comp to hire top talent cheaply.

It shows how to attract A-players for your side hustle without handing away real equity or overpaying upfront.

What it is: The "earned equity" framework — pay top hires with equity they only get if they hit measurable value targets.

How it works:

  • Don't gift equity. Instead of "Here's 5%," say "Here's a *path* to 5% if you create measurable value."
  • Stack the comp in three layers. Example COO offer: base $180k + target bonus $120k + an equity earnout pool.
  • Gate the equity on hard metrics. The 5% pool vests only when EBITDA grows from $2M to $4M *and* margins improve by 5 points.
  • Sell the upside. Show the hire that pool is worth $3–5M at exit — so they're motivated to hit the numbers.
  • Protect your downside. Miss the goals? They're still paid well (base + bonus), but you keep your equity and don't "lose your shirt."
  • Guiding principle: Munger's "incentives follow the incentives" — structure pay so their payoff only happens when yours does.
