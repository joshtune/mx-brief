What it is: A short clip of Palmer Luckey (founder of Anduril) answering a conference question: "If you started from zero with no connections, what would you do?"

How it works (the pitch):

  • The idea: Fight divorce with oxytocin-assisted marriage counseling.
  • The science claim: Mammal pair bonding is well understood, and you can increase oxytocin uptake.
  • The market claim: Many people want to stay together, and he argues most couples who split should stay together, especially for their kids.
  • The regulatory angle: Luckey says the space is totally unregulated, and unregulated spaces are easier to build in.
  • The sourcing: He says you can buy a kilogram of oxytocin from Sigma-Aldrich right now.
  • The host take: The hosts admire that he had a novel, surprising idea and a polished pitch ready on the spot.
