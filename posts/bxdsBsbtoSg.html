AI Can FINALLY Edit Videos For You

Matt Wolfe · 2026-07-08 · 15,259 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Claude-powered AI video editor Palmier automates your timeline.

It's a real-world look at agentic UX — pull an AI alongside your app and let it drive the workspace by prompt — which is the interaction pattern you could bolt onto your own web apps.

What it is: Palmier (aka Palm AI) — a prompt-driven video editor where Claude handles the timeline instead of you dragging clips.

How it works:

  • You open the editor with Claude docked alongside it and type intents like "organize my media" — it auto-sorts files into folders
  • Say "edit it for me" and it trims clips, adjusts speeds, adds text and sound effects
  • Missing a shot? It generates B-roll on the fly via video-AI models and drops it into the timeline
  • Matt hasn't personally tested it — this is a first-look reaction, not a hands-on review

Pricing reality check:

  • $29/mo → 5,000 credits
  • 5,000 credits ≈ only 3–7 minutes of generated video (~2–4 short-form clips)
  • Revisions, extra usage, and heavy AI-footage projects stack costs fast — still likely cheaper than a human editor, quality TBD

Tools & links:

  • Adobe Premiere / Illustrator / InDesign — shipping the same text-prompt-driven editing pattern natively
  • futuretools.io — Matt's AI tool directory

Why it matters for you: The signal isn't video editing — it's the "agent docked next to your app, driven by natural-language intents that mutate app state" pattern, which is exactly the kind of AI UX you could design into your web apps.
