The Equation of Success

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-22 · 38,236 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Time plus consistency is the real success formula.

A 60-second gut-check that the side hustle you keep abandoning would probably work if you just stuck with it past the 18-month mark.

What it is: A short, motivational riff arguing that wealth comes from staying power, not genius or speed.

How it works:

  • Cites that the average millionaire is 49 and the average billionaire hit a billion at 67 — wealth compounds slowly.
  • Notes the average American stays at a job ~1.5 years, often making lateral moves that don't even pay more.
  • Argues that simply having the focus to stick with one thing past 18 months already puts you above average.
  • Lands on the thesis: time + consistency = success. No secret tactic, just refusal to quit.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned in this clip — it's a pure mindset piece.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret here is anti-tactic — the #1 predictor of a side hustle paying off isn't the idea, it's not bailing on it after a year; pick one and outlast your own boredom.
