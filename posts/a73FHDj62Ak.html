M6 Mac mini vs M4: first benchmarks

It's a 2-minute first look at the M6 Mac mini against the M4 mini, but it never covers AI workloads or prices, so it's not the video for your cost question.

What it is: A short first-look benchmark of the new M6 Mac mini against his M4 Mac mini, plus an M5 MacBook Pro for reference.

How it works:

  • Geekbench 6: M6 scored 4,085 vs 4,190 for the M5 MacBook Pro, so the M6 looked slower.
  • Geekbench 7 rerun: the M5 drops to 3,715 under the new version, so the two scores can't be compared across versions. The M6 Geekbench 7 number isn't given.
  • Speedometer (browser): the M6 hit 73, the highest he's ever seen.
  • Python fractal test: a multi-core stress test that maxes out every core. The M4 has 10 cores and the M6 has 12.
  • Power draw: about 40W on the M4 and about 48W on the M6.
  • Result: the M6 scored 32.8 against 21.5 for the M4, roughly 50% faster. He never says what the unit is and just calls it "a huge difference."

What's missing:

  • No AI or LLM tests. There's no local model, no tokens/sec and no RAM configuration.
  • No pricing. The M6 mini isn't priced against anything.
  • No exact specs. Chip variant and memory aren't stated in the transcript.
