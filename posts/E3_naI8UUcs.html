How To Make A LOT of Money

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-14 · 45,628 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Sell oxygen, not dessert: pick painful, urgent problems.

If your side hustle sells something customers can ignore for 30 days, this 60-second filter tells you to pivot before you waste your nights and weekends.

What it is: A mental model sorting businesses into 'dessert' (nice-to-have) and 'oxygen' (must-have) to predict which ones print money.

How it works:

  • Dessert business: sells a want, like birthday cakes, life coaching, or a brand refresh. The customer can postpone forever, so budgets are small and the pain is low.
  • Oxygen business: sells a need, like emergency plumbing at 2 a.m. or payroll software when 40 employees expect a check Friday. Customers find the budget, and the problem hurts.
  • Two-part test: need alone isn't enough. The problem must also be painful and have a budget behind it.
  • The 30-day test: what happens to your customer if they buy nothing from you for 30 days? If the honest answer is 'nothing much', you're selling dessert.
  • The 2x2 matrix: plot budget against pain. Aim for the top-right corner, where customers come to you.
  • Instructional secret: the most profitable businesses sell something nobody *wants* to buy but everybody *has* to. Pain plus budget beats passion.
