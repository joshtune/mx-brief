Matt's grab-bag of 12 overlooked AI and tech demos

Mostly AR and robotics eye candy with little for a web app builder, so skip it unless you want a pulse check on where AI video is heading.

What it is: A 12-item roundup of AI and tech demos that missed Matt's weekly news videos. The transcript covers only the first item, Simulon, in full.

How it works:

  • Simulon is an iPhone app (Android is coming) that places 3D characters in your real room through the camera.
  • You record a take, then the app uploads it to the cloud. A re-render redraws the video with the character reacting to your hands, props, and speech.
  • The reactions aren't scripted. The founder's Dracula clip had the character jump off an RC car at the table edge without any prompting.
  • The render takes a couple of minutes. Matt's test with a gorilla on his desk did react to petting and to a pen poke.
  • Free plan: 100 starter credits that run out fast, then $15/month.
  • The founder's long-term goal is live AR glasses.

Tools & links:

  • Simulon (https://simulon.com), the AR character app above. The URL isn't given in the transcript, so verify it.
  • The rest are teased but cut off in the transcript: a Tencent world model, an AI voice demo (said to rival Google's), glasses that read silent speech, a ball-catching robot, and a walking robot with an old Android phone as its brain. Nothing is detailed and no links are given.
  • FutureTools is Matt's directory, which he points to for tools.

Why it matters for you: Very little. The content is consumer AR and robotics with no web dev angle. The one loose tie is that video-in, scene-aware generation is heading toward cloud APIs you could someday call from an app.
