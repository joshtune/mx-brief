Does Your Appearance Affect Your Career?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-14 · 28,536 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
How looking professional makes you more money.

If you run a service side hustle, how you and your crew show up at the door directly determines whether customers trust you enough to pay you.

What it is: Codie's contrarian take that appearance is a measurable revenue lever, not vanity — backed by research and applied to small service businesses.

How it works:

  • Uniforms first — every person on your team dresses the same; instant signal of legitimacy and being a real operation.
  • Pro vs. amateur test — ask "do I look like a pro or an amateur?" If you want pro pay, you have to look the part.
  • Symmetry & cleanliness over "hotness" — it's not about being attractive; it's clean, professional, put-together. No stains, no untucked shirt, no off-putting first impression.
  • The trust-at-the-door test — when your window washer shows up, would a single mom alone in the house feel safe opening the door? If the honest answer is no, you're losing money.
  • Cites Harvard and Stanford studies that presentation correlates with earnings.

Why it matters for you: For a side hustle in any service trade (cleaning, landscaping, handyman), the cheapest growth hack is a $30 uniform and a clean appearance — it converts skeptical customers into paying ones before you say a word.
