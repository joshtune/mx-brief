$98K in 60 days from a $9 free community

It's a near-zero-cost side hustle blueprint: turn whatever you already know into a free community, then sell $15K programs entirely over DMs.

What it is: Nico built a free online community (about $9 to start), grew it to ~250 members from a few low-view TikToks, and sold ~$98K of high-ticket coaching in ~60 days. He closed it all in DMs, with no sales calls.

How it works:

  • Free group as the funnel: He launched a free online community and promoted it with a few TikToks that usually get only a few hundred views. No ads.
  • Tiny conversion, huge ticket: Of ~250 members, only about a dozen bought. At ~$15,000 per program, that is nearly $100K.
  • Close in DMs only: He never gets on a sales call. The episode promises the exact DM scripts he uses to close $15K deals.
  • Three-button poll: A poll inside the community shows who is ready to buy. This is promised later in the episode, so the details aren't in this excerpt.
  • Platform promo hacks: The episode closes with four tactics for getting free promotion from the app that hosts the community. They aren't named in this excerpt.
  • Not just AI coaching: Most buyers aren't buying AI. The offer works for fitness, mindset, or any skill you already have.
  • Low barrier, per Nico: He says you can be running in 24 hours with no product, no AI knowledge and no desire to build a product. If it fails, you're out $9.

Why it matters for you: It's a cheap test of the side-hustle idea: pick a skill you have, build a free audience, and sell a high-ticket offer by DM. Be skeptical, though. These are Nico's own numbers, only ~12 buyers, and a 2-month track record.
