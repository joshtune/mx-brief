How To Be An Excellent Leader

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-02 · 15,580 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Great leaders choose brutal honesty over being liked.

If your side hustle grows into a team, the instinct to be nice will quietly cap how good your people—and your business—ever get.

What it is: A short manifesto on leading by honesty over niceness.

How it works:

  • Reframes the goal of leadership: your job isn't to be liked, it's to make people *better* for having worked with you.
  • Accepts the cost up front — honest leadership means some people leave, some can't keep up, some get upset. That's treated as a feature, not a bug.
  • Core operating line: *"We do not do what we want. We do what is required."* Discipline over comfort, for you and your team.
  • Separates kindness from softness — being honest *is* the kind act; niceness that avoids hard truths is the selfish one.

Tools & links:

  • No tools, repos, or external resources referenced in this clip — it's pure principle.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle stalls the moment you can't have hard conversations with the first person you hire; this is the mindset that lets you build a team instead of a fan club.
