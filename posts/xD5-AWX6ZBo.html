How it works (story 1, the narcissism test):

  • After the exit he felt lost and hired a life-direction coach.
  • The coach had him take an unusual personality test at the start.
  • The results came back weeks later with low scores across most categories and one off-the-charts score: narcissism, about 110 on the scale.
  • The coach's reading: the trait is not an insult. It means you feel special, and the norms and averages of the world don't fully apply to you.
  • The upside: it pushes you to act despite the odds. '90% of businesses fail? I'll be different.' Shaan says that belief has served him.
  • The downside: the specialness is vague. You don't know what is special about you, so you look to others to confirm it.
  • That drives seeking validation through social media, content creation, and public speaking, which Shaan admits he has built his life around.
  • Validation gives a high when you get it and a lower-than-normal low when you don't. You don't need criticism to feel it. Just not feeling seen is enough.
  • Shaan's framing: 'the dose makes the poison.'
