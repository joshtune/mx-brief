The Most Profitable Business You Can Run From a Phone

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-08 · 14,559 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Leasing semi-trailers to truckers, run entirely from a phone

It's a side hustle with a near-zero startup cost: lease a trailer for about $350/mo, sublease it for about $750/mo, and let Facebook Marketplace find the customers.

What it is: Trailer leasing (rent-to-own-style rentals) for truckers. Eli leases 53 ft semi-trailers from a big supplier and subleases them to owner-operators at a markup.

How it works:

  • Origin: In Nov 2019 Eli was a Connecticut real estate agent dispatching truckers (matching them with loads for roughly $80 a pop). Drivers kept asking where they could get equipment.
  • First deal: His uncle had poor credit and asked to rent a trailer. Eli found a supplier, leased a 53 ft trailer for about $350/mo and rented it to his uncle for about $750/mo, a margin of about $400/mo on one unit.
  • Startup cost: Insurance only. He says that was the whole cost to start.
  • Customer pitch: Truckers with weak credit can't easily get equipment from big lessors, so he rents to them where others won't.
  • Lead gen: Facebook Marketplace posts. He claims about $7,500/mo recurring within the first two months.
  • Scale: After about 6 years he had 300 trailers and over $50k/mo profit. The teaser also claims $250k/mo, which is presumably revenue, though the clip doesn't explain it.
  • Staffing: Two virtual employees he has never met in person. He runs it from his phone while living in Colombia.
  • Dispatching background: Dispatching needs no license. Brokering loads (representing the shipper) does. Dispatchers find loads on load boards like DAT and take a thin per-load margin. Eli said that margin was too small, which is why he moved to equipment.
  • AI angle: He says any AI can take you 90% of the way there on the paperwork and setup.
  • Caveat: The transcript cuts off during the dispatching backstory. It never reaches supplier sourcing, insurance specifics, contracts, deposits or collections, so those details are still unknown.
