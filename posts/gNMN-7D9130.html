How it works:

  • Pauses: it tightens every pause without speeding up his voice, so it feels fast but still sounds like him.

  • B-roll: it finds clips itself, using real footage or screen recordings of the sites he mentions.
  • AI video: it generates AI video when no real footage exists.
  • Motion graphics: it builds them in.
  • Audio: it mixes music about 11 dB under his voice and times a sound effect to each on-screen action.
