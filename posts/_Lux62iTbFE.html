What it is: A short clip retelling a (secondhand, unverified) private-dinner anecdote about what Elon Musk would do if SpaceX and Tesla both failed and he lost all his money.

How it works:

  • The scenario: SpaceX and Tesla both fail and he loses every dollar.
  • Step 1, downsize: Move to the cheapest apartment he could find.
  • Step 2, get a computer: Buy one and start building the next thing within 24 hours, not a week later and not after processing the loss.
  • Emotional rule: Losing the money wouldn't bother him, but losing the companies would devastate him. He'd allow one night of that, then start building the next day.
  • The next idea: He doesn't know what it would be, and not knowing wouldn't slow him down. 'The thing finds you. You just have to be building when it arrives.'
  • Host's takeaway: The speaker says their dad gave them the same advice: 'It's about motion, not direction.' Once you're moving, direction gets easier to figure out and is cheap to change.
