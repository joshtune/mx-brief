He Built an AI System That Runs His Entire Business

Matt Wolfe · 2026-11-11 · 90,192 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Peter Yang's skills-based AI chief-of-staff setup

Mostly creator-business automation, not web app building, so skip it unless you want a pattern for portable, reusable Claude Code skills.

What it is: Matt interviews ex-Roblox PM Peter Yang about the personal AI system he uses to run his one-person content business. (The transcript supplied covers only the intro and system overview.)

How it works:

  • Portable skills: a skill is a saved set of instructions for one job. The same skills are shared across ChatGPT, Claude Code and Grok Bot, so he isn't locked into one model or tool.
  • Chief of staff: runs twice a day, reads email, calendar and Slack, and says what needs attention.
  • Advisor: checks decisions against his goals for the year and calls him out when he drifts.
  • Council: several AI perspectives that he runs his biggest decisions past.
  • Content skills: podcast guest research, interview guides, titles and thumbnails, video editing, intros, and turning one recording into a newsletter plus social posts.
  • Scheduled tasks: emailed business stats, a fitness check-in, and weekend family activity ideas.
  • Skill editor: a meta-skill whose job is keeping his other skills clean as he tweaks them. Matt calls it the smartest part, but the supplied transcript cuts off before it's explained.

Tools & links:

  • Claude Code — one of the three harnesses sharing his skills
  • FutureTools — Matt's AI tools directory (channel link, not covered in the video)

Why it matters for you: The only transferable idea for web app work is the tool-agnostic skills library, plus a meta-skill that keeps it tidy. Everything else is content-creator and personal productivity workflow.
