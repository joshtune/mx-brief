The Simplest $300/Hour Side Hustle Anyone Can Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-29 · 28,566 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Couch flipping: buy cheap furniture, resell for 10x.

It's the lowest-barrier side hustle in the Koerner catalog — no capital, no code, no full-time commitment, and the margins are absurd enough that a 17-year-old is clearing thousands.

What it is: Buy used couches and furniture cheap (or free) off marketplace listings, clean them up, photograph them well, relist at a massive markup. Chris frames it as a ~$200–300/hour side hustle with essentially zero downside risk.

How it works:

  • Source cheap inventory. People constantly want stuff *gone* — free curb pickups, cheap Facebook Marketplace/Craigslist listings, moving-out dumps. Chris mentions grabbing a free item and reselling it for ~$1,000, and buying one couch for $100.
  • Fix the presentation, not the couch. The whole arbitrage is that sellers post bad photos in bad rooms. Snap a photo, run it through an AI image tool to generate a clean staged background, and the same couch looks like retail.
  • List high, let buyers negotiate down. That $100 couch got listed at $1,780. A buyer countered at $1,580 the same night — a ~15x return on one item.
  • Time-box it. Chris describes going out for 20 minutes and clearing $300 on a single couch. It's transactional, not a business you have to run daily.
  • The moat is physical labor. Quoting the 1-800-GOT-JUNK CEO: "As long as pianos weigh 700 pounds, we're going to be in this." Heavy, awkward objects that nobody wants to move are structurally AI-proof — this opportunity exists in 5 and 20 years.
  • Proof of concept: the episode is a last-minute road trip to Highland, Utah to interview Bentley, a 17-year-old flipping couches for thousands a month.

The real lesson: The margin isn't in the furniture, it's in the photography. You're not arbitraging couches, you're arbitraging listing quality — which is exactly the kind of gap an engineer can systematize (bulk scraping listings, auto-staging images, price comping).

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle with no startup capital, no ongoing time commitment, and no technical debt, this is a weekend-hours cash machine — and the AI-staging step is a workflow you could automate and scale well past what a 17-year-old does by hand.
