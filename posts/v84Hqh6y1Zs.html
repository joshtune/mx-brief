"Delivery Is the Currency of Trust" - How to Answer Mistake Questions

A Life Engineered · 2026-06-21 · 12,834 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Framework for answering interview 'mistake' questions well.

If you're interviewing, this hands you a clean framework for the dreaded 'tell me about a mistake' question — and a separate trick to turn a good interview into a standout one.

What it is: A two-part interview playbook — how to answer mistake/failure questions, plus how to elevate any interview from good to exceptional.

How it works:

  • The framing (via former Amazon VP Ethan Evans): *delivery is the currency of trust*. Mistake questions are really probing whether you lose trust by failing to deliver what you said you would.
  • The answer framework: state the reason for your decision → acknowledge it turned out incorrect because of ambiguity and risk → own that you're not perfect. Don't hide the miss; explain the reasoning that was sound at the time.
  • The standout move: an okay interview becomes exceptional when you *teach the interviewer something they don't already know* — ideally in a subject they know deeply.
  • Real example: a systems engineer candidate aced the coding round, then taught the interviewer about systems. He was hired and his career took off fast.
