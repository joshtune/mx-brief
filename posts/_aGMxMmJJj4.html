The Easiest AI Side Hustle Anyone Can Start

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-06-26 · 23,360 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Build free AI websites for local businesses, charge monthly rent.

If you want a side hustle with near-zero cost and recurring revenue, this is a copy-paste playbook for selling websites to local businesses on a subscription.

What it is: A productized service side hustle — build websites for local businesses using AI, then rent them back at $50–100/month with near-zero ongoing cost.

How it works:

  • Pick a high-volume niche (he chose handyman — 100k+ in the US, so the same site copy-pastes endlessly, swapping name + location)
  • Use Go High Level's vibe-coding tool ("Ask AI" tab) to generate the site, integrated directly with its built-in CRM/calendar/workflows
  • Prompt-engineer first: ask Claude (Opus 4.8) or ChatGPT to write a *meta-prompt* that knows how to talk to GHL — instructs it to wire up CRM, calendar, workflows, SEO/AEO meta-descriptions, and list every DFW suburb over 50k people
  • Feed it a reference URL (he googled a random handyman site) so the AI matches a known-good layout
  • Build the site *before* outreach, then cold-text local owners from your real phone: "Hey, I built you a free website" → show it → offer to let them keep it for ~$75/mo
  • Economics: ~$0 upfront, ~$0/mo to maintain, and each new client adds $0 cost — so revenue scales while costs stay flat. Free site = foot in the door to upsell pricier services later
  • Teases a "text message hack" to stand out against SMS spam (not revealed in this clip)

Tools & links:

  • Go High Level — CRM + AI website/vibe-coding platform; his link gives 30 days free (vs. 14 elsewhere), AI tools free 30 days then ~$50/mo
  • Claude / Opus 4.8 — used to generate the master prompt for GHL
  • ChatGPT or Grok — interchangeable alternatives for the prompt step
  • TKOPOD newsletter and TKOwners community

Why it matters for you: Concrete, low-capital side hustle — productize one AI-built website template, sell recurring subscriptions to local businesses, and let the meta-prompt + GHL do the heavy lifting so each new customer is pure margin.
