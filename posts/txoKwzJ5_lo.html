How I Made My First Million (And You Can Too)

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-10 · 25,025 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Use your 9-5 to buy businesses incrementally.

It reframes the side hustle as a step-by-step ownership ladder funded by your day job—no risky quit required.

What it is: Codie's "business buyer ladder"—a three-step path to your first million by buying ownership instead of quitting to start a startup.

How it works:

  • Step 1: Stay employed and use your 9-5 paycheck to buy *tiny* ownership stakes in existing businesses on the side—small cash outlay.
  • Step 2: Recycle the cash flow from those small deals into bigger pieces of businesses.
  • Step 3: Eventually buy *whole* businesses outright—while everyone else waits for the "perfect time."
  • The core mechanism is compounding: paycheck funds equity → equity throws off cash flow → cash flow funds larger equity.

The contrarian take:

  • She calls "just quit and go all in" the dumbest financial advice online.
  • Her own first million came while still employed in corporate—she was too scared to quit because the salary was good, and that fear was an asset, not a weakness.
  • Buying businesses "doesn't have to change your entire life"—you start really small.

Why it matters for you: The instructional secret is that a side hustle doesn't have to mean building something from scratch or risking your income—you can ladder into ownership using the steady paycheck you already have as the fuel.
