Shaan: AI's real money is atoms, not bits

Not a profile of one operator: it's Shaan's thesis that AI in software and content is a race to the bottom, and the money is in physical industries like drugs, food and warfare.

What it is: A thought-leadership riff (Ep. 880) where Shaan Puri argues the next 5 years of AI upside is in "the world of things" (physical industries), not digital products. Sam Parr co-hosts. No single founder is profiled. The transcript I was given cuts off about 7 minutes in, during the first industry (drugs).

Featured individuals & businesses (what's in the transcript):

  • Anthropic — held up as the best business in capitalism so far.
  • Roughly $2T of value created in about 5 years (the hosts' claim).
  • 7 co-founders, about 2,500 days since founding, about $96M of net worth created per day collectively.
  • Each founder reportedly owns about 2-2.5% now.
  • Used as the "why am I not doing something big" motivator, not as a case study.
  • The GitLab founder — described as having cured his own cancer.
  • Sequenced his own tumor.

  • Shaan says about 7 other people are doing the same self-cure approach.
  • Revenue details weren't given; the service is the only monetization mentioned.
  • Eli Lilly — praised for leaning hard into AI.
  • Buying large amounts of compute.
  • The transcript cuts off mid-sentence here.

The thesis, step by step:

  • Rule: "skate to where the puck is going." Ask what you'll look back on in 4-5 years and say "that was the time."
  • 5 years ago the right bet was working at, or building on, model companies:
  • Open AI / Anthropic
  • Runway for video
  • Midjourney for images
  • ChatGPT for text
  • It wasn't obvious then, and only a few people saw it.
  • Today's AI hype (coding, personal agents, LLM wrappers) is the crowded far-left corner.
  • Shaan's verdict on AI applied to bits:
  • AI content: race to the bottom.
  • AI-built software/SaaS/tools: race to the bottom ("do it if your heart's in it").
  • His alternative: AI applied to atoms, "the world of things."

Industry #1: Drugs & miracles (the only one with detail in the transcript):

  • Drugs traditionally take about 10 years and $2B, with an 80-90% failure rate.
  • Those numbers mean only large pharma can play.
  • AI collapses discovery cost, which opens the game to individuals and small teams.
  • Evidence is people running personalized, AI-driven cancer treatment on themselves.
