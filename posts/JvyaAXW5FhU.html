The question on everyone’s mind: How do you hire the right person?

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-14 · 6,037 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Hire proven winners, not promising beginners.

If your side hustle ever needs someone besides you, this is the filter that keeps you from paying to train a stranger who might flop.

What it is: A short hiring heuristic from Codie: define what "good" looks like, then hire only people who have already won at that exact thing.

How it works:

  • Know what good looks like first. Write a profile for the role before you interview anyone. Without a benchmark you're hiring on vibes.
  • Past behavior predicts future behavior. Look for a track record of winning: top of the sales board, ran a team, ranked #1 somewhere else.
  • Filter out the whiners. Someone with a history of excuses rather than results is a low-odds bet for a top performer.
  • Hire for the exact thing. Don't say "I think they'll be great at something they've never done." Training costs your hours and your cash flow.
  • Target day-one contributors. Look for people you can plug in who hit the ground running.
  • Operator ≠ employee. An operator (an A player) should be an independent center of gravity who runs their lane without you pushing.

The instructional secret: The hire is decided by the scorecard and their win history, not by the interview. Define the win, then find someone who has already done it.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. The "profile" is an internal scorecard, so a one-page doc per role works.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle has no slack for training time or bad hires, so hire people who've already won at the job and can run it without you.
