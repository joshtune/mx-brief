Buying Costco return pallets cheap, reselling online for profit

It's a low-barrier, cash-in-hand side hustle with a documented 10-day sprint to $10K profit — no code, no audience, no product to build.

What it is: Chris buys Costco customer-return merchandise (pallets/lots) for pennies on the dollar and resells the items individually online — with millionaire reseller Shannon flown in from California to run a live $10K-in-10-days challenge in Texas.

How it works:

  • Costco offloads returned inventory in bulk rather than restocking it — big-ticket, bulky items (gazebos, patio sets, furniture) are the sweet spot because return shipping is what killed them for Costco in the first place
  • You buy the lot at a steep discount to retail. Transcript example: a $3,400 gazebo acquired for $200 all-in including shipping; an $1,800 patio set also for $200
  • Resell locally/online at a fraction of MSRP and still clear multiples on cost — the margin is so wide you don't need to hit retail price to win
  • Chris's framing: "It's not gambling. If you put in the number, you leave." Set your max bid per lot, walk away above it, and the math stops being a gamble
