Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-17 · 13,155 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Three phrases that quietly kill your credibility at work.

If you're running a side hustle while pitching investors, vendors, or customers, these verbal tics are the difference between being taken seriously and being dismissed before you finish your sentence.

What it is: A quick behavioral callout — not a business tactic, but a communication fix that affects how seriously people take your business.

How it works:

  • "I'm probably wrong" — pre-emptively discredits your own point before anyone else can; say the thing, let it stand or fall on its merits
  • "I'm the worst" — self-deprecating filler that your subconscious files as literal evidence against you over time
  • "Sorry" (overused) — apologizing for showing up, speaking, or existing signals you don't believe you belong in the room

Tools & links:

  • None — this is a mindset/communication note, no tools or resources referenced

Why it matters for you: As a side-hustle operator, you're constantly pitching yourself — to lenders, landlords, employees, customers — and these three phrases are self-sabotage that undercuts your authority before you've made your case.
