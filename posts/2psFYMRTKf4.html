Do You Want The Excuse Or The Life

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-30 · 4,137 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Your true excuse is still keeping you broke.

If you're sitting on a side hustle idea and a valid reason not to start, this is a 60-second kick in the pants about why that reason is the trap.

What it is: A short motivational pep-talk, not a tutorial. The instructional secret is a mindset reframe: stop using a true story as a ceiling and use it as fuel.

How it works:

  • Valid ≠ useful. Your excuse can be 100% real and still be what's costing you money.
  • Pain explains the start, not the stop. Your history tells you where you began. It doesn't get a vote on where you end up.
  • Daily choice. Passion and potential share a body. Each morning you pick which one makes the decisions.
  • True excuses are the dangerous ones. People validate them, so nobody challenges you. You get to stay comfortable while the story "slowly steals your future."
  • Sympathy doesn't pay. Understanding is nice, but it won't build a business or cover the mortgage.
  • Same story, different decision. Someone else has your exact backstory and treated it as fuel instead of a ceiling.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned. No tools, repos, or frameworks, just a reframe.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle usually dies from a reasonable excuse (no time, no money, bad past), not from a bad idea. Spot yours, then decide whether it's a ceiling or fuel.
