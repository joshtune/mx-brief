Anyone Can Build. THIS Will Get You Noticed

A Life Engineered · 2026-11-03 · 214 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stop building tutorial projects; solve real people's problems.

If your portfolio is chatbots and to-do apps, this tells you why hiring managers skim past it and what to build instead.

What it is: A pitch to swap generic portfolio projects for real solutions to real problems, with measurable outcomes. It ends with a plug for his TopTier coaching program.

The message to engineers:

  • Your chatbot, to-do app, and Titanic predictor are noise. Hiring managers have seen 500 of them.
  • "I can build things" is no longer a differentiator, because AI can build things too.
  • Showcase impact, not skills. Ask "Whose pain can I alleviate?", not "What can I build?"
  • Companies hire people to solve problems that make them money. They don't hire for knowledge alone.

What to prepare for:

  • Build-skill alone won't get you noticed in a market where AI does the building.
  • Keeping up with AI and layoff rumors while shipping more than ever is exhausting. He treats that as the new normal.
  • The people who get ahead know what their company rewards and make sure the right people see them deliver it.

Instructional: do this week:

  • Reach out to 3 people in your network: a startup friend, a small-business contact, or a former colleague.
  • Ask one question: "What's the most annoying, repetitive part of your job right now?"
  • Then just listen. Don't pitch or solve it live.
  • Pick one of those problems as your next project. It needs a real person, real stakes, and a real problem.
  • Write it up as an outcome, not a tech stack.
  • Weak: "I built a chatbot using Python and Flask."
  • Strong: "I reduced manual data entry by 6 hours per week for a real estate agency."
