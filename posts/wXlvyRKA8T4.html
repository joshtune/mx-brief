Books For Great Leaders

Codie Sanchez · 2026-11-23 · 15,854 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Four books Codie says make you a better leader

If your side hustle will ever need help from other people, this is a 4-book reading list for hiring and leading them, and the secret is to stop doing it all yourself.

What it is: A short recommendation list of four leadership books from someone who says she has hired thousands of people.

How it works:

  • Who Not How — Before you hunt for more information, ask who could hand you the whole playbook. Delegating the learning is the cheat code.
  • 33 Strategies of War — Running a business is like war, so you have to understand what the enemy looks like to lead people through it.
  • Owner Beyond — Codie's own book, which she admits is self-serving. It's about hiring people better than you and letting them make you better. She also says to hand it to those hires as a guidebook, so even a weak leader can give them the cheat code.
  • Principles by Ray Dalio — More theoretical. It breaks down the leadership principles that, if you adopt them, might also make you a billionaire (her words, said half-jokingly).

Instructional secret: Leadership is mostly leverage. Replace research with a person who already knows, hire above your level, and give people a written playbook instead of re-explaining yourself every time.

Tools & links:

  • No URLs in the transcript. The books are *Who Not How* (Dan Sullivan and Benjamin Hardy), *The 33 Strategies of War* (Robert Greene), *Owner Beyond* (Codie Sanchez) and *Principles* (Ray Dalio).

Why it matters for you: A side hustle hits a ceiling when you are the only worker, and these books cover how to buy back your time by finding the right people and handing them the playbook.

Caveats: It's a list, not a tutorial. There are no frameworks or steps beyond the one-line pitch for each book, and one of the four is her own.
