The Secret To Sales

Codie Sanchez · 2026-08-02 · 10,007 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
The APPLE framework replaces sales talent with a system.

If your side hustle stalls because you hate selling, this reframes sales as a repeatable process you can build once and hand off — not a personality trait you're missing.

What it is: A 5-step sales framework (APPLE) that swaps charisma and "magic closing lines" for a system anyone can run.

How it works:

  • A — Avatar: Stop trying to convince people. Go find the ones already in enough pain that they're *thrilled* you solve it. Selection beats persuasion.
  • P — Prime: Show the gap between where they are and where they should be. You're not selling the product — you're selling the bridge across that gap.
  • P — Proof: Stack overwhelming, undeniable evidence until doubt looks silly. Reviews, photos, before-and-afters, and (her favorite) video testimonials.
  • L — Lock: Close the deal — but by this point the close should feel like handing someone a gift they already own. You're just wrapping it.
  • E — Engine: Systematize it so hard that "a 22-year-old with a ring light and a CRM" can run it in a week. McDonald's doesn't hire burger geniuses; it builds teenager-proof systems.

The core contrarian take:

  • Obsessing over closing lines is content bait, not revenue. It gets views; it doesn't close business.
  • You don't need to be a good salesperson to make a lot of money — you need a good sales *engine*.

Why it matters for you: A side hustle dies the moment it depends on you personally being persuasive — APPLE is the argument for building sales as a documented, delegable pipeline from day one, so the thing keeps earning when you're not in the room.
