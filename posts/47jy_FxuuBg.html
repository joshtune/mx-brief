Shaan (100M+ podcast downloads, millions of YouTube subs) breaks down the counterintuitive rules he actually used, not generic "post consistently" advice.

What it is: Shaan Puri (co-host, sold companies, built My First Million into a 100M+ download podcast) shares his non-obvious audience-building lessons, sponsored/produced with HubSpot as a companion guide.

How it works:

  • Lesson 1 — Who, not how many: Optimize for the *right* audience, not raw follower count. Cites Tim Ferriss: 100 Fortune 100 CEOs > 100,000 random readers.
  • Car analogy: GM sells 2.6M cars/year and is worth $50B; Ferrari sells 13,000 cars/year and is worth $80B — fewer, higher-value customers can beat mass reach.
  • Implication: aim for upmarket/niche-intelligent content over mass-market if that's your audience's nature.
  • Lesson 2 — Find your inner nerd: Don't ask "what does the audience want" — ask "what do I nerd out about to an abnormal degree." Push out what's already in you rather than reverse-engineering demand.
  • Lean into weirdness rather than sanding it down — the internet has enough scale to find your niche audience no matter how narrow.
  • Examples cited: a perfume-tour creator in NYC who out-nerds everyone on scent; a menswear creator obsessing over pant cuffs, hems, and cuts.
  • Video is cut short in the transcript before Lessons 3+ (rule of "100 videos," "A+ content with C-minus delivery," "choose one thing to do better") are fully explained — full breakdown likely in the complete video.
