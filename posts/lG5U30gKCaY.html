How a Mom of 3 Makes $50K a Month With AI (No Experience)

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-02 · 54,591 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Upwork gigs sold with zero skills, delivered by ChatGPT

It's the cleanest proof that the side-hustle bottleneck is now sales and nerve, not capability — she landed a $3K/mo SEO retainer knowing nothing about SEO, in 1–2 hours a day around a full-time job and three kids.

What it is: Case-study interview with Sandy Lee, who went $0 → $52K/month in 9 months by winning freelance client work in skills she didn't have and delivering with ChatGPT.

How it works:

  • Start on Upwork, not with a product. She applied to random odd jobs with no experience, no testimonials, no portfolio. Seventh application landed a $3,000/mo SEO retainer.
  • Sell the outcome, let AI supply the skill. She knew nothing about SEO. The stack was a $20/mo ChatGPT sub. Chris's framing: "everyone is now a PhD in everything — we just need to connect the dots."
  • Constraint-first design. Full-time 9-to-5, three kids under eight → the whole thing had to run in 1–2 hours/day, with client delivery capped at ~1 hour/day. Anything requiring more got cut.
  • Application volume is the real skill. 7 applications → 1 retainer. The funnel is the product; the deliverable is downstream.
  • The mindset lever Chris hammers: irrational confidence has never been cheaper. "You either win or you learn" — he cites his own stack of failed businesses as answered questions, not losses.
  • Backstory as proof of motivation, not method: raised across seven countries alone from age 11, $2/day food budget in college, evicted, slept on a Hawaii beach until police moved her along, then fired alongside her husband the same day from her first job.

Caveat — read this before you copy it: the transcript covers the origin story and the first $3K retainer in detail, but the jump from $3K to $52K/mo is asserted, not broken down. Chris teases "here are some secret sauce" and the mechanics land later in the episode. Treat the first-client playbook as the actionable part; treat the $52K as a headline you still need to verify.

Tools & links:

  • Upwork — the acquisition channel; cold applications to gigs outside your skill set
  • ChatGPT — $20/mo, the entire delivery layer for SEO work she had no training in
  • beehiiv — sponsor; newsletter platform Chris has run for 18+ months, 30% off first 30 days

Why it matters for you: As an engineer you're already past the delivery bar she cleared with a $20 subscription — your gap is doing the unglamorous part she actually nailed, which is firing off applications for work you haven't done yet.
