Your life changes the moment you pick a date.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-18 · 9,758 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Put your big scary decision on the calendar.

The whole side-hustle bottleneck isn't strategy — it's that you never committed to a launch date, and this 60-second nudge fixes exactly that.

What it is: A one-action commitment hack — take the big decision you've been avoiding and assign it a literal date on your calendar.

How it works:

  • Identify the big decision already living in your head (quit the job, launch the product, fire the person, leave the relationship).
  • Put it on the calendar — a specific time and date, not a vague "someday."
  • Keep the date. The act of committing to a deadline is what forces the leap.
  • Codie says she's done this 3 times: quitting her job, going all-in on her business, and leaving her hardest relationship — and wouldn't have moved without the date.
  • The kicker: if you don't own your calendar, other people will — and whoever owns your calendar owns your life.

Tools & links:

  • No external tools, repos, or links mentioned — the only "tool" is your calendar app.

Why it matters for you: Most side hustles die in the "thinking about it" phase; setting a hard launch/quit date converts intention into a forcing function, which is the cheapest productivity lever you have.
