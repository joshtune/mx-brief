Most Powerful 16" Gaming Laptop Has a Secret

Alex Ziskind · 2026-07-02 · 29,936 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
MSI gaming laptops repurposed as local AI workstations.

If you're weighing a Windows/Nvidia box against a Mac for local AI, this pits RTX 5080 (16GB) vs 5090 (24GB) laptops as AI machines.

What it is: MSI Raider 16 Max HX gaming laptops (RTX 5080 and 5090 variants) tested as local-AI workstations — LLMs, coding agents, image generation — not games.

The two machines:

  • RTX 5080 version — 16 GB VRAM, all usable by LLMs
  • RTX 5090 version — 24 GB VRAM, fits larger models, faster generation
  • Both: Intel Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus, 16" 240Hz OLED (100% DCI-P3, HDR True Black 1000), 400W power brick, up to 300W total system power
  • Cooling: Cooler Boost Trinity — 3 fans, 6 heat pipes, 5 exhausts
  • Upgradeable: quick-access panel for RAM + SSD
  • Ports: SD card + USB-C/USB-A on sides; HDMI, ethernet, USB-A, power on back (runs a desk setup without a dock)

The AI angle:

  • Discrete Nvidia GPU means full CUDA workflows / the complete Nvidia AI stack — the thing a Mac can't do natively
  • VRAM is the gating factor: 16GB vs 24GB decides model size and speed
  • Actual benchmark numbers are held for the full video (this is a teaser)

Tools & links:

  • MSI Raider 16 Max HX — the laptops tested
  • Both available at Micro Center

Cost vs Mac mini caveat: He doesn't give prices here — but these are flagship RTX 5080/5090 gaming laptops with OLED panels and 400W bricks, so expect $2,500–$4,000+, well north of any Mac mini. The Mac mini trades raw VRAM/CUDA for far lower cost and power draw.

Why it matters for you: This is exactly the Nvidia-vs-Mac local-AI comparison you care about — the machine is the RTX 5080/5090 MSI Raider, but you'll need to wait for the full video (or check Micro Center) for the actual price gap against a Mac mini.
