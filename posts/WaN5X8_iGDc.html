The best businesses might just be hiding in your neighbor's to-don't list

Codie Sanchez · 2026-06-15 · 33,146 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Three sub-$100 service businesses you can start today.

If you want a side hustle with near-zero startup cost, this hands you three proven, demand-backed services you can launch this weekend with a cheap Amazon kit and a few door knocks.

What it is: A teardown of three dirt-cheap service businesses built on chores people hate doing — the 'to-don't list' of your own neighborhood.

The instructional secret: Don't chase a big idea or capital. Find a task people already pay to avoid, buy the cheapest viable toolkit, and sell directly to neighbors with before/after proof. Demand exists before you start — you're just showing up for it.

How it works:

  • Car washing (~$39.88 kit): Multi-brush kit; start with friends/family, shoot before/after photos, post online to spark comment-section inquiries, convert to paying jobs.
  • Dog waste cleanup (~$29.99 kit): Bin, scooper, bags. Target the obvious — the neighbor with the big dog. Knock, pitch, get hired on the spot for a chore everyone hates.
  • Headlight restoration (~$22.88 kit, cheapest): Easiest of the three; learn from a few hours of YouTube tutorials, spot foggy headlights around town, leave windshield notes, offer in-driveway service.

The playbook underneath all three:

  • Pick a hated, recurring chore (built-in demand, repeat revenue)
  • Buy the minimum-viable kit (<$40)
  • Prove value with before/after photos
  • Distribute door-to-door and via social comments — no ad budget
  • Service comes to them (driveway/home convenience as the selling point)

Tools & links: No specific products linked in the transcript — kits referenced are generic Amazon car-wash, dog-waste, and headlight-restoration kits at the prices above.

Why it matters for you: The real secret isn't the niche — it's the model: monetize chores people will pay to skip, start under $100, and sell on proof + proximity instead of capital. That's a side hustle you can validate this week.
