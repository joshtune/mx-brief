This ring replaces your keyboard?

Matt Wolfe · 2026-07-20 · 14,421 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A titanium smart ring for voice-to-text input.

Skip it — this is consumer hardware with zero bearing on building web apps.

What it is: The Oasis 1, a $289 titanium smart ring that replaces keyboard/mouse input with whispered voice dictation plus a thumb trackpad.

How it works:

  • Noise-isolating mic captures whispered speech; Wispr Flow transcribes it to text
  • Top of the ring is a miniature 2D trackpad — swipe with your thumb to scroll, move the cursor, navigate, or edit dictated text
  • Haptic feedback, ~16 hours battery
  • Targets Macs, iPhones, and Apple Vision Pro

Tools & links:

  • Wispr Flow — the speech-to-text engine powering dictation
  • Oasis 1 — pre-orders open, $289, not sponsored per Matt

Why it matters for you: It doesn't — this is a gadget teaser, not something that touches how you build or ship web apps. The only tangentially useful nugget is Wispr Flow as a dictation tool, and even that's a stretch.

Bottom line: 90-second hardware hype clip. Nothing to build with here.
