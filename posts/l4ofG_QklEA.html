Stop Letting People Decide For You...

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-24 · 9,366 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Stop giving everyone equal votes on your decisions.

Side hustles die in the gap between 'I have an idea' and 'everyone I know thinks it's dumb' — this gives you a 4-question filter to decide whose doubt actually counts.

What it is: A short mindset clip: you don't have to stop caring what people think, you have to stop letting their opinions make your decisions.

How it works (the instructional secret — a 4-question criticism filter):

  • Have they actually done what I'm trying to do? No experience = opinion, not data.
  • Are they affected by the decision? No skin in the game, no vote.
  • Do they actually want me to win? Some critics are protecting their own comfort, not your outcome.
  • Do I even care what they think? If not, drop it and move on.
  • Reframe: The goal isn't indifference. It's *decoupling* feedback from action. Listen, then run it through the filter before you act.
  • Final vote: Yours. 'The opinion that gets the biggest vote is you in the mirror.'
  • Note: The transcript is cut off at the end (the 'Now you go do the...' line), so the closing call to action is likely 'go do the thing', but that is an inference.
