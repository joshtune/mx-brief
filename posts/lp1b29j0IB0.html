How to Tailor Your Stories to Company Values

A Life Engineered · 2026-07-08 · 5,526 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Match interview stories to each company's core values.

Your STAR stories won't land unless they hit the specific value each company screens for — this shows you how to reframe them per employer.

What it is: A short reminder that behavioral interview stories must be tailored to what a given company actually values, not framed around generic "big project" impact.

How it works:

  • Interviewers rarely ask the obvious "tell me about a big project" — they probe for impact beyond traditional metrics.
  • Different companies weight different values, so the same story needs a different emphasis per employer.
  • Amazon (per his tenure) prioritizes customer satisfaction — so surface stories where customers loved the outcome.
  • Other companies may not care about customer satisfaction at all; research each company's values before interviewing.
  • The signal you want to trigger in an interviewer: "this person fits *here*."

Prep checklist:

  • Identify the target company's stated (and cultural) values before the loop.
  • Map each of your stories to the value it best demonstrates.
  • Re-frame the same experience to lead with the value that company screens for (e.g., customer impact at Amazon).
  • Build a small library of stories so you can pull the right angle on demand.

  • None mentioned in the transcript. (For Amazon specifically, map stories to its Leadership Principles.)
