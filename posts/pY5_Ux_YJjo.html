Codex Ultrafast vs standard: speed, quality, usage cost

If you're weighing a $500/mo Codex plan, this shows what 8x speed actually buys you (and burns) on real agentic tasks.

What it is: Nate benchmarks Codex's new Ultrafast mode (GPT-6 Astra, $500/mo plan) against standard on real tasks, comparing speed, output quality, and weekly usage burn.

How it works:

  • Ultrafast spec: Only on GPT-6 Astra, only on the new $500/mo plan (not the $100 or $200 tiers) or via API. Claimed 8x faster than standard and 4x faster than regular Fast mode, up to ~300 tokens/sec. Coming to GPT-6.1 Soul, which is cheaper.
  • Test 1, retrieval: Asked Codex "what did I talk about in this week's community Q&A call?" It found the right call, pulled the transcript, and summarized a 54-minute call in 19 seconds.
  • Test 2, video edit: Asked Astra to cut a 60-second, 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) showreel of his Google San Jose trip. Inputs: two files totaling 11 min of him reflecting on the trip, plus a folder of 145+ GB of raw footage.
  • Setup: Both runs used extra-high reasoning, one on standard and one on Ultrafast.
  • Ultrafast result: ~19 minutes, burned 17% of weekly usage on the $500 plan.
  • Standard result: ~50 minutes, burned 4% of weekly usage.
  • Quality: Nate preferred the Ultrafast cut. It felt more Google-branded and told a better story. Standard clipped his sentences off before they finished. He notes the difference may be partly run-to-run variance, not purely the mode.
  • Takeaway: Ultrafast is about 2.6x faster in wall-clock on the heavy task but costs ~4x the usage. You pay for time, not efficiency.
  • Caveat: The transcript cuts off mid-comparison, so further examples and his final verdict on usage are not covered here.
