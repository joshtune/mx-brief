Want to Make Money With AI? Stop Just Chatting With It

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-29 · 33,414 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Use Claude connectors to find and sell digital products

If you want a side hustle with near-zero startup cost, this shows how to have Claude mine your existing content for digital products you can sell today.

What it is: A Koerner Office interview with a Beehiiv exec on using Claude + MCP connectors to spot your expertise and turn it into sellable digital products (the transcript cuts off mid-example).

How it works:

  • MCP = connectors. Give Claude access to your apps (Gmail, Calendar, Linear, Beehiiv, Stripe, Mercury, Notion, Zapier, Figma, Google Drive) so it works from real context instead of you pasting or screenshotting things.
  • Setup takes seconds: Claude > Customize > Connectors > add a custom connector > paste the MCP URL > authenticate in one click.
  • The side-hustle play:
  • Connect wherever your content lives: newsletter, X, LinkedIn, Drive.
  • Prompt Claude to find where you're a subject matter expert.
  • Ask it to suggest digital products: courses, templates, guides, coaching sessions.
  • Let it check your Drive for work you've already done. The guest's example: Claude found an old webinar presentation that was already 80% of a newsletter playbook.
  • Package it and sell it, in this case through Beehiiv's new digital products feature.
  • Claimed results (teased in the intro, not shown in this excerpt):
  • About $10,000 from roughly 1,000 sales at $10, from an idea Claude generated.
  • About $12K in a couple of hours from three prompts.
  • Chris calls it "found money".
  • Bonus use case: Hook up several finance MCPs (Kubera, Stripe, Mercury, Robinhood) and ask Claude "what dumb decisions are hurting my net worth?" Chris's example finding: you're undercharging in Stripe.
  • Caveat: The video is sponsored by Beehiiv (code CHRIS30 for 30% off 3 months), and the guest works there. Treat the income claims as promo until you see the receipts.
