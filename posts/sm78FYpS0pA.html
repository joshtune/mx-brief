Why a Sr. Engineer took $36M over $200M

My First Million · 2026-08-20 · 70,080 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Meta's cash offers are gutting AI startup hiring.

A concrete, dollar-figure look at what Meta is actually paying senior AI infra engineers — and the risk math that makes guaranteed cash beat a 10x startup lottery ticket.

What it is: A short clip where a founder (Hark) recounts losing a senior AI infrastructure engineer to Meta over a competing comp package — and argues Meta's talent-buying spree is not a one-off blip.

The featured deal — the actual numbers:

  • Candidate: Senior engineer on the AI infra side, coming from xAI
  • Hark's offer: Series A equity package worth $15–20M in stock
  • Hark's pitch: "We could 10x Hark pretty quick" → upside framed as ~$200M if the company hits big, ~$20M if it merely does fine
  • Meta's offer: $36M over 4 years (~$9M/yr), described as "kind of guaranteed cash"
  • Outcome: He took Meta. Hark's founder notes he doesn't have Meta stock himself — i.e., no way to price the counter

The math the engineer actually ran:

  • Startup equity: illiquid, dilution-exposed, binary — a lottery ticket priced at a 409A number that may never clear
  • Meta package: liquid RSUs on a public ticker, vesting on a fixed schedule, downside floor of $36M
  • Expected-value framing: $36M certain > $200M × P(success) once P is honestly discounted for a Series A
  • Risk-adjusted, a Series A stock grant at $15–20M notional is worth a fraction of face value; Meta pays near-face

Why this isn't a blip:

  • Speaker admits he assumed Meta's mega-offers were a ~1-year phenomenon that would fade — "They've not stopped"
  • Meta is buying its way into the AI race with comp rather than winning on mission or product
  • Every candidate Hark speaks to is fielding "some absurd absurd thing" — the offer inflation is now the baseline, not the exception
  • Practical effect: Series A startups can no longer compete for senior AI infra talent on equity alone

Why it matters for you: If you're a senior engineer near AI infra, the ceiling on your market price is now public-company cash in the eight figures — and if you're the one hiring, equity upside stories no longer clear the bar against a guaranteed $9M/yr.
