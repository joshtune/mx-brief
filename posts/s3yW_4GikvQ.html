Build a niche paid job board with AI

If you want a low-cost, low-maintenance side hustle, this is a concrete blueprint: a niche job board that companies pay to post on, built in an afternoon for about $10/month.

What it is: A niche job board (one industry, one website) where employers pay per listing, built with an AI app builder instead of code.

How it works:

  • The pitch: Companies pay roughly $200-500 to post a single job. Big boards like Indeed bury niche roles, so industry-specific boards (dental offices, truck drivers, churches) are still wide open.
  • The math: $99/posting x 20 postings/month = ~$2K/month. Add a few featured top-of-page spots (~$199 each) and it's closer to $3K/month. *(The transcript says "$1.99"; that looks like a transcription slip, and $199 fits the numbers.)*
  • Costs: About $10/month for the AI tool plus about $10/year for the domain.
  • Niche picking: Ask several AI models the same question ("What's an underserved niche job board I could build? One specific industry or role, who pays to post, and what would they pay per posting?") and compare. In the part of the video I could see, route LLM suggested sterile processing technicians for outpatient surgery centers, at roughly $150/job. The other models' answers were cut off.
  • Build: Describe the site in plain English to Abacus AI Agent. It builds the app, database, hosting and payments, then publishes it.
  • Finding supply: He says he finds 20 real job openings in any niche in about 4 minutes, so the board isn't empty at launch. That method wasn't covered in the part available to me.
  • Selling to employers: Send companies already hiring in the niche a short outreach email and invite them to post. At the end he has an AI agent email 100+ companies, and replies go in the pinned comment. Prompts and the email template are promised in a free Google Doc in the description.
  • Exit: Job boards sell for roughly 2-3x yearly profit on marketplaces, so this is also a flip-able asset.

Why it matters for you: It's a cheap side-hustle template: pick a boring niche, build a board in an afternoon, cold-email employers, and you have a small recurring-revenue site you can later sell. The real work is getting employers to pay, not building the site.
