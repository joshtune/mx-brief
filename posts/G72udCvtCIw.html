The people who try to bring others down are the ones that actually have nothing going on.

Codie Sanchez · 2026-09-26 · 3,366 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Critics with no wins of their own try to shrink you.

There's no tactic here — it's a mindset check for anyone building a side hustle who's about to get discouraged by a hater instead of a customer.

What it is: A short mindset rant, not a tutorial — Codie's framework for filtering feedback by who's giving it.

How it works:

  • People who are winning don't feel threatened by your win — they share, intro, and support instead of poking holes.
  • People who question every move, nitpick your business, or try to "humble" you when you level up usually haven't built anything themselves.
  • She draws this from years in finance/private equity, where the incentive was to keep you small and employed, not independent.
  • Her filter: judge advice by the builder's track record, not their confidence or tenure.

Tools & links:

  • None mentioned — pure mindset content, no product, framework name, or resource cited.

Why it matters for you: When you start a side hustle, the loudest critics are often coworkers or friends with nothing built — treat their doubt as a signal to ignore, not a reason to stop.
