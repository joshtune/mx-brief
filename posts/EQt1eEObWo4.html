Non-coders selling pre-built AI automation to local businesses

It's a concrete side-hustle template: build an AI service once, sell it to local businesses for a few thousand dollars, and collect a monthly retainer.

What it is: An AI automation agency for local businesses. You sell a pre-built AI workflow for a one-time setup fee plus a monthly retainer, and you don't need to code.

How it works:

  • Setup fee: about $2,000-$3,000 per client. The workflow is already built, so you're reselling it, not building from scratch.
  • Retainer: $500-$1,000/month per client. The AI does the ongoing work, and "a robot writes the monthly report" you send them.
  • Cost to run: one clip cites about $50 all-in. The excerpt doesn't say what that covers, so treat it as unverified.
  • Who buys: local businesses. The pitch that closes is a dollar figure. The guest's 19-year-old son signed a client told the service would save over $100K/year.
  • Guest's path: he tried 8 different AI businesses in 6 months while still employed. The 9th now clears $1M+/year in profit, with no office and a team of 3.
  • Claim to verify: the title says $2.7K/day, but the excerpt only supports "over a million dollars this year."
