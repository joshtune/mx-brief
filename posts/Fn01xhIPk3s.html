How it works (the investing timeline):

  • Passed at ~$5B (Series C era): Believed the market had no room for a *second* LLM behind OpenAI, and feared Anthropic couldn't raise enough capital to compete.
  • The unlock — Amazon's ~$20B round: Amazon led a later round valuing the company around $20B. This is the moment the thesis flipped: it proved Anthropic had "clear access to capital."
  • Bought at $180B: By the next round he saw, the valuation had hit $180B — and he invested then.

The lesson he's selling:

  • His mistake wasn't doubting the *product* — it was underpricing *capital access* as the real moat in the LLM arms race.
  • Once a hyperscaler (Amazon) committed, the funding-to-compete risk evaporated, and so did the cheap entry price.
