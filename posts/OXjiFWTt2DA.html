Part 2/2 of Vibe coding an app and finding customers LIVE!

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-08-22 · 3,554 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Non-coder finishes a Replit app, hunts first customer

It's a live, unedited look at someone with zero coding background shipping a real micro-SaaS and immediately trying to sell it — the exact side-hustle loop, warts included.

What it is: Part 2 of a live build: Chris Koerner finishes onlywaivers.com, a digital liability-waiver tool for youth sports/church groups, then goes straight into customer acquisition on stream.

The idea:

  • Problem he actually has: getting parents to sign youth liability waivers is a paper, handwritten, allergy-field-laden mess.
  • Fix: replace the clipboard with a link. Parents sign on a phone; organizer gets the data.
  • Pricing already live: $13 and $17 tiers, plus a 100% satisfaction / lifetime refund policy — deliberately over-generous to remove all friction on customer #1.
  • Built entirely in Replit with no coding background ("didn't know what HTML stands for").

How it works (build side):

  • Total build ≈ 3 hours of live streaming in part 1, plus ~1 unplanned hour before this stream (he admits to breaking his own "don't touch it" rule).
  • Latest work was a single prompt: "add more trust signals."
  • Trust-signal tactics worth stealing:
  • Shot a selfie video for the Facebook ad, uploaded it to Google Drive, handed Replit the Drive link, told it to embed the same video on the homepage. Redundant on purpose — the visitor sees the exact face they just clicked on, so it doesn't read as a bait-and-switch.
  • Pointed Replit at chrisjkoerner.com and told it to scrape his own bio + headshot into the homepage rather than retyping it. Prompt-as-ETL.

How it works (customer side):

  • Facebook ads: already built and configured in part 1, just needs reactivation — the setup work was the hard part, not the spend.
  • Reddit: posts into a couple of narrowly targeted subreddits.
  • Facebook Groups: youth-sports / parent-organizer groups.
  • Marketing to date: essentially zero (one post with zero views). Revenue: zero. He's transparent that "find customers" is the part he's worried about.
  • Answers the classic objection from chat ("shouldn't you find the audience first?") with: he's doing both simultaneously, in public.

Tools & links:

  • Replit — the vibe-coding environment doing all the building from natural-language prompts
  • onlywaivers.com — the product itself, live
  • chrisjkoerner.com — bio source he scraped into his own landing page
  • Google Drive — cheap video host for the embedded trust-signal clip
  • Multi-platform simulcast: LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube (build-in-public distribution, one recording)

Caveats worth noting:

  • No customers yet as of this stream. This is a build-and-distribute demo, not a proof of demand.
  • Product is "basically done" by his own admission, meaning bugs are unfound, not absent.

Why it matters for you: As an engineer the build is the easy half — the transferable part here is the distribution playbook (trust signals, pre-built ad campaigns, targeted subreddits) and the reminder that a $13 product with a lifetime refund policy is a legitimate way to buy your first customer.
