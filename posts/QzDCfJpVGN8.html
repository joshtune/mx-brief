Shopping Online Is About To Change Forever

Matt Wolfe · 2026-06-08 · 11,847 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Sponsored pitch for agentic commerce app Glance.

It previews the agentic-commerce UX pattern — agents doing discovery before the user searches — which is a product direction you could build into a web app.

What it is: A #Ad video pitching "agentic commerce" via a sponsored app called Glance — AI agents that pre-shop for you before you search.

How it works:

  • Premise: instead of searching endless sites, AI agents do the discovery up front and match you to products.
  • Glance uses a *visual* dialogue with the agent, not just chat-based prompting.
  • Onboarding from a single selfie: it analyzes skin tone, eye color, face, and infers that warm colors suit you.
  • Generates outfit looks based on style, budget, and even your calendar events.
  • Virtual try-on so you see outfits worn, with direct buy links per item.
  • Conversational refinement: chat with the agent for alternatives.
  • Distribution angle: not a download — pre-installed on Samsung, Motorola, and DirecTV devices.
  • Pitched as expanding beyond clothes into travel, home, cars, etc.

Tools & links:

  • Glance — sponsored agentic-commerce app (try-free link in Wolfe's bio)
  • FutureTools — Wolfe's AI tools directory

Why it matters for you: Mostly an ad with zero technical depth — but the UX pattern (agent does discovery/personalization *before* the user queries, then renders a visual dialogue) is a concrete idea worth stealing for your web apps.
