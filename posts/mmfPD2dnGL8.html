The Most Profitable Business You Can Run From a Phone

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-08 · 18,696 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Semi-trailer leasing side hustle run entirely from a phone

It's a near-zero-capital, phone-only side hustle: lease trailers, sublease them for a markup, and a guy with no industry experience scaled it to 300 trailers.

What it is: Trailer arbitrage. You lease 53-ft semi trailers from a big company and rent them to truckers for more than you pay.

How it works:

  • Origin story: Eli was a Connecticut real estate agent in 2019 who matched truckers with loads for about $80 a pop.
  • First deal: His uncle asked to rent a semi trailer, and Eli knew nothing about the business. He found a company to lease him a 53-ft trailer for about $350/mo and rented it to his uncle for about $750/mo. That's about $400/mo spread on one unit.
  • Startup cost: An insurance policy, and nothing else. In the intro Eli says he "just had to pay my insurance."
  • Lead gen: Facebook Marketplace posts. Eli says he hit $7,500/mo recurring within his first 2 months from those posts alone.
  • Scale: About 300 trailers after 6 years, profiting over $50K/mo, with two virtual employees he never met in person.
  • Ops: The whole business runs from a phone. Eli has been traveling for years, including running it from Colombia. He says any AI can take you "90% of the way there."
  • Caveat: The transcript is only the teaser and intro. The actual steps (which lessors, the insurance type and cost, pricing, contracts, how to vet renters) are not in what I was given. Watch the full episode for those.

Tools & links:

  • No tools or URLs are named in the intro. Facebook Marketplace is the lead channel, and an AI assistant is suggested for filling in the knowledge gaps.

Why it matters for you: If you want a side hustle you can run from your phone with almost no capital, this is a template: find the supply, find the demand, pocket the spread. Check the details (insurance, lessor terms, renter risk) before copying it.
