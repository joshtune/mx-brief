A concrete, part-time-friendly side hustle: build a demo AI lead-gen widget, pitch local businesses, and charge recurring monthly fees. Bob reports ~$25–30K/month after a year.

What it is: An 'AI concierge for small businesses' service. Bob puts an AI helper on a client's website to capture and qualify leads, then charges a monthly retainer.

How it works:

  • The offer: An AI chat helper on a small business's website that engages visitors and turns them into leads.
  • Proof point: One client sells $13,000 engines. In month one, the helper produced 50 people raising their hands (50 new leads). Chris's rough math puts that at $500K to $1M in potential sales for the client.
  • Pricing: Priced against the value of the leads, not the hours worked. Bob's numbers are $25–30K/month total, an average engine of $13K, and a $1,000/month average client. The full pricing math is teased for later in the interview and is not in the excerpt provided.
  • Sales motion: He builds a custom demo for the prospect *before* walking in, shows it to them, and they get hooked. Close rate is about 30%.
  • Time cost: Roughly 1–2 hours per month per client once it is running.
  • Scale math: There are 33M small businesses in the US. At $1,000/month per client, 100 clients is $100K/month.
  • Side-hustle fit: Chris says you can do it nights and weekends alongside a full-time job. Bob is 60 and describes himself as not a tech person.
