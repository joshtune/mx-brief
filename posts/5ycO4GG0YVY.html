Upgrading an ASUS GX10 AI box to 4TB

You'll learn which machine he's running AI on (ASUS GX10, a GB10 / DGX Spark-style box), but this one is about storage and gives no Mac mini price comparison, so skim it.

What it is: A teardown-lite showing how to swap the ASUS GX10's stock 1TB SSD for a 4TB drive by cloning it.

How it works:

  • The machine is the ASUS GX10, the "cheapest" GB10 (DGX Spark-class) box. It ships with only 1TB of SSD.
  • He clones the stock drive onto a Corsair MP700 Micro 2242 4TB NVMe with a standalone M.2 duplicator. Source goes in slot A, blank drive in B, then hold the button for 3 seconds.
  • He pops the new drive in and it boots with no extra setup.
  • `df -h` still shows about 916GB, because the root partition is still 1TB.
  • The Disks utility shows the 4TB drive with an EFI partition, a 1TB root partition and about 3TB of free space.
  • You can either make a separate partition for model storage or extend root into the free space.
  • His verdict: easy, "not Apple rocket science".
