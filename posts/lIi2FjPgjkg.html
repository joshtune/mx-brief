This Tech CEO Is Building the “Linux of AI”

Matt Wolfe · 2026-07-12 · 17,374 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Qualcomm CEO bets on distributed AI across cloud and edge.

It's a hardware-vision interview with almost nothing you can act on for building web apps.

What it is: A short interview with Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon pitching "distributed intelligence" — AI that runs wherever it makes sense (cloud, edge, device).

How it works:

  • Core thesis: the next AI battle is about *where* AI runs, not who has the biggest model — "resistance to the edge is futile."
  • "Fabric of computes": workloads route to cloud or edge (phones, cars, PCs, wearables) based on fit.
  • Hardware play: Dragonwing/Dragonfly C1000 CPU aimed at data-center "orchestrators" — pitched as highest-performance, lowest-power.
  • Software play: Qualcomm's agreement to acquire Modular, aiming to make it "the Linux of AI" — an open, horizontal layer that runs models across any hardware.
  • Framing: closed systems lose, open horizontal systems win.

Tools & links:

  • Modular — AI infra/compiler stack (Mojo, MAX) Qualcomm is acquiring to abstract across hardware
  • Qualcomm — chips from phones to data centers
  • futuretools.io — Matt's AI tool directory

Why it matters for you: Almost nothing here touches web-app building — it's chip and infra strategy; the only distant relevance is that a cross-hardware "Linux of AI" could eventually make on-device/edge inference easier to target, but that's years and layers away from your stack.
