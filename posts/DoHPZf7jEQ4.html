The 3 AI Agency Mistakes Keeping You From $20K/Month Retainers

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-08-25 · 15,516 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Conference talk on why AI agency deals go sideways.

This is Nate off-script at a Montenegro conference — no n8n canvas, no repos, just positioning advice, so skip it unless you want the business framing behind his builds.

What it is: A recorded keynote ("Still Relevant Next Year") from the Workless AI event in Montenegro, framed around a client who told Nate on a Zoom call: *"We are not getting the value that we paid for."*

The three mistakes:

  • Built the wrong thing. He shipped the viral personal-assistant agent because the client asked for it — but that assistant wasn't the actual constraint in the client's business. Demand came from the YouTube video, not from a diagnosed bottleneck.
  • No KPI. Nothing was agreed up front as the measure of success, so "value delivered" became a matter of opinion — and the client's opinion was the one that counted.
  • Guessed on price. Pricing was pulled out of the air rather than derived from the value of the constraint being removed.

The reframe he's selling:

  • Your job is to diagnose and prove, not to take tickets and build what's on them.
  • Progression: ticket-taker → doctor (diagnose the pain) → prove the value, because unproven value doesn't convert into more business.
  • "AI automation" is mostly rebranded automation. He's ex-Goldman Sachs analyst (Univ. of Iowa 2024, business analytics + marketing), automated plenty there, and jokes his agency is really "a data infrastructure agency" — then "just a boring automation agency." The AI label is what made business owners finally care.
  • Non-technical background; the data fluency, not the coding, is what transferred.
