Grok Bot Manages My Inbox (and has its own)

Nate Herk | AI Automation · 2026-09-16 · 35,109 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Two Grok bots: one cleans Nate's inbox, one trades stocks via email.

A copyable 5-10 minute setup for an AI agent that auto-labels your inbox and pings you on ClickUp, plus a wilder pattern for giving a bot its own email address as a webhook trigger.

What it is: Two Grok-bot builds — "Nat," an inbox-cleaning agent, and "Trader," a bot with its own email address that executes stock trades when it receives an email.

How it works (Nat, the inbox manager):

  • Create a new bot and prompt it directly: define its name/persona and the label taxonomy you want (Nate used Primary, Notifications, Outreach, Urgent, then added Newsletters when the bot suggested it).
  • Describe routing rules in plain language — e.g. bank/service emails → Notifications, sponsorship/pitch emails → Outreach, active conversations or bills → Primary/Urgent (marked as read).
  • Connect the inbox via the bot marketplace (search Gmail or Outlook), and give each connected account a label if managing multiple inboxes — each can get its own agent.
  • Set a twice-daily schedule (Nate runs his at 6:45 a.m. and 6:45 p.m.).
  • Have it send a ClickUp DM notification each run summarizing urgent items, with a "sent by Nat" tag — the bot only labels/marks-read, it doesn't draft replies.

How it works (Trader, the email-triggered trading bot):

  • The bot's routine fires on a webhook (an incoming email), not a timer.
  • A separate GPT-5 ("Astra") agent does trade research and emails Trader's dedicated inbox with instructions like "place this trade" or "sell this."
  • Trader reads the email and executes the trade in the stock market — effectively using email as an agent-to-agent messaging/trigger layer.
