The Most Profitable Side Hustle You've Never Heard Of

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-07-02 · 20,459 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Plant bingo: a zero-cost, high-margin local event business.

It's a genuine $0-startup side hustle doing ~$1,000/hour profit that anyone can copy by piggybacking on existing bars, venues, and viral TikTok demand.

What it is: Host bingo nights where prizes are houseplants, run at bars/restaurants/venues for a ticket fee.

How it works:

  • Charge attendees a ticket price ($10 entry at the smallest event; events now clear ~$3,000 profit each)
  • Every attendee goes home with a plant — cost averages $10-12/person (mix of $5-10 plants and $25-50 statement plants)
  • Zero startup cost: partner with venues that already run weekly trivia and want another busy night
  • Bars/restaurants provide the room and often the crowd; you bring the game and plants
  • Piggyback distribution: partner venues/plant vendors with 20k-30k followers share your content, so reach is built-in even with ~200 personal followers
  • No influencer status needed — first event was mostly strangers pulled from a couple of TikTok/Instagram posts
  • Validate cheap first: their first event was a $10 loss-leader with free pizza to prove the model and work out kinks

The origin play (how to copy):

  • Idea spotted from a viral TikTok of a California group doing plant bingo (several million-view videos)
  • Validated demand by checking the original's website: 10 events next month, half already sold out = proof of concept
  • Scale signals: ~$21k in February, ~$25k projected the next month, $30k profit in first two months; sold out first event in 4-5 days

Tools & links:

  • Bizee — LLC formation service (video sponsor, used to spin up the business entity)
  • TikTok / Instagram — the entire acquisition channel; visual format is inherently shareable

Why it matters for you: This is the archetype of a copy-a-proven-viral-idea side hustle — low capital, validated demand, and distribution you borrow rather than build — exactly the shiny-object-with-a-playbook you're scanning this channel for.
