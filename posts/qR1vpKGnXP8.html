MSI RTX Spark laptop vs DGX Spark, local LLMs

He runs local AI on an MSI Prestige N16 Flip AI+ (128 GB) and a DGX Spark, but the excerpt gives no prices and no Mac mini comparison, so you'll need the full video or the MSI link for cost.

What it is: Alex tests the first RTX Spark laptop, the MSI Prestige N16 Flip AI+, running big local LLMs against his DGX Spark, on wall power and on battery.

Machines he's running AI on:

  • MSI Prestige N16 Flip AI+ — a thin laptop with 128 GB unified memory, and the GPU can use about 96 GB of it
  • NVIDIA DGX Spark — his existing desktop-class box, used as the comparison baseline

How it works:

  • A ~120B model loads in 28 s and uses 61 of the 96 GB of GPU-addressable memory
  • It generates 65 tok/s, and 65.9 tok/s on battery, so unplugging doesn't slow GPU work
  • vLLM with 8 simultaneous users reaches 127 tok/s total, and those users could be agents
  • Qwen 3.8 Flash (~125B) takes 81 GB and runs at 33.7 tok/s
  • It works as a fully local coding assistant inside the Zed agent, and nothing leaves the machine
  • Open question: whether it throttles after 20 minutes of heavy load, as MSI claims it won't. The excerpt doesn't show the answer or the head-to-head numbers against the DGX Spark
