How to Buy a Truck Without a Bank

Codie Sanchez · 2026-07-18 · 79,352 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Buy equipment by assuming the seller's existing loan.

It's a no-credit, no-bank way to control expensive equipment for your side hustle using someone else's financing.

What it is: "Subject-to" financing — you take over the seller's existing loan payments instead of buying the asset outright or getting your own bank loan.

How it works:

  • Target asset: an $80,000 truck. Seller still owes $45k at 5%, paying ~$1,350/month.
  • Instead of $80k cash or a 90/10 bank loan, you take over the $1,350/month payment.
  • Sweeten it: pay the seller an extra $500/month for 20 months to make the deal worth their while.
  • Your total nut is $1,850/month, but you control an $80k asset with zero bank application.
  • The mindset shift: operators buy payments, not prices. Poor mindset says "the bank said no." Operator asks "whose existing debt can I structure a deal around?"

What you actually need to pull it off:

  • Real, written contracts (don't do this on a handshake).
  • Proper insurance on the asset.
  • The cash flow / discipline to actually make the payments.

Why it matters for you: If you're starting a cash-flow side hustle that needs equipment (hauling, landscaping, mobile services), this is how you acquire the gear without credit or a big cash pile — you finance growth off assets that already have debt in place.
