The Most Passive Business You Can Start With $20

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · 2026-10-31 · 16,838 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
Build niche directory websites with AI for passive income

A concrete, near-zero-cost side hustle: build a simple directory site with AI, monetize with ads or leads, and spend about 30 minutes every few months maintaining it.

What it is: Directory websites (curated lists of a niche, like thrift stores) built with an AI coding tool, ranked on Google, and monetized with ads or lead sales.

How it works:

  • The idea: Pick a niche people search for and make a simple list. Example: Frey shut down a shoe business after 5 years, learned how people find websites on Google, and built a directory of thrift stores.
  • Build: No coding background needed. Frey watched YouTube tutorials and started building. A Claude Code subscription is the main startup cost (the video pitches it as roughly $20).
  • Monetize: Ads brought in $1,200 in the first month and peaked around $3,000/month. Directories can also sell leads. One site had Lorem Ipsum on the homepage and still landed a $20,000 lead.
  • Get found: Learn how Google ranks sites (SEO). Frey credits one $50 link from another website as a big reason the thrift store site has made about $150k total.
  • Maintenance: About 30 minutes every four months. Sometimes he didn't touch it for 6 months.
  • Exit value: The highest offer on his first directory was around $50k.
  • Case studies teased: A 3-month-old site that is just a list of links to government websites already gets 42,000 organic visitors/month. Another site reportedly reached $10K/month in six months.
  • Still to come (not in the excerpt): The exact five-sentence email used to get a $50 backlink, plus the full process for finding ideas, building with AI, and ranking on Google.

Tools & links:

  • Claude Code (https://claude.com/claude-code) — AI coding tool used to build the site
  • Google Search / SEO — the main traffic source
  • Ad network (unnamed in the excerpt) — the first revenue stream

Why it matters for you: If you want a low-cost, low-maintenance side hustle, directories are one of the cheapest to start, and the transcript only covers the intro, so watch the full episode for the idea-finding method and the outreach email.
