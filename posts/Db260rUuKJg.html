If you build on Anthropic models, the US government just forced Fable 5 and Mythos 5 offline overnight — a hard lesson in API/model supply-chain risk.

What it is: A weekly AI news recap whose lead story is the first-ever US government-forced shutdown of a commercially released AI model.

How it works:

  • US gov ordered Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 + Mythos 5 access for any foreign national (inside or outside the US), including foreign-national employees.
  • Impossible to enforce selectively, so Anthropic pulled both models for ALL customers worldwide — gone Friday June 12th, less than a week after release.
  • Trigger: a 'highly credible trusted partner' testing Fable found a jailbreak of the guardrails; admin told Dario to fix it or pull the model, he refused, citing it as not serious.
  • Plot twist: the whistleblower was reportedly Amazon CEO Andy Jassy — despite Amazon being one of Anthropic's biggest investors.
  • Context: Dario had spent months calling these models nuclear-bomb-dangerous and, days earlier, published an essay arguing the gov SHOULD be able to block/reverse model releases FAA-style — then got exactly that.
  • Wolfe's read: less about security, more about gov–Anthropic friction; the admin pulled it because Anthropic dismissed the issue rather than taking it seriously.
