Founder’s Three Jobs

Codie Sanchez · 2026-10-15 · 36,893 views  ·  Watch on YouTube →
A CEO's only three jobs: clarity, speed, optimism.

If your side hustle calendar looks like an employee's, this is the 90-second audit that shows you're still working in the business, not running it.

What it is: Codie's short-form framework for what a founder/CEO should actually spend calendar time on, and nothing else.

The instructional secret: Audit your calendar against three jobs. If there's no time blocked for them, you're an employee inside your own machine.

How it works:

  • Job 1: Make it crystal clear what people work on. Your team (or contractors, VAs, freelancers) should never guess what matters. Put one owner and one deadline next to every item that's due.
  • Job 2: Push everyone to move faster. Money loves speed. Speed rarely dies from laziness; it dies because a decision is waiting or something was unclear. Find the bottleneck and remove it every single day.
  • Job 3: Be delusional in your optimism. Not ignoring the numbers, but being the person the team believes in. When things go sideways, your job is to say "we got this."
  • The test: Open your calendar. Where are the blocks for clarity, unblocking, and morale? If they're missing, you're doing employee work.

Side hustle translation:

  • Clarity: Keep a one-page list: task, owner, due date. Even if the only owners are you and one freelancer.
  • Speed: Daily 10-minute "what's stuck?" check. Most stuck items are decisions only you can make, so make them.
  • Optimism: When a customer churns or a launch flops, your reaction sets the tempo for everyone helping you.
