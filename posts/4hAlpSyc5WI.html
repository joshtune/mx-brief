How it works:

  • The signal isn't what someone knows about you, it's whether they ask about you.
  • Red flag: dates and relationships where you do all the question-asking and they just enjoy the mirror.
  • The test: have they asked "Who are you? What lights you up?"
  • Gut-check for an existing relationship: ask "What do you find interesting about me?" and watch the answer.
